*A finished unofficial three-book Forbidden Lands line for harsher journeys, long campaigns, and wilderness that fights back.*

This is Apostol Apostolov’s fan-made second-edition manuscript set: Corebook, Gamemaster’s Guide, and Book of Beasts, plus a skill suite for GMs and AI coworkers who want to expand the line in the same register. It is not an official Free League release.

## What’s New in 1.1.5

- Learn **CHEF** Rank 1 by surviving in the wild (XP + Quarter Days), without a teacher.
- Finish training Quarter Days before you can afford the XP, then learn the skill or talent when you pay.
- Clearer mounted archery and weather / HEAT rules in the Corebook.
- Deeper lore tooling for kin culture (including orc society) kept separate from religion references.

See the full [changelog](./CHANGELOG.md).

## Highlights

Ordered the way the Corebook reads. These are the systems this edition actually built out or rebuilt hard — not a chapter list.

- **Expanded Skill Uses.** Resolution now has clearer stakes for hard results, group stealth, surprise, and chases. Optional Linked Rolls let a strong beat (or a necessary failure) change the next roll in a coordinated action. Myths add a growing die of secret knowledge earned from sages and recovered books, used when that narrow field actually matters.

- **Demonic Magic Path.** Sorcerers can walk the Path of the Unclean into Demonic Magic, a seventeenth discipline built around *mog* and hunted in settlements. The path ranks change what the caster can risk and what the world sees.

- **Expanded Combat with Weapon Reach.** Close combat cares more about spacing, brace, and shields, including how monsters cut in. Fear and panic have a clearer Wits-break sequence so terror is a procedure, not a one-off mood.

- **Expanded Critical Injuries.** Criticals include acid, cold, and swallow tables, and the worst results can leave lasting ruin instead of only a corpse. The chapter is explicit about healing, luck, and living with what the dice did.

- **Expanded Magic Engine.** Demonic Magic sits beside twenty-plus new spells across existing disciplines, with clearer learning rules, burn, and mishaps that can wait for the next casting. Chance casting and delayed mishaps give casters more options and more ways to fail badly.

- **Expanded Journeys and Settlements.** Make Camp is no longer a full overnight heal on one success; extra successes buy concealment and concrete camp work. In villages and towns, reputation opens notice boards, work, petitions, and carousing — and Crime and Punishment turns trouble into investigation, ordeal, weregild, sanctuary, or outlawry.

- **Mercenary Companies.** Form a company on the Quarter Day clock: recruit by settlement, pay and provision fighters, take contracts, keep Named Men, and weld several bands into a Host when the war gets bigger than one payroll.

- **Lifepath Character Creation.** The life generator runs full cycles with mishaps, advancement, and mustering-out, leaving scars and unfinished business at the same mechanical power as standard creation. Champion is a secondary Fighter calling you can roll into or earn through a formative charge, with its own talents and advanced path.

- **Traderoad Caravans.** Move freight on real routes with transport choices, priced cargo, seasonal markets, and mishaps when the road turns. Standing orders and warehouse stronghold work keep trade alive between sessions.

## The books

### Book 01 — Corebook

The full player engine in one volume. Creation through journeys, strongholds, mercenaries, traderoads, and lifepaths sit together for campaign play — not a thin adventuring chassis.

### Book 02 — Gamemaster’s Guide

The Ravenlands as a working campaign engine. Settlement play, faction pressure, mass combat, and a bestiary of humanoid bands give long sandboxes procedures instead of vibes. Artifacts, encounters, and adventure sites were folded in so campaign tools live where the referee needs them.

### Book 03 — Book of Beasts

A much larger bestiary with legends, encounters, and salvage meant for alchemists and casters — monsters that pull the next expedition, not filler stats.

## Skills / tooling

Repo-local skills help a GM or agent keep new regions, monsters, subsystems, and prose in the same line voice. Copy or symlink only what you need from `skills/` into `.github/skills/` or `.agents/skills/`, keeping each folder name and `SKILL.md` intact.

| Skill | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| `yze-design` | New Year Zero Engine games; transplant and stress-test mechanics |
| `forbidden-lands-bestiary` | Native monsters, weaknesses, encounters, legends, salvage |
| `forbidden-lands-design` | Rules, sandbox procedures, playtesting, campaign mechanics |
| `forbidden-lands-lore` | Canon vs custom corpus, places, kin, factions, institutions |
| `forbidden-lands-medieval-authenticity` | Material life, logistics, customary law, grounded detail |
| `forbidden-lands-synergy-analysis` | Stacking, spotlight, loopholes, campaign-pressure bypasses |
| `forbidden-lands-writing-voice` | Rules clarity, GM guidance, fiction, manuscript register |
