## TALENTS

> _Bredeman the minstrel sang through broken teeth and lips to please his captors, in a voice much too large for his scrawny body. Not until blood poured from the ears of the warriors and giant Handra fell from her bench did the horsemen realize what was going on. By then it was already too late._

Adventurers survive by claiming a trick, rite, or calling that no one else can match. Those signature edges are called _talents_. Each talent can change how you use skills, how you recover from damage, or let you bend the rules of the world in ways others cannot.

### STARTING TALENTS

At character creation, you gain talents from three sources: your kin, your profession, and your age (see Chapter 2). Every starting talent is taken at rank 1 unless noted otherwise. You can increase their rank and learn more talents during play.

### TYPES OF TALENTS

- **Kin talents** spring from blood and heritage. They can advance through five ranks, but higher ranks only unlock once you have spent XP on other talents and skills (see Kin Talents below).
- **Profession talents** define how each class fights, schemes, heals, or crafts miracles. Each profession offers multiple Paths, and every Path is detailed through five ranks.
- **Communication talents** cover the shared languages, literacy, and teaching traditions of the Ravenlands. They function like general talents but are grouped separately for clarity.
- **General talents** are organized into thematic subgroups and can be learned by anyone who meets the narrative requirements.

### TALENT RANKS

All talents in this chapter use five ranks. You retain the benefits of lower ranks when you buy a higher one. If a profession talent lists multiple effects, you can only spend Willpower Points (WP) on one effect at a time during an action unless the talent explicitly states otherwise.

### DEVELOPING TALENTS

You gain XP during play and can spend it to increase the rank of your talents or learn new talents (see Chapter 2 for training rules). Some talents require teachers or special components. Kin talents ignore the teacher requirement but still follow the XP costs shown on the training tables.

## Kin Talents

Your kin gift awakens slowly. To unlock ranks above 1, tally every XP you spend on talents or skills that are not tied to your kin. Hitting each threshold lets you buy the next rank at the normal (with-teacher) XP cost and never requires a tutor.

- 15 XP in non-kin advancements unlocks rank 2.
- +30 XP (45 total) unlocks rank 3.
- +45 XP (90 total) unlocks rank 4.
- +60 XP (150 total) unlocks rank 5.

Very long campaigns can keep counting in additional 75 XP blocks if the GM wants to unveil legendary kin boons included in future supplemenets. Buying the rank still costs XP as listed in Chapter 2, but the “no teacher” rule always applies to kin talents.

### THE HUMAN TALENT: ADAPTIVE

Humans survive by twisting every lesson to fit the danger at hand.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Spend 1 WP before you roll to replace the declared skill with another that uses the same attribute. Describe how the substitute applies.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Once per Quarter Day you can spend 1 WP to upgrade a help die-yours or an ally's-to a D8. Only one die per roll can come from this talent.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** Rank 1 no longer cares about matching attributes; the WP swap lets you trade any skill for any other.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** When you use Rank 2 you may upgrade two help dice at once, and you can do so once per hour.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Increase one attribute by +1 (max 6). You can buy this rank up to three times, picking a different attribute each time.

### THE HALF-ELF TALENT: PSYCHIC POWER

Half-elves channel stubborn will into bruising surges of power.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** The first WP you spend on a talent or spell each time counts as two, and the second WP counts as three, and so on.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Once per Quarter Day you can spend 1 WP to push any roll, rerolling 💀 from the initial attempt even if it is a roll you normally could not push.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** After each session, once you halve your remaining WP, gain 1 WP if your total is below your Wits.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** After each session, once you halve your remaining WP, gain 1 WP regardless of current totals. This stacks with Rank 3.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** When at 0 WP you may burn 1 point of Wits per round and treat it as WP.

### THE ELF TALENT: INNER PEACE

Elves slip into stone-still reveries, reshaping body and memory.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Spend 2 WP to meditate for a Quarter Day. When you rise you have healed all damage and one critical injury (except lost limbs).
- ✦ **RANK 2:** While meditating you can spend 1 WP to alter your features or slide one attribute point to another (staying within 2-5, or 2-6 for Agility). Minor changes take days; major ones can take a month.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** During meditation you may spend up to 3 WP to ask questions about a person, place, or relic. Each WP grants one clear answer, fragment of legend, or last known location.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Spend 2 WP during meditation to gain +1 to any attribute for a day or until you meditate again.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Spend up to 6 WP during meditation, gaining +1 per 2 WP as above. You also know how to root your gem shard and become an Ent-an awakening that takes 2D6 years and ties you to the sun and soil. Work with the GM if your story reaches this point.

### THE DWARF TALENT: TRUE GRIT

Dwarves grit their teeth and keep fighting long after others fall.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Spend WP to push the same roll multiple times. Each WP buys one extra push, but you do not gain WP from ability damage caused by those extra pushes.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** After the first extra push, every additional WP spent buys two extra pushes instead of one.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** If a pushed roll shows three or more 💀 and no ⚔️, treat it as a single ⚔️.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Once per attack you can spend 1 WP to shrug off 1 point of non-self-inflicted damage. You also earn up to 1 WP from one 💀 when pushing more than once.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Once per spell you can spend 1 WP to drop its Power Level by 1. You also earn up to 3 WP from three 💀 when pushing more than once.

### THE HALFLING TALENT: HARD TO CATCH

Halflings slip through danger with infuriating ease.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** After rolling DODGE you may spend any number of WP to add that many ⚔️. Attackers must also roll one extra ⚔️ to GRAPPLE or HOLD you.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** When a ranged attack hits you, spend 1 WP to take D6-1 Strength damage instead of rolling armor (minimum 0). For other damage types, reduce the final total by 1.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** Spend 1 WP to reduce trap, fall, or mishap damage by 2. Decide after rolling damage.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Spend 1 WP after rolling MOVE to add one ⚔️.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Once per Quarter Day you may spend 1 WP to auto-succeed at a DODGE roll before you roll.

### THE WOLFKIN TALENT: HUNTING INSTINCTS

Wolfkin lock onto prey and never let the trail grow cold.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Spend up to 2 WP to mark a visible target (or a target whose scent you can follow) as prey. Each WP lets you track it for one full day.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** You can now spend up to 4 WP when marking prey.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** In combat you gain +1 to attacks against your prey for every WP you invested (max 1 bonus per attack). The bonus lasts until the target is Broken or released.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** While the mark lasts you ignore Wits damage from environmental horrors tied to that hunt-howling winds, maddening whispers, or the Stillmist's dread.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** You can spend up to 2 WP per attack for +2 when striking your prey.

### THE ORC TALENT: UNBREAKABLE

Orcs force their bodies back to their feet no matter what broke them.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** When an attribute hits zero you may spend WP to restore it (1 point per WP). This does not heal critical injuries.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Restored attributes can now climb up to 3 points above their starting value; extra points fade after the scene.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** When you restore an attribute you may transfer the damage to another attribute that is not Broken. If that attribute later hits zero, you suffer the critical injury then.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** After you recover from being Broken you can spend WP to enter a rampage on your next turn. Each WP grants +1 damage on all melee attacks until your next turn (limited by Empathy).
- ✦ **RANK 5:** When the rampage ends you regain the WP you spent to start it (minimum 1). If you drop to 0 WP during the rampage, gain 1 WP instead.

### THE GOBLIN TALENT: NOCTURNAL

Goblin eyes love shadow and their steps vanish when the sun dips.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** You see perfectly in darkness. In dim light you may spend WP after rolling STEALTH to add the same number of ⚔️.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** When you roll STEALTH in darkness, each WP you spend counts as one automatic ⚔️ (up to the number you rolled). If your action is interrupted or you drop what you carry, reduce your total ⚔️ by 1.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** Spend 1 WP to turn a SCOUTING or INSIGHT roll in darkness into an automatic ⚔️. This stacks with Rank 2.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Instead of flat ⚔️ from Rank 2 you may add a D8 Artifact Die after rolling.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Upgrade that die to a D12, replacing any smaller die you added from this talent.

## The Champion's Talents

Champions bind themselves to fate, vows, and roaring valor, drawing strength from conviction instead of coin.

### PATH OF FATE

You peer a heartbeat ahead in every clash.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** At the start of your turn spend 1 WP to read a foe’s intent. Until your next turn all attacks and defenses against that opponent gain +1.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** After an attack hits you, spend WP to reduce the final damage by 1 per point (even after armor).
- ✦ **RANK 3:** On your turn you may spend 1 WP to slip into a slow-motion trance. This lets you make a free FAST action immediately, or get a free reactive PARRY/DODGE until the start of your next turn. You can spend WP equal to your RANK in this Talent until you must REST to regain this ability.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** At the start of each round, after initiative is rolled, you may spend 1 WP to read the flow of battle. Until the end of that round, once when you roll zero ⚔️ on a melee or ranged attack, PARRY, or DODGE (before any push), you may treat that roll as if it had exactly one success. You can’t use this on pushed rolls.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Whenever you suffer a critical injury, regain 1 WP. When you spend WP for Rank 3, you can spend 2 WP to make a SLOW action immediately; you cannot repeat this until you REST.

### PATH OF THE HOLY VOW

Your oath is a blade you can draw at will, but it also cuts you when you falter.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** When you learn this talent, you do not keep a Pride note in the ordinary sense. Instead, choose a Vow or Oath in clear, binding terms with the GM. It must point your will and drive toward a very specific path, cause, or duty: a hard road, a sacred burden, a revenge, a rescue, a restoration, or some other life that cannot be finished easily or safely. It cannot be something already achieved, something trivial, or a promise so broad that it can fit any deed. You are not claiming a trophy; you are swearing yourself to a struggle. Your Vow or Oath Pride is never lost if you apply it to a check that fails. You also choose one **Anathema**—a deed you may never willingly perform and the GM must agree to it. If you break that Anathema, you immediately lose your Pride. This is the only way you can lose your Pride.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Add a second Anathema that sharpens your code, that the GM must agree on. When a roll clearly upholds your Vow you may spend 1 WP to add a D8 Artifact Die; if you refuse a beneficial opportunity because it would break an Anathema once per session, regain 1 special WP that doesn't count to your limit (you cannot gain another special WP until you spend it).
- ✦ **RANK 3:** You may now invoke your Pride twice per session, and one of those uses can empower an ally who is actively pursuing or aiding you in your Vow. They roll your Pride die but you pay the cost and count the use.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Add a third Anathema that the GM must agree on. The GM may now set formal **Goals** tied to your Vow — short-term goals award you +2 XP when completed (and +1 XP to each ally who meaningfully helped), while long-term Goals award +4 XP to you and +2 XP to assisting allies. These Goals must be weighty challenges where you are the spearhead.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** You can invoke Pride an additional time per session, and this use can only benefit yourself. Once per session, when you use Pride in direct service of a Goal, you also grant each assisting ally +1 die on their next roll until the end of their next turn and you regain 1 WP if the action succeeds.

### PATH OF PROTECTION

You measure success by the wounds you intercept.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Spend 1 WP (SLOW action) to tackle an enemy within SHORT range and knock them prone. If their Strength exceeds yours, they can roll MIGHT to stay standing. No effect on monsters.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Spend 1 WP (FAST action) to instantly swap places with an ally within NEAR range.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** Spend 1 WP at any time to leap in front of an ally within NEAR range and absorb 1 point of incoming damage per WP. Armor applies separately to you and the ally.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Spend any amount of WP to project a bulwark and choose up to that many enemies within your reach. Each chosen foe suffers –2 dice on attacks against your allies (they can still strike you normally) as long as they begin their turn within your reach. The penalty lasts until you move out of reach, they do, or you become Broken.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** When you use Rank 3 or Rank 4, the first WP you spend counts towards two Enemies as once, and each chosen foe suffers -3 dice on attacks.

### PATH OF VALOR

You shout courage into being even when terror drowns the crowd and steady hands begin to shake.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Spend a FAST action to rouse yourself and your allies. Gain a Valor pool equal to WP spent; each point of Valor can be spent to soak 1 point of Attribute damage from PUSHED rolls to attack or defend in combat. When you soak damage in that way, you do not get WP. The pool vanishes at the end of the encounter, or if you become Broken.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** You may allow allies within NEAR range to spend your Valor to soak Attribute damage from PUSHED rolls. When they soak damage in that way, they do not get WP. If you or your allies spend Valor on a melee attack, the attack deals 1 additional damage.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** Once per combat when you gain a Valor pool, gain +1 extra point. As long as you have Valor, you and nearby allies gain that many bonus dice (up to maximum of 5) against FEAR attacks.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** When you or your allies spend at least one point of Valor on a PUSHED roll, they can PUSH that roll one additional time (only one extra PUSH per roll). Each time a melee attack is pushed, it deals +1 extra damage.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Once per combat when you gain a Valor pool, gain +2 extra points instead of +1. As long as you have Valor, nearby allies (but not their mounts or companions) do not flee when they become Broken by FEAR, unless you are also Broken by that same attack.

## The Druid's Talents

Druids read the world’s breath—mending wounds, stirring tempests, donning hide and antler, and listening to the drone of countless insects. Each Path also unlocks spell ranks within its discipline (see Chapter 6).

### PATH OF HEALING

You channel the patient pulse of the land into broken bodies.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** You can cast rank 1 Healing spells and sense intense physical pain with a touch.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Rank 2 spells become available. Spend a quiet minute with your hand on a wound to numb the pain for a few minutes; this harmless flourish lets fellow healers know you are an Adept of the Path of Healing.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** Rank 3 spells become available. Prolonged focus tells you whether the target shoulders crushing stress, grief, or panic, even if they hide it well.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Rank 4 spells become available. You can mask wounds older than a day, appearing hale even while hurt.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Rank 5 spells become available. You look preternaturally healthy despite poison, disease, or age; though, you remain within your age category.

### PATH OF NATURE

You read the weather as easily as ink on parchment.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** You can cast rank 1 Nature spells. Concentrate on a NEAR zone to sense if civilized folk disturbed it within the past week.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Rank 2 spells become available. With a minute of focus you can coax the weather into small displays—summon a gust, pause the rain, cue a distant thunderclap, or send a scent drifting—signaling to other wardens that you are an Adept of the Path of Nature.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** Rank 3 spells become available. You no longer need a camp when resting in woods or dark woods (treated as 6- and 9-dice camps). Each outdoor rest foretells tomorrow’s weather; spend 1 WP per extra day of forecast (up to a week).
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Rank 4 spells become available. By holding an ally who is COLD and spending 1 WP you take the condition yourself, skipping the initial damage but making ENDURANCE rolls as usual.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Rank 5 spells become available. You leave no trace while moving through natural terrain; TRACKING you requires one extra ⚔️.

### PATH OF SHIFTING SHAPES

Your body remembers every paw-print you ever followed.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** You can cast rank 1 Shapeshifting spells. You may mimic familiar animal calls, and for 1 WP they become perfect.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Rank 2 spells become available. Spend 1 WP to alter small features—eyes, teeth, claws, ear shape—for up to an hour; showing this trick lets other shapeshifters know you are an Adept of the Path of Shifting Shapes.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** Rank 3 spells become available. Concentration reveals another creature’s mental pain. Spend 2 WP to carry an animal musk for a turn; clothing keeps the scent for a day.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Rank 4 spells become available. Choose a totem animal; your features echo it, shaping how others see you.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Rank 5 spells become available. Animals of your totem treat you as uncanny kin—still wary if they must be, but never dismissive.

### PATH OF SIGHT

Your senses sharpen until even dreams seem loud.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** You can cast rank 1 Awareness spells and instantly notice when someone touches you or your gear (pickpockets need one extra ⚔️ to avoid that).
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Rank 2 spells become available. By touching a creature for a minute you can heighten one sense for a turn (so long as it isn’t crippled by injury); offering this favor tells other seers you are an Adept of the Path of Sight.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** Rank 3 spells become available. Firelight stretches your sight 10 meters farther into darkness.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Rank 4 spells become available. You wake the moment danger erupts nearby.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Rank 5 spells become available. You feel small tremors—quakes, massive footsteps, distant explosions—before anyone else notices.

### PATH OF THE SWARM

You glimpse divinity in buzzing wings and tireless mandibles. Most civilized kin revile the path’s devotion to decay, so its adepts are branded as blasphemers, shunned, and often hunted.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** You can cast rank 1 Swarm spells and urge nearby insects to gather or scatter around you, though biters may still feed if they choose.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Rank 2 spells become available. With a whisper of focus you can link to a small swarm for a turn, reshaping its movement or formation; this link tells other Swarm Adepts you follow their path.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** Rank 3 spells become available. Spend WP to gain automatic ⚔️ on rolls that avoid insect swarms. You can command a BLANKET OF INSECTS to follow you at one zone per three turns.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Rank 4 spells become available. Spend 1 WP to cocoon yourself inside the BLANKET OF INSECTS for a Quarter Day, gaining the comfort of a 5-dice camp while it stands watch with 6 dice of SCOUTING. Once the Quarter Day ends the blanket disperses unless you spend another WP.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Rank 5 spells become available. Spend 1 WP to order the BLANKET OF INSECTS to SCOUT, TRACK, or FORAGE for you (6 dice), or to wrap around you as armor (Armor Rating 3). Each point of damage it prevents drains 1 point of the blanket’s Strength; when it reaches zero the swarm disperses and reforms after 3 Days (2 in woods, 1 in swamp). While shielding you it cannot attack, DODGE, or PARRY, takes only 1 damage from targeted strikes, and double from area attacks.

## The Fighter's Talents

Steel-sure fighters perfect brutal disciplines that turn every heartbeat of battle to their advantage.

### PATH OF THE BLADE

You train until the blade feels like bone and sinew.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** When a close combat attack hits, spend 1 WP to ignore the target’s armor or natural armor.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** After you have spent all your actions in a round, spend 1 WP to make one additional close combat attack (max once per round).
- ✦ **RANK 3:** Before your foe rolls armor, spend WP to add +1 damage per point.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Spend 1 WP to have your weapon count as both a blade and one other melee weapon type, letting multiple weapon talents apply.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Rank 4 now treats your weapon as every melee weapon type at once for the action.

### PATH OF THE BODY

You turn every tendon and ligament into a weapon.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** After succeeding with SHOVE, GRAPPLE, or BREAK FREE, you may spend WP to add the same number of ⚔️. Decide after the roll; this applies only to unarmed maneuvers.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Your unarmed strikes deal +1 damage and count as LIGHT BLUNT weapons for parries and talents. Once per round you may spend 1 WP to PARRY unarmed even without a held weapon or shield (attacks that normally disallow parries such as most monster strikes still cannot be parried). Gain +2 on that parry if the incoming attack wields a HEAVY weapon.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** When an unarmed attack hits, spend 1 WP to apply any one close-combat stunt (SHOVE, DISARM, GRAPPLE, or RELOCATE) using the ⚔️ result of the unarmed attack.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** While GRAPPLING a foe, spend 1 WP at the start of a round to treat them as cover: ranged attacks against you suffer –2 dice, and attacks that miss you deal damage to the grappled target equal to number of 💀 rolled. When a GRAPPLED foe is Broken from damage from its allies, regain 1 WP.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Heart-Stop Strike. Once per encounter, before rolling an unarmed attack you may spend 2 WP to add a D10 Artifact Die. The strike ignores armor if it inflicts any damage. If the target becomes Broken by this attack, they immediately roll two critical injuries and suffer the worse result (PCs roll once but add +1 to the d66).

### PATH OF THE ENEMY

You read the battlefield as if it were a board game.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** During the first round of combat (unless surprised), spend 1 WP to shift your initiative segment by up to +2 or -2. You also gain +1 die on one melee attack against one enemy who will act after you that round. This rank still can’t be used against monsters.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Spend 1 WP to block your target from using PARRY or DODGE on your next attack.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** When your PARRY shows more ⚔️ than the attack, you can spend 1 WP to convert each extra ⚔️ into that many points of damage that can’t be parried or dodged. RANK 1 can now be used against monsters, and you may shift your segment by up to +3 or -3, or keep your current segment unchanged when you roll initiative next round. You can use this once per combat.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** When you BREAK a foe, you may spend 1 WP to take their initiative segment on the next round (or keep your own if it is higher). This cannot be used in combat with monsters. RANK 2 now blocks the opponent from using both PARRY and DODGE.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** RANK 1 can now be used every round, and you may choose to keep your current segment instead of rolling initiative once per round. RANK 4 can be used against monsters.

### PATH OF THE SHIELD

You are the bulwark others shelter behind.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** When a friend within NEAR range is attacked, spend 1 WP to parry for them. If the parry succeeds, you may choose to take any remaining damage; otherwise the attack resolves against the original target.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** When you successfully parry for an ally with RANK 1, you may immediately swap positions with them or pull them a meter behind you without disengaging or provoking attacks. You become engaged with the parried Enemy.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** After you successfully parry an attacker within ARM'S LENGTH, you may spend 1 WP to bind them with your shield. Bound foes suffer –2 dice on all attacks and cannot move out of engagement until they spend a FAST action to break free (opposed MIGHT vs your STRENGTH), or you release the bind by moving or using your weapon or shield for an attack.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Once per round you may spend 1 WP and PARRY a physical monster attack even if it normally forbids parries. Your shield shatters from the force and your shield hand is stunned until the end of the next turn.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** At the start of each round choose up to two allies within NEAR. Until your next turn they gain +1 Armor Rating, and when either is about to suffer damage you may immediately spend 1 WP to have the post-armor damage apply to you instead. If you prevent all damage to at least one chosen ally during the round, regain 1 WP (max once per round).

## The Hunter's Talents

Hunters survive by listening to the wind, reading every hoofprint, and striking before a foe even knows they were found.

### PATH OF THE ARROW

Your bow answers as soon as you draw breath.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** When your ranged attack hits, spend 1 WP to ignore the target's armor or natural armor.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** After you have spent all of your actions in a round, spend 1 WP to fire one extra prepared shot (maximum once per round).
- ✦ **RANK 3:** When your ranged attack hits, you may spend WP before the target rolls armor to add +1 damage per point.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** If you wound with a ranged attack, spend WP to add a SHOVE or DISARM stunt. Each WP counts as one ⚔️ toward the effect; the WP is still spent even if the target resists (for example through FIRM GRIP or STEADY FEET).
- ✦ **RANK 5:** When you wound a non-monster with a ranged attack, you may spend 2 WP to inflict an automatic critical injury. Armor still rolls normally before the injury is applied.

### PATH OF THE BEAST

You share the hunt with animals that treat your word like pack-law. This path is usually paired with the [TAMER](#general-talents) talent, which lets you teach companions tricks; without that training they respond based on instinct and simple cues. Each rank deepens the bond and widens the stable of creatures willing to bleed for you.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Choose one Small companion such as a fox, hunting dog, hawk, or other scout. It trusts you enough to follow you and warn of danger. They do not engage in combat, will flee to a nearby distance, and grant you +1 die on SCOUTING or SURVIVAL rolls made in the terrain that matches the companion’s natural habitat (e.g., forests for foxes, hills for hawks, wetlands for otters).
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Gain a second companion and unlock Medium predators—war hound, wolves, boars, lynxes—who view you as their pack leader. They hunt and fight beside you even when wounded and will not abandon you unless ordered to flee. Companions act in the lowest segment each round.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** You may replace one Medium beast with a flyer, swimmer, or burrower (eagle, raven, otter, badger). Companions now mirror your instincts in battle: they act in your segment each round.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Large apex beasts (bear, great stag, saber cat) accept you as kin. Once per Quarter Day you may spend 1 WP to share senses with a companion for a few minutes—seeing, hearing, or smelling what it perceives while it prowls ahead.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** One non-demonic monster with animal cunning (basilisk, giant lizard, iron boar) recognizes you as its chosen handler. It will risk itself to save you and, if separated, will carve a path back unless magic or death intervenes. When you become Broken you can immediately spend 1 WP to send any companion into a protective frenzy: until you recover or the beast is Broken, it adds a D8 Artifact Die to attacks against the enemy that felled you, acts on your initiative even if you are unconscious, and may leap in front of further blows aimed at you.

#### TAMING A NEW COMPANION

Gaining access to a creature type doesn’t hand you a ready mount—you still need to find and befriend it. When exploring a hex, spend a Quarter Day to search for signs of a suitable beast. Roll _HUNTING_ and gather a number of successes equal to the rank of the companion you seek. Once spotted, make an _ANIMAL HANDLING_ roll with a difficulty equal to half the rank (rounded up) to earn its attention; on a success it will follow you from a distance, often wary and prone to leave on a failure. Finally, bond with it for a number of days equal to that rank, spending a Quarter Day each day to earn its trust with an _ANIMAL HANDLING_ roll each Quarter; two consecutive failures will cause a strife and the process of bonding must start anew. Once bonded, the animal becomes a Companion.

### PATH OF THE FOREST

You move through the woodland like a shadow among old trees. All benefits from this path apply only while you are in forest or deep forest terrain unless otherwise noted.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** When you FORAGE, HUNT, SCOUT wildlife, handle mounts, or LEAD THE WAY through a forest, you may spend 1 WP to automatically succeed (decide after a failed roll). You may also spend 1 WP to treat difficult forest ground as normal for one Quarter Day; if you do, allies hiking with you gain +1 die on an ENDURANCE roll to keep up.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Spend 1 WP while making camp to weave a woodland shelter; the camp counts as a 9-dice shelter that Quarter Day, the fire leaves no trace, and KEEP WATCH gains +1 die. When rolling to endure COLD in a forest you may spend 1 WP to automatically succeed and give allies within NEAR range +1 die on their ENDURANCE rolls against that exposure.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** While fighting in woods, when declaring a DODGE you may choose to slip behind a nearby tree instead of falling prone. - ✦ **RANK 5:** Spend 2 WP to vanish into the foliage for up to one Quarter of a Day. Until you act in a way that reveals you, or leave the forest hex, you count as undetectable unless an observer scores a critical success (three ⚔️) on SCOUTING. Allies within ARM'S LENGTH gain +1 die on their STEALTH rolls while you remain hidden.

### PATH OF THE MOUNTAINEER

You carve trails where cliffs try to deny passage. Benefits apply in steep rock hills, mountains, and high passes.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** When climbing or scrambling, spend 1 WP to automatically succeed and set a piton line. Allies following your line gain +1 die on their CLIMB rolls and ignore the first failed roll (they simply slip back). You may also spend 1 WP at the start of a Quarter Day to scout the slope and mark switchbacks; for the rest of the day your party gains +1 die on LEAD THE WAY rolls in that mountain hex.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Spend 1 WP at the start of a Quarter Day to run thin-air drills; everyone within NEAR range (including beasts) ignores altitude COLD penalties for that period and gains +1 die on ENDURANCE rolls against wind or chill. Alternatively, spend 1 WP before a climb to rig hauling harnesses so every ally treats their carried load as one step lighter for the Quarter Day; if someone slips, the rope team may roll CLIMB to arrest the fall without damage.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** While adjacent to a cliff or rope line, you may fight in melee as long as one hand and foot stay on the stone. Enemies without similar purchase suffer –2 dice on melee attacks against you. Once per round you may spend a FAST action to anchor yourself; until your next turn, shove or grapple attempts to knock you off suffer –2 dice and taking damage doesn’t force a CLIMB roll. You still suffer –2 dice on ranged attacks while clinging to the face.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** While holding on a rope, when you declare a DODGE you swing away instead of dropping prone, but your trajectory is unpredictable. Before the start of your next turn, make a MOVE roll; on success you gain momentum dice to your next attack against a single enemy equal to the number of successes minus one and stop Swinging; on a failure you continue Swinging and cannot take attacks until the end of your turn unless you spend a SLOW action to stop Swinging. While Swinging, you cannot attack enemies with melee attacks, and you cannot be attacked by melee attacks unless the enemies are flying. You also suffer -2 dice penalty to attempts to resist being SHOVED or GRAPPLED and ranged attacks against you suffer -2 dice. You cannot continue CLIMBING unless you stop Swinging. This maneuver requires an anchored rope or handhold, which may be attacked while you are Swinging.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Like an avalanche master, once per encounter you spend 2 WP while on mountainous ground to trigger a controlled slide. Choose a zone uphill from your foes; every enemy in the target zone suffers a 11-die attack (Blunt, ignores armor) and must pass an opposed MOVE vs AVALANCE successes or be knocked prone and fall into the avalanche. Allies in that zone can attempt MOVE to brace and take half damage; on a failure they take full falling damage. After the slide you and your party may immediately reposition one zone aside or downwards without spending actions, representing riding the debris.

## The Minstrel's Talents

Minstrels salt every tale with power—mending spirits, twisting tempers, or shattering nerve with a single note.

### PATH OF THE HYMN

Your hymn knits spirits back together.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Help a Broken ally within SHORT range rise again. For each WP spent, they recover one point in the attribute that hit zero. You can also spend WP on a one-for-one basis to add ⚔️ to an ally's ENDURANCE roll; declare the slow action before seeing the roll.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** A number of your allies, equal to the number of WP you spend and within SHORT range, each recover one point of damage to an attribute. You decide which attribute they all recover.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** Spend any amount of WP to transfer that much WP to a comrade within LONG range. The half-elf talent PSYCHIC POWER cannot modify this.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** When you use RANK 2, each ally can take a free FAST action to stand up from prone, or get a free non-standing DODGE until the start of their turn.

### PATH OF INFLUENCE

You bend moods with a word and a smile. All uses below require that your audience can hear you and isn’t actively hostile. A given individual or crowd can only be affected once per day. When you plant an intention they must attempt it at the next reasonable opportunity or suffer Empathy damage equal to your successes; if that would Break them, fear or social pressure forces compliance anyway. You can never compel obvious self-harm or betrayal of their core interests. Failing a roll still spends the WP and leaves the target or group wary or skeptical (–1 die to further attempts unless your reputation meaningfully improves). This talent only affects Kin, not monsters.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Spend 1 WP to make an opposed MANIPULATION roll with +2 dice against a single listener. On success you shift their attitude one step toward helpful and may plant a simple intention such as “open the gate” or “mention my offer.”
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Apply Rank 1 to up to WITS listeners with one roll if they all listen to you. Each who fails to resist treats the idea as their own plan for the day. If one knowingly takes the Empathy damage to refuse, the rest suffer –1 die should they later try to shrug off the same intention. A failed roll makes the entire group skeptical rather than hostile.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** Spend 1 WP and roll PERFORMANCE to sway an entire crowd at a market, tavern, sermon, or council. If the crowd is unruly or already skeptical, treat it as a Challenging roll (requires at least two ⚔️). Success sets their mood for the next scene and turns your slogan, rumor, or watchword into gossip that spreads through the settlement for the rest of the day. On a mishap the rumor twists against you and the crowd turns wary.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Put your reputation on the line. Spend 1 WP to convert one point of Reputation in this settlement or group into a D8 Artifact Die when influencing your target. If the Artifact Die shows no successes, that Reputation point is lost and you cannot try this ability in the same settlement or group until you gain a point in Reputation in it.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Spend 1 WP to open parley with monsters, demons, or other non-Kin intelligences within SHORT range. Roll MANIPULATION (+2 dice); hostile monsters require a Challenging success, and demons need one additional success unless the parlay aligns with their interests. On success they shift two attitude steps toward neutral and must halt the current combat round to hear you out. For the rest of the scene you may treat them as eligible targets for Ranks 1–4 so long as you maintain line of sight and speak a language they understand. If you betray them or issue commands that would obviously doom their lair or brood, the effect ends and they gain +2 dice on their next attack against you. On a critical success (three ⚔️ or more), demons agree to one request of no more than a sentence before negotiations conclude.

### PATH OF THE SONG

Your melodies can soothe a throng or cut like glass.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Spend WP while singing to command everyone's attention on yourself within NEAR range for a few minutes. They halt what they are doing unless already fighting, and any activity requiring concentration they attempt is penalized by the WP spent.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** As Rank 1, but you can cajole payment. Total silver equals WP spent plus one for each ⚔️ on your PERFORMANCE roll, limited by what the audience can afford. You may forfeit silver to plant instead a rumor or lower the crowd hostility by one step.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** Before combat or as a SLOW action during combat, spend 1 WP to deal 1 Strength damage to up to WITS listeners within SHORT range. Each target may roll INSIGHT; on a success they negate the damage but suffer –2 dice on their next attack against you or your allies. No effect against monsters.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Spend 1 WP to unleash a battle hymn that seizes attention. As long as you carry the song, every hostile within SHORT range must either focus their next attack on you or suffer –2 dice when attacking anyone else until your next turn. Monsters that use special attacks simply have to resolve their next attack against you (if possible) or take the penalty of -4 dice if they go after another target.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** As a SNEAK ATTACK at SHORT range you can sing a lullaby that deals 1 Wits damage per WP (targets also become sleepless). Continue paying WP each turn to maintain it. Targets that drop to zero Wits fall asleep and later remember the experience only as a dream. Monsters take no damage but ignore non-hostile actions until the song ends, at which point they recall everything clearly.

### PATH OF THE WARCRY

Your shout steadies allies and rattles even seasoned foes.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Spend WP to grant all comrades within SHORT range +1 to all attacks per point until your next turn.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Spend WP to impose –1 per point on the close-combat attacks of all opponents within SHORT range until your next turn (no effect on monsters).

- ✦ **RANK 5:** Spend 1 WP to let every comrade within SHORT range reduce the next attribute damage they suffer by 1 (to a minimum of zero). The protection lasts until the encounter ends and also cancels damage from pushed rolls without granting WP.

## The Peddler's Talents

You run your stall with rule of bone and bellows—if you can’t buy it, you cobble it.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** In a few minutes and with scavenged scraps you can jury-rig a weapon, tool, or armor piece. Each WP spent (max 3) grants 1 gear die or Armor Rating, but the item looks crude (–2 MANIPULATION when flaunted), can’t be repaired, and takes damage on any gear die that shows a ⚔️.

- ✦ **RANK 3:** Spend 1 WP and a Quarter Day to toughen a weapon, item, or armor. The next point of item damage it would suffer is ignored, after which you can reapply the care. Once per day, if you finish a paid repair or custom build for a client in a settlement (or caravan/guild) where you haven’t already done so since dawn, regain 1 WP; you must move to a different settlement or customer base before you can trigger this refund again.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Spend 1 WP to set up a traveling workshop for a Quarter Day. While it is active you count as having full tools for any crafting or repair, you may treat one missing ingredient (other than IRON ORE) as improvised, and allies who craft or repair within the camp gain +1 die on their CRAFTING roll. ### PATH OF THE CARAVAN

You maintain ledgers, caches, and caravan partners across the Ravenlands. Spending WP means settling a tab or calling in a favor—when something “arrives,” it is an item you already purchased and warehoused, delivered through your network. Goods routed to you in the wild are travel-worn (muddy seals, chipped edges, no crafter’s mark) and count as personal kit—not presentable enough to resell. At Rank 2 you can establish a caravan at a trading post that trades up to 25 weight (it costs 2D6x10 silver to establish, minus 2D6 per WP spent a day of manual labor organizing it); it produces no wealth on its own and requires you to personally supervise deliveries.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Spend WP to arrange the delivery of a trade good worth up to 1 silver per point from your ledger. The GM decides how it reaches you in that hex (runner, mule, hidden stash). No HEAVY items at this rank.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Your ledgers can now cover standard weapons as well as trade goods up to 1D6 silver per WP spent. You may also create or replace a caravan cache to store and deliver wares; caravans remain idle unless you are present.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** Each WP can free up to 2D6 silver worth of goods, and you can assign trusted factors to run your caravan in a new settlement without you being present. While you are away it may earn passive income or complete deliveries, but the GM rolls each Day you’re absent: on a mishap the caravan suffers theft, taxes, or damage (lose 1D6 silver worth of goods per ☠️, or damage to the caravan for 1D6x10 silver in repairs if two mishaps occur consecutively) unless you return to resolve it. Once per day, when you close a sale by delivering ledger or caravan goods to a buyer in a settlement where you have not already done so since dawn, regain 1 WP; you must trade in or deliver to a different settlement before gaining WP again.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Your network now covers HEAVY items, armor, shields, tools, or raw materials in addition to lighter goods.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** When calling in an item you may either designate it as masterwork (+1 gear/Armor Rating) or double the purchasing power of the WP spent (each WP counts as 2D6 silver). Once per day, if you earn a special order in a new settlement and your MANIPULATION roll scores at least three ⚔️, you may mark that contract; when the goods arrive and satisfy the buyer, regain 1 WP (only one contract may be marked at a time).

Once goods reach you, record them on your sheet—they now occupy carry weight or caravan space until sold.

### PATH OF TREASURE

You live by margins and whispers, turning junk into silver and silver into secrets.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Spend 1 WP while buying gear to trim the price by one-fifth per point (max 4 WP, reducing the cost to one-fifth). After you spend the WP, you gain +1 die on the next MANIPULATION roll tied to securing that deal (haggling, convincing the shopkeep, etc.).
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Spend 1 WP while appraising loot for an hour to know its true value (artifacts remain priceless). When selling, each WP boosts the payout by 25% (max 4 WP); the GM may cap spending in tiny settlements, but at least 1 WP usually works. After you spend the WP, you gain +1 die on the next MANIPULATION roll used to pitch that item or close the sale.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** Spend 1 WP to automatically succeed on a Supply roll for an Uncommon item (2 WP for Rare). You may roll first and spend after a failure. Spending 1 WP while studying an object also gives you a hazy legend about it and points you toward the buyer most likely to pay top coin. Once per day, if you broker a profitable buy/sell deal in a settlement where you haven’t already triggered this since dawn, regain 1 WP; to do so again you must change settlements or trading circles.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Spend 1 WP while searching a room to sense if hidden doors or caches exist. You still need a SCOUTING roll to pinpoint them.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** As Rank 4, but spending 1 additional WP guarantees you uncover every secret door or stash in that room before or after the SCOUTING roll. Once per day, when you uncover a cache for a new patron (SCOUTING roll that scores at least three ⚔️), you may mark that find; when you later close the sale or hand the secret to that patron, regain 1 WP. Only one marked deal can be pending at a time.

### PATH OF WORDS

You bargain with a smile sharp enough to cut armor.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Spend 1 WP while conversing to attune to cadence and posture. For the rest of the conversation the GM must tell you whether the person you speak with feels sincere or evasive (but not which part of a long sentence is false).
- ✦ **RANK 2:** The same read now exposes intent. After using Rank 1 on someone, spend 1 WP to ask what they hope to gain from this exchange (coin, time, status, information). This doesn’t force honesty or reveal secrets, but it tells you the motive steering their words.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** When you succeed at MANIPULATION to buy, sell or sign contract, spend 1 WP to seal the bargain immediately. The target waives any counter-offer or further negotiations and would lose 1 point of Reputation if they break the deal first, or renege on their agreement. Once per day, if you seal a contract or truce in a settlement where you haven’t already gained this refund since dawn, regain 1 WP. Move to a new settlement or negotiating circle before earning it again.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** When someone challenges your story, spend 1 WP to “own the beat.” They can’t roll INSIGHT against you and have to either accept the tale or break the social contract (raise voices, draw steel, storm off) and put end to the conversation. If they choose to break it, you immediately gain +2 dice on your next MANIPULATION roll against them (or the watching audience, if you pivot to them).
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Spend 1 WP to automatically succeed on a LORE roll to evaluate a rumor or legend; the GM must then tell you whether it is likely true, mostly false, or wildly exaggerated, even if the storyteller believes it. Once per day, when you publicly declare the truth or falsehood of a rumor in a new settlement (MANIPULATION or PERFORMANCE roll with at least three ⚔️), you may mark that proclamation; when it sways a settlement or a faction’s stance, regain 1 WP. Only one proclamation may be marked at a time.

## The Rider's Talents

### PATH OF THE COMMANDER

You bark an order and riders move before the hoofbeats fade.

Allies must hear and understand the command.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Rank 1 can now grant any FAST action. You also gain a FAST reaction: roll PERFORMANCE and spend WP to blunt a FEAR attack for allies within SHORT range, reducing its effect by one ⚔️ per WP. If you are also targeted, you take Empathy damage instead of Wits.

- ✦ **RANK 3:** While you are on your mount, its BARDING ARMOR applies to your ARMOR against attacks you take: when you roll armor for that attack, add your mount’s barding Armor Rating to your own (the mount takes no damage unless the GM rules the blow clearly hit it). Outside combat you can spend 1 WP to let the mount KEEP WATCH while you REST in the saddle without forcing the mount to push on, and you can spend this Quarter Day to recover 1 point of Strength or Agility. You also gain +2 dice on ANIMAL HANDLING or HEALING rolls made to calm, spur, or heal your personal mount.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** When separated from your mount, spend 1 WP to add a D12 artifact die (a Pride die based on your bond) to one roll made to reach it, provided you aren’t ignoring a simpler route. You cannot repeat this until you become in contact with your mount. As long as you are riding your mount, it cannot be Broken by FEAR attacks.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Years of drills teach your mount to anticipate plans like a fellow rider. During downtime you may spend 1 WP to “script” a complex routine (fetch a specific pack, circle to loose reins, intercept a pursuer). You can teach it number of routines equal to its WITS. Later, spend 1 WP—even beyond DISTANT range—to trigger any number of these routines without further cues.

Riding herds are common near villages, rare in deep wilderness.
- **Approach/Break:** Once you find a suitable animal, make an ANIMAL HANDLING roll with difficulty half the rank (rounded up). On a success it accepts tack and follows you; on a failure it bolts and you must start over.
- **Bond:** Spend one Quarter Day per rank working the mount (brush, feed, tack). Each day roll ANIMAL HANDLING; two failures in a row spook it and you must begin anew. After bonding completes, the steed becomes your Companion mount.

- ✦ **RANK 3:** When you attack from a mount in close combat, and your attack roll succeeds, you can spend WP to increase damage. Every point spent increases the damage by one. You must state how many WP you use before your opponent rolls for armor.

- ✦ **RANK 5:** You may spend the Strength or Agility of your mount instead of spending your own WP to power these talents. You may do this up to once per round.

### PATH OF THE PLAINS

You and your steed devour leagues together. Benefits assume plains or rolling hills unless noted; in cramped terrain treat these as standard mounted travel.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Spend 1 WP to drive your mount one extra hex per Quarter Day across open plains (rolling hills count as difficult terrain and cost 2 WP per extra hex). Allies riding with you may attempt ANIMAL HANDLING; on a success they share the extra distance. You may also spend 1 WP while making camp to locate lush grazing and water—the party’s mounts and pack animals skip fodder consumption for the next Quarter Day.

The screen collapses if either rider dismounts or is Broken.

Until the round ends, every mounted ally within SHORT range gains +1 die and +1 damage on attacks, enemies cannot PARRY attacks during this charge, and your mount may move one zone after each attack you make until the end of your turn, without spending extra actions. For this charge to provide benefits to your allies, each charging ally should move through the same zones as you do. If the coordinated charge Breaks or drives off at least one enemy, regain 1 WP.

## The Rogue's Talents

### PATH OF THE BURGLAR

### PATH OF THE FACE

You slip into other lives as easily as most change cloaks.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Spend 1 WP and a moment of prep to mimic someone of the same kin and sex. Anyone who knows them can roll INSIGHT at NEAR range to pierce the disguise; each extra WP spent imposes –1 on that roll.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** You can now copy voices and mannerisms. Knowing associates still roll INSIGHT as above.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** You can impersonate across genders or similar-sized kin; otherwise treat as Rank 2.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** While in disguise, add a D8 Artifact Die to STEALTH, MANIPULATION, and PERFORMANCE rolls.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** The die from Rank 4 becomes a D10.

### PATH OF THE FENCER

Every lunge is a question you already know the answer to.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** You can FEINT (FAST action, MANIPULATION vs INSIGHT) to bait the opponent into dropping their guard. On a success you may immediately spend up to 3 WP; each point adds +1 damage to your next melee attack against that target if it lands before the end of your next turn. The stored bonus is lost if you break engagement, fall prone, or attack someone else. Cannot target monsters.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Gain +1 die on the attack following a successful FEINT. You can also spend WP after the FEINT to reduce the target’s armor by 2 dice per point until their next turn (no effect on monsters).
- ✦ **RANK 3:** If your last action was a FEINT, a successful PARRY lets you immediately COUNTERATTACK. Spend WP to add bonus dice or convert the counter into a SUNDER STRIKE. Rank 1 can now affect monsters.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** After a successful FEINT you may DISENGAGE up to two meters within the same zone without provoking attacks, letting you re-engage elsewhere. Ranks 2 and 3 now work on monsters.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** When your next attack after a FEINT hits, regain 1 WP per enemy within ENGAGED (these bonus WP expire at the end of your next turn). You may also spend 3 WP after a FEINT to make two attacks with one SLOW action. Gain the WP boon from each attack that hits.

### PATH OF THE KILLER

Some quarrels are settled in the square. Yours end in closets, alleys, and whispered apologies.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** When you land a SNEAK ATTACK with a LIGHT weapon (see [Chapter 3 (Skills)](./03-skills.md)), you may spend up to 3 WP; each point adds +1 damage if the blow connects.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** After you successfully HIDE from a target, you can spend 1 WP to make a SNEAK ATTACK as a FAST action even if the mark knows you are nearby. You may hold that strike for later turns so long as you remain hidden.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** When you hit someone with a LIGHT weapon while they are unaware, distracted, or tied up with another foe at ARM'S LENGTH, you may spend WP to press the advantage. Each point adds +1 damage and ignores armor entirely.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Spend 1 WP and a Day of legwork in a settlement, network, or stronghold to research a single target (or equally trained unit). Until a week passes, your attacks against that target deal +1 damage on a hit and rolls to shadow, infiltrate, or approach them gain +1 bonus die. You can maintain only one researched target at a time.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** As Rank 4, but you may track up to a dozen targets, the research lasts for a month, and the related skill rolls gain +2 bonus dice.

### PATH OF THE POISON

You travel with a pouch of tinctures and venoms, forever brewing something that smells of almonds or grave earth. Your reagent kit counts as one SMALL item that never encumbers you and can supply allies with doses that last up to four Quarter Days. Fresh poisons spoil too quickly to turn into trade goods unless the GM says otherwise. Monsters that lack explicit poison immunity suffer the listed effects; lethal toxins deal 1 Strength damage plus 1 more per full 10 HP at the start of each of the victim’s turns. (Poison rules appear in [Chapter 10 (Gear)](./10-gear.md).)

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Spend 15 minutes and any number of WP to brew a lethal poison dose with Potency equal to WP × 3.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** You may instead craft any poison described in [Chapter 10 (Gear)](./10-gear.md). Non-lethal brews gain +2 Potency dice.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** You can finish brewing with the poison already smeared on one weapon—no action required when you strike. You also gain +1 to ENDURANCE rolls against poison.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Any poison you apply gains +3 Potency dice and lingers on the weapon for one extra successful attack.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** As Rank 4, but the coating endures for two extra attacks and your ENDURANCE bonus against poison rises to +2.

If you lose the reagent kit, visit a trading post (or similar) to restock before using this path again—spend 1 WP and 2D6 silver to replace the supplies.

## The Sorcerer's Talents

### PATH OF BLOOD

Your own vitae is spellbook, shield, and price. Each rank also unlocks the matching Blood Magic spells.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Cast rank 1 Blood Magic spells. Your blood clots with uncanny speed, and any wound that failed to Break you leaves no scar once it heals.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Cast rank 2 spells. You can make fresh blood roil, pale, froth, or even coax a wound to bleed again for a moment; this display tells other Blood Adepts you walk their path.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** Cast rank 3 spells. By concentrating and spending 1 WP you can halt severe bleeding that would otherwise demand ENDURANCE rolls (as long as that roll has no negative modifiers).
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Cast rank 4 spells. Spend 1 WP to alter a sample of your blood so it imitates another kin. Trackers relying on spilled blood or scent need one extra ⚔️ to find you.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Cast rank 5 spells. Your body now replenishes blood through sheer will; you cannot bleed out, and by spending 2 WP you ignore all bleeding effects for up to a Day.

### PATH OF DEATH

You listen for the silence between heartbeats. Higher ranks open the corresponding Death Magic spells.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Cast rank 1 Death Magic spells. You gain a death-sense, smelling rot or corpses out to VERY FAR range.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Cast rank 2 spells. You can make bodies twitch, skeletons clatter, or rot smell sweet for a moment; this macabre showmanship lets other necromancers know you share their path.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** Cast rank 3 spells. You can eat carrion or spoiled meat without risk so long as the remains still possess structure (roughly less than a month old).
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Cast rank 4 spells. Spend 1 WP to chill your body, cloud your skin, and pass as a freshly dead corpse—or even an UNDEAD—until someone pierces the ruse with INSIGHT.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Cast rank 5 spells. Spend 2 WP to enter a meditative coma that mimics death for a Day, extending it a further Day per additional WP. While in this trance you need no food or water, though you must still breathe if only barely.

### PATH OF SIGNS

You speak through lines, sigils, and hidden loops. Each rank also grants the matching Symbolism spells.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Cast rank 1 Symbolism spells. With a LORE roll you can read symbols left by other Symbolists, learning the spell used if your ⚔️ meet or exceed the effect’s rank.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Cast rank 2 spells. You can sketch harmless sigils that glow, sting with heat or cold, or stir a fleeting emotion; this flourish tells other Symbolists you are an Adept of their path.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** Cast rank 3 spells. You may hide your symbols inside art, script, or fabric; spending 1 or 2 WP raises the SCOUTING requirement to 2 or 3 ⚔️ to detect them.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Cast rank 4 spells. Spend 2 WP to render your symbols invisible to everyone except Half-Elves and Symbolists of Rank 2 or higher.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Cast rank 5 spells. Spend 1 WP to shape a symbol purely within your mind as a FAST action, satisfying any spell that normally demands a drawn mark.

### PATH OF STONE

You hum with the rhythm of bedrock. Each rank also opens the equivalent Stone Song spells.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Cast rank 1 Stone Song spells. You can sleep comfortably on bare stone as if it were a bedroll.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Cast rank 2 spells. With a 15-minute chant and 1 WP you can make pebbles warp into crude shapes or etch simple runes; showing this chant lets other Stone singers know you are an Adept.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** Cast rank 3 spells. Extend the song to an hour and spend 1 additional WP to create more detailed shapes or drawings in stone.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Cast rank 4 spells. A Quarter Day of song lets you sculpt lifelike figures from a boulder or cliff face.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Cast rank 5 spells. Spend 1 extra WP during Rank 4’s process to store a vision or message that only Dwarves and Stone Sorcerers can draw forth by touch or meditation. After a decade the memory starts to fade and distort.

### PATH OF THE UNCLEAN

Demonic mog seeps into you as you tinker with the unclean nature of demons. Each rank also unlocks the matching Demonic Magic spells.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Cast rank 1 Demonic Magic spells. Your fingertips darken to a bruised lilac that never fades. Raw mog no longer burns your skin — you can handle it bare-handed, though it still corrodes your gear and harms anyone else who touches it.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** Cast rank 2 spells. Lilac-green veins trace visibly up your arms and neck. The marks are involuntary — anyone who sees your bare skin can tell what you are. Other demon-workers recognize the stain on sight, and unbound demons hesitate one round before attacking you, studying you the way a wolf studies another wolf.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** Cast rank 3 spells. Once per day, spend 1 WP to sweat a thin film of mog from your palms — enough to serve as the ingredient for one Demonic Magic spell without spending a carried unit. The mog evaporates within a round if not used. Your sweat smells of bile and hot copper even when you are not casting.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Cast rank 4 spells. Patches of your skin have gone hard and translucent. Once per day, spend 1 WP to flush mog through your body for one turn: you become immune to poison, disease, and mog damage (including your own spells), and you gain Armor Rating 2 from toughened tissue. Your eyes turn solid black while this lasts.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Cast rank 5 spells. You can drink a unit of mog to regain 1 WP and 1 Strength. Each time you drink, roll a D6: on a 1 or 2, you gain 1 point of corruption. What devours other creatures feeds you instead.

## Communication Talents

### EDUCATED

Most ordinary folk cannot read and write beyond a few common words used in barter trade, but your community has retained the knowledge of the written word and has passed it to you. The Peddler profession begins play with Rank 1 of this talent for free (see Chapter 2).

Orc, Goblin, or Wolfkin detest the purpose of literacy; they cannot benefit from a tutor when training this skill.

- **✦ RANK 1:** You can read and write in LANGUAGE (your kin). You are versed in trade measures and accounting and can apply them to the trade of a merchant using the MANIPULATION skill.
- **✦ RANK 2:** You can read and write in one additional language you have LANGUAGE talent at least Rank 1, except those learned outside your group (civilized or uncivilized), and those learned through the POLYGLOT talent.
- **✦ RANK 3:** You get the same effect as RANK 2, again. You also get +2 dice to attempt to decipher hidden meanings in texts, solve riddles, and try to grasp the concept of thieves' cant or other faction languages.
- **✦ RANK 4:** You get the same effect as RANK 2, but now you can take Languages outside your group or those learned through the POLYGLOT talent. You can also read and write in old scriptures and dead languages or grasp the context of demonic speech (even if you cannot read its contents) using the LORE skill.
- **✦ RANK 5:** You can add a D8 artifact die to all rolls to read or write and decipher the hidden meanings of ancient texts or faction communication.

### LANGUAGE (KIN)

Each spoken language in the Forbidden Lands is a separate talent. This talent does not give you the education to read or write written text, only to communicate verbally accompanied by body language if needed. When you create a character, you gain Rank 2 Language (your kin), and one Rank 1 Language (any other kin of the same group as your kin, civilized or uncivilized; such as dwarf for a human kin; or orc for a goblin kin). Half-kin (such as half-elves) may choose one parent’s tongue at Rank 2 and the other at Rank 1. Civilized tongues typically include **human, elf, halfling, and dwarf**; uncivilized tongues include **wolfkin, orc, goblin, ogre,** and similar wild-blooded peoples. A “trade tongue” based on the human language remains common in market towns, but most non-human kin still prefer to converse privately in their own speech.

You can learn or increase ranks in locales with enough kin that speak the language by learning by practice, or be taught by a teacher. You do not have to make WITS rolls if the teacher is of the same kin as the language, even if they do not have the TEACHER talent.

- **✦ RANK 1:** You speak the language crudely in its basic form. Everyone can tell this language is not native to you, and your accent may be jarring to some. You must make an INSIGHT or LORE roll to understand the meaning of complex spoken form such as diplomatic parlay, storytelling, or poetry.
- **✦ RANK 2:** You speak the spoken language fluently, with an accent of your locale. You have full grasp of the language in even complex conversations.
- **✦ RANK 3:** You speak the language with mastery over its many accents. You may pass as a local of a village you visited for at least a day, or as someone raised by that kin unless a Challenging LORE or INSIGHT roll is passed by more than one ⚔️.

#### LANGUAGES OF THE RAVEN LANDS

**RAVALNDIC:** Talked by the Ailanders, but it has become the most common secondary language among others. About 90% Alderlandic with a mix of Elven, Eori and Dwarven. So you may might understand some single words in overheard conversations, when the languages shares similarities. **Dialects:** Choose a region in the Ravenlands.

**ALDERLANDIC:** The mother tongue of the Alderlanders. The humans of the Ravenland see it as a noble language and it is common that many books are written in Alderlandic. Both the rust brothers and the snake church see it as their official language which have helped to preserve its status in the Ravenlands. **Dialects:** Rust speech (mostly spoken in the western Ravenlands) and Old speech/Snake tongue (eastern Ravenlands).

**ASLENI:** The mother tongue of the Aslene. A fiery and expressive language. Those who speak Ravalndic can recognize many words, for example things like gold, nobility and titles, horrors, theatrical plays, and also some around nomadic life-style. Uses an alphabet that is similar to the old English one. **Dialects:** One for each of the Aslene clans. Quardic (from those that arrived and settled) and Galandic (the latter, more nomadic) are the common ones in the Ravenlands.

**ELVEN:** The language of the elves. It is soft and melodic that most listeners perceive as beautiful and singing. Those who speak Ravalndic can recognize many words that have to do with nature, magic and art. Uses soft rolling runs in writing. **Dialects:** There are no dialects among the elves in the Ravenlands, but it is instead hard to master. Increase the cost to learn it by +1 point, you may learn a “broken version” for 1 point, and that will make you understand each other.

**DWARVEN:** The language of the dwarves. Often perceived as mushy and cruel with long sentences. Those who speak Ravalndic can recognize many words that have to do with mountains, forging and mining. Uses angular runes in writing. **Dialects:** One per clan. In the Ravenlands choose between: Belderanic, Meromannic, Canidic or Crombic.

**ORCISH:** A language developed from a mix of Dwarf, Elven and later also Ravalndic. Orc males’ speech difficulties have had a strong impact on its development. Has no written language of their own, but literate orcs use the general alphabet. **Dialects:** One per clan, in Ravenlands those would be Urhur, Roka and Visir. Plus one for the Viraga.

**EORI:** This language is spoken by halflings and goblins, though in two distinctly different dialects. Those who speak Ravalndic can recognize many words that have to do with cooking, beekeeping and brewery. **Dialects:** Goblin and Halfling.

**WOLF TONGUE:** The language of the wolfmen. No connection to other languages. Sounds most like animal sounds for the uninitiated. Can be howled over long distances. No written language.

**SAURIAN:** The language of the Saurian people. Have no connections to other languages. They only use the vowels a, u, and e, but have their own consonants of which eight are throat clicks that are reminiscent of “g”, and smacking sounds that are close to “t”. Their speech most closely resembles long, rambling harangues of syllables.

### POLYGLOT

You have had dealings with rare cultures or creatures that have developed their own spoken language or similar means of communication. You may learn exotic Languages, for example: **Ancient** (still spoken by some Giants and Trolls), **Deep Dwelver, Demonic, Dragon speech, Whiner;** or other languages the GM mentions. These forgotten or forbidden languages do not belong to any common kin and cannot be learned with the LANGUAGE talent directly except through this talent. This talent can only be taught by teachers or members of rare cultures and never learned by practice.

- **✦ RANK 1:** You gain a Rank 1 Language from the list above.
- **✦ RANK 2:** You gain a Rank 1 Language from the list above.
- **✦ RANK 3:** You increase one Language learned through this talent from Rank 1 to Rank 2, and gain another Rank 1 Language from the list above.
- **✦ RANK 4:** You increase one Language learned through this talent from Rank 1 to Rank 2, and gain another Rank 1 Language from the list above.
- **✦ RANK 5:** You increase one Language learned through this talent from Rank 2 to Rank 3, and another from Rank 1 to Rank 2. You can even fairly quickly decipher unknown and dead languages with a LORE roll.

### TEACHER

You have become studied and patient enough to pass your vast knowledge in digestible form to the eager and impressionable young minds who will inherit your wisdom for the next generation. Your services become more valuable, you normally can charge up to 20% more for your services per rank in this talent, should Students be able to afford such a price increase for the services you provide.

- **✦ RANK 1:** Your student no longer needs to make a WITS roll when they spend a Quarter of a Day to learn a skill or talent from you.
- **✦ RANK 2:** While you teach a Student a skill or talent, you may observe their use of another skill or talent that you may learn from them within the same Quarter of the Day. The Student must be willing to reveal and exchange their experience with you this way, and such exchange may allow some Students barter their knowledge instead of paying for tuition. The skill must use the same attribute, or the talent must be the same category (Class or General, but Kin Talents are not learned this way). All normal rules for learning apply for you as a Teacher, and you must make a WITS roll as normal without the benefit of RANK 1.
- **✦ RANK 3:** While taught by you, your Student can now learn faster than normal by making a WITS roll. If they get two ⚔️ or more, this teaching session counts as that many Quarters of the Day towards learning the skill or talent. Also, when you use RANK 2, you benefit from RANK 1 and no longer make WITS rolls.
- **✦ RANK 4:** When your Student makes a WITS roll using RANK 3, they may add number of bonus dice equal to your WITS attribute. You also benefit from RANK 3 when the Student exchanges a skill with you during the same Quarter of a Day.
- **✦ RANK 5:** When your Student makes a WITS roll using RANK 3, they can exchange one die for a D8 Artifact Die. You also benefit from RANK 4 when the Student exchanges a skill with you during the same Quarter of a Day.

This is a talent that is typically found among NPCs in the Forbidden Lands and makes learning from them faster and more guaranteed than among players. However nothing stops players to invest in Rank 1 of the Talent to avoid having to make WITS rolls.

## General Talents

### ACROBAT

Your bodily grace amazes.

- **✦ RANK 1:** You get a +1 bonus when you roll for MOVE to reduce FALL, BALANCE, RETREAT, or FLEE.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** If you successfully negate an attack by DODGING and remain standing, you may simultaneously RETREAT one zone, ignoring nearby enemies.
- **✦ RANK 3:** You can add a D8 Artifact Die when you roll for MOVE to FALL, BALANCE, RETREAT, or FLEE.
- **✦ RANK 4:** Halve any damage received from falling, being thrown or failing a FLEE action or
  retreat. Halve damage before applying reductions and round down.
- **✦ RANK 5:** Replace the D8 die from RANK 3 with a D12 die.

### ADRENALINE RUSH

Through countless fight or flight situations, you've harnessed your adrenaline to act calculated rather give in to your emotions. This talent allows you to gain special WP that are not added to your WP pool. You also can only have 1 special WP at the same time; extra are lost. Special WP must be spent until the end of your turn (your current turn if you gain it during your turn, or the end of your next turn if you gain it outside of your turn) or they are lost. You can also always burn that special WP to get 1 bonus die to an ATTACK or DODGE. Special WPs are lost at the end of a combat encounter.

- **✦ RANK 1:** When you take 2 or more damage from an enemy attack during combat, you gain 1 special WP.
- **✦ RANK 2:** When you DODGE completely a monster attack during combat, you gain 1 special WP.
- **✦ RANK 3:** The first time you BREAK an enemy opponent in combat during each round, you gain 1 special WP.
- **✦ RANK 4:** You can also have up to 2 special WP at the same time. You now lose only one special WP at the end of a turn instead of all of them. When you burn special WP, you gain 2 bonus nice to ATTACK or DODGE instead of one.
- **✦ RANK 5:** You no longer treat WP gained from RANK 1 to 3 as special. They are added to your WP pool and not lost at the end of your turn or the end of the combat encounter.

Extracting such an ingredient from a monster requires a HEALING roll, and gives one dose per ⚔️ rolled. Example of potions can be found in the table below. You can find more examples under the Resources heading for monsters in the Bestiary.

**STORAGE:** Up to ten doses can be stored in a bottle or vessel that counts as a normal item. Five doses can be stored in a vessel that counts as a light item. The shelf life is very long, probably longer than the adventurers’short, hard lives.

- **✦ RANK 1:** You can use the HEALING skill to concoct various types of potions. Each ⚔️ rolled gives you one dose.
- **✦ RANK 2:** As per rank 1, and you get +1 to all rolls for concocting potions.
- **✦ RANK 3:** As per rank 2, but you can add a D8 Artifact Die to all rolls for concocting potions.
- **✦ RANK 4:** Replace the D8 die with a D10 die.
- **✦ RANK 5:** Replace the D10 die with a D12 die.

### AMBIDEXTROUS

You are skilled in fighting with one close combat weapon in each hand. You have a
primary weapon in your favored (or sword) hand and a secondary weapon in your other hand. You can draw both weapons with a single DRAW WEAPON action.

**EXTENDED OPTIONS**

These options and actions become available when you take the SMITH talent.

- **✦ Non-metal materials:** If you want to replace any nonmetal material in an item, for example a wooden handle with some found wood of a more exquisite quality or so, your GM could add a +1 bonus dice to your crafting roll per "rating" of that material, up to a maximum of +1 per "level" of added masterwork you try to create.
- **✦ Non-leather shields:** You can make shields without leather, removing all Leather and Tanner from the requirements. Such shields will however become fragile; taking damage from even on non-pushed rolls.
- **✦ Metal ferrule:** You can make masterwork versions of all wooden melee weapons, like staffs and clubs, by adding a metal ferrule to them, counting them as metal headed weapons. This adds 1/2 Iron, Smith and Forge to the requirements.
- **✦ Scrapping items:** You can grind down items made out of metal, regaining all its metal parts, or at least some of it, if it is severely damaged. No crafting roll is necessary for this. With the GMs approval, a masterwork weapon can be grinded down in this way to gain "wrought iron", "steel", and so on. The materials are still lost (not-recyclable) if you fail a craft roll while making an item though.
