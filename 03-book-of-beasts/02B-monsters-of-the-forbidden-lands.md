### Monsters of the Forbidden Lands

This chapter develops the larger threats drawn from the monster notes in
`files/08-monsters-of-forbidden-lands.md`. These are draft entries for
beasts, spirits, constructs, curses, and elder horrors that do not yet
sit in the main d66 bestiary. Where a source note repeated a creature
already covered elsewhere in this book, the repeated draft has been left
out. Where a source note leaned too close to another game's property,
it has been turned into a Ravenlands-native creature instead.

### New Statblock Elements

These draft entries keep the core bestiary format, but add three fields
when the creature needs them.

- **PASSIVE:** An always-on rule that shapes the whole fight.
- **SPECIAL DEFENSE:** A standing protection that changes how the
  creature is harmed.
- **SPECIAL WEAKNESS:** A reliable fault, omen, or material answer that
  gives prepared adventurers a better chance.

### Tunneler

> _The furrow opens without plough, hoof, or hand._

- **ATTRIBUTES:** Strength 8, Agility 2
- **ARMOR RATING:** 6 (burrowing carapace)
- **MOVEMENT:** 1
- **PASSIVE:** A Tunneler beneath the ground can only be struck where it breaks the surface.
- **SPECIAL WEAKNESS:** Fresh-turned earth and tremor-lines deny its ambush to any adventurer who succeeds with a SURVIVAL roll before the fight begins.

#### Monster Attacks

| **D6** | **ATTACK** |
| ------ | ---------- |
| 1 | **BURST BITE!** The Tunneler erupts at ARM'S LENGTH under one adventurer. Roll the attack with eight Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. If the attack hits, the victim is thrown prone. |
| 2 | **ACID SPIT!** A clot of smoking yellow acid strikes one adventurer within SHORT range. Roll the attack with eight Base Dice and Weapon Damage 1. If damage is dealt, the victim keeps taking 1 point of damage each round until the acid is washed away with a MOVE roll. |
| 3 | **HOOKING MANDIBLES!** The Tunneler catches one adventurer at ARM'S LENGTH and drags them toward the breach. Roll the attack with eight Base Dice and Weapon Damage 1. If the attack hits, the victim is GRAPPLED. |
| 4 | **EARTH SHOWER!** The beast bucks up through stone and clay. All adventurers within NEAR range suffer an attack with six Base Dice and Weapon Damage 1. Anyone hit is blinded by dirt until the end of the next round. |
| 5 | **UNDERFOOT!** The ground opens under the adventurer with the highest Strength. That adventurer must make a MOVE roll. On failure, they suffer an attack with six Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2 and land prone in the torn earth. |
| 6 | **DOWN THE BURROW!** The Tunneler seizes one adventurer at ARM'S LENGTH and tries to drag them under whole. Roll the attack with eight Base Dice and Weapon Damage 1. If the attack hits, the victim is dragged into the breach, takes 1 point of damage each round, and escapes only by inflicting 3 points of damage from inside or by being pulled free by an ally. |

A Tunneler is a plated burrow-hunter bred for clay banks, collapsed mine
cuts, and the under-soil of long-settled fields. It hunts by patience,
not speed, and is clever enough to learn where animals are tethered, who
walks first across the yard, and which road keeps being repaired after
rain.

#### Lore Roll

| D6 | LORE ROLL |
| -- | --------- |
| 1 | Tunnelers live beneath fields, roads, and old cuts, and strike by bursting up under the unwary. |
| 2 | Quarrymen say the ground warns before the strike: a shiver in the mud, a soft collapse in the edge of the rut, a line of damp earth where none lay before. |
| 3 | Old road-wardens teach that the beast's own acid is its pride and its weakness. Once the head is up and spitting, fire and long poles do better work than swords. |

#### Random Encounter: the Collapsing Cistern

> _The bucket rope jerks three times and goes slack._

A village cistern has begun swallowing buckets, goats, and one child in
three days. The headman offers silver to kill whatever is under it, but
if the adventurers break the cistern floor they ruin the only clean
water for forty souls until autumn rain. The village already decided to
blame the widow whose yard sits nearest the collapse, because her dead
husband dug the shaft.

- **Terrain Types:** Village, Plains

#### Random Encounter: the Toll Road Furrow

> _The toll post stands upright. The ox beside it is gone from the neck down._

A petty toll-keeper has known about the Tunneler beneath the road for a
full season and has used it to frighten merchants into paying double for
his "safe crossing" boardwalk. Now the beast is large enough to start
coming through the planks. If the adventurers kill it, the keeper is
ruined and the nearby hamlet loses the silver that bought their winter
salt. If they leave it alive, the next cart down the road will likely be
full of children sent to temple service.

- **Terrain Types:** Plains, Hills

> **RESOURCES**
>
> An adventurer with the ALCHEMIST talent, with a HEALING roll, draws
> one **acid gland** or one **burrow-claw** per ⚔️ rolled.
>
> **Acid gland:** One gland keeps for a week in glass. Broken at a
> doorway or gate, it eats through worked wood or soft iron in one
> stretch.
>
> **Burrow-claw:** Bound to a digging tool by a SMITH, it grants a D8
> Artifact Die to one CRAFTING or SURVIVAL roll involving earth, clay,
> or trenching each Quarter Day.

### Pale Ape

> _White hair hangs from the cave mouth where no beast should fit._

- **ATTRIBUTES:** Strength 8, Agility 3, Wits 2, Empathy 2
- **ARMOR RATING:** 4 (matted hide)
- **MOVEMENT:** 1
- **PASSIVE:** Pale Apes know cave-dark better than daylight and suffer a –2 penalty to all attacks in full sun.

#### Monster Attacks

| **D6** | **ATTACK** |
| ------ | ---------- |
| 1 | **HAMMER FIST!** The ape drives one fist into an adventurer at ARM'S LENGTH. Roll the attack with nine Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. If the attack hits, the victim is knocked prone. |
| 2 | **ROCK THROW!** A fist-sized stone strikes one adventurer within SHORT range. Roll the attack with eight Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. |
| 3 | **CHEST ROAR!** The ape beats its breast and roars in the dark. All adventurers within NEAR range suffer a fear attack with six Base Dice. |
| 4 | **HOOKING ARM!** One long arm sweeps low. Roll the attack with eight Base Dice and Weapon Damage 1 against one adventurer at ARM'S LENGTH. If the attack hits, the victim is GRAPPLED. |
| 5 | **CAVE RUSH!** The ape throws its whole weight into two adventurers at ARM'S LENGTH. Roll the attack with seven Base Dice and Weapon Damage 1 against both. Anyone hit is knocked prone. |
| 6 | **BONE CRACK!** The ape lifts one GRAPPLED victim and smashes them against the cave wall. Roll the attack with nine Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. |

Pale Apes are cave clans gone feral or beasts risen almost into the shape
of men and then left there by hunger. They gather shiny things, salt,
and children with singing voices. In old mine-country there are still
families who leave fruit and offal at cave mouths rather than risk their
stock on a hungry spring.

#### Lore Roll

| D6 | LORE ROLL |
| -- | --------- |
| 1 | Pale Apes dwell in caves, old mine mouths, and high black cracks in limestone. |
| 2 | Miners say they hate the full light and grow clumsy in it, but in cave-dark they know every ledge before a man sees his own hand. |
| 3 | They take trinkets, children, and singing things to their dens. An ape that has started keeping trophies is almost always guarding young. |

#### Random Encounter: the Salt Cave

> _A line of apples sits on the cave ledge, each one with a bite taken from it and set back in place._

The villagers who live below the ridge have kept the Pale Apes quiet for
three years by leaving fruit and offal at the cave mouth. This winter
salt ran short, and the apes came down to take a girl instead. The
villagers want her back, but only after the adventurers first promise to
keep the cave-mouth tribute going through next winter from their own
packs if need be.

- **Terrain Types:** Hills, Mountains

#### Random Encounter: the Blind Singer

> _The child tied to the mule is humming so the dark things know where she is._

A father is leading his blind daughter up to an old silver adit because a
Pale Ape carries her brother there but will only answer the girl's song.
If the adventurers interfere, the ape will tear the brother apart before
it yields the cave. If they let the father go on, the ape gives back the
boy and keeps the girl. The father already decided which child he can
carry home alive.

- **Terrain Types:** Hills, Mountains, Ruins

> **RESOURCES**
>
> An adventurer with the HUNTER talent, with a HEALING roll, recovers
> one **white pelt** or one **knuckle-bone** per ⚔️ rolled.
>
> **White pelt:** Worn as a cloak, it grants a D8 Artifact Die to one
> SCOUTING roll made in cave-dark or snow-glare each Quarter Day.
>
> **Knuckle-bone:** Ground into grease by a CHEF or ALCHEMIST, it grants
> one adventurer a D8 Artifact Die on one MIGHT roll involving climbing,
> hauling, or bracing stone.

### Star-Watcher

> _Blue light opens in the ruin before the eyes do._

- **ATTRIBUTES:** Strength 12, Agility 3, Wits 4, Empathy 4
- **ARMOR RATING:** 6 (hide and void-hardened flesh)
- **MOVEMENT:** 1, flying
- **PASSIVE:** The Star-Watcher's central gaze quenches magic in the zone it faces.
- **SPECIAL WEAKNESS:** Each lesser eye can be destroyed by any attack that inflicts 3 points of damage and is aimed with a –2 penalty.

#### Monster Attacks

| **D6** | **ATTACK** |
| ------ | ---------- |
| 1 | **LESSER EYE!** One lesser eye fires at an adventurer within SHORT range. Roll the attack with eight Base Dice. On a hit, the GM chooses fear, blindness, or paralysis. |
| 2 | **TELEKINETIC FLING!** One adventurer within SHORT range suffers an attack with eight Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. If the attack hits, the victim is thrown to SHORT range and lands prone. |
| 3 | **SOUL GLARE!** All adventurers within NEAR range suffer a fear attack with eight Base Dice. |
| 4 | **STONE RAIN!** The Star-Watcher tears loose ruin-stone and sends it down. All adventurers within NEAR range suffer an attack with seven Base Dice and Weapon Damage 1. |
| 5 | **CRUSHING BITE!** The floating horror drops to ARM'S LENGTH and bites. Roll the attack with nine Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. |
| 6 | **VOID OPENING!** The Star-Watcher opens its central gaze on one adventurer within NEAR range. That adventurer must win an opposed WITS roll or suffer an attack with ten Base Dice that damages Wits. |

The Star-Watchers are not kin, not demons, and not anything the Raven
Sisters name gladly. They seem drawn to broken observatories, high ruin
courts, and old portals that no longer open cleanly. They do not guard
such places out of loyalty. They feed on ordered passage, and it
pleases them when the wrong thing goes through a broken gate.

#### Lore Roll

| D6 | LORE ROLL |
| -- | --------- |
| 1 | Star-Watchers haunt high ruins and places where old magic once watched the sky. |
| 2 | Their wide central gaze stills workings, charms, and all but the crudest sorcery. Hunters say to keep moving across its face, never stand in it. |
| 3 | A Star-Watcher does not see with one will but with several. Blind the side eyes and the beast loses its temper before it loses the fight. |

#### Random Encounter: the Tower Register

> _The tower door is open for the first time in forty years, and every villager wants what lies on the top floor._

A Star-Watcher opened the old watchtower when the Red Wanderer burned
bright, and now the village elder insists the tower register belongs to
her bloodline, while the Raven Sisters say it names the women burned to
keep the tower sealed. The creature floats three floors up and will only
attack once someone climbs the stairs with a lit charm or spell.

- **Terrain Types:** Hills, Ruins

#### Random Encounter: the Wrong Star

> _The shepherd has nailed the lambs indoors and is watching the sky through a bronze bowl of water._

A shepherd believes the Star-Watcher in the ridge ruin can tell him which
child is marked to die before winter. The village has come behind him in
silence because each household wants the answer and none wants to ask it
first. If the adventurers drive the creature off, the village keeps its
children and loses the certainty it came for. That uncertainty will not
sit gently among them.

- **Terrain Types:** Hills, Mountains, Ruins

> **RESOURCES**
>
> An adventurer with the ALCHEMIST or SORCERER talent, with a HEALING
> roll, gathers one **eye-stone** or one **void-lens** per ⚔️ rolled.
>
> **Eye-stone:** Ground into dust and breathed before a watch, it grants a
> D8 Artifact Die to one SCOUTING or LORE roll involving stars, omens, or
> portals.
>
> **Void-lens:** Set into a bronze frame by a SMITH, it creates a hand
> lens that can snuff one magical effect of Power Level 1 once per
> Quarter Day.

### Air Spirit

> _The noose on the gibbet twists though the morning is still._

- **ATTRIBUTES:** Strength 10, Agility 6, Wits 3, Empathy 2
- **ARMOR RATING:** 6
- **MOVEMENT:** 2, flying
- **PASSIVE:** Missiles fired through the heart of the spirit's body suffer a –2 penalty.

#### Monster Attacks

| **D6** | **ATTACK** |
| ------ | ---------- |
| 1 | **WIND SLAM!** One adventurer at ARM'S LENGTH suffers an attack with eight Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. |
| 2 | **WHIRLWIND HOLD!** One adventurer within NEAR range suffers an attack with eight Base Dice and Weapon Damage 1. If the attack hits, the victim is GRAPPLED in spinning air. |
| 3 | **SHARD OF SKY!** A hard knot of air strikes one adventurer within SHORT range. Roll the attack with eight Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. |
| 4 | **DUST BLIND!** All adventurers within NEAR range suffer an attack with six Base Dice and Weapon Damage 1. Anyone hit fights as in darkness until the end of the next round. |
| 5 | **THROWING WIND!** All adventurers within NEAR range are hurled down and take 1D6+3 meters of falling damage. |
| 6 | **LIGHTNING BREATH!** One adventurer within SHORT range suffers an attack with nine Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. |

Air Spirits gather where men have hanged one another, on cliff shrines,
open barrows, and headlands where the weather changes fast. They do not
hate the living in any human way. They simply have no patience for the
weight of flesh and like to see it broken against the land.

#### Lore Roll

| D6 | LORE ROLL |
| -- | --------- |
| 1 | Air Spirits haunt exposed places where the wind keeps no steady shape. |
| 2 | Shepherds say an Air Spirit thickens itself before it strikes; when the dust gathers close, get low or get under stone. |
| 3 | They are hardest to hit from afar and easiest to anger when men build gibbets, weather-vanes, or shrines where the sky wants empty ground. |

#### Random Encounter: the Gallows Hill

> _The hangman has gone home, but the rope is still taking names._

A village used the old hill again after twenty quiet years and hanged two
men for theft to calm a bad harvest. Now an Air Spirit walks the ridge.
The dead men's widows want the rope cut and burned. The headman wants it
left standing because fear has made the village orderly for the first
time in years.

- **Terrain Types:** Hills, Plains

#### Random Encounter: the Ferry Without Oars

> _The ferry drifts back and forth across the narrow water though nobody is pulling the line._

The ferryman's son bound an Air Spirit to keep the crossing moving after
his father died. It works. It also throws one traveler in ten into the
black water below when the spirit grows bored. If the adventurers break
it, the crossing dies and the winter trade with the next village dies
with it.

- **Terrain Types:** Lake, Quagmire, Marshlands

> **RESOURCES**
>
> An adventurer with the DRUID or SORCERER talent, with a HEALING roll,
> catches one **wind-knot** or one **storm-glass** per ⚔️ rolled.
>
> **Wind-knot:** Loosed from a pouch, it grants a D8 Artifact Die to one
> MOVE roll or one shot from a bow that same round.
>
> **Storm-glass:** Hung in a window or gatehouse, it warns of violent
> weather one Quarter Day before it strikes.

### Earth Spirit

> _The standing stone leans forward as if it means to hear the quarrel better._

- **ATTRIBUTES:** Strength 15, Agility 2, Wits 3, Empathy 2
- **ARMOR RATING:** 8 (living stone)
- **MOVEMENT:** 1
- **SPECIAL DEFENSE:** Weapons that suffer Gear damage while striking an Earth Spirit lose one extra point of durability.

#### Monster Attacks

| **D6** | **ATTACK** |
| ------ | ---------- |
| 1 | **STONE FIST!** One adventurer at ARM'S LENGTH suffers an attack with nine Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. If the attack hits, the victim is thrown to NEAR range. |
| 2 | **EARTH SPIKE!** A spear of stone rises under one adventurer within SHORT range. Roll the attack with eight Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. |
| 3 | **GROUND BREAK!** All adventurers within NEAR range suffer an attack with six Base Dice and Weapon Damage 1. Anyone hit lands prone. |
| 4 | **PEBBLE RAIN!** The spirit bursts shards from its own body. All adventurers within NEAR range suffer an attack with seven Base Dice and Weapon Damage 1. |
| 5 | **WALL HAND!** One adventurer within NEAR range suffers an attack with seven Base Dice and Weapon Damage 1. If the attack hits, the victim is held fast until an ally inflicts 2 points of damage on the spirit. |
| 6 | **BACKHAND OF STONE!** One adventurer within ARM'S LENGTH suffers an attack with eight Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. If the attack hits, the victim is thrown to SHORT range. |

Earth Spirits are the oldest temper of the land, usually roused by
quarrying, oath-breaking on cairn ground, or graves opened in greed.
They are slow to wake and slower to forget. Once angered, they stand on
one patch of land and make all passage there costly.

#### Lore Roll

| D6 | LORE ROLL |
| -- | --------- |
| 1 | Earth Spirits wake where cairns are broken, quarries bitten too deep, or graves opened for gain. |
| 2 | Smiths say bad steel chips itself to death on them. Hammers fare better than blades, and patience better than both. |
| 3 | They are creatures of one place. If the living leave the ground they defend and deny them anything to strike for, some of them settle back into stone. |

#### Random Encounter: the Quarry Debt

> _The cut face of the quarry is marked with old handprints in ochre under the fresh hammer scars._

The quarry master knew the handprints were burial signs and cut anyway,
because the village needed stone for a bridge before the river rose. Now
an Earth Spirit stands in the cut and three workers are trapped behind
it. Save the workers and the bridge never gets built. Finish the bridge
and the trapped men go thirsty in the stone throat by evening.

- **Terrain Types:** Hills, Mountains

#### Random Encounter: the Open Barrow Road

> _The new road is smoother than the old one because it runs straight over the old graves._

A chieftain had the barrows flattened so wagons could pass in winter. The
Earth Spirit that rose from the cut mound now blocks the road on market
mornings only, when the need is greatest. The merchants want it broken.
The families buried under the road want the barrows raised again and are
quite willing to lose the trade.

- **Terrain Types:** Plains, Hills

> **RESOURCES**
>
> An adventurer with the DRUID or SMITH talent, with a HEALING or
> CRAFTING roll, gathers one **heart-stone** or one **grave-dust** per
> ⚔️ rolled.
>
> **Heart-stone:** Built into a wall or gate, it gives +1 to the
> stronghold's Fortification for one season.
>
> **Grave-dust:** Thrown across a doorway, it grants a D8 Artifact Die to
> one INSIGHT roll made to detect sorcery or trespass over broken ground.

### Giant Scorpion

> _The shale slope moves in one hard line from top to bottom._

- **ATTRIBUTES:** Strength 10, Agility 3
- **ARMOR RATING:** 10 (plate chitin)
- **MOVEMENT:** 1
- **SPECIAL WEAKNESS:** The eyes may be targeted with a –3 penalty. Against such attacks, the scorpion gains no Armor Rating.

#### Monster Attacks

| **D6** | **ATTACK** |
| ------ | ---------- |
| 1 | **PINCER SLICE!** One adventurer at ARM'S LENGTH suffers an attack with nine Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. |
| 2 | **PINCER THROW!** One adventurer at ARM'S LENGTH suffers an attack with six Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. If the attack hits, the victim is thrown to SHORT range. |
| 3 | **PINCER HOLD!** One adventurer at ARM'S LENGTH suffers an attack with eight Base Dice and Weapon Damage 1. If the attack hits, the victim is GRAPPLED. |
| 4 | **SCUTTLE CHARGE!** The scorpion rushes every adventurer within NEAR range. All affected suffer an attack with six Base Dice and Weapon Damage 1. |
| 5 | **TAIL STING!** One adventurer at ARM'S LENGTH suffers an attack with six Base Dice and Weapon Damage 1. If damage is dealt, the victim is exposed to a paralyzing poison with Potency 6. |
| 6 | **DOUBLE PINCER!** One adventurer at ARM'S LENGTH suffers two separate attacks, each with six Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. |

Giant Scorpions dwell in sun-shot ruin courts, quarry pits, and warm
stone caves where the rock keeps the day's heat. They do not pursue far.
They kill what passes through their ground and go back to stillness
before the blood has dried.

#### Lore Roll

| D6 | LORE ROLL |
| -- | --------- |
| 1 | Giant Scorpions haunt warm stone, shattered ruins, and quarry slopes. |
| 2 | Masons say the eye is the answer if a man has the nerve to take it, because the shell elsewhere throws good steel aside. |
| 3 | They wait longer than hunger should allow. Many men have walked by one and only learned where it lay after the tail came over the shoulder. |

#### Random Encounter: the Lime Kiln Pit

> _The kiln has gone cold, but the ash around it is raked smooth each dawn._

A kiln-owner has been feeding condemned prisoners to the scorpion in the
pit under the kiln because its presence keeps thieves away from the lime
store. Now his own nephew has been accused of theft and tied at the pit
mouth. The owner wants the beast dead. The village wants the same beast
left alive after it eats the right boy.

- **Terrain Types:** Hills, Ruins

#### Random Encounter: the Warm Shrine

> _Every votive bowl on the shrine steps is full of live mice._

A shepherd clan keeps a Giant Scorpion at an old roadside shrine because
travelers who fear the beast leave better offerings than those who trust
the gods. The beast has started breeding. If the adventurers kill it,
the shrine's keepers lose their income and the next market season starves
half the clan. If they leave it, the brood hatches before frost.

- **Terrain Types:** Plains, Hills, Ruins

> **RESOURCES**
>
> An adventurer with the HUNTER or ALCHEMIST talent, with a HEALING roll,
> recovers one **venom sac** or one **eye-plate** per ⚔️ rolled.
>
> **Venom sac:** Brewed by a POISONER, it yields one paralyzing poison of
> Potency 6.
>
> **Eye-plate:** Set in a helm or shield by a SMITH, it grants a D8
> Artifact Die to one SCOUTING roll made to spot ambush or hidden stone.

- **ARMOR RATING:** 8
- **MOVEMENT:** 1
- **SPECIAL DEFENSE:** Acid restores lost Strength instead of damaging the golem.
- **SPECIAL WEAKNESS:** The joints may be targeted with a –3 penalty and gain no Armor Rating.

#### Monster Attacks

| **D6** | **ATTACK** |
| ------ | ---------- |
| 1 | **KILN FIST!** One adventurer at ARM'S LENGTH suffers an attack with nine Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. If the attack hits, the victim is thrown prone. |
| 2 | **DOUBLE SMACK!** One adventurer at ARM'S LENGTH suffers two separate attacks, each with six Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. |
| 3 | **DUST RUSH!** All adventurers at ARM'S LENGTH suffer an attack with seven Base Dice and Weapon Damage 1. |
| 4 | **CLAY HAND!** One adventurer within NEAR range suffers an attack with seven Base Dice and Weapon Damage 1. If the attack hits, the victim is held fast until an ally inflicts 2 points of damage on the golem. |

| 6 | **HEEL KICK!** One adventurer at ARM'S LENGTH suffers an attack with eight Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. If the attack hits, the victim is thrown to SHORT range. |

Clay Golems are cheap obedience made large. Temple vaults, barrow doors,
and lordly treasuries keep them because they do not need food, sleep, or
forgiveness. The soul inside, if there is one, is usually a detail the
owner prefers not to discuss.

#### Lore Roll

| D6 | LORE ROLL |
| -- | --------- |
| 1 | Clay Golems guard vaults, tombs, and hidden chambers. |
| 2 | Potters say bad acid strengthens them. Strike where they bend or where the maker pinned the joints. |
| 3 | Some of them hold a bound voice inside. A golem that turns its head toward speech is often listening for the name it was made to keep out. |

#### Random Encounter: the Chapel Guardian

> _The donation box has not been opened in nine years, but the priest still counts the coins each night._

A poor village chapel keeps a Clay Golem in the crypt because the tithe
silver under the altar buys winter grain from the next valley. The golem
was bound with the soul of a murderer promised burial if he served. His
widow has come to beg release now that the village is safe enough to risk
hunger instead.

- **Terrain Types:** Village, Hills, Ruins

#### Random Encounter: the Sealed Bride Chest

> _The chest in the bridal chamber is lashed shut from the outside, not the in._

A lord bound a Clay Golem to keep his daughter's dowry and the letters of
the man she loved from leaving the room. The daughter is dead. The
marriage contract the village now needs to avoid a feud is still inside
the chest, and the golem kills anyone who touches the iron clasps. Break
it and the dowry silver is gone. Leave it and the feud starts by dawn.

- **Terrain Types:** Hills, Ruins, Village

> **RESOURCES**
>
> An adventurer with the ALCHEMIST or SMITH talent, with a CRAFTING roll,
> gathers one **kiln-dust** or one **sigil shard** per ⚔️ rolled.
>
> **Kiln-dust:** Mixed into mortar, it grants +1 Fortification to one
> stronghold wall section for a season.
>
> **Sigil shard:** Pressed into wax over a chest or door, it grants a D8
> Artifact Die to one SLEIGHT OF HAND or CRAFTING roll made to seal or
> ward it.

### Iron Golem

> _The furnace-door in its chest glows orange before the hands move._

- **ATTRIBUTES:** Strength 24, Agility 1
- **ARMOR RATING:** 10
- **MOVEMENT:** 1
- **SPECIAL DEFENSE:** Fire restores lost Strength instead of damaging the golem.
- **SPECIAL WEAKNESS:** The eyes may be targeted with a –3 penalty and gain no Armor Rating.

#### Monster Attacks

| **D6** | **ATTACK** |
| ------ | ---------- |
| 1 | **IRON SLAM!** One adventurer at ARM'S LENGTH suffers an attack with twelve Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. If the attack hits, the victim is thrown prone. |
| 2 | **POISON VENT!** All adventurers within NEAR range are exposed to a lethal poison with Potency 8. |
| 3 | **GROUND STOMP!** All adventurers within NEAR range take 2D6 meters of falling damage and land prone. |
| 4 | **IRON CLAP!** Two adventurers at ARM'S LENGTH suffer an attack with ten Base Dice and Weapon Damage 1. |
| 5 | **THROWING GRIP!** One adventurer within NEAR range suffers an attack with eleven Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. If the attack hits, the victim is thrown to SHORT range. |
| 6 | **DEFENSIVE STANCE!** The golem braces and then lashes out at one adventurer with eight Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. Until the next round its Armor Rating rises by 2. |

Iron Golems are not for guarding grain or pious boxes. They guard the
things men expect armies to die for: old war stockpiles, royal foundries,
and the hidden rooms where the books that built dynasties were sealed
away from their heirs.

#### Lore Roll

| D6 | LORE ROLL |
| -- | --------- |
| 1 | Iron Golems guard great treasuries and war chambers. |
| 2 | Fire feeds them and poison lives in their chests. Smiths say the eyes are the quickest answer when the rest of the body refuses all reason. |

Wereboars come from bad feasts, blood grudges, old farm gods, and men
who mistook appetite for strength. They are angriest around stores,
pens, and winter meat because those are the things they wanted to keep
when they first changed.

#### Lore Roll

| D6 | LORE ROLL |
| -- | --------- |
| 1 | Wereboars are man-shapes twisted by tusk, rage, and appetite. |
| 2 | Old hunters keep silver for them and never fight one in a doorway if they can help it. A boar wants the rush and the crush. |
| 3 | Some keep enough man in them to chain themselves on the moon's worst nights. Those who stop chaining themselves are already half gone. |

#### Random Encounter: the Winter Slaughter

> _The smokehouse is locked from the outside and something inside is trying not to answer the smell of blood._

A farmer knows he is turning and has locked himself in the smokehouse
through slaughter day so he does not butcher the children with the pigs.
His wife wants him let out because the family needs his hands to finish
curing meat before it spoils. The children want the adventurers to leave
him chained because this is not the first winter he asked that door to be
barred.

- **Terrain Types:** Village, Plains

#### Random Encounter: the Trial Feast

> _The accused sits at the head of the table because nobody else dares take the seat tonight._

A village uses a cursed feast to test theft: whoever eats and then turns
by moonrise is guilty in the eyes of the old law. This year the accused
is innocent. The true thief is the headman's son, who means to let the
curse take the stranger at the table and keep the village peace at the
same time.

- **Terrain Types:** Village, Forest, Hills

> **RESOURCES**
>
> An adventurer with the HUNTER or ALCHEMIST talent, with a HEALING roll,
> gathers one **tusk marrow** or one **boar hide** per ⚔️ rolled.
>
> **Tusk marrow:** Eaten hot, it grants a D8 Artifact Die to one MIGHT or
> ENDURANCE roll in the same Quarter Day.
>
> **Boar hide:** Worked by a TANNER, it yields armor that grants +1 Armor
> Rating against charge attacks.

### Werewolf

> _The muzzle print in the blood is almost a man's hand._

- **ATTRIBUTES:** Strength 12, Agility 4, Wits 2, Empathy 2
- **ARMOR RATING:** 0
- **MOVEMENT:** 1
- **SPECIAL WEAKNESS:** Silver weapons deal double damage.

| **D6** | **ATTACK** |

| 4 | **BACKHAND!** One adventurer within NEAR range suffers an attack with six Base Dice and Weapon Damage 2. If the attack hits, the victim is thrown to SHORT range and lands prone. |

| 6 | **MAD BLOOD FRENZY!** All adventurers at ARM'S LENGTH suffer an attack with eight Base Dice and Weapon Damage 1. |

Werewolves belong to moon trails, old packs, and the part of a man that
learns too late how much he enjoyed the first kill. Some are cursed. Some
seek it. The result at the door is much the same.

#### Lore Roll

| D6 | LORE ROLL |
| -- | --------- |
| 1 | Werewolves are moon-taken killers that move like men until they do not. |
| 2 | Silver is the old answer, but not the only one. Hounds, bells, and the names of packmates once loved have stayed a werewolf's hand for a breath or two. |
| 3 | A werewolf that still comes back to the same house is not free of its human life. That tie may save someone, or damn the whole household, depending on who pulls it first. |

#### Random Encounter: the Full-Moon Escort

> _The man in chains is walking willingly between his two sisters and the sisters are both carrying keys._

They are taking their brother to the old lime pit where he changes away
from the village on full-moon nights. One sister wants him kept alive.
The other has brought a silver knife under her cloak because she is tired
of burying strangers found on the road each month. Their brother knows
which sister has the knife and keeps asking for the other one by name.

- **Terrain Types:** Hills, Forest

#### Random Encounter: the Pack's House

> _Every door in the hamlet is barred except one. From that house comes singing._

A hamlet has made terms with the werewolf that lives among them: one goat
and one song every full moon, and the beast takes nothing human. Tonight
there is no goat. The singer in the open house is the widow who lost her
son to the same pact five winters ago. She is singing because she means
the werewolf to take her instead of another child.

- **Terrain Types:** Village, Forest, Hills

> **RESOURCES**
>

> **Moon-tuft:** Burned in a watch fire, it grants a D8 Artifact Die to

>
> **Wolf tooth:** Set into a blade hilt or necklace, it grants a D8
> Artifact Die to one fear attack or one ENDURANCE roll against cold,
