# Proposal: Flexible Weapons and Shield Pressure

## Purpose

The current weapon tags already cover reach, leverage, and general parry pressure.
What they do not cover cleanly is the way some lash weapons work around the rim of a shield instead of smashing through it.

This proposal adds a small, narrow tag for that job.
It is meant to fit the fiction of whips and similar weapons without turning shields into dead weight.

## HEMA Read

From a HEMA standpoint, a flexible weapon does not ignore a shield.
It works around the edge, over the top, or into the exposed hand and forearm when the defender's line is weak.

That is a real pressure point.
It is also not the same thing as the brute, clumsy parry pressure of a chain weapon.

So the rule should model shield discomfort, not shield deletion.

## Main Problem

Whips already read as weapons that harry, entangle, and sting.
That is good fiction, but the current manuscript gives them no clear shield-specific hook.

At the same time, the manuscript already has `CHAINED`, which is a broad penalty to `PARRY`.
If the same weapons also get a broad shield-bypass rule, the line becomes too strong and too hard to read.

The missing space is a smaller rule:

- enough to make shield users think twice
- not enough to make shields pointless
- narrow enough to stay distinct from `CHAINED`

## Proposed Property

Add a new weapon property:

**FLEXIBLE:** When a target uses a shield to `PARRY` this weapon, the shield bonus is reduced by 1, to a minimum of 0. This does not affect `PARRY` with a weapon.

This keeps the shield in play.
It just makes the shield less certain when the weapon can slip around the rim.

## Best Fit

This property should be used first on weapons where the fiction already supports it:

- `WHIP`
- `BLADED WHIP`

It should not automatically be added to every chain weapon.
A flail is not a whip, and it should keep its own identity.

Suggested handling for other related weapons:

- `FLAIL` and `TWO-HANDED FLAIL` keep `CHAINED`
- `WHIP` and `BLADED WHIP` get `FLEXIBLE`
- only give both tags to the same weapon if playtest results say it is still too weak

## Why This Works

This rule gives flexible weapons a real combat job.
They matter against a shield line, but they do not erase it.

It also keeps the game readable:

- `CHAINED` is broad parry pressure
- `FLEXIBLE` is shield-edge pressure
- `HOOK` is control and shove pressure
- `LONG-REACH` is distance pressure

That division is easy to remember at the table.

## Balance View

### What It Rewards

- shield users who manage distance well
- flexible weapons used for line pressure instead of raw damage
- weapons that feel different from swords, axes, and maces

### What Players Will Probably Believe It Rewards

Players will expect flexible weapons to threaten shields without making them useless.
That expectation matches the proposed rule.

### What The GM Will Likely Need To Manage

- whether a weapon is truly a flexible weapon or just a chain weapon
- whether a shield is being used actively in close combat
- whether the weapon should be tagged `FLEXIBLE`, `CHAINED`, or both

That is manageable if the tag stays narrow.

## Risks

### Too Much Shield Bypass

If the penalty is larger than 1, shields start losing their identity too fast.
That would be bad for play.

### Too Much Overlap With `CHAINED`

If the same weapon gets both tags by default, the weapon becomes a soft shield-breaker without ever saying so plainly.
That is exactly the kind of hidden pressure the manuscript should avoid.

### Too Much Realism

HEMA supports the idea that a lash can find an opening.
HEMA does not support turning every flexible weapon into a universal answer to shield defense.
The rule should stay on the game side of that line.

## Recommended Revision

Accept the new property, but keep it small:

1. add `FLEXIBLE`
2. apply it first to `WHIP` and `BLADED WHIP`
3. keep the shield penalty at `-1` only
4. do not make it a second version of `CHAINED`

That gives flexible weapons a clear job and keeps shields worth carrying.
