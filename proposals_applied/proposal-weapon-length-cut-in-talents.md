# Weapon Length Talents

## Verdict

The CUT IN action and weapon-length rules in Chapter 5 create a reach game worth playing. Closing costs a FAST action and a MOVE roll. Against a LONG-REACH weapon it costs −2. BRACE punishes the rush. INTERCEPT covers the ambush. The core game handles the basics.

What it lacks is a reason to specialize. No talent rewards the knife fighter who reads the overextension and slips inside the haft. No talent rewards the spear holder who keeps fighters at the steel's edge round after round.

This proposal adds two general talents:

- **INSIDE THE GUARD** — the closer, who steps past longer weapons
- **KEEP THEM OUT** — the holder, who punishes every step forward

The original draft included a third talent (Pass The Measure) for pressing advantage after closing. That role is folded into Inside The Guard's higher ranks. Two talents with clear identities are better than three with overlapping ones.

## Design Goals

These talents must:

- reward reading distance and choosing the right moment to commit
- create real decisions about whether to close, hold, or break off
- preserve BRACE as the deliberate anti-charge stance
- preserve INTERCEPT as the generic ready action
- keep meaningful costs on activation — Willpower, action loss, or both
- feel worth the XP at ranks 4–5, where the investment is steep

These talents must not:

- stack three reactive punishments on one approach attempt
- make LONG-REACH irrelevant by letting everyone close cheaply
- remove the cost of closing from the game
- create a mandatory talent tax for melee fighters
- duplicate what BRACE and SPEAR FIGHTER already do

## Shared Rule: One Reactive Attack Per Approach

A single CUT IN attempt, charge, or approach can trigger at most **one reactive attack** from any source — BRACE, INTERCEPT, SPEAR FIGHTER rank 2, INSIDE THE GUARD, or KEEP THEM OUT. If more than one could fire, the defender chooses which one triggers. Other non-attack effects (such as KEEP THEM OUT's lockout at rank 1) can still apply alongside the chosen reactive attack.

This prevents a spear user from stacking BRACE, SPEAR FIGHTER, and KEEP THEM OUT into three hits on one closing attempt.

## INSIDE THE GUARD

You read the overextension and move before the haft recovers. Applies against any enemy whose melee weapon has longer reach than yours. Does not work against monsters.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** When an enemy attacks you with a melee weapon of longer reach than yours and rolls no ⚔️, you may spend 1 WP to close the distance, once per round. This counts as a successful CUT IN but costs no action. Alternatively, on your turn you may spend your FAST action to LUNGE, extending your weapon one reach band for one SLOW melee attack on the same turn. That attack suffers −2 and does not change the reach.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** After you successfully CUT IN, your next melee attack against that enemy this round gains +1.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** After you CUT IN against a LONG-REACH weapon, the enemy suffers −2 to their next MOVE roll to change reach against you before your next turn.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Whenever you roll a CUT IN against a longer weapon and score 2 or more ⚔️, you may make one free melee attack against that enemy.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** You can add a D8 Artifact Die to all your CUT IN rolls. This talent can now be used against monsters.

### Design Notes

Rank 1 is the identity. Sometimes the enemy overextends and you step inside. Sometimes you do not dare the full close and instead spend your FAST action on a hard, reaching lunge. One path wins the distance. The other steals one attack without changing it.

Rank 2 converts the entry into a slight edge. After a CUT IN it improves the follow-up. On a LUNGE it softens the penalty and makes the probing strike more credible. Rank 3 fouls their footwork after the close, making any MOVE roll to widen or rework the reach harder before your next turn.

Rank 4 is the decisive close. You commit to the rush, overwhelm the gap with skill, and your blade is already moving when you arrive. The 2+ successes threshold rewards investing in MOVE and means weak closers never see this fire. With the R5 artifact die, it becomes reliable enough to build a fighting style around.

Rank 5 is the mastery. The D8 Artifact Die makes every CUT IN more reliable — and now monsters with long limbs, tails, and polearm-length claws are fair game. The closer has trained against every kind of reach, not just steel.

## KEEP THEM OUT

You fight from the edge of your steel and punish every step forward. Applies when you hold a melee weapon with longer reach than your opponent's. Does not work against monsters.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** When an enemy rolls no ⚔️ on a CUT IN roll or on a LUNGE attack against you, you may spend 1 WP to lock the distance. They cannot attempt CUT IN or LUNGE against you again until after their next turn. This lockout cannot trigger on the same approach that triggered BRACE or INTERCEPT — choose one or the other.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** While your weapon has longer reach than your enemy's and they have not closed the distance, your melee attacks against them gain +1, and your PARRY attempts with that weapon gain +1.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** When you use RANK 1 to lock an enemy out, they also cannot PARRY your attacks until after their next turn. They can still DODGE.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** Once per round, when you use RANK 1, you may also make one immediate STAB against that enemy if your weapon can reach. This cannot trigger if BRACE, INTERCEPT, or SPEAR FIGHTER rank 2 already struck on that same approach.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** You can add a D8 Artifact Die to your RANK 4 attack. This talent can now be used against monsters.

### Design Notes

Rank 1 is the identity: the enemy rolled no ⚔️ while trying to break your distance, whether by CUT IN or LUNGE, and now they cannot try again for a full round. Tempo control, not damage.

Rank 2 rewards the discipline of keeping distance. The +1 attack and +1 PARRY both apply only while the gap holds — one successful CUT IN and both bonuses vanish.

Rank 3 stacks on rank 1. The enemy who rolled no ⚔️ on the close or on a LUNGE cannot PARRY your follow-up. Combined with rank 2's +1 attack, the failed rush hands you a round where you swing with a bonus against an enemy who can only dodge or take the hit. Simpler than tracking a −1 penalty. Stronger too.

Rank 4 draws blood. The failed rush triggers a reactive STAB — no additional WP, the cost is already paid through rank 1. It cannot stack with BRACE, INTERCEPT, or SPEAR FIGHTER on the same approach — you choose one.

Rank 5 is the mirror of Inside The Guard rank 5. The D8 Artifact Die makes the rank 4 STAB more reliable and harder — and now monsters with short limbs, paws, and claw-length reach are valid targets.

## Balance Safety

These talents are safe only if the base CUT IN action stays expensive. If CUT IN becomes free, both talents collapse into dominant lines.

The shared reactive-attack rule is the primary safety valve. Without it, a spear user with BRACE + SPEAR FIGHTER rank 2 + KEEP THEM OUT rank 4 would trigger three separate hits on one approach. The rule limits the stack to one reactive attack plus non-attack effects (lockout, prone, penalties).

Inside The Guard rank 1 now offers a second line: a LUNGE. That matters because it does not change reach and does not bypass the close game for free — it still costs a FAST action and strikes at −2 before rank 2 softens the penalty. KEEP THEM OUT can still punish a lunge that rolls no ⚔️, so the longer weapon retains a real answer.

Keep Them Out rank 5 adds a D8 Artifact Die to the rank 4 STAB and unlocks monster use. It mirrors Inside The Guard rank 5 in structure — both talents cap with a permanent dice upgrade and bestiary access.

## Integration Points

These talents belong in Chapter 4 among the general talents.

They interact with:

- **CUT IN** (Chapter 5) — the core action both talents modify
- **LUNGE** (Inside The Guard rank 1) — gives the closer a limited strike at extended reach without changing the distance; rank 2 improves that attack, and KEEP THEM OUT can punish it if the lunge rolls no ⚔️
- **MOVE rolls that change reach** (Chapter 5) — Inside The Guard rank 3 penalizes the next such roll against you, whether it is used to CUT IN, withdraw, or reopen distance
- **BRACE** (Chapter 5) — shared reactive rule governs stacking with KEEP THEM OUT rank 4
- **INTERCEPT** (Chapter 5) — shared reactive rule governs stacking
- **SPEAR FIGHTER** (General talent) — shared reactive rule prevents rank 2 from stacking with KEEP THEM OUT rank 4 on the same approach
- **MELEE CHARGE** (General talent) — a charge from NEAR to ARM'S LENGTH counts as an approach for the shared reactive rule
- **COMBAT EXPERIENCED** (General talent) — positional maneuvers (push, prone, overextend) apply after the close resolves, not during it
- **PATH OF THE BLADE** (Fighter) — damage bonuses apply normally to Inside The Guard rank 4's free attack
- **PATH OF THE ENEMY** (Fighter) — defensive debuffs from that path can make Inside The Guard rank 4's follow-up strike more punishing once you get inside
- **COMBAT EXPERIENCED** (General talent) — combat maneuvers apply after either talent's reactive strike resolves, not during it
- **Mounted combat** — mounted charges that close distance count as approaches for the shared reactive rule; KEEP THEM OUT applies against riders normally

## Recommendation

Accept both talents if the manuscript keeps CUT IN as a paid FAST action and BRACE as the dedicated receive-the-rush stance.

The shared reactive-attack rule should be stated once in Chapter 5 near the BRACE and INTERCEPT definitions, then referenced by name in both talent entries. That prevents the stacking problem at the source instead of patching it in each talent.
