# Pass The Measure

## Verdict

If the game keeps **LUNGE**, it should not live as a side clause inside another talent. It should have its own identity, its own cost curve, and its own limits.

This proposal creates one new **general talent** built around the lunge attack. Early ranks make the move risky and expensive. Middle ranks make it dependable. High ranks turn it into a signature flourish that can either draw blood from a safe distance or carry you through the line in one hard motion.

This also solves a structural problem in the current draft. `INSIDE THE GUARD` is now focused on winning the close, but both `INSIDE THE GUARD` rank 2 and `KEEP THEM OUT` still reference **LUNGE**. If lunge remains in the game, it should be anchored in its own talent.

Tentative talent name: **PASS THE MEASURE**.

## Design Goals

This talent must:

- preserve **CUT IN** as the normal way to truly close distance
- let a fighter threaten one reach band farther without changing the distance
- make the early lunge costly enough that it is a commitment, not a default attack
- let ranks 2-3 turn that commitment into a usable fighting style
- let ranks 4-5 feel heroic without making longer weapons irrelevant
- give `KEEP THEM OUT` a clear counterplay hook
- remain easy to run at the table without adding a full new subsystem

This talent must not:

- replace **CUT IN** in every serious melee
- let a **SHORT-REACH** weapon threaten a **LONG-REACH** target from two bands away
- let **LONG-REACH** weapons extend even farther
- trigger a stack of reactive attacks that Chapter 5 is trying to limit
- become a mandatory tax for anyone who fights with a knife or sword

## PASS THE MEASURE

You strike from the edge of distance and steal one hard blow from a space your enemy thought was safe.

This talent applies only when your weapon is exactly **one reach band shorter** than the target's fighting distance.

- A **SHORT-REACH** weapon can lunge against a **NORMAL REACH** fighter.
- A **NORMAL REACH** weapon can lunge against a **LONG-REACH** fighter.
- A **SHORT-REACH** weapon cannot lunge straight into a **LONG-REACH** target.
- A weapon that already has **LONG-REACH** cannot use this talent.

A **LUNGE** never changes the distance by itself. It is not a **CUT IN**. It is a strike thrown from the edge of the gap.

This talent does not work against monsters until **RANK 5**.

- ✦ **RANK 1:** Spend 1 WP and your **FAST** action to **LUNGE**. Your next **SLOW** melee attack that turn can reach one band farther than your weapon normally allows. The attack suffers **-3** and does not change the distance. If you roll no ⚔️, you cannot **PARRY** or **DODGE** that enemy until the start of your next turn.
- ✦ **RANK 2:** The attack penalty becomes **-2**. A failed lunge no longer strips your defenses.
- ✦ **RANK 3:** The attack penalty becomes **-1**.
- ✦ **RANK 4:** When your lunge roll scores **2 or more ⚔️**, choose one: deal **+1 damage**, or immediately **CUT IN** against that enemy for free after the attack resolves.
- ✦ **RANK 5:** Add a **D8 Artifact Die** to all lunge attacks. This talent now works against monsters. When a lunge scores **2 or more ⚔️**, you may choose **both** effects from **RANK 4**.

## Design Notes

### Why this should be a separate talent

A lunge is not the same decision as a close.

- **CUT IN** says: you commit your body and try to win the distance.
- **LUNGE** says: you steal one strike without truly taking the ground.

Those are different fighting logics. Putting both inside `INSIDE THE GUARD` muddies that talent's identity. Giving lunge its own talent makes each style clearer.

### Why the early ranks are harsh

At **RANK 1**, the talent is a gamble.

You pay:

- 1 WP
- your FAST action
- a steep penalty
- and, on a total miss, your own safety

That is deliberate. A lunge should feel like an overcommitted reach, not like free extra reach stapled onto every attack.

### Why ranks 2-3 stop at acceptable instead of effortless

The lunge should become usable, but it should never fully replace **CUT IN**.

Even at **RANK 3**, you still:

- spend 1 WP
- spend your FAST action
- keep the distance unchanged

That means the talent buys you one real attack from a bad distance, not permanent control of that distance.

### Why ranks 4-5 are heroic

The heroic version of a lunge is not just "the penalty is gone."
It is that the lunge starts to change the whole exchange.

At **RANK 4**, a strong lunge can either hit harder or flow straight into the close.
At **RANK 5**, the move becomes a true signature attack: more accurate, usable even against monsters, and able to both wound and carry you through the line.

That creates the right arc:

- risky at first
- reliable in the middle
- spectacular at the top

## Balance Safety

This proposal stays safe only if the following limits remain explicit:

1. **One-band limit only.**
   No short blade should threaten a long spear from two bands away.

2. **No long-reach looping.**
   Polearms and other **LONG-REACH** weapons do not gain even more reach from this talent.

3. **No free distance change until high rank.**
   The lunge remains distinct from **CUT IN** until **RANK 4**.

4. **WP cost remains through the acceptable ranks.**
   That keeps lunge from becoming the default replacement for closing.

5. **The distance does not change on ordinary use.**
   This preserves the identity of longer weapons as controllers of space.

## Integration Points

If accepted, this talent belongs in **Chapter 4** among the general talents.

It interacts with:

- **CUT IN / BACK** (Chapter 5) — lunge is an alternative attack line, not a replacement close
- **KEEP THEM OUT** — its existing lunge clause now has a proper anchor
- **INSIDE THE GUARD** — any remaining lunge language in that talent should be removed or transferred here
- **KNIFE FIGHTER** — attack bonuses apply normally to lunges made with knives and daggers
- **DUELIST** — attack bonuses apply normally if the hand requirement is met
- **PATH OF THE BLADE** — damage boosts apply normally if the lunge hits
- **COMBAT EXPERIENCED** — combat maneuvers can apply only if the lunge attack qualifies for them as a normal attack
- **BRACE** and **INTERCEPT** — a lunge does not move into reach and should not trigger approach-based reactions by itself
- **Monsters & Reach** — monster use stays locked until **RANK 5**

## Recommendation

Accept this as a separate proposal if the game wants to keep the lunge idea alive.

Do **not** fold it back into `INSIDE THE GUARD`.
That talent should stay about winning the close.
This talent should be about stealing one strike from outside it.

If this proposal is adopted, the manuscript should:

1. give `PASS THE MEASURE` a proper place among the general talents
2. remove stray lunge references from talents that no longer grant it
3. leave `KEEP THEM OUT` as the natural answer to a failed lunge
4. keep `CUT IN` as the normal route for taking and holding distance
