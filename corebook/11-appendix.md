# Optional Rules

## Heroic Campaigns in Forbidden Lands

> “Gither came Conan, the Cimmerian, black-haired, sullen eyed, sword in hand, a thief, a reaver, a slayer, with gigantic melancholies and gigantic mirth, to tread the jewelled thrones of the Earth under his sandaled feet.”

Player characters in Forbidden Lands are fragile things. A single blow can leave them bleeding to death on the floor, an unlucky roll can compromise a carefully laid plan, and a rushed action can risk the lives of the entire party. The Heroic Campaign optional rules reshape that baseline into a larger-than-life cadence with a Sword & Sorcery pulse, where characters hurl themselves into peril and barely escape with their honor intact.

Heroes in this mode are not saviors or saints. Their victories are personal, their motives sharp, their appetites plain: coin, revenge, and the next hard‑won victory. This tone fits high heroism and the darker breath of demonic menace alike, where the world is cruel, the stakes are real, and the heroes still press forward.

Whether by divine providence or hard‑won narrative momentum, they can resist more blows and take risks that no ordinary mortal would dare. They may not be the heroes of the land, but they are the heroes of their own saga.

What keeps these characters from succumbing to their foes is their magnificent VIGOR and RESOLVE, and their extraordinary WILLPOWER.

### Vigor and Resolve

The normal rules regarding character attributes still apply to this rule set, but two additional characteristics are derived from them: VIGOR and RESOLVE.

VIGOR is the character's physical endurance. It equals the average value of Strength and Agility, rounded up. It's a pool of points representing the hero's ability to deflect blows and avoid wounds before taking damage to their Strength or Agility.

RESOLVE is the character's mental endurance. It equals the average between Wits and Empathy, rounded up. It's a pool of points representing the hero's ability to resist mental attacks and fear before taking damage to their Wits or Empathy.

Whenever a character receives damage from an attack, the total is subtracted from VIGOR or RESOLVE before reducing the actual related attributes. Losing VIGOR or RESOLVE doesn't affect the number of dice a character rolls in consecutive challenges, but losing attribute points does as per the base Forbidden Lands rules.

VIGOR and RESOLVE can be recovered as follows:

- Spending WILLPOWER to recover one point of both VIGOR and RESOLVE. This does not consume an action and it can be done at the start of a turn in combat, or anytime outside of combat.

### Willpower

WILLPOWER can be earned in the same way as in the base Forbidden Lands rules: by pushing rolls and rolling BANES (results of "1" in the D6s).

However, in a game of High Adventure the player character doesn't take damage from BANES on pushed rolls, instead they increase the pool of MENACE by one point for each BANE rolled. MENACE measures the overall impending sense of doom that follows the characters in the story, and it's explained in detail elsewhere in this document.

WILLPOWER can also be used to roll up to three additional dice on any single roll, at the cost of one die per WILLPOWER point spent. Dice rolled this way are not rerolled on a pushed roll.

All player characters lose two WILLPOWER points whenever they REST or SLEEP, representing the cooling down of the energy and bravery that pushed them this far.

### Born on the Battlefield

> "Barbarism is the natural state of mankind," the borderer said, still staring somberly at the Cimmerian. "Civilization is unnatural. It is a whim of circumstance. And barbarism must always ultimately triumph."

#### Damage and Recovery

The rules for damage in tales of High Adventure are the same as in the base Forbidden Lands rules, but player characters receive damage to their VIGOR or RESOLVE first before damaging their attribute scores.

If VIGOR or RESOLVE are depleted, characters will take damage to their physical (Strength and Agility) or mental (Wits and Empathy) attributes, which will result in a reduced number of dice being rolled on skill checks, as normal.

VIGOR and RESOLVE can be recovered with a short rest equal to one turn (15 minutes), which fully restores both. Refer to the section on VIGOR and RESOLVE for specific ways to recover them during combat.

#### Broken Shields

When using a PARRY action with a shield, the character can choose to discard the shield to reduce all damage from the parried attack to zero if it had at least one success on the PARRY roll. This leaves the shield completely broken.

#### Displays of Might

Characters can use a FAST action to intimidate Minions after a successful attack against a MOB where at least two Minions have been killed on the same turn by the character making the Display of Might.

The character makes a test of MIGHT against their INSIGHT to scare them off and disband them from the MOB, where the number of successes equals the number of Minions that ran away. This only works against Minions with WITS (it doesn't work against undead, for instance).

#### Critical Injuries and Death

Whenever a character takes any amount of damage to an attribute after depleting their VIGOR or RESOLVE, they roll on the critical injuries table. This can happen regardless of them being BROKEN or still have some attribute points left.

- If the attribute does not reach zero (the character received damage, but is not BROKEN), the player rolls on the critical injuries table and takes the lowest D6 result as the tens, and the highest D6 result as the units.
- If the attribute reaches zero (the character has been BROKEN), the player rolls on the critical injuries table and takes the highest D6 result as the tens, and the lowest D6 result as the units.

For instance, a character with 3 Strength is hit for 2 damage. She rolls on the Critical Injuries table with a 4 and a 6. Since she is not BROKEN, this means she rolled a 46 and receives a more merciful critical injury. However, if the blow had caused 3 or more points of damage instead, she would be BROKEN and the same dice roll of a 4 and a 6 would have meant a result of 64 on the critical injuries table.

### The Day of Doom

> "Cast in the mould of humanity, they were distinctly not men. They were winged and of heroic proportions; not a branch on the mysterious stalk of evolution that culminated in man, but the ripe blossom on an alien tree, separate and apart from that stalk. Aside from their wings, in physical appearance they resembled man only as man in his highest form resembles the great apes. In spiritual, aesthetic and intellectual development they were superior to man as man is superior to the gorilla. But when they reared their colossal city, man's primal ancestors had not yet risen from the slime of the primordial seas."

#### Menace

MENACE is the imminent danger the heroes face as they push on their pursuit. It's the evil narrative sense of doom hanging over their heads, which in terms of play translates as a pool of dice (D6s) that the GM can use to fuel the talents of Foes and introduce other complications to the story as narratively appropriate.

The GM starts the game with a pool of MENACE equal to the sum of WILLPOWER points of the heroes, if any. The following are guidelines on how to use MENACE, but GMs shouldn't be afraid of improvising new ways of embodying the approaching ruin threatening the heroes and their goals.

- Fuel talents of Foe level NPCs at the cost of one MENACE per WILLPOWER required by the description of the talent.
- Introduce Minions into the fray at the cost of one MENACE per Minion. Minions are generally introduced in MOBS.
- Use bonus dice for NPC rolls at the cost of one MENACE per bonus die, to a maximum of +3.
- Introduce a minor complication into the scene at the cost of two MENACE (for instance: the escape door is jammed and the players need to force it open quickly or escape using a different route)
- Introduce a medium complication into the scene at the cost of four MENACE (for instance: the room catches fire and it will spread destroying treasure if the players don't act quickly; or a player character drops its weapon after a failed attack; or perhaps a Foe or Monster finds the strength to make a second attack on the same turn)
- Introduce a major complication at the cost of six MENACE (for instance: the bridge is not so stable after all and it breaks under their feet, requiring a MOVE roll to save their skin; or a Foe or Monster gains the Berserker talent).

#### Enemies

Heroes shouldn't be really threatened by the Minions of their real adversaries: powerful Foes and horrifying Monsters. The rules for High Adventure games introduce a new classification of enemies and rules to handle them easily without slowing down action scenes: Minions, Foes and Monsters.

#### Minions

Minions fulfill the important role of providing contrast with powerful Monsters and dangerous Foes. They only suppose a real danger in great numbers and their main function is to support Foes, their leaders, and make the player characters feel like heroes in an action scene.

Minions have only two points on each attribute, they have no talents, no VIGOR or RESOLVE and can’t PARRY or DODGE. Minions are generally unarmoured.

To create a Minion, use the skill stats for their respective average KIN or Monster (for the few monsters that use skills like normal NPCs) as detailed in the game master book, and reduce all attribute scores to two.

Minions can be grouped into MOBS of four individuals, sharing their attacks and health. Refer to the section on MOBS for further details.

#### Foes

Foes are NPCs at the power level of the player characters. They have attributes and skills like a normal character, as well as VIGOR, RESOLVE, and Talents fueled by MENACE instead of WILLPOWER. They can PARRY or DODGE attacks as normal, as well as discard the shield to survive a strong blow. Foes are generally named NPCs and they are created exactly as any player character.

Foes may be leading a mob of Minions, being shielded by their inferior counterparts. Refer to the section on MOBS for further details.

#### Monsters

Monster rules are as per the base Forbidden Lands rules, but their attacks can be parried if they are not disproportionately larger than humanoids, such as trolls or giants. The decision is ultimately left to the GM.

#### Mobs

The MOB mechanic is introduced to increase the speed of combat when large numbers of Minions are present; but also to increase the feeling of Sword & Sorcery and High Adventure genres, where the heroes fight large numbers of enemies and take down a numerous amount before getting to the real enemy.

A group of four Minions can form a MOB. Minions in a MOB attack as a group with a single action instead of each individual attacking on its own.

To account for group tactics, the MOB attack adds a number of bonus dice to their attack roll equal to the number of Minions above one. This means three bonus dice for a full MOB with four Minions, but this number will decrease as Minions are eliminated from the group.

A MOB shares a health pool and armour rating. When an attack is directed to a MOB, a common armour roll is made for the whole MOB, which is assumed to have the same amount of armour for each Minion. If the MOB takes damage from an attack, since each Minion has only two points on each attribute, each two points of damage will incapacitate or kill one Minion.

MENACE can be spent by the GM at any time to reinforce a MOB with additional Minions. Don't worry about where the Minions come from; this is a High Adventure game.

A Foe can be included in a MOB to make a formidable opponent. In this case, the Foe is the only one in the MOB making attacks, but the Minions will support their leader providing up to three bonus dice to the Foe attacks.

When attacking a MOB, all damage dealt will eliminate Minions before the Foe can be targeted without a -2 penalty. This is to represent the Minions hindering the attacks from the Heroes, sacrificing themselves for their master, being used by their master to stay out of harm’s way, or a combination of the tactics above.

### Evil Sorcery

> "First there was the blackness of an utter void, with the cold winds of cosmic space blowing through it. Then shapes, vague, monstrous and evanescent, rolled in dim panorama through the expanse of nothingness, as if the darkness were taking material form. The winds blew and a vortex formed a whirling pyramid of roaring blackness. From it grew Shape and Dimension; then suddenly, like clouds dispersing, the darkness rolled away on either hand and a huge city of dark green stone rose on the bank of a wide river, flowing through an illimitable plain. Through this city moved beings of alien configuration."

The base Forbidden Lands rules for magic already have a very powerful feel of Sword & Sorcery, where magic is a dangerous affair and power doesn’t come free. However, to increase the intensity of magical rituals and make them more in-tune with traditional Sword & Sorcery themes, the following rules for ritual sacrifices and cabals are introduced.

#### Ritual Sacrifice

Ritualistic sacrifices of conscious intelligent beings act as incredibly potent lure of dark horrors beyond the veil of reality, which help the sorcerer to achieve whatever blasphemous goal has in her perverted mind.

In terms of game mechanics, a ritual sacrifice doubles the WILLPOWER points spent by the sorcerer. This means that the base Power Level is doubled, and the sorcerer must roll double the dice to determine if the spell is overcharged or a magic mishap occurs.

The sacrifice must happen on the same turn as the spell is cast, and it requires a FAST ACTION to perform the sacrifice itself. Therefore the sacrifice must be properly prepared in advance; most likely needing additional characters to ensure the ritual is uninterrupted and the sacrifice about to be offered doesn’t fight back.

#### Cabals

Several sorcerers with aligned interest can join forces and sign a pact before their gods and the magical void beyond our world.

Cabals can share their WILLPOWER points when casting spells, supporting each other with strange rites and murmuring forgotten psalms. However, their souls are joined by a strange and powerful magic beyond their control, and therefore if any of the members of the cabal is BROKEN, all other members, no matter their location, will suffer an attack to their WITS with 10 base dice and damage equal to the amount of sorcerers in the Cabal.

There is no theoretical limit to how many sorcerers can collaborate in this way, but there is a practical limit: sorcerers tend to be individualistic creatures, pursuing their own interest before any other things. It is rare for more than two sorcerers to form a cabal among the NPCs, and this is still very rare. If all your players want to form a cabal of sorcerers, let them! It can be a fun game to play working together towards a common goal of their own; just make sure to introduce discord in the group and powerful enemies to thwart their scheming.

### Perilous Lands

“The thing was not a human — it could not be; it was a growth of Life from the pits of blasphemous creation — a perversion of evolutionary development.”

The following is a selection of archetypal Minions, Foes and Monsters for a Sword & Sorcery scenario.

#### Petty Minions

Minions don’t PARRY, DODGE or use talents. Minions should be used in MOBS with one weapon type.

##### Mercenaries - Hired Blades

Bandits in time of peace, soldiers in time of war; these men and women are ruthless killers selling their blades to the highest bidder, no matter their intentions.

✦ **STRENGTH** 2, **AGILITY** 2, **WITS** 2, **EMPATHY** 2
✦ **SKILLS**: Melee 2, Marksmanship 2, Scouting 2
✦ **GEAR**: Falchion (+1, 2 dmg, Arm, Edged Pointed), Light Crossbow (+1, 2 dmg, Long, Slow Loading)

##### Savages - Tribal Warriors

Ancient tribes living off the land; hunting and making war with each other to defend their territory. Sometimes these tribes ally themselves with powerful leaders or worship foul gods of a forgotten time, doing their bidding in exchange for influence over their equals.

✦ **STRENGTH** 2, **AGILITY** 2, **WITS** 2, **EMPATHY** 2
✦ **SKILLS**: Melee 2, Marksmanship 2, Survival 2
✦ **GEAR**: Short Spear (+1, 1 dmg, Near, Pointed), Short Bow (+2, 2 dmg, Short, Light)

##### Cannibals - Degenerate Kin

Groups of cannibals poison the land with their mere existence. They lurk in forgotten paths, ambushing unlucky travellers to eat their flesh and use their blood in abhorrent rituals.

✦ **STRENGTH** 2, **AGILITY** 2, **WITS** 2, **EMPATHY** 2
✦ **SKILLS**: Melee 2, Stealth 2, Move 2
✦ **GEAR**: Wooden Club (+1, 1 dmg, Arm, Blunt), Rocks (-, 1 dmg, Near, Light)

##### Cultists - Dangerous Lunatics

Usually found serving a higher power, they will do anything to achieve their unholy goal: kidnappings, sacrifices, torture...Nothing is beyond limits to please their foul master.

✦ **STRENGTH** 2, **AGILITY** 2, **WITS** 2, **EMPATHY** 2
✦ **SKILLS**: Melee 1, Insight 3, Manipulation 2
✦ **GEAR**: Dagger (+1, 1 dmg, Arm, Edged Pointed), Flail (+1, 2 dmg, Near, Blunt)

##### Pirates - Scourge of the Sea

Roaming the waters in search for prey and treasure; sometimes they let the victims of their robbery live another day, hoping to encounter them in the future. Sometimes.

✦ **STRENGTH** 2, **AGILITY** 2, **WITS** 2, **EMPATHY** 2
✦ **SKILLS**: Melee 2, Move 2, Scouting 2
✦ **GEAR**: Scimitar (+1, 2 dmg, Arm, Edged Pointed Hook Parrying)

### Dangerous Foes

Foes can PARRY, DODGE and use talents fueled by MENACE instead of WILLPOWER. They can be used supported by a MOB.

#### Mercenary Leader - War Veteran

Hardened warrior leading soldiers or bandits into battle. A dangerous veteran that it’s not strange to death. He answers to riches, not to threats, and his loyalty is expensive and doesn’t change easily.

✦ **STRENGTH** 4, **AGILITY** 3, **WITS** 2, **EMPATHY** 3
✦ **SKILLS**: Might 3, Melee 4, Insight 3
✦ **GEAR**: Battleaxe (+2, 2 dmg, Arm, Heavy Edged Hook), Large Shield (+2), Chainmail (6 armor, Heavy, half armor against stabs)
✦ **TALENTS**: Kin talent, Path of the Blade (Rank 1), Axe Fighter (Rank 2)

#### War Chief - Tribal Leader

Chief of the village, the hunting rites and the war band when the tribe is in conflict. This Foe fights with two weapons at the same time, attacking with both each turn in a display of incredible skill.

✦ **STRENGTH** 3, **AGILITY** 4, **WITS** 3, **EMPATHY** 2
✦ **SKILLS**: Melee 4, Survival 2, Insight 3
✦ **GEAR**: Short Spear (+1, 1 dmg, Near, Pointed), Falchion (+1, 2 dmg, Arm, Edged Pointed)
✦ **TALENTS**: Kin talent, Ambidextrous (Rank 3)

#### Venerated Shaman - GM of Secrets

Holder of secret rites, elder among the old. This Foe studies the stars at night and waits for the full moon to make sacrifices to the old gods in exchange for the prosperity of her tribe.

✦ **STRENGTH** 2, **AGILITY** 2, **WITS** 4, **EMPATHY** 4
✦ **SKILLS**: Might 2, Melee 4, Insight 3
✦ **GEAR**: A pouch full of carved teeth that can be used to predict the weather and other, more frightful, things. The shrunken heads of tribe members, hanging from a belt made of the skin of a giant serpent. They may hold two value for the right buyer.
✦ **TALENTS**: Kin talent, Path of Sight (Rank 3)

#### Pirate Captain - Rough Master

It takes a strong hand to lead inferior men into battle; and even a stronger hand and a rough mood to control them at sea. This Foe can intimidate the strongest of warriors and their men feared him more than death itself.

✦ **STRENGTH** 3, **AGILITY** 4, **WITS** 2, **EMPATHY** 3
✦ **SKILLS**: Melee 3, Move 3, Manipulation 4
✦ **GEAR**: Scimitar (+1, 2 dmg, Arm, Edged Pointed Hook Parrying), Studded Leather (3 armor)
✦ **TALENTS**: Kin talent, Path of Poison (Rank 1), Sword Fighter (Rank 2)

#### Evil Sorcerer - Black-hearted Nemesis

Strong will and even stronger ambition. This Foe will not stop at anything to achieve power, making deals with local tribes, gathering cultists and sacrificing souls to fuel unholy experiments and rituals. The only harmless sorcerer is a dead sorcerer.

✦ **STRENGTH** 2, **AGILITY** 3, **WITS** 4, **EMPATHY** 3
✦ **SKILLS**: Lore 4, Insight 3, Manipulation 3
✦ **GEAR**: Sacrificial Dagger (+1, 1 dmg, Arm, Light Edged Pointed)
✦ **TALENTS**: Kin talent, Path of Blood (Rank 2), Path of Death (Rank 2)

### Horrifying Monsters

“It was no ape, neither was it a man. It was some shambling horror spawned in the mysterious, nameless jungles of the south, where strange life teemed in the reeking rot without the dominance of man, and drums thundered in temples that had never known the tread of a human foot.”

Monsters work as in the base Forbidden Lands rules, but GMs are encouraged to allow characters to PARRY attacks if it makes sense in the narrative; especially if they are willing to sacrifice their shield in the process. Allow these monsters to act twice per round if they are not horrifying enough.

#### Giant Serpent

These massive serpents can swallow whole creatures of surprising size. These enormous predators are prized among degenerate cultists and sorcerers as guardians.

✦ **STRENGTH** 18, **AGILITY** 5
✦ **ARMOR**: 2 (scales)
✦ **MOVE**: 1 (acts once per round)
✦ **WEAKNESS**: An attack aimed at the mouth suffers a -2 penalty, but is not protected by any armor.
✦ **ATTACKS**:
1 - HYPNOTIZING LOOK! - The eyes of the serpent are like horrific promises. Roll an attack with 8 base dice and any damage 1. If it hits, the target loses their next SLOW action.
2 - CRUSHING EMBRACE! - The serpent wraps one character with their massive body. Roll an attack with 8 base dice and damage 1 (Blunt). If it hits, the target is GRAPPLED and the serpent automatically succeeds on a SWALLOW HOLE on the next turn.
3 - OTHERWORLDLY HISS! - The serpent hisses at its prey. Roll an attack with 10 base dice and deal 1 to all characters within Short range.
4 - POISONOUS BITE! - The serpent extends its teeth and attacks. Roll 8 base dice with damage 2 (Stab). If it hits, the target suffers a lethal poison of potency 6.
5 - TAIL SLASH! - The serpent dangerously slashes its tail. Roll an attack with 10 base dice and damage 1 (Blunt) against all enemies in Near range.
6 - SWALLOWED WHOLE! - The serpent opens its maw and bites to swallow a character whole. Roll 6 base dice with damage 3 (Stab). If it hits, the target is trapped inside the beast and will suffer an attack with 10 base dice and damage 1 each turn inside.

#### Feral Ape

These humongous apes are the result of an evolutionary offshoot of humans that evolved into hyper aggressive gorilla-type creatures. Their incredible strength is only matched by their lust for gore.

✦ **STRENGTH** 14, **AGILITY** 8
✦ **ARMOR**: 4 (thick hide)
✦ **MOVE**: 2 (acts twice per round)
✦ **WEAKNESS**: Afraid of fire (attacks against a character holding fire have a -2 penalty to all attacks)
✦ **ATTACKS**:
1 - CHEST BEATING! - The ape beats its chest with untamed fury. Roll an attack with 8 base dice and 1 fear damage against all characters in Short range.
2 - RUSHED JUMP! - The ape jumps towards a character in near range. Roll an attack with 8 base dice and damage 2 (Blunt). If it hits, the target falls prone and drops a weapon.
3 - CHARGE! - The ape charges head on against a character in Short range. Roll an attack with 6 base dice and damage 2 (Blunt). If it hits, the character falls prone and is pushed a zone.
4 - HIDEOUS BITE! - The ape bites a character, trying to rip off some flesh. Roll an attack with 8 base dice and damage 1 (Stab).
5 - BONE BREAKER! - The ape mauls its victim to break their bones. Roll an attack with 6 base dice and damage 2 (Blunt).
6 - RIP APART! - The ape grabs a character with its victim and pulls with a tremendous force. Roll an attack with 4 base dice and damage 3 (Slash).

#### Enormous Spider

These large spiders only grow in the darkest corners of the earth, ambushing travellers and animals of all sizes, growing large as they feed on the corpses of their victims and lay eggs on the spoils of the carcasses.

✦ **STRENGTH** 12, **AGILITY** 10
✦ **ARMOR**: 2 (hard skin)
✦ **MOVE**: 2 (acts twice per round)
✦ **WEAKNESS**: Only attacks once per turn when in bright light.
✦ **ATTACKS**:
1 - DANCE OF DEATH! - The spider moves in a tantalizing way, playing with its prey. Roll an attack with 8 base dice and damage 1 (Horror) against all characters within Short range.
2 - SILK EMBRACE! - The spider shoots a web and entangles a victim in Short range. Roll an attack with 8 base dice and damage 1 (Blunt). If it hits, the victim falls prone and is covered in sticky silk, which greatly hinders all physical actions until cleaned.
3 - MESMERIZING EYES! - The spider looks into the eyes of its victim. Roll an attack with 8 base dice and damage 1 (Horror). If it hits, the victim loses its next FAST action.
4 - GRUESOME BITE! - The spider bites its victim. Roll an attack with 8 base dice and damage 1 (Slash).
5 - BODY SLAM! - The spider uses its body to crush its victim. Roll an attack with 8 base dice and damage 2 (Blunt).
6 - POISONOUS STING! - The spider stings its victim. Roll an attack with 8 base dice and damage 1 (Stab). If it hits, the victim suffers a paralysing poison of potency 6.

#### Forgotten God

A creature older than the earth that it inhabits and feeds from; sucking all life and consuming all things around it in the short periods they are awake. They usually live in ruined cities and swamps, spoils of their last feed-rampage, but they are willing to move in search for new prey when their hunger is insufferable.

✦ **STRENGTH** 25, **AGILITY** 5
✦ **ARMOR**: 10
✦ **WEAKNESS**: Bronze weapons ignore armor and cause double damage to this foul creature.
✦ **ATTACKS**:
1 - SIGHT OF AGES PAST! - The horror sends visions of past times and other worlds so alien that cause fear on the heart of the bravest warrior. Roll an attack with 10 base dice and damage 2 (Horror) at all characters in Short range.
2 - TENTACLE GRAB! - The horror tries to grab a character in Near range and squeeze their organs out with tremendous force. Roll an attack with 10 base dice and damage 2 (Blunt).
3 - OTHERWORDLY SCREECHING! - The horror produces an unbearable sound from an unknown source. Roll an attack with 6 base dice and damage 3 against all characters in Near range.
4 - ACIDIC SECRETIONS! - The horror spits acid from several pustules in its body towards a character. Roll an attack with 8 base dice and damage 2 (Special). Any damage reduced by armor is permanently reduced from its armor rating.
5 - BEAK ATTACK! - The horror uses its beak to break a character in two parts. Roll for an attack with 6 base dice and damage 3 (Slash). If it hits and causes damage, it causes Critical Injuries from both Slash and Horror tables.
6 - TENTACLE SMASH! - The horror smashes a character. Roll an attack with 6 base dice and damage 3 (Blunt). If it hits and causes damage, it causes Critical Injuries from both Slash and Horror tables.
