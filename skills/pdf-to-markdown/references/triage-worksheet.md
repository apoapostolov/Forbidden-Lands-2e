# OCR Triage Worksheet

Use this worksheet before committing to a cleanup strategy.

## Classification

- Source type:
  - selectable PDF
  - scanned PDF with OCR
  - raw markdown dump
  - half-cleaned markdown

- Dominant document type:
  - corebook
  - supplement
  - spell compendium
  - bestiary
  - lifepath generator

## Damage Estimate

- Heading damage:
  - low
  - medium
  - high

- Table damage:
  - low
  - medium
  - high

- Paragraph flow damage:
  - low
  - medium
  - high

- Lore term corruption:
  - low
  - medium
  - high

## Recommended Strategy

### Automation Only

Choose this when:

- headings are mostly intact
- tables mostly survive
- paragraph flow is the main issue

### Automation Plus Manual Repair

Choose this when:

- structure is partially present
- a subset of tables needs reconstruction
- the opening pages are ugly but recoverable

### Full Manual Reconstruction

Choose this when:

- tables are flattened throughout
- headings are unreliable
- key rule sections cannot be recovered safely by pattern alone

## Escalation Note

If table damage is high and lore corruption is high, assume a stronger review
pass will be needed even after automation.
