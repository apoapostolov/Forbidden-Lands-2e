# Playtest and Balance Protocol

Use this reference to replace impressionistic balance claims with evidence.
Testing should discover both numerical failures and experience failures.

## Write Testable Claims

For each feature, record:

- intended fantasy
- intended user and campaign phase
- expected frequency
- expected cost
- expected success band
- expected strongest combination
- pressure channel relieved
- pressure channels preserved
- acceptable failure severity
- explicit stop conditions

Convert vague goals into hypotheses:

- “A prepared specialist succeeds often, but still considers pushing.”
- “The ability matters once or twice per session, not every round.”
- “Failure costs time or resources without blocking the expedition.”
- “The rare profession feels distinct without outperforming the Fighter's broad
  combat role.”

## Establish Baselines

Compare against real manuscript options with similar:

- advancement cost
- action cost
- resource cost
- access rarity
- breadth
- use frequency
- danger
- campaign authority

Use at least:

1. an ordinary baseline
2. a strong specialized baseline
3. the proposed strongest legal build

Do not compare only headline damage or dice. Include setup, reliability,
counterplay, recovery, and opportunity cost.

## Scenario Matrix

Test across:

| Axis | Low | Typical | High |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Experience | new player | familiar | optimizer |
| Resources | depleted | ordinary | fully prepared |
| Opposition | weak | peer | superior |
| Environment | favorable | mixed | hostile |
| Frequency | first use | repeated | campaign routine |
| Fortune | unlucky | median | lucky |
| Interpretation | strict | expected | permissive |

Not every combination needs a full session. Select cases most likely to expose
dominance, irrelevance, confusion, or campaign erosion.

## Four Test Scales

### Resolution

Measure:

- success probability before and after pushing
- expected Banes and gear damage
- expected effect size
- probability of extreme results
- number of modifiers and lookups

### Scene

Measure:

- actions consumed and denied
- rounds to resolution
- damage or pressure prevented
- counterplay opportunities
- spotlight minutes
- number of meaningful decisions

### Session

Measure:

- uses and resource renewal
- repeated optimal lines
- niche overlap
- GM corrections or forgotten exceptions
- recovery and downtime displaced

### Campaign

Measure:

- advancement efficiency
- wealth and resource loops
- travel range and attrition
- injury and recovery consequences
- faction or information authority
- whether encounter and site design must change around the feature

## Adversarial Build Test

Assume players:

- choose the best kin and profession pairing
- reach the most efficient talent rank
- use optimal gear and artifact dice
- coordinate party support
- prepare through travel, stronghold, and downtime rules
- use favorable timing
- read ambiguous text in the strongest legal way

Trace every input and output. Look for:

- refunded costs
- extra actions feeding premium actions
- automatic success
- repeated control
- defense layers without counters
- recovery faster than damage accumulation
- information abilities that solve rather than investigate
- access gates bypassed by broad wording

The purpose is not to punish optimization. It is to ensure mastery produces
interesting power rather than one compulsory line.

## Experience Observation

Record behavior, not just opinion:

- time spent deciding
- questions asked before commitment
- rules lookups
- whether alternatives were considered
- who spoke and chose
- excitement, hesitation, frustration, withdrawal
- whether the player understood the consequence
- whether failure generated a new plan
- whether the tenth use remained engaging

Afterward ask players separately what they believed the rule did. Divergent
answers reveal clarity problems even when play appeared smooth.

## Failure Injection

Test deliberately:

- zero successes
- a damaging push
- minimum resources
- the key NPC refuses
- the route closes
- the target is immune or absent
- the specialist is Broken
- the party retreats
- a rival uses the same option

The game must remain playable when the feature fails or is unavailable.

## Sandbox Regression

For a new feature, ask whether it:

- makes one route, faction, or site type irrelevant
- lets one roll replace investigation
- turns preparation into a fixed checklist
- forces every threat to include a specific counter
- gives the GM hidden work to preserve uncertainty
- bypasses the consequences that make locations distinct
- centralizes party decisions in one build

Test at least one situation where players pursue an unanticipated goal.

## Severity Classes

- **Critical:** infinite or self-funding loop; automatic broad success; routine
  removal from play; core pressure channel erased.
- **Major:** dominant option; severe niche collapse; repeated control; unclear
  rule with materially different outcomes.
- **Moderate:** overtuned frequency; excessive lookup; narrow exploit; weak
  counterplay.
- **Minor:** wording friction, rare edge case, small numerical drift.

Critical and major findings block publication. Moderate findings require a
revision or explicit monitoring plan.

## Fix Order

Use the least invasive effective fix:

1. clarify trigger or scope
2. restore action or resource cost
3. limit frequency
4. require setup or fictional position
5. add counterplay
6. narrow breadth
7. change access or rank
8. reduce effect size
9. redesign the mechanism

Numerical reduction is not always the best fix. A boring dominant passive often
needs a decision or counterplay, not merely a smaller bonus.

## Regression Set

After revision, rerun:

- ordinary baseline
- strongest build
- depleted state
- repeated-use case
- strict and permissive readings
- one failed use
- one unplanned sandbox use
- adjacent profession or talent niche

Record both the original failure and the corrected result.

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Hypotheses were written before results were interpreted.
- [ ] Real manuscript baselines were used.
- [ ] The strongest legal combination was tested.
- [ ] Repetition and campaign effects were measured.
- [ ] Unlucky and depleted cases remained playable.
- [ ] Player comprehension and spotlight were observed.
- [ ] An unplanned sandbox goal was tested.
- [ ] Fixes addressed the cause, not only the number.
- [ ] Adjacent niches survived regression.
- [ ] Assumptions and uncertainties are documented.
