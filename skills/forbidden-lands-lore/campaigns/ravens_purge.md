# Raven's Purge — Campaign Reference

**Lead Writer:** Erik Granström
**Published:** Free League Publishing, 2018
**Campaign Type:** Open-world, non-linear, faction-driven
**Scope:** Entirely within the core Ravenlands region

---

## What Is a Campaign?

A campaign is a major plotline with additional lore, named
factions, detailed locations, and a central conflict that gives
players a story to pursue. Campaign lore *extends* the core
world — adding new places, characters, and events — rather than
replacing it. Everything in a campaign happens inside the
existing world of the Forbidden Lands.

Because campaigns include reveals, secrets, and plot twists that
are best discovered through play, some content here is marked
**SPOILER**. Before reading any section marked SPOILER, ask the
user if they consent to spoiler content.

---

## Campaign Overview

Raven's Purge concerns the elven crown **Stanengist** and the
struggle over who will control the Forbidden Lands after the
Blood Mist has lifted. Six ancient elves shed their flesh and had
their heart-rubies set into the crown to watch over the land.
The crown was stolen by the demon **Merigall** over a thousand
years ago; three of the rubies were later moved to other
artifacts. Now, with the land open again, every major power
faction moves to claim the crown or the rubies — each for their
own reason.

The campaign has no single correct resolution. The players'
choices determine which faction rises, which falls, and what
shape the Forbidden Lands take next.

**Conditions to begin the finale at Vond:**
- The adventurers possess Stanengist and at least one of the
  other three ruby-bearing artifacts.
- They have met most of the key players and know their
  intentions.
- They have heard the legends of Zytera, Krasylla, and Vond.

## The Central MacGuffin: Stanengist

### Public Legend

> *It is said that the first six elves of the Forbidden Lands —
> the ones who planted the trees, drew the furrows of the rivers
> and gave the animals their home — after completing their
> mission, had their rubies gathered in the royal crown,
> Stanengist, the name of which means "The Hanging Stones."
> In the crown they rested deservedly, but at the same time,
> kept watch over their creation and gave advice to their
> descendants. For a long time, the crown was worn by the kings
> of the land and granted them the power to keep the kingdom
> intact, but sometime right before the humans arrived in
> Ravenland, the thief Merigall stole three of the stones from
> Stanengist, and the land has been sundered ever since.
> Whoever re-assembles the stones in the crown and places it on
> her head shall according to legend gain the power to rally all
> kin and rule over the Forbidden Lands.*

### Three Companion Artifacts

Three other named artifacts carry elven rubies from the
original crown. Their public legends are in
`references/artifacts.md`:

- **Maligarn** — the Giant Slayer sword, emerald Gall-Eye
- **Nekhaka** — the royal scepter, ruby Algared
- **Blood Star (Hemella)** — cloak clasp, ruby Iridne

## Key Players — Non-Spoiler Summaries

What the world knows, or suspects, about each player.

### Zytera

The creature the Rust Brothers call their prophet. Formed when
the sorcerer **Zygofer** and his daughter **Therania** agreed
to merge with a demon prince after the Fourth Alder Wars. The
result is a many-legged spider-creature with two human bodies
growing from its back. The Rust Brothers say Zytera protects
humanity from demonic invasion by absorbing and redirecting the
demon energies; the truth behind this claim is unclear.

Zytera holds the fortress of **Vond** at the base of Mount Ora.
The Mogarium at the mountain's peak is its laboratory.

**Public Legend:**
> *They say that the sorcerer Zygofer, during the final battle
> of the Fourth Alder War, summoned a demonic horde that
> slaughtered King Algarod and the Alderlander army. To
> consolidate the alliance, Zygofer and his daughter, Therania,
> agreed to being merged with a demon prince to form the
> man-spider Zytera, whom the Rust Brothers still venerate as
> the prophet of the god Rust. Allegedly, Zytera still rules
> the holdfast of Vond, from which the monstrosity sends out
> the unnatural demonic creatures that haunt the Forbidden
> Lands.*

### Merigall

A shapeshifting demon of uncertain origin that travels the
Forbidden Lands as a bard, a spy, and a provocateur. No one
knows Merigall's gender; it changes form at will, though its
eyes are always yellow. It has offspring scattered across all
kin in every region. It is as likely to aid a stranger as to
destroy one. Some believe it is a god.

Merigall is officially Zytera's closest ally. In public it helps
the sorcerer with demonic experiments.

**Public Legend:**
> *The name Merigall soars like a vague shadow with yellow eyes
> across the Forbidden Lands. No one knows who the creature is
> or even if it is a woman or a man. Maybe there are several
> Merigalls, for the creature has been spotted in places far and
> wide from one day to the next. The young are seduced in
> castles as well as cottages so that the yellow-eyed offspring
> of Merigall can be found in the most distant regions and among
> all kin. Some believe the creature to be a demon, others
> revere it as a god, for the truth is that it is as likely to
> help those who encounter it as it is to destroy them.*

### Krasylla

A demon prince who survived the Alder Wars and signed a contract
with Zytera rather than face the impossible journey back to
Churmog. He has settled in Vond and melted down over the
centuries into a shapeless, vast mass in his chamber. The Rust
Brothers worship and fear him. He guards Shadowgate Pass.

**Public Legend:**
> *At the end of the Alder Wars the demon prince Krasylla stood
> bereft of a way home to his native world of Churmog. They say
> that Zytera made a deal with the terrible creature then,
> giving it sanctuary in Vond in return for guarding Shadowgate
> Pass against the Aslene that Krasylla himself had ravaged.
> It is whispered that the human sacrifices that the villages
> are forced to provide to the Rust Brothers become sustenance
> for Krasylla and that the demon nowadays lies fat and immobile
> in the interior of the castle.*

### Virelda Bloodbeak

A Raven Sister who began as a Sister of Heme, broke with the
Rust Church after reading forbidden historical documents, and
now hunts Teramalda's iron armor as a symbolic blow against the
Church. She is impatient and violent by Raven Sister standards.
She travels in the guise of a flock of ravens; her white hair
cannot be cut because it bleeds.

**Public Legend:**
> *The Ailander Raven Sisters are known as a peaceful
> sisterhood. They discreetly help the villagers with the art
> of healing and spells for the harvest, but are hunted
> relentlessly by the servants of the Rust Church. Sister
> Virelda Bloodbeak is not like other Raven Sisters. With her
> bushy white hair she is supposed to have started out as one
> of the goddess Heme's Sisters, but is said to have changed
> sides after an epiphany. She is said to have slain tens of
> the Iron Guard and their demons, and some even claim that she
> has faced Teramalda's burning armor in battle.*

### Zertorme

Zygofer's son — a half-elf sorcerer who leads the Aslene rider
clans at Amber's Peak. He broke with his father, allied with
the Elvenspring, and gave the Galdane refugees land in exchange
for military service. He has no face: he sacrificed it to the
fire demon Brinhelda for her fire magic. He wears a mask in
public. The Quards believe him to be the prophet of Horn.

**Public Legend:**
> *Zertorme is said to be the sorcerer Zygofer's very own son.
> When the father caught Zertorme snooping about in his demonic
> grimoires he is supposed to have torn the face off his son,
> as beautiful as he was skilled in magic, as punishment.
> Zertorme then fled east to ally with his father's enemies.
> From the plains, he is said to lead his armies with a masked
> visage and to have gathered the horse clans from Aslene, who
> believe him to be the prophet of the volcano god Horn and the
> future savior of Aslene, under his banner.*

### Arvia of Crombe

A dwarven princess outside her clan's lineage — promised to the
god Huge at birth, she leads a religiously fanatical group of
Crombe dwarves and Galdane riders with one stated goal: retake
the fortress of Vond, kill Krasylla, and kill Zytera. Scarred,
jet-black braided hair, fierce.

**Public Legend:**
> *The dwarven princess Arvia stands outside the kinship of the
> clan, for it is told that she was born after her childless
> mother fornicated with a stone statue and thereafter promised
> all of her offspring to the god Huge. They say that Huge
> marked Arvia for feats of war and sacrifice for his own glory
> at her birth. Dwarves fear and are terrified of the woman,
> but respect her all the same as one chosen by the gods.*

### Empress Soria of Urhur

Wife to Emperor Hroka, the real power behind the orc empire
building at Eye of the Rose. She leads the **Viraga** — the
female orc coalition that makes the actual political decisions.
Intelligent, precise, privately haunted by a ruby that whispers
to her husband in his sleep.

**Public Legend:**
> *It is whispered among the Isir and the drifters that the orc
> empress Soria is actually a learned human female who has
> nestled herself in with the Urhur clan in the fortress of Eye
> of the Rose to undermine their war-like manhood. With fair
> words and elegant seduction she has ensnared Emperor Hroka
> completely, whose warriors have even been made to eat leavened
> bread! The emperor is said to have given his spouse an
> invaluable cloak clasp in which is set a living elven ruby.*

### Kalman Rodenfell

An ancient elf — one of the commanders of the Redrunners —
who has kept his interest in the living world while most of his
kind have retreated to the Stillmist. Minimalist appearance,
features barely suggested. Spends time in Dankwood but conducts
field missions when the need is serious. His name means "He who
fell from the red one."

**Public Legend:**
> *Almost all of the ancient and learned elves are said to live
> deep in the northern forests of the Forbidden Lands or even
> to have retreated into the mysterious Stillmist as rubies, but
> Kalman "The Aurochs" Rodenfell is supposed to have kept his
> interest for the living world to this point. As one of the
> commanders of the Redrunners, he is said to lead the fight
> against demons, humans and other intruders in the land. They
> say that his feet are rooted like trees and that he sleeps
> standing in the blood of his enemies so as to draw nourishment
> and knowledge from them.*

### Rust Prince Kartorda

The religious leader of the Rust Brothers. Obese, sixties,
ochre-draped. He has a second face on the back of his head —
placed there to mimic his master Zytera — that has suffered a
blood clot and drools. He is feared across the Forbidden Lands.
He divides his time between Haggler's House and Vond. General
Manderel is his military confidante.

**Public Legend:**
> *The leader of the Rust Church is said to live in Vond or
> Haggler's House, and is feared across the Forbidden Lands.
> His two faces, which are said to see all and know all, mirror
> the dual nature of the prophet Zytera. Some whisper that the
> Rust Prince is the old god Guard incarnate — the all-seeing
> guardian of all change, of all that comes and goes, the guide
> for travel between sleep and waking, between life and death.*

## Adventure Sites — Non-Spoiler Reference

The campaign unfolds across eight named sites plus the Vond
complex. Sites can be visited in any order. Each entry gives:
location, what players publicly know, and the legend that can
be shared with players.

### Grindbone (Village — Slaver Settlement)

A village of slavers and thieves, operating since the Alder Wars
when prisoners of war flooded the region. Ruled jointly by
the **Slaver's Guild (Bone Ferrets)** and the **Thieves' Guild**.
A mill on the river grinds continuously day and night; corpses
are bound to its wheel. Double palisade surrounds the village,
stakes pointing both inward and outward. Orcs and humans both
accepted here. A theater, tavern, and fighting arena serve the
wealthy.

Captain Kratullos commands the garrison — the nearest thing to
neutral authority the village has.

**Public Legend:**
> *Hunters whisper of Grindbone, the village of slavers and
> thieves that lies beyond the forest. Many have heard the cry
> of the village's mill from the river but few have dared to
> approach. It is said that the millstones grind the souls of
> the enslaved to dust since slaves have no use for spirituality.
> Some come to Grindbone through compulsion, others in
> exultation. There, those captured by the cruel arrive. From
> there, those sold by the greedy leave.*

### Ravenhole (Ogre Quarry)

An abandoned dwarven marble quarry — all the blue marble was
mined out, and the dwarves left. Ogres moved in. Every full
moon an enormous din rises from the stone cauldron as the ogres
hold wild feasts. The sound causes animal stampedes for miles.
Murble Tribbletwine, a dwarven diplomat, has established
cautious relations with the ogre leader Murge.

**Public Legend:**
> *It is said that the dwarves mined all the blue marble to be
> found in the Ravenhole quarry and then left the site, at
> which time the ogres moved in. One thing is certain: every
> full moon, a din rises from the immense stone cauldron, a
> noise that causes animals to stampede for miles around. No
> one in their right mind approaches Ravenhole while the ogres
> hold their wild feasts.*

### Amber's Peak (Fortress — Rider Camp)

A ruined fortress on the plains beside the Raukwaye Bridge,
inhabited by Zertorme and his fire experiments. Two Aslene
rider clans camp around it: the **Quards** (who arrived recently
and treat Zertorme as a prophet of Horn) and the **Galdanes**
(older allies, more skeptical). The fortress interior contains
unnatural colored flames and locked halls. Zertorme's wyvern
mount, **Pyravia**, guards the grounds.

**Public Legend:**
> *It is whispered that the half-elf Zertorme was one of the
> first generals of the monster Zygofer, but that he was
> disgusted by the villain's tyrannical rule and joined the
> Elvenspring of the eastern Forbidden Lands to fight his
> former master. A melancholy has fallen over Zertorme ever
> since the Blood Mist lifted. He is said to have retreated to
> the old ruined fortress of Amber's Peak, where magical flames
> burn ever stronger. People say that the sorcerous Zertorme
> is trying to tame fire itself in order to turn it against
> Zytera, but many villagers are terrified as the number of
> fires around Amber's Peak have increased.*

### Eye of the Rose (Elven Fortress — Orcish Empire Capital)

An ancient elven fortress on a cliff, above the ruins of an
old elven city with a park and chapel. Now the court of **Emperor
Hroka the First and the Greatest** of the Urhur orc clan.
Hroka is attempting to build an actual orcish civilization:
craftsmen, a library being salvaged, trade. His wife Soria and
the **Viraga** female coalition hold the real political power.
The fortress is guarded and holds several hundred orcs.

The elf woman **Klotinda** — an Ent-shapeshifter ten meters
tall — circles the area outside, believing the orcs have stolen
and imprisoned her beloved.

**Public Legend:**
> *The orcs of the Forbidden Lands have sought their place in
> the world ever since they were sent to war against the humans
> and were then abandoned by the elves and dwarves. Word is now
> spreading that an orc leader is holding court in the abandoned
> elven fortress of the Eye of the Rose. He calls himself
> Emperor Hroka the First and the Greatest. Merchants who've
> visited the place whisper that the emperor has bought a
> priceless ruby that he intends to bestow upon his spouse so
> as to prove his worthiness as emperor.*

### Pelagia (Druid Sea Temple)

A druid sanctuary of **Elvenspring** on a coastal plain, marked
by four tall monolithic cliffs. The druids claim the winds of
the land are born between the four monoliths. They operate a
fishing harbor, a healing house, an inn, and a pottery. Access
to the sacred chamber of the winds requires knowledge of the
**Maha language** — the Elvenspring cipher-tongue. A sealed
stone chest in the eastern cliff contains what the druids call
the Gift of the Sea. The druids themselves cannot open the chest.

Several dwarven clans have sent representatives, each with their
own claim on what is inside.

**Public Legend:**
> *The humans are said to have first tread the soil of
> Ravenland at Pelagia. According to the druids, here the winds
> of the land are born between the four holy monoliths to be
> loaded with rain so that the clouds can carry water to the
> fields and forests. In these days, expectations are high at
> the temple of Pelagia. The temple is supposed to have been
> granted another gift, an occurrence that, according to
> legend, has only happened four times before, a gift that only
> the worthy can claim.*

### Stonegarden (Canide Dwarven Fortress)

The most sacred dwarven site in the Forbidden Lands — most
dwarves make a pilgrimage here. The fortress of the Canide
clan (the **Iron Hounds**), ruled by King Karonax and Queen
Sulma, sits on sheer vertical walls. Below it: the **Catacombs**,
the **Hand-lift of Ancestors**, and the deep grottoes where
the pale **Dwelvers** (ancient sub-surface dwarves) still dwell.
The **Belderran** royal children have arrived to discuss the
succession of the dying King Turik. Beneath the deepest levels,
something is imprisoned.

**Public Legend:**
> *It is said that the moon, stunned by the beauty of
> Stonegarden, will stay a while in its course above it. At
> some time in their lives, most dwarves go on a pilgrimage
> there as the site is considered by all except the Crombe
> dwarves to be the dwarves' place of origin in the world. This
> year's Veneration of the Earth in the impregnable fortress of
> the Iron Hounds looks to be the most extraordinary event in
> years. It is said that the royal children of Belderan have
> come to privately discuss their father's succession. Their
> mute ancestors have risen from the depths, for below
> Stonegarden lie the forbidden grottoes leading to the elder
> layers of the world.*

### Stoneloom Mines (Abandoned Meromannian Mines)

A mountainside mine complex abandoned after the Third Alder
Wars. The stench of corpses greets the approach. Inside: spiral
stairs fifty meters down, rusted chains, a pumping station, a
sealed iron gate, phosphorescent algae in deep chambers, and
brass bells throughout the halls that attract whatever hunts
inside. No one who has entered in recent memory has returned.

**Public Legend:**
> *Some say the Meromannian dwarves abandoned the Stoneloom
> Mines after the Third Alder Wars, but the truth is that they
> were eaten by something that moved into their home. Rumors
> whisper that the mad wizard Zygofer cursed his family as
> they fled his ever more frightening presence. Tainted by a
> demonic infection, his daughter, Marga, sought shelter among
> the dwarves in the Stoneloom Mines, where she turned into a
> monster and consumed her hosts. None have dared enter the
> caves since.*

### Haggler's House (Greatest Temple of Heme)

The greatest temple of the goddess Heme in the Forbidden Lands,
situated in the west. Annual harvest feasts are not optional
— villagers come to have their effigies blessed by the priests.
Those who do not come face crop failure, disease, famine. This
year the **300th anniversary of Zytera's birth** is celebrated
with a particularly lavish sacrifice. It is rumored that Zytera
will appear in person. The **Iron Guard** secures the perimeter.
The **Whispering Council** of Rust Brothers has long been
active here.

**Public Legend:**
> *One does not like to mention Haggler's House, the goddess
> Heme's greatest temple in the Forbidden Lands, for many have
> lost their loved ones in its domain. Yet people go on
> pilgrimage at the yearly harvest feast to honor the bloody
> mother, for those whose idols have not been blessed by the
> priests can expect failed crops, disease and famine in the
> coming year. Survival in the harsh west is paid with
> sacrifice: crops, silver, young blood and the bodies of dead
> kin. This particular year, the 300th anniversary of the birth
> of Zytera and the Rust Brothers, is celebrated with an even
> more lavish sacrifice. It is rumored that the messenger of
> the gods will visit the temple in person and speak to the
> people about the country's future.*

### Vond — City Ruins and Fortress Complex

The ancient Crombe dwarven fortress at the base of **Mount Ora**,
now controlled by Krasylla and the Iron Guard. The surrounding
city was razed during the Alder Wars. The fortress sits among
extensive ruins crawling with Iron Guards, misgrown creatures,
and demon-tainted beasts. The Crombe dwarves demolished the
underground passages when they fled, so the structure is
compromised. At the peak of Mount Ora, connected by a path up
the mountain: **Merigall's Lair** and then **Zytera's Mogarium**
at the summit, where lightning strikes constantly between
towers.

**Public Legend:**
> *The fortress of Vond was once the Crombe dwarves' strongest
> fortress in Ravenland. The largest part of the fortifications
> is supposed to have been situated below ground, but was razed
> by the dwarves when they were driven away. It is rumored that
> it is the Butcher of Aslene — the demon prince, Krasylla —
> that now rules over the fortress that guards the pass beneath
> the shimmering metal slopes of Mount Ora. The ruins of the
> city of Vond surround the area. The ruins, as well as the
> fortress, are most likely crawling with Zytera's troops, along
> with the fanatical worshippers of Rust and demonic creatures,
> but few who have been brought here have ever returned to
> testify. The peak of Mount Ora is crowned by Zytera's living
> palace, where even demons dare not venture. Lightning strikes
> incessantly between the tower steeples and the dark clouds
> that circle the area.*

### The True Elven History of Stanengist

The six first elves are real: Gemelda, Neyd, Nebulos, Iridne,
Viridia, and Algared. They each shaped an aspect of the world
before retreating into their rubies. Key truths not in the
legends:

- **Viridia** fought the giant Scrome, was eaten, and her ruby
  was transformed by his evil eye from red to green. She is
  now the emerald **Gall-Eye** in the sword Maligarn — angry,
  bloodthirsty, no longer the shieldmaiden she was.
- **Algared** is the elf who became the first king of Alderland
  in disguise — the entire Algarian dynasty (kings Algamar,
  Alvagard, Algarod) are his half-elven descendants. His ruby
  is in the scepter Nekhaka.
- **Iridne** chose to become the cloak clasp Blood Star not to
  die for love (the public legend) but to help orcs by advising
  their leaders through dreams. The orc lover "Horena" in the
  legend is a fabrication. The elf Klotinda (now an Ent)
  still believes orcs killed Iridne and wants violent
  reclamation.
- The **Shardmaiden** (a seventh elf, the most powerful)
  shattered herself to distribute fragments to her
  half-elf descendants — the Elvenspring who carry shard
  in their foreheads are the **Order of Maidens** and have
  extraordinary magical powers.
- **Nebulos** built the **Stillmist** — the elven sanctuary
  beyond the world. He may have done it to escape the dwarves
  he found crude and clumsy.

### The True Nature of Zytera

Zytera = Zygofer (ancient Frailer sorcerer, half-elf) +
Therania (his daughter) + a demon prince, all merged on the
back of a giant spider by Merigall's design.

**Zygofer** opened the demon portal in the Fourth Alder Wars
to defeat King Algarod — he caused the Demon Flood himself.
He is the source of the problem he now claims to be solving.

**Therania** tolerates her father only because she still needs
his knowledge of mog (demonic substance). She plans to sever
him and steal a woman's lower body to become whole.

**Zygofer** plans to sever his daughter once he no longer needs
her life force. He wants to wear Stanengist alone.

If Zytera dies without the Protonexus being sealed, the portal
runs amok — uncontrolled demonic invasion follows.

**Weakness:** If the completed Stanengist is placed on Zytera
(or any demon, including Merigall and Krasylla), it drives them
insane proportional to the number of stones beyond the first
three. This is known only to Merigall, Kalman Rodenfell, and
the elves of the crown — not to Zytera, Krasylla, or Kartorda.

**Weakness:** Zygofer's wife **Martea** (in the Stoneloom
Mines) has a lock of Zygofer's hair. If thrown into the
Protonexus, demons will swarm from Churmog to destroy Zygofer
for disturbing them.

**Weakness:** Zytera can also be split with the sword **Asina**
— a single hit of 4+ damage severs the two halves.

### Merigall's True Agenda

Merigall is not Zytera's loyal ally. It is bound to Zytera only
because Merigall's life essence was stored in a statue in
Zytera's Mogarium when Zygofer reshaped Merigall from the
residue dissolved in Lake Harga. Zygofer holds Merigall's
existence hostage.

Merigall's real goal: retrieve its life essence from the statue
in the guardian chamber, which would free it from Zytera's
control permanently.

Merigall's second goal: resurrect its beloved **Viridia** from
the emerald Gall-Eye — this requires finding a suitable body
of an elven woman to raise her in.

Merigall secretly helped Zygofer's wife Martea and second
daughter Marga escape from Zygofer. If Zygofer discovers this,
he will try to destroy Merigall.

Merigall has already silenced the **Whispering Council** at
Haggler's House — it does not want the Blood Mist restored.

Merigall can teleport to any of its twelve offspring across the
Forbidden Lands. If its body is destroyed, it regenerates
beside one of them. If all twelve offspring die, Merigall is
permanently killed.

### Krasylla's True Agenda

Krasylla wants to:
1. Get hold of the contract binding him to Zytera (hidden in
   Zygofer's decaying cloak).
2. Kill Zytera once free of the contract.
3. Collect Stanengist to seal the Protonexus — to prevent
   competing demon princes from entering the Forbidden Lands.
4. Replace Zytera as the dominant power, with Krasylla ruling
   as the mightiest creature of the Forbidden Lands.

Krasylla is in the process of transforming into **sarmog** —
a more powerful demon form. Slaying him in the precursor phase
only accelerates the transformation. As sarmog, he is a
ten-meter humanoid wearing the stones of his own chamber as
armor.

Krasylla can only be killed with an **Arrow of the Fire Wyrm**.
The fire wyrm Erinya is the natural enemy of those who destroyed
Aslene — once marked by the arrow, Erinya hunts Krasylla and
devours him in a spectacular eruption of lava.

### Zertorme's Double Game

Zertorme's public presentation: heroic half-elf opposing his
demonic father, leading the Aslene to freedom.

**Actual plan:** He is playing all sides. He is secretly allied
with his sister Therania, intending to betray her as well once
he seizes power. He wants Stanengist for himself to rule the
Forbidden Lands. He has collaborated with both Zytera's enemies
and Zytera's allies.

If Zertorme wins, he creates a human-dominated kingdom
(Ailanders and Aslenes) with the Raven Sisters as religion.
He eliminates the Rust Church and all demons. Eventually he
invades Alderland.

**Vulnerability:** He is demon-tainted from his deal with
Brinhelda. If he dons Stanengist, he goes insane like his
father.

**Note:** His face is literally on Brinhelda's face — she wears
it as a living mask. She considers it hers. Recovering it would
enrage her.

### The Protonexus

Zytera has constructed a primitive portal between worlds at the
peak of Mount Ora — the Protonexus. It is not yet fully
functional but is growing.

**If Zytera dies without it being sealed:** The portal runs
amok and becomes an uncontrolled nexus. Demons of all kinds
swarm Vond and spread across the Forbidden Lands. The
surrounding nature destabilizes.

**How to seal it:**
- Throw Stanengist (with at least four stones set) into the
  portal. The crown is destroyed. The portal closes.
- Alternative: Zygofer himself can seal it — but has no
  incentive unless forced.

**How to destabilize it:**
- Zygofer can turn the Protonexus into an open nexus by
  sacrificing a being of royal blood. He may do this if he
  believes all is lost.

### The Stoneloom Mines — True Situation

**Marga** (Zygofer's youngest daughter, demonically infected):
Her avatar form is an 11-year-old girl. Her actual body is an
immense gray-green shapeless mass filling the deepest tunnels.
She can form tentacles, create smaller creatures from her body,
and appear anywhere in the mines. She thinks of herself as
playful; she kills as reflexively as breathing.

**Martea** (Zygofer's wife, fused with an Abyss Worm): She
is fused grotesquely with the worm's body, preserving the
faces of her devoured victims on its exterior. She still
believes she is a good mother. She carries a lock of Zygofer's
hair. Her instincts override her will when she smells blood.
She may carry the sword **Maligarn** (if the GM places it
here).

**Useful items in the mines:**
- Zygofer's hair lock (on Martea) — thrown into the Protonexus,
  summons demons to destroy Zygofer.
- The mine map (held by the trapped dwarven warrior Milva).
- The sword Maligarn, if placed here by the GM.

### The Scarne Truth

The dragon **Scarne** is imprisoned 1,500 years beneath
Stonegarden under an elastic metallic net. The public legend
says Oramund the dwarven commander defeated her with
Scarnesbane. This is false — she was imprisoned, not slain.
She is highly intelligent, multilingual, and furious.

She wants:
1. The hammer Scarnesbane thrown into the ocean or destroyed.
2. The Crombe stronghold Dragon's Tooth obliterated.
3. Freedom.

If freed, she will devastate the Crombe homeland, then settle
in the northern mountains to breed a new tribe of dragons.

### The Iridne Truth

The public legend says Iridne died of suicide rather than be
separated from her orc lover Horena. This is completely false.
Iridne chose to become the Blood Star cloak clasp to help orcs
from within, advising their leaders in dreams. There was no
lover. There was no father sending ships.

The elf **Klotinda**, Iridne's actual partner, does not know
this truth. She has become an Ent and is hunting the orcs she
believes kidnapped Iridne. She will attack violently if she
believes the adventurers are complicit with the orcs.

If Iridne speaks to Klotinda through the clasp, the confrontation
can be resolved. Iridne will ask Klotinda to support Empress
Soria's orcish march on Vond instead.

### The Four Artifacts — Full Details

#### Stanengist Crown

- **Elven rubies:** Gemelda, Neyd, Nebulos (all three already
  set), plus three empty sockets (for Gall-Eye, Algared, Iridne).
- **Suggested location:** Vale of the Dead (see Gamemaster's
  Guide).
- **Personality:** Reflection, tranquility, creativity,
  superiority.
- **Power:** As rubies are added, the crown gains powers from
  each stone plus the demonic insanity effect (see below).
- **Gemelda/Neyd/Nebulos trio:** Auto-DISPEL MAGIC (Power D6)
  on every spell within NEAR. Affects all magic including
  friendly casters.
- **DEMONIC INSANITY:** If any demon or demon-tainted creature
  dons Stanengist, they take D6 Wits damage per stone beyond
  the base three. Zero Wits = permanent insanity, then death
  or self-destruction. This is the key to defeating Zytera.
- **Sealing the Protonexus:** Four or more stones + thrown into
  the portal = portal sealed permanently. Crown destroyed.

#### Maligarn — the Giant Slayer

- **Elven stone:** Viridia's emerald, Gall-Eye.
- **Suggested location:** Stoneloom Mines.
- **Personality:** Rage, cruelty, impatience, greed.
- **Power:** +3 Weapon Bonus, Weapon Damage 2. D8 Artifact Die
  on all attack rolls (not Parry). Scales to D10 with 2 enemies
  adjacent, D12 with 4+. Detects enemies at NEAR range (green
  glow proportional to proximity and hostility).
- **Drawback:** –2 on all PARRY or DODGE rolls. If wielder
  Breaks an enemy, roll INSIGHT or go berserk. Resource dice
  for FOOD and WATER must always be rolled twice.

#### Nekhaka — the Royal Scepter

- **Elven stone:** Algared's ruby.
- **Suggested location:** Weatherstone (see Gamemaster's Guide).
- **Personality:** Sorrow, dejection, fear of failure.
- **Power:** D12 Artifact Die on MANIPULATION and INSIGHT. Once
  per Quarter Day: EARTHQUAKE spell (Power D6+2) by thrusting
  the scepter into the ground. D12 Artifact Die applies to all
  stronghold upkeep and construction rolls while the wielder
  is present.
- **Drawback:** Wielder suffers 1 Agility and 1 Wits damage
  every Quarter Day awake.

#### Blood Star (Hemella) — the Cloak Clasp

- **Elven stone:** Iridne's ruby.
- **Suggested location:** Eye of the Rose (held by Empress Soria).
- **Personality:** Soul-searching, caring, self-sacrificing.
- **Power:** D12 Artifact Die on SCOUTING, LORE, and PERFORMANCE.
  Wearer can levitate (same ground speed, one round) with cloak
  attached. Stronghold Defense Rating +1 while wearer is inside.
- **Drawback:** Each time the wearer Breaks an enemy in combat,
  lose 1 Empathy.

### Outcome Possibilities

**Zytera defeated and Protonexus sealed:**
The Forbidden Lands are free of its grip. Who fills the power
vacuum depends on the allies the adventurers gathered.

**Zytera defeated, Protonexus unsealed:**
Demonic invasion. The surrounding nature destabilizes. The
adventurers may have won and created a worse crisis.

**Zygofer and Therania severed from Zytera:**
Zytera dies. Both halves die within hours without re-attachment
through magic. If Zygofer alone survives, he reassembles and
continues his work. If Therania alone survives, she steals a
body and continues her own path.

**Scarne freed:**
She destroys Dragon's Tooth (Crombe stronghold), settles in
the northern mountains, breeds a new dragon tribe. Mark
Dragon's Tooth with a crater on the map.

**Blood Mist restored:**
If the Whispering Council's work is carried forward, the
Blood Mist returns in select areas. The adventurers may use
it as a defensive perimeter around their stronghold. Rust
Brothers and Wolfkin are pleased.
