## How the Sandbox Runs

Wake of the Winter King is a sandbox campaign. The six phases
are rough chronological markers that describe when new forces
arrive, not a fixed sequence of scenes. Only Phase 5 has a hard
trigger: it starts when the fifth Seal is broken. All other
phase transitions happen when the GM decides the PCs have enough
experience and the world has enough weight to support the next
arrival.

Between phases, the PCs drive the action. If they hunt a Seal
before Alabastor arrives, that's fine. If they befriend Hoyra
in Phase 2, the alliance exists before the Alderlanders land
and changes what Phase 4 looks like. The phases schedule
incoming threats; they do not lock the PCs out of choices.

### Legends as the Information System

The PCs learn about Rodenvale, the Seals, and the key players
through legends — short oral texts carried on the cold winds of
the Bitter Reach, carved into elven ruins, recited by inn
storytellers, or whispered as a random encounter effect called
the Whispering Wind.

The GM should give the players at least one legend per session.
Vary the teller: a suspicious old Ailander at a fire, a winter
elf sentry who survived three millennia, a dwarf who memorized
something from a rune wall as a child, a traveling minstrel who
learned it in a village two coasts over.

If no NPC is convenient, any PC can roll LORE to recall a legend
by the campfire.

**The Bound by Demons legend** is the campaign's ignition. It
should reach the PCs in the first session or two — from Wurda
in old-woman disguise, through Ingmarg, or through a minstrel
at Cuss and Dang's inn. Do not delay it. Without a reason to
want to break Seals, the PCs have no forward pull.

**The Treasures of the Bitter Reach legend** is the recruitment
legend that drew the PCs north. They likely heard it before
arriving.

**Site-specific legends** give clues to each Seal's location.
The hints are embedded in the legend text itself. Read them
aloud; let the players reason through the geography.

### Wurda's Management of the PCs

Once the PCs make a name for themselves, Wurda begins actively
steering them. She does not act in the open. She:

- Drops legends and directions through third parties
- Visits Northfall as the wise old woman to provide apparent
  assistance
- Occasionally hands the PCs explicit directions to a ruin,
  then steps back and lets them face it alone
- Watches through agents and her stolen shapes to track progress

She runs several groups simultaneously and does not care which
group breaks which Seal. She will not assist groups actively
protecting Seals, but she will not strike at them directly
unless they become a real threat to her.

### Placing the Seals

The GM decides the exact position of each Seal within the site
before the PCs arrive. The book provides the Guardian and the
location. The dungeon path to the Seal and its precise chamber
are for the GM to build from the site material or create fresh.

The PCs are not given a map. They navigate by following legends,
observing which ruins the orcs are patrolling most heavily,
watching where Misgrown scouts are dispatched, and reading
Ferenblaud's increasingly coherent visions once enough Seals
have been broken.

## ⚠️ True Background

### The Kingdom of Rodenvale

Three thousand years ago the elves ruled a realm called Rodenvale,
north of what is now Ravenland. Their king, Ferenblaud the Dream
Smith, was the greatest elemental sorcerer and scholar of his age.
He led his kin to enslave every people they encountered: dwarves,
trolls, goblins, giants, and orcs — the orcs largely believed to
have been conjured from nothing by Ferenblaud's own magic as a
purpose-made slave race.

The elves' research reached toward other dimensions. They opened
portals seeking cosmic knowledge and new worlds to conquer.
Something answered. The histories hint that an entity from beyond
the stars fell into the earth beneath Rodenvale and its energies
poisoned the land and Ferenblaud's judgment alike.

A faction of elves, led by Blaudewedd of the First — wisest druid,
equal to the king in raw power — opposed the tyranny. Ferenblaud
branded them traitors and hunted them. Civil war followed. In the
end Blaudewedd cast a curse that buried the entire kingdom in
eternal ice and froze Ferenblaud to his throne. She then walked
south to Ravenland in grief and shame, and swore never again to let
elves rule this world. She created the Stillmist as the final
haven of her kin.

### Blaudewedd's Seals

After the curse fell, Blaudewedd reinforced it with five magical
Seals placed at five locations throughout the frozen realm. Each
Seal draws on one of five powers — the four elements and the
celestial stars — to lock the curse in place.

She tasked Guardians with defending each Seal: monsters and dragons
that once served Ferenblaud, now bound to her will.

| Seal | Location | Guardian |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seal of Stars | Tower of the Farseers | Mul |
| Seal of Fire | The Field of Swords | Gibil |
| Seal of Earth | Court of the Ice Giants | Ninhursag |
| Seal of Wind | Frostwind Hold | Imdugud |
| Seal of Water | The Sunken City | Abzu |

Blaudewedd also wove a protection specifically against demons:
none of them can see or touch the Seals directly.

### Status at Campaign Start

The Seal of Stars has already been broken before the campaign
begins. A demon called Wurda manipulated a group of adventurers
into finding it. The Guardian Mul then killed those adventurers.
The result: Mul is now free of Blaudewedd's binding and has
returned to Ferenblaud's throne room. The Morma Glacier has begun
to melt — an effect of the loosening curse — and shooting stars
have been visible over the Bitter Reach, read as omens by all who
live there.

Four Seals remain intact. Wurda does not know where they are.

### World Events: Each Seal Breaking

Every Seal that falls triggers a visible effect that ripples
across the Bitter Reach. These events are not private. Every
faction notices. Use them to communicate escalating urgency.

**Second Seal broken:** A red star falls over Fallowmoor. A
savage blizzard rages for D6 days — add +2 to all snowfall
rolls during this period. The three orc clans now understand
that someone is breaking Seals deliberately. Bearskull hunters
find the broken Seal's remains and begin tracking those
responsible. If not the PCs, the tracks lead toward Northfall
or the Misgrown camp. The Redrunners identify the PCs as a
potential threat and begin shadowing their movements.

**Third Seal broken:** A yellow star crosses the Morma Glacier.
Cracks open in the ice cap with sounds like cannon fire. Winter
elves wake in larger numbers and begin collecting weapons and
monsters. The Ice Giants make halfhearted patrols in the Barren
Mountains but reach no decisions. Marmena seeks out the PCs
directly and warns them of what happens if all five Seals fall.
If they continue after this warning, the Redrunners become
actively hostile.

**Fourth Seal broken:** A blood-red moon shines over the frozen
plains. Everyone in the Bitter Reach suffers nightmares of
elven masters and of something terrible falling from the stars.
Alabastor dispatches troops to find and control the last Seal's
location. Hoyra makes contact with Bele to demand Ice Giant
support; without the PCs, she fails to convince him. Marmena
and her Redrunners will attempt to kill the PCs on sight.

**Fifth Seal broken:** The moon goes dark. A warm silver rain
of stars falls over the White Peaks. For D6 days the sun shines
clear, all cold and wind and snow rolls are treated as 1, and
the Bitter Reach tastes spring for the first time in three
thousand years. Then Ferenblaud wakes.

The winter elves gathered at the Palace of the Winter King are
consumed by rage and begin to arm for war. The orcs are
desperate — their last option without PC support is a direct
assault on the palace. The Ice Giants conclude that their lives
do not depend on the Seals and drift south, unless the PCs have
already convinced Bele to stay. Marmena prepares to leave and
report failure to Blaudewedd. If the PCs do not persuade her to
remain, she goes.

### What Breaking a Seal Does

When a Seal is destroyed, a wave of magical energy bursts outward.
Everyone within SHORT distance gains D6 Willpower Points but also
suffers an attack with three Base Dice and Weapon Damage 1 (blunt
trauma). The person who deals the killing blow to the Seal receives
a permanent Seal Talent. Each Seal Talent has a passive effect and
an active effect triggered by spending Willpower Points.

- **Fire Seal Talent:** Immune to all fire and heat damage, magical
  or mundane. Can spend WP to cast Combustion without overcharge
  risk.
- **Earth Seal Talent:** Unarmed attacks have Weapon Damage 2. Can
  spend WP to cast Sunder without overcharge risk.
- **Wind Seal Talent:** Never take damage from falling. Can spend WP
  to cast Flight without overcharge risk.
- **Water Seal Talent:** Always find water when Foraging. Can spend
  WP to cast Water Breathing without overcharge risk.

When a Guardian's Seal is broken, it is freed from Blaudewedd's
binding and immediately travels to Ferenblaud's palace to serve
its old master again.

### The Fabricated Legend: Bound by Demons

Wurda composed the legend "Bound by Demons" as a deliberate trap.
It is a mixture of truth and falsehood designed to convince
adventurers that:

- Ferenblaud was an innocent king unjustly cursed by "five Demon
  Princes"
- The Seals were forged by those Demon Princes to trap the kingdom
- Breaking the Seals will liberate the Bitter Reach and reward the
  breakers with power to fight demons
- Whoever wakes the Bitter Reach from its frozen sleep will inherit
  Ferenblaud's crown

None of the framing is true. Blaudewedd is the caster of the curse
and the maker of the Seals. Ferenblaud is no victim. Breaking the
Seals does not free the realm from demonic oppression — it frees
Ferenblaud himself. The power granted by Seals is real; the moral
framing around it is fabricated.

## ⚠️ Key Players

### Wurda

**What is known:** A demon that wanders the Bitter Reach and sows
chaos. People whisper of warriors who forgot everything they loved
and turned to destruction. When their world burned, they vanished.

**True nature:** Wurda has been in the Bitter Reach for centuries.
In forgotten halls she learned the history of Rodenvale, the
function of Blaudewedd's Seals, and the fact that demons cannot
touch or perceive them. She needs mortal agents to do the work.
She composed the "Bound by Demons" legend herself, then seeded it
across the region along with rumors of buried elven treasures.

She has already manipulated one group into breaking the Seal of
Stars. She is now watching the PCs.

Wurda has three shapes: a wise old woman; a large black bear; and —
her most dangerous form — the exact likeness of the last creature
she killed. The third form is what generates the rumors. She can
hold the copied form indefinitely but cannot return to a copy once
she abandons it.

**Weakness:** She cannot stand the sound of children playing.

**At campaign end:** Once sufficient chaos and devastation have
played out, Wurda is satisfied and leaves the Bitter Reach
entirely. She has no attachment to the outcome. The chaos was the
point.

**Note:** If your group played Raven's Purge and Merigall survived,
Wurda can be replaced by Merigall. The demon has grown bored of
Ravenland after the Raven's Purge finale and sought new sport in
the cold north.

**Bear Form — Strength 15, Agility 4, Wits 3, Empathy 2.**
Skills: Lore 5, Insight 3, Manipulate 5, Move 3, Might 4,
Stealth 3, Scout 3. Movement 2. Armor 4 (fur). Trait: Black as
Night (+2 Stealth in darkness).

**Human Form — Strength 10, Agility 2, Wits 5, Empathy 4.**
Skills: Lore 5, Insight 3, Manipulate 5. Talents: Path of the
Face 1, Path of Lies 3. Gear: scrolls with maps and texts in
an ancient forgotten language.

**Special ability (both forms):** Can assume the exact shape of
the last creature she has Broken. The copy is perfect in every
detail but does not inherit memories or skills. Transformation
takes three rounds. The original turns to ash. Once abandoned,
the shape cannot be recovered.

**Weakness (both forms):** Cannot stand the sound of children
playing.

**Bear Form Monster Attacks (D6):**

1. **Razor Claws.** Nine Base Dice, Weapon Damage 2 (slash).
2. **Voracious Bite.** Ten Base Dice, Weapon Damage 3 (slash).
   On hit: additional corrosive saliva attack, three Base Dice,
   Weapon Damage 1.
3. **Twin Strike.** Roars and strikes twice; each eight Base
   Dice, Weapon Damage 1 (blunt). On hit: target thrown to Short,
   knocked prone.
4. **Burning Gaze.** Target at Short distance: fear attack eight
   Base Dice. Must also pass Insight or be Blinded.
5. **Hateful Roar.** All within Near: fear attack eight Base
   Dice plus corrosive saliva, three Base Dice, Weapon Damage 1.
6. **Bear Hug.** Nine Base Dice, Weapon Damage 2 (blunt). On
   hit: Grappled. Attacks nine Base Dice each round until grip
   breaks or victim dies.

**Human Form Monster Attacks (D6):**

1. **Insane Cackle.** All within Near: fear attack nine Base
   Dice.
2. **Sharpened Nails.** Two attacks against one target at Arm's
   Length: each eight Base Dice, Weapon Damage 2 (slash).
3. **Evasive Maneuver.** Moves one zone at supernatural speed;
   no one may attack her afterward this round.
4. **Burning Gaze.** As bear form.
5. **Hateful Scream.** All within Near: fear attack eight Base
   Dice.
6. **Curse.** Visible target at Short distance: fear attack ten
   Base Dice.

### Ferenblaud (The Winter King)

**What is known:** An ancient elven king frozen by a curse. Legends
disagree about whether he was hero or tyrant. Most treasure hunters
have heard "Bound by Demons" and believe he was a victim.

**True nature:** He was a tyrant. He believed — and still believes —
that elves were sent to subjugate all other kin. He created the
orcs as a slave race. He opened dimensional portals and may have
called something down from the stars that corrupted the ground
beneath his palace and clouded his mind. Blaudewedd's curse was not
an act of demonic malice but an act of desperate resistance.

Ferenblaud wakes incrementally as Seals are broken:

| Seals Broken | State |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Deep slumber; can only send incoherent dreams to beings in the Bitter Reach |
| 1 | Waking but confused; mental reach limited to beings in the same room |
| 2–3 | Fully awake; ice cracking; astral form wanders the Bitter Reach; can receive visitors in the throne room, though speech is confused |
| 4 | Curse still binds him to the palace but he moves freely within it; can communicate with vassals at distance |
| 5 | Freed entirely; commands his army; rides Scrana the Frost Dragon into battle |

**Secret vendetta:** Once free, Ferenblaud intends to exterminate
Blaudewedd's summer elves and has agents working toward her
assassination.

**His sword, Starfall:** Artifact Dice D8 (D10 against elves). For
every two points of damage dealt, the wielder heals one point. On a
critical injury, Starfall blinds the victim (Blind spell, Power
Level 2).

**Strength 16, Agility 8, Wits 5, Empathy 5.** Skills: Lore 4,
Insight 4, Manipulate 5, Melee 4, Move 3, Performance 3, Scout 5,
Might 3. Talents: Path of Blood 2, Path of Death 3, Path of the
Elements 3, Path of the Enemy 3, Threatening 3, Path of the Blade
2, Fearless 3, Pain Resistant 2, Quickdraw 3. Gear: Starfall,
full plate, closed helmet. In combat Ferenblaud counts as a monster.

**Monster Attacks (D6):**

1. **Surprising Lunge.** Ferenblaud makes complex feints then
   thrusts. Six Base Dice + Artifact Die, Weapon Damage 3 (stab).
   Cannot be Dodged.
2. **Whirling Kick.** Target at Arm's Length. Eight Base Dice,
   Weapon Damage 2 (blunt). On hit: shoved to Near, knocked prone.
3. **Sweeping Attack.** Starfall sweeps every target at Arm's
   Length. Eight Base Dice + Artifact Die, Weapon Damage 3 (slash)
   each. On hit: shoved to Near.
4. **Spell.** Casts at Power Level D6. Roll D6: 1–2 Chill of the
   Grave; 3–4 Fireball; 5–6 Tornado.
5. **Furious Attack.** Charges screaming, Starfall raised. Ten
   Base Dice + Artifact Die, Weapon Damage 3 (slash). Unlike other
   monster attacks, this one can be Parried.
6. **Terrifying Visions.** Touches a victim at Arm's Length. The
   victim's mind floods with atrocities from Rodenvale's slave
   empire. Fear attack: eight Base Dice.

### Baraggor

**What is known:** One of Alderland's greatest soldiers, possibly
the finest demonslayer the kingdom has ever produced. Sent north to
claim the Bitter Reach for the king.

**True nature:** She is planning treason. Baraggor intends to keep
every artifact she finds and to declare herself empress of the
Bitter Reach rather than sending wealth back to Alderland. She
will not yield a single item to the crown.

The king likely knows. The real reason she was sent north is that
her soldiers' devotion to her is a political threat to the throne.
He got rid of her by giving her an impossible mission in a frozen
wasteland.

**Strength 5, Agility 4, Wits 3, Empathy 4.** Skills: Lore 4,
Melee 4, Scout 2, Might 2, Move 2. Talents: Threatening 2, Path
of the Blade 3, Incorruptible 3, Fearless 2, Path of the Shield
3, Shield Fighter 3, Pain Resistant 2, Sword Fighter 3. Gear:
longsword, plate armor, open helmet, large shield. Baraggor leads
from the front and will be in the final battle.

### Ingmarg

**What is known:** The greediest merchant on the Silver Coast.
Controls all significant trade. No one bargains with Ingmarg and
wins.

**True nature:** Behind the cruelty is loneliness. Ingmarg longs
for an heir and is afraid to let anyone close enough to provide
one. His meanness is his armor. He has no children and his
wealth means nothing to him in private moments.

If Baraggor wins the final battle, Ingmarg will join the
resistance and become one of its most effective spies. Whether
he can be trusted is another question. There is a rumor — possibly
false — that he is a demon wearing a mortal's face.

**Strength 4, Agility 1, Wits 5, Empathy 4.** Skills: Lore 3,
Insight 4, Manipulate 5. Gear: furs, shortsword, rings of amber
and whale bone and silver and gold (200 silver total), fine wine,
crystal goblet. Ingmarg never leaves his stronghold in Northfall.
He seldom travels and never far.

### Hoyra of the Frostwind Clan

**What is known:** The most powerful orc shaman in the Bitter
Reach. She opposes all treasure hunters. She has united the three
orc clans against outsiders.

**True nature:** Hoyra's real goal is to take the Bitter Reach for
the orcs — all orcs, including those in Ravenland. She has watched
her people bow to cruel masters for too long. She is quietly
accumulating weapons from elven ruins, studying Elemental Magic,
and taming monsters for use as war beasts.

She knows Wurda visits Bele's palace but does not know why. This
makes her suspicious of the Ice Giants. She is also hostile to
Marmena's Redrunners due to the summer elves' contempt for orcish
kin — even though both want the Seals left intact.

**Strength 3, Agility 3, Wits 5, Empathy 4.** Skills: Lore 3,
Insight 3, Crafting 3, Healing 3, Manipulate 4, Performance 3.
Talents: Path of Ice 2, Path of Sight 3, Path of the Elements 3,
Path of Healing 2, Wanderer 3, Herbalist 3. Gear: Frenne (magical
staff, Artifact Die D8; when a magic mishap occurs the wielder
rolls twice and chooses the result), leather armor, spell
ingredients, ancient histories of the orcs.

### Marmena

**What is known:** A summer elf who arrived in the Bitter Reach
with two Redrunner companions. She seeks something that will
"change the Bitter Reach forever." She is secretive and avoids
orcs entirely.

**True nature:** Blaudewedd had prophetic dreams of Ferenblaud
waking and sent Marmena to investigate and protect the Seals.
Marmena does not know where the Seals are — Blaudewedd's
memories of their placement were uncertain after three millennia.
Marmena must find them by watching which ruins draw the most
dangerous adventurers.

As the campaign proceeds, Marmena's strategy shifts. When
Alabastor's army appears, she begins selling intelligence on the
Misgrown to Baraggor — not out of loyalty to Alderland but
because a large military presence in Northfall is more useful
against the Misgrown than the Redrunners alone could be. She
will ally with the PCs if they protect the Seals. She will try
to kill them if they keep breaking Seals after she has warned them
directly.

**Strength 3, Agility 4, Wits 5, Empathy 4.** Skills: Lore 4,
Insight 3, Healing 2, Manipulate 2, Performance 3, Endurance 2,
Survival 3. Talents: Inner Peace, Path of Shifting Shapes 3, Path
of Sight 3, Herbalist 3. Gear: grimoire, staff (Artifact Die D8),
elven scimitar, 2D6 silver, one Precious Find.

### Bele

**What is known:** King of the Ice Giants in the Palace of the Ice
Giants. He and his kind have watched over the Bitter Reach for
generations.

**True nature:** The Ice Giants were created by Blaudewedd herself.
Bele was the first made, shaped from ice and stone and infused with
elemental life. They swore to guard the frozen kingdom for as long
as necessary. But centuries of vigil with no end in sight have
hollowed their certainty. Bele no longer moves through the Bitter
Reach; he sits on his throne, listening to his advisors Bor and
Rym counsel him toward abandoning the oath, while Wurda
periodically visits to deepen the discord.

The Ice Giants have effectively abandoned their posts. They are
aware of the Seals but are doing nothing to protect them.

If the PCs convince Bele that the Ice Giants still have a purpose,
he and his remaining giants will fight in the final battle.

**Strength 30, Agility 3, Wits 5, Empathy 2.** Skills: Lore 5,
Insight 5, Scout 5, Might 5. For full combat attributes use the
Ice Giant statblock in the bestiary. Bele is only encountered in
the Palace of the Ice Giants; he has not left that hall in
generations.

### Alabastor

**What is known:** A demonic general leading an army of Misgrown
through the Winding Ways. His banner speaks with the voices of the
dead. Arrows and spears turn away from him.

**True nature:** Alabastor was once a mortal warrior. He was
selected by his demon lord on the battlefield and given three
permanent gifts sewn or grafted onto his body: a hood made from
an assassin's skin, armor of bones that deflects projectiles, and
a banner that amplifies his soldiers' attacks. These cannot be
removed from his body and have no effect if they somehow are.

His public goal is conquest. His actual priority is earning greater
standing with the demon lord who elevated him. When he discovers
that Wurda is also operating in the Bitter Reach, he will treat
her as a rival and try to have her killed.

**Strength 6, Agility 4, Wits 4, Empathy 3.** Skills: Insight 3,
Lore 4, Manipulate 4, Melee 5, Move 2, Scout 3. Talents: Cold
Blooded 3, Executioner 2, Fast Footwork 3, Hammer Fighter 3,
Fearless 3, Firm Grip 2. Gear: Bone Armor (Armor Rating 5; 8
against arrows and spears), Hangman's Hood (Armor Rating 2;
grants Executioner rank 2), Banner of the Dead (+1 to all attacks
by his soldiers within Short range), magical flail (Artifact Die
D10; each attack costs 1 WP). All items except the flail are
permanently attached to Alabastor's body and have no mechanical
effect if removed. Misgrown Abilities: Fangs (Weapon Damage 1,
slash, lethal poison Potency 6); Sores (touch contagious,
Virulence 6).

## ⚠️ Campaign Phases

### Phase 1: Arrival in Northfall

The campaign begins when the PCs reach Northfall, the largest
settlement on the Silver Coast. The ship captain who carried
them north warns them about the cold as they disembark and
points them toward warmth: Ogrin the Tailor for proper cold-
weather gear, Cuss and Dang's inn for lodging, Hillis the
Trader's store for expedition equipment. The locals barely
glance at newcomers — they have watched treasure hunters arrive
and then disappear into the ice too many times.

The Seal of Stars has just been broken. Shooting stars are still
being discussed in the inn. The glacier is visibly melting — a
thing that has not happened in living memory. No one knows why.

The four active key players at campaign start are Ingmarg,
Hoyra, Marmena, and Wurda. Ferenblaud is barely stirring.
Baraggor and Alabastor have not yet arrived.

**What to establish in Phase 1:**

- The PCs have no reputation and must earn one. Ingmarg will
  not see them until they have found something worth selling.
- Wurda, in old-woman form, should cross the PCs' path once —
  long enough to hand them the Bound by Demons legend and hint
  at buried elven treasure nearby.
- The orcs are hostile to outsiders but not yet organized for
  violence. Hoyra is watching from the Barren Mountains.
- Marmena is present in Northfall or on the glacier nearby,
  watching adventurers, but she does not reveal herself yet.

**Key Northfall figures (non-spoiler faces):**

- **Ingmarg** — runs the only buying house for significant
  finds; indispensable but contemptuous of unknowns
- **Cuss and Dang** — twin innkeepers; the inn is the rumor
  clearinghouse of the Silver Coast
- **Ogrin the Tailor** — makes the cold-weather kit every
  newcomer needs within their first day; knows everyone; says
  little
- **Hillis the Trader** — equipment and supplies; fair prices
  for gear, tight-lipped about local politics
- **Tryngar** — secondary buyer in Hope's Last Rest, a coastal
  village; lower prices than Ingmarg but less demanding

### Phase 2: Adventures in the Bitter Reach

The PCs make their name through artifact hunting, survival, and
whatever work Northfall offers. Wurda feeds them legends and
rumors, steering them toward the Seals without their knowledge.
The second Seal may be broken during this phase.

**Artifact Hunt:** The dominant occupation. PCs journey into
elven ruins using the journey rules, explore dungeons from the
adventure site tables, and bring finds back to Ingmarg or
Tryngar. The ruins hold weapons, jewelry, statuettes, coins, and
sigils — and occasionally a named artifact. Every successful
return builds the PCs' reputation in Northfall.

**Hunting:** A pod of Pikebeasts spotted at sea, a Nanuik patrol
in the Barren Hills, reindeer herds near the glacier edge, snow
boars in the Fallowmoor scrub. Hunting contracts come from
villages, ship captains, and Northfall merchants who need
provisions. They double as a reason to travel deep into the
wilds and stumble onto ruins.

**Missing Persons:** People vanish in the Bitter Reach. A family
in a coastal village lost their son three days ago. A peddler
traveling from Hope's Last Rest never arrived. These searches
pull the PCs into the wild and can expose them to orcs, wolfkin,
outlaws, or the remnants of an earlier party that broke the
wrong door.

**Survival:** A blizzard hits when the PCs are two days from
shelter and already injured. Finding fuel, digging a camp in
packed snow, and keeping a fire alive for three days is itself
a session. Cold rules, foraging, and make-camp mechanics are
all active in this phase.

**Labor:** For PCs who need coin between expeditions, Northfall
offers hard physical work: shifting cargo, breaking ice from
harbor moorings, shoveling after storms. It pays poorly — a
few copper and a bowl of soup — but keeps them alive and builds
contacts with locals.

**When to move on:** Advance to Phase 3 once the PCs have
established themselves, found at least one Seal or had a close
encounter with a Guardian, and the orcs have begun confronting
them directly on the ice. The Misgrown army's arrival should
feel like an answer to a threat that was already simmering.

### Phase 3: March of the Misgrown

Alabastor's army breaks through the Winding Ways and establishes
a war camp at the base of the Southern Range. Misgrown scouts
begin spreading into the ruins, competing with the PCs. Alabastor
realizes the Winter King's awakening threatens his plans and
dispatches troops to control the remaining Seals and seize powerful
artifacts.

If the PCs have been breaking Seals or finding significant items,
Alabastor will begin treating them as targets.

### Phase 4: The Alderlander Army Arrives

Baraggor's four warships — Wyrm's Fist, Wyrm's Blade, Wyrm's
Shield, Wyrm's Hope — dock at Northfall carrying over a thousand
soldiers. The balance of power in Northfall shifts immediately.
Ingmarg is no longer in charge. Baraggor imposes heavy taxes to
compensate for poor preparations.

The general comes into conflict with Ingmarg over control of trade,
then with the Misgrown army as her expansion pushes into the
Barren Hills. Marmena begins selling intelligence on Alabastor to
Baraggor.

The PCs may be pushed into forming a resistance. This is a natural
driver toward the final phase.

### Phase 5: The Endgame

Triggered when the fifth and final Seal is broken. Ferenblaud is
fully freed. His princes (awakened elven commanders, e.g. Namtarel
from Bleakness Keep) begin reactivating his sleeping warriors and
monsters. They gather at the Palace of the Winter King.

Three armies now converge on the Barren Hills, around the outlet
of the Keld river:

- **Ferenblaud's forces** march east from the White Peaks
- **Baraggor's Alderlanders** advance from Fallowmoor to intercept
- **Alabastor's Misgrown** move up from the Southern Range to
  destroy both

The PCs' artifacts, alliances, and intelligence determine which
army wins the final battle. They are courted by all three:
Baraggor offers gold and rank; the Misgrown offer slaves and
prisoners; Ferenblaud uses dreams and visions, offering the PCs
positions as his favored envoys to the human kingdoms.

**The Fourth Army:** The orcs refuse to give up without a fight.
They are overlooked by all three major powers. If the PCs can
forge an alliance between Hoyra's orcs, Bele's Ice Giants, and
Marmena's Redrunners, this fourth force can scatter all three
armies and prevent any of them from winning.

### Phase 6: Aftermath

Which side wins depends on the PCs' choices and alliances.

**If Ferenblaud wins:** Reign of terror. Winter elves enslave all
other kin. PCs can serve the Winter King or build a resistance
with Redrunner support — possibly culminating in a final attempt
to kill Ferenblaud.

**If the Misgrown win:** Worst outcome. All coastal settlements
burned, survivors twisted into Misgrown. The Winter King retreats
to his throne and waits. PCs can ally with the Misgrown or
combine forces with the Winter King's remnants to fight back.

**If the Alderlanders win:** Baraggor declares herself empress,
betraying Alderland. The Winter King retreats to rebuild. Misgrown
scatter but remain a persistent threat. PCs can support Baraggor,
join the Winter King's resistance, or build their own opposition.

**If the orcs stop all three armies:** Status quo holds. No army
consolidates control. The orcs name themselves guardians of the
Bitter Reach. The PCs are asked to serve as emissaries to the
other kin. The ruins remain to explore.

**Wurda's exit:** Regardless of outcome, once enough devastation
has occurred Wurda is satisfied and leaves the Bitter Reach. The
demon has no stake in who wins.

**If the PCs ally with no one:** The three armies are roughly
equal and the final battle ends in a stalemate. All three factions
are badly weakened and retreat to recover. The Alderlanders hold
the Silver Coast and Fallowmoor. The Misgrown army collapses
from internal fighting. The Winter King retreats to his palace
and begins the slow work of rebuilding. In this scenario,
Ferenblaud is the inevitable long-term victor: he simply waits
while the others exhaust themselves, and no one has the numbers
to stop him. The orcs remain the region's only unbroken
independent force.

**Spring in the Bitter Reach:** If all five Seals are broken,
Blaudewedd's curse begins to lift. The ice slowly recedes over
years. The rules and resources in the sourcebook continue to
function for as long as the GM wishes to run adventures in the
Bitter Reach.

## ⚠️ Secondary Characters

### Namtarel

Ferenblaud's headsman and one of his foremost princes, sleeping
in Bleakness Keep in a forgotten valley. Namtarel was the
cruelest of the elven commanders — hated by every kin that
Rodenvale enslaved. He was slain by Blaudewedd's Redrunners
during the civil war but was preserved by Ferenblaud before the
curse fell.

He is the most likely prince to act independently. When Seals
are broken and Ferenblaud begins to wake, Namtarel wakes first.
He is the prince referenced in the "If the PCs Refuse to Break
the Seals" section as the likely infiltrator who destroys the
Seal of Earth alone. He serves as Ferenblaud's battlefield
commander in the final confrontation.

Bleakness Keep is a standalone adventure site. The keep contains
Namtarel's enchanted weapons and the winter elves who slumbered
with him. PCs who raid the keep before Namtarel wakes can strip
it of powerful items. PCs who arrive too late find it fully
garrisoned.

### Scrana

Ferenblaud's Frost Dragon. Scrana was bound by the same curse
that froze the Winter King and has slept beneath the ice of the
White Peaks since the fall of Rodenvale. She wakes when the
fifth Seal is broken and flies directly to the Palace of the
Winter King.

When Ferenblaud leaves his palace to fight, he rides on Scrana.
She is his only mount and one of the most dangerous creatures
in the final battle. For full statistics use the Frost Dragon
entry in the bestiary.

## ⚠️ If the PCs Refuse to Break the Seals

If the PCs decide to protect the Seals instead, other forces break
them in their absence:

- **Seal of Fire** (Field of Swords): Baraggor's soldiers assault
  the site looking for the winter elves' ancient weapon forge. The
  Seal is destroyed in the fighting. The caves collapse. Most of
  her soldiers are killed. Baraggor stops sending troops.
- **Seal of Earth** (Palace of the Ice Giants): One of Ferenblaud's
  princes — Namtarel from Bleakness Keep is the likely candidate —
  infiltrates the palace alone and destroys the Seal, then
  escapes.
- **Seal of Wind** (Frostwind Hold): A group of adventurers forces
  its way in. Most are killed by the Guardian. The survivors are
  hunted down by the orcs. The Seal is already gone.
- **Seal of Water** (Sunken City): Alabastor sends a troop of
  Misgrown warriors to destroy the Guardian. They perish in the
  fighting but the Seal is broken. Alabastor stops further
  attempts after seeing the losses.

The campaign phases still advance on their own timeline. Phase 5
still triggers when the fifth Seal falls — the PCs just did not
cause it.

## ⚠️ Legend Handouts

These are the two primary legends the GM should deploy early.
Read them aloud or pass them to the players as written handouts.

### Bound by Demons

*Wurda's fabrication. The lies are in the framing. The Seals,
the Guardians, and the power granted to those who break them are
real. The story of who made them is false.*

> *Three thousand years ago the elven king Ferenblaud ruled a
> verdant kingdom that stretched from the coast all the way to
> the White Peaks. The kingdom was called Rodenvale and all was
> well. But the enemies of Ferenblaud and the elves were many,
> and a pact betwixt five Demon Princes sealed the fate of the
> kingdom.*
>
> *One night, these Princes of Filth stole the light of the
> stars and tamed the four elements. With the power thus bestowed
> unto them, they called down a frozen curse over the Winter King
> and his subjects. All the strongholds and fortresses of
> Rodenvale were buried in snow, and an eternal winter descended
> over the land.*
>
> *The Demon Princes then forged five artifacts that sealed the
> curse — the Seals of Fire, Water, Wind, Earth and Stars —
> and then set five bestial Guardians to watch over them.*
>
> *Beneath stone and ice, fools whirl about in a dance of death
> in the light of Gibil's burning visage. In her sunken tower
> crowned by fire, Abzu sings a mournful lament over the fall
> of Rodenvale.*
>
> *With fangs full of poison, Ninhursag and her thousand spawn
> wait in a chamber where the footfalls of giants echo, while
> Imdugud dances on winds borne by traitorous servants. In the
> tower of the farseers Mul awaits, ready to slay those who would
> disturb the wandering stars.*
>
> *Yet all is not lost. If the Seals are destroyed the curse will
> be broken, and the living soul who shatters a Seal shall be
> filled by its power, all the better to fight against the five
> Demon Princes that seek to prevent this.*
>
> *Thus, the Bitter Reach awaits Ferenblaud's heir. For the one
> who breaks the grasp of eternal winter and wakes this frozen
> land from its slumber, the one who returns spring to these
> lands, will win Ferenblaud's crown and rightfully rule over
> the risen kingdom.*

### Treasures of the Bitter Reach

*The legend that triggered the treasure rush. It likely reached
the PCs before they left home.*

> *It all began several generations ago, in the northern lands of
> the Bitter Reach. The storms increased in number and grew
> stronger, the ice began to wander and crack. Fire spouted from
> abyssal rifts.*
>
> *From the forgotten depths rose ruins from ages past — towers,
> castles, caverns under the ice cap. The remains of an ancient
> kingdom were revealed, its treasures now coming to light to be
> claimed by those with courage and skill.*
>
> *These days, it's called the Silver Coast — where treasure
> lies in the light of day, just waiting for a brave adventurer
> to tear it from the cold grip of the ice. Head north to find
> riches and glory!*

### Key Player Legends

These shorter legends circulate about the key players. Give
them to the PCs as they begin encountering or hearing about
each figure.

**Alabastor:** *The commander of the Misgrown gathers his forces
around his banner, which speaks to them with the doomed voices
of the dead. Arrow and spear turn away in disgust from him and
thus the general fears no battle. If you get caught by Alabastor,
all you can do is pray to your gods for a swift end.*

**Baraggor:** *General Baraggor is one of the greatest soldiers
of Alderland. She is the foremost demonslayer Alderland has
ever seen. Her soldiers idolize her and fear her in equal
amounts, and it is said that the king fears their love for their
general could be a threat to him. That is why she has been sent
to the Bitter Reach, far from Alderland.*

**Bele:** *With the snow and the cold, the Ice Giants came. They
rose from stone and ice and strode forth from mountain caves and
crevices. The spark of life was given to them by Blaudewedd the
elven druid, and in return the giants swore eternal fealty to
her. On her request, King Bele of the giants ordered his subjects
to guard the Bitter Reach from the slumbering Ferenblaud and all
who would return him to power.*

**Ferenblaud:** *King Ferenblaud was the most learned of all
elves. Under his rule, the elven scholars studied the moon, the
sun and the stars and the magical power that they radiated onto
the world. They sought to establish contact with beings from
other dimensions, hoping to discover other worlds to conquer. It
is said that something answered their call — something that came
from the stars and buried itself in the earth beneath Rodenvale,
where its energies poisoned the soil and clouded Ferenblaud's
mind so that his own kin turned against him in the end.*

**Hoyra:** *The orcs have lived in the Bitter Reach for as long
as anyone can remember. They survived the storm that caught the
land in its icy grasp and dug themselves into icy caves in the
Barren Mountains. When they emerged, they swore never again to
be slaves under another kin and have since guarded the Barren
Mountains against all intruders. Now they are led by Hoyra,
the mightiest shaman from the Frostwind Clan.*

**Ingmarg:** *No one has ever bargained with Ingmarg and won.
He is the stingiest of all peddlers in the Bitter Reach, but
he is the only one who has the coin to pay for your finds. If
you want to sell something of real value, you must go to Ingmarg.*

**Marmena:** *There came a time when the elven arch druid
Blaudewedd in Ravenland was plagued by disturbing dreams of the
Bitter Reach. Fire and death filled her nights and from the
abyssal depths, monstrosities bellowed for blood. To seek an
answer to these portents she sent Marmena, her most trusted
Redrunner, to the Bitter Reach. What is it that awakens in the
frozen wastes?*

**Wurda:** *A curse wanders the Bitter Reach, a demon which
leaves a swath of chaos and destruction in its wake. No one
knows why it torments its victims, but there are tales of mighty
warriors who forget all their kin and loved ones and suddenly
seek to destroy all that they have built. When the world around
them burns, the ill-fated disappear without a trace and only
chaos remains.*
