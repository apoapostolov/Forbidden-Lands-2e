# Kin Deep Dive

This file extends `setting.md` with the
detailed kin material from the Gamemaster's Guide chapter on Kin.
The master peoples document keeps the physical-type anchors and
quick reference; this file gives the cultural depth — clan names,
governance, customs, statblock anchors, and named individuals.

For the three human peoples (Alderlander, Aslene, Ailander), see
the master document. The supplementary detail on each is below.

## Humans (the Forbidden Lands village mix)

A village of fifty to a hundred adults of able age, more in
fertile spots, fewer at the edges. The mix in any village is
typically: native **Ailanders** led (where present) by Raven
Sisters; **Quard** riders from Aslene who came before Shadowgate
Pass closed; **Alderlanders** behind the wall; stern **Rust
Brothers**; a few **Frailers** with limited magic; mixed bloodlines
of all of these.

Killing the elderly when they can no longer contribute is "viewed
as unavoidable but sad and is usually accepted by the victims as
a better alternative than being forced to venture out from the
village on their own."

### Alderlanders

The dominant stock north of the Iron Lock. Newcomers — criminals,
banished, missionaries — appear occasionally and are received with
open arms because they bring news. Heraldry: swords and **alder
branches**. The alder tree is sacred; festival decoration uses its
branches. Two emotional registers: melancholy for what was lost,
and defiance to forget what was and build new.

**Typical Alderlander:**

- STR 3, AGI 3, WIT 3, EMP 3
- Skills: Melee 2, Move 1, Insight 2
- Gear: Shortsword, short bow

**Subgroup — Ailanders:** First humans north into Ravenland,
fled religious persecution 700 years ago. Worship the Protector
as **Raven**. Led by **Raven Sisters**. Same physical appearance
as Alderlanders. Pacifist tendency, equality-minded. **Koracia**
is the rumored hidden village with a Raven shrine.

**Typical Ailander:**

- STR 3, AGI 3, WIT 3, EMP 3
- Skills: Melee 2, Move 2, Marksmanship 2, Healing 1
- Gear: Shortsword, short bow

**Subgroup — Frailers:** Half-elves of Alderland heritage,
descended from elven hostages. Long-lived, slender, often with
slightly pointed ears. Spellbinders. Greedy for power and
knowledge — the Frailer pathology. **Zygofer** is a Frailer.

**Typical Frailer:**

- STR 2, AGI 3, WIT 4, EMP 4
- Skills: Lore 2, Insight 2, Manipulation 2
- Gear: Dagger, quill and ink

**Subgroup — Reapenters:** see `gods.md`.

**The Silent:** Deaf-mute guards on the Iron Lock. Bang on metal
cylinders to warn, then shoot. Open ground below the wall is
"littered with the skeletons of those who heard the warnings and
ignored them."

**Typical Silent Guard:**

- STR 4, AGI 3, WIT 3, EMP 2
- Skills: Might 2, Melee 3, Insight 4
- Gear: Short spear, large shield, chainmail, great helm, heavy crossbow

### Aslenes

Colorful in dress and braid. Various skin tones with healthy
luster; vibrant hair regardless of color; both sexes braid
artfully. Earth-tone clothes patterned with color fields; gold
and bronze jewelry preferred over silver. Shorter and stouter
than Alderlanders. Worship **Horn**. Strong honor culture.

**Typical Aslene:**

- STR 3, AGI 4, WIT 3, EMP 2
- Skills: Melee 2, Marksmanship 2, Move 2, Animal Handling 2
- Talents: Horseback Fighter
- Gear: Short spear, short bow, leather armor, riding horse

**Subgroup — Quards:** Hierarchical riders. Came through Shadowgate
Pass 600 years ago, mixed with Ailanders, rose to power, built
fortresses. Skill in saddle and blade is highest cultural value.
Many flock to **Zertorme** at Amber's Peak, Horn's chosen savior.

**Subgroup — Galdanes:** Western Aslene clan. Their lands were
razed by Zygofer's demons in 875 AS. Survivors fled to Margelda;
**Zertorme** gave them land in exchange for defending it. Nomadic;
despise farmers and walls. Hated the Quards in the old country
and still do, in songs. Hate Alderlanders and anything demonic
including wolfkin. Drink too much. Sing sad songs. Dream of
returning to **Ashen Vale**.

## Elvenspring

Half-elves descended from the **hostages** the humans gave to the
elves at the Shift. Elf-trained in nature magic; initiated druids
in the **Golden Bough** handle most practical druidic chores.
Few but long-lived (three times human lifespan). Many fight Zytera
and the Rust Brothers in the Shardmaiden's name.

Despise Frailers as morally corrupt despite both being half-elves.
Find humans embarrassing, primitive, and bad-smelling, but feel
responsibility for them. Stoic, restless, ambitious. Taller and
thinner than humans; retain youthful appearance into old age.
Worship **Clay** and the **Shardmaiden**; venerate older land-gods
and the Raven god. Sacred substances: earth and clay. Ritual
items: clay figurines and Maha-script clay tablets.

The most militant order is the **Order of Maidens** — see
`gods.md`.

## Misgrown

Humans seduced and twisted by **Zytera** into demonic forms.
Greatest concentrations around **Alderstone**. Led in the field by
the **Wardens of the Rust Lord**. Many Rust Brothers are Misgrown.
Feared and hated.

## Elves

Long-lived. No formal hierarchy; status by age, skill, and
experience. Other kin divide them into **Stillelves** and **the
Unruly** by behavior. Elves divide other kin into "soft berries
that rot when autumn approaches" — they themselves are "lasting
stone fruit" because their flesh renews from the **ruby in their
chest**.

Blood relation does not matter to elves. They have no hierarchies
beyond respect for age and knowledge. They are not religious; they
do not deny gods but treat them as honorable teachers.

### Elven Rubies

Origin disputed: dwarves say the rubies were forged by Huge;
elves say they fell as seeds from the **Red Wanderer**. Bodies
renew constantly; elves are always young, beautiful, and capable
of choosing their shape and color over months. If flesh is
destroyed but ruby remains, the elf can be recreated by old
masters in the **Stillmist** at the temple **Rosenstaad** —
which no one can find on their own. If ruby is cut from flesh,
the body shrivels in hours. If the ruby is shattered, the elf
is lost forever.

Young elves start with a shard of ruby that grows like a pearl
in an oyster. Wealthy humans wear elven rubies as jewelry — the
elf inside often appears in the wearer's dreams. **Zytera** breaks
rubies into shards for his demonic experiments. The **Rust
Brothers** sacrifice rubies on anvil-altars.

Elven proverb: _"Three things mortal hands cannot mend: broken
birds' eggs, the heart ruby, and ravaged oaths between kin."_
(Dwarven stonesingers love mending broken eggs to spite the elves.)

### Stillelves

Long-lived elves who have lost interest in the world. Some lie
under an oak for years observing seasons; some develop esoteric
art projects; some fuse their ruby with a tree and become an Ent;
some leave their bodies and have their rubies mounted in temples
in the Stillmist. They chose not to resist when humans invaded —
sent their orc servants to the dwarves and withdrew. The orcs
have not forgiven this betrayal.

Named: **Blaudewedd of the First**, said to remain to guide
younger elves from her dwelling in the Stillmist.

**Typical Stillelf:**

- STR 3, AGI 3, WIT 5, EMP 2
- Skills: Melee 2, Crafting 4, Lore 5, Insight 4
- Talents: Path of Shifting Shapes 2
- Gear: Wooden staff

### The Unruly

Active elves. Lust for vengeance, experience, defense of life.
Carry secret disdain for the Stillelves' lethargy. Believe they
have a duty to commit great deeds during their long lives. Player
characters most often come from this group.

**Typical Unruly Elf:**

- STR 3, AGI 4, WIT 3, EMP 2
- Skills: Melee 2, Endurance 2, Stealth 3, Marksmanship 2, Scouting 2, Survival 2
- Gear: Longbow, short spear, knife

### Ents

Stillelves who have fused their ruby with an oak or ash and become
walking trees. Revered by elves and Elvenspring. Sought for advice;
gifts left in their glades. Shepherds of the great forests.
Aggressive ents are extremely dangerous.

The death rite of an Ent is a procession of five walking trees
carrying a sixth toward **the Stillmist**, where the dead Ent's
ruby heart is mounted as memorial in **Rosenstaad**. They walk in
silence; if disturbed they say **"Be gone, cursed meat bags!"**

### Melders

Active elves traveling as scientists and artists — finding the
perfect pine cone, cataloguing beetles, planting flower-landscapes
as art. Adorn themselves with organic patterns made by rearranging
the dark sap under their skin. Patterns work as forest camouflage.
First discovered by **Veliman the Painter**, said still to roam.
Generous and curious; love to talk about their special interest.

**Typical Melder:**

- STR 3, AGI 4, WIT 4, EMP 3
- Skills: Melee 2, Crafting 4, Stealth 5, Lore 4, Survival 3
- Talents: Path of Sight 2
- Gear: Knife

**Watchers** are a Melder sub-group who roam to gather impressions
and curios for the Stillelves. They appear as travelling minstrels.

### Redrunners

Militant elves who stayed to defend the Forbidden Lands.
Mobile groups of archers and swordsmen. Named for the Red Wanderer
star. Carry flowers, dried starfish, or red-star jewelry. Angry
and ashamed of having lost the land. Hate orcs (for the betrayal
narrative — Redrunners cannot grasp that the orcs feel the same
way) and Zytera's undead and the Rust Brothers most of all.

Aristocratic disdain. Will sometimes take human or half-elf
lovers _to experience the bittersweetness_ of watching them die.
They write songs about it. Hunt and recover lost elven rubies.
Function as the elves' intelligence service.

**Commander: Kalman Rodenfell** — one of the oldest active
Redrunner commanders, based in **Dankwood**. His name translates
as "He who fell from the red one." Ancient, worn, still committed
to physical action when most of his age-contemporaries have become
Stillelves. Leads intelligence operations across the Forbidden
Lands, maintains contacts in every major faction. Has a settled
romantic attachment to the bardic demon Merigall — a relationship
both consider impractical and neither has stopped.

**Typical Redrunner:**

- STR 4, AGI 5, WIT 3, EMP 3
- Skills: Endurance 3, Melee 4, Stealth 2, Move 3, Marksmanship 4, Insight 3, Survival 2
- Talents: Path of the Enemy 2
- Gear: Longbow, broadsword, dagger, leather armor

## Dwarves

Created by **Huge** to fill the void of space with matter.
Believe the world is a **sphere they are building** out from a
core. The sun and stars are **Hearths and Outer Forges**. They
build relentlessly underground; raise stone to the surface;
expand. Believe in reincarnation as Huge's tools. They view the
zone between the bones of the ground and the void of space as
unchecked growth tended by elves — useful but lazy.

Dwarves can forge stone in enormous underground workshops and
carry it to the surface. The "useless" massive ruins across the
Forbidden Lands are dwarven foundations for the next layer of
the world. They cultivate **troll excrement** to extract rare
minerals. Distrust open spaces; pale; hard on eyes and skin in
sunlight. Quarrelsome, meritocratic, honor competence.

### The Belderranians — Clan of the Sons

Belderand is the dwarves' first homeland, ruled by the Belder
royal family. Only dwarves enter; a few elves have reached upper
halls. **King Turik** is old, careful, criticized for letting
humans and orcs take Ravenland. Four heirs:

- **Rigalda Beak of Belder**
- **Berwyld** the stone singer
- **Gandeman Stone of Belder**
- **Trondeval** the dreamer prince

Belderand is the main temple of stone singers — sorcerer-priests
who shape rock with rune-song.

**Typical Belderranian:**

- STR 4, AGI 2, WIT 3, EMP 3
- Skills: Might 3, Melee 3, Crafting 2
- Gear: Battleaxe, large shield, chainmail, closed helmet

### The Meromannians — Mining Clan

**Wailer's Hold** fell 300 years ago to demons and Alderlanders.
Survivors split: some at Belderand, some at Stonegarden, some
hardy diehards still in the mountains. War-like, vengeful,
bitter. Openly racist toward humans and orcs. Drink and drug
themselves on narcotic mushrooms. Sing old songs of Wailer's Hold.
**Tormund Halfhand** is gathering an army to retake the city.

**Typical Meromannian:**

- STR 4, AGI 3, WIT 3, EMP 2
- Skills: Might 2, Melee 2, Insight 2, Marksmanship 2
- Gear: Warhammer, crossbow, studded leather armor

### The Canides — Iron Dog Clans

Surface-adapted dwarves: darker skin and hair. Took responsibility
for the dwarves' surface duties. Retreated to **Stonegarden** when
humans invaded. Rule: **King Karonax** and Queen **Sulma "Iron
Bitch"**, both great warriors. Black armor; heavy axes; armored
**war hounds**. Some Canide nobles in Stonegarden are secretly
contacting the **Urhur orc clan** for an anti-human alliance.

**Typical Canide:**

- STR 5, AGI 3, WIT 2, EMP 2
- Skills: Might 4, Endurance 2, Melee 3, Scouting 2, Survival 2
- Gear: Two-handed axe, plate armor, closed helmet

### The Crombes — Guardian Clan

Hold **Scarnehall** on **Dragon's Tooth**. Withdrew from the wars
after Vond fell. Believe themselves above other dwarves; closer
to the elves; arrogant and complacent in others' view; eat fish.
**King Tademir Stonefriend** rules. **Totela's Head** — a giant
portrait carved by king **Totela Goldbeard** with the hammer
**Scarnesbane** — is on the slope above. Huge punished his vanity
with beard-rot and cast the hammer out into the world.

**Vond** was originally the Crombe clan's strongest fortress in
the Ravenlands, built to guard **Shadowgate Pass** against Aslene
incursion from the southwest. The dwarves demolished the
underground passages when they were driven out during the Alder
Wars. Zytera's forces have held it since the Demon Flood.
A faction of Crombe dwarves — led by the zealot warrior **Arvia**,
born outside the kinship line and marked by Huge for war — is
sworn to the single cause of retaking Vond, killing Krasylla, and
killing Zytera. Other Crombe regard Arvia's followers with a
mixture of respect and unease.

The legend that Scarne was killed is false; Scarne is imprisoned
beneath Stonegarden.

**Typical Crombe:**

- STR 3, AGI 3, WIT 4, EMP 3
- Skills: Melee 2, Crafting 3, Lore 2, Performance 3, Healing 2
- Gear: Mace, leather armor

### The Elders — Dwelvers

Pale, chalk-white dwarven-like creatures from the deep structures
beneath the world. Roughly halfling-sized. The ancient ancestors
of the dwarven kin — the original stone-crafting beings from
before the clans divided. Treated with respect by dwarves as
forefathers. Given mineral gifts; retreat underground with one
or two stones into impassable crevices.

Dwelvers live 1000 meters and more below the surface in the elder
layers under **Stonegarden**. Dwarven clans are no longer allowed
to tread those layers (doing so means becoming clanless). Dwelvers
can **partially or completely soften their skeletons** to press
through cracks as narrow as an inch in the rock. They cannot speak
at surface level — the air is too thin for their anatomy; they
speak freely at 200 meters depth and below. They cannot see the
sun or touch land above without harm. Suspected by dwarven scholars
of being able to control **iron dragons**.

Dwelvers are master Stone Singers; they can teach the
skeleton-softening technique to human Stone Singers who earn their
trust. As of 1165 AS, a faction among the dwelvers is secretly
debating whether to release the imprisoned dragon **Scarne**, whose
net prison is weakening after 1500 years.

## Ogres

Offspring of dwarves and humans — descendants of the human
hostages who stayed with the dwarves at the Shift. So large they
were forced from dwarven caves; live now on the rocky moors of
**Feulenmark**. Live in huts, caves, burrows. No society to speak
of; no kinship felt; no loyalty or law.

Love violent music; howl and pound objects in thunderstorms.
Wear roughly cut blackened-leather clothes with crude metal
baubles. Stretch ears and noses with metal weights. Brand
themselves with hot irons. **Large ears and nose are signs of
status.**

Cruel and unpredictable: rip a prisoner's arms off, free him,
wager beer on how far he can run before bleeding out. Or "adopt"
a captive and cut his tendons so he cannot leave.

**Typical Ogre:**

- STR 6, AGI 2, WIT 1, EMP 2
- Skills: Might 4, Endurance 2, Melee 2
- Gear: Large wooden club
- Magic Resilience: spell Power Level reduced by D6 against an ogre

Not all human-dwarf offspring become ogres — some stay among the
dwarves as blacksmiths and craftsmen.

## Orcs

According to dwarven and elven myth, **Clay** created the orcs as
servants for the elder kin. Many believe instead that there was
an original kin enslaved when the land was divided. Dwarves and
elves say the orcs are stunted, ugly, unintelligent because they
were created to work. The orcs were sent into battle against the
invading humans; held their own until the humans bound demons.
Then the elves and dwarves abandoned them.

### Orc Society

Of every eight newborns, one is female and seven are male, on
average. Half the male orcs die before maturity from internal
violence. **Females rule the clans** because the males have poor
judgement; this is uncontroversial within orc society and not
advertised outside it.

Rank is everything to males. Insults demand satisfaction. Scars
are beautiful. Defeat is not dishonorable, but **surrender or
fear permanently reduces the orc to slave status** — slaves often
geld themselves to make peace with it. Half of all male orcs are
slaves. Slaves do the dirtiest work and have no rights.

Sharp duels are rare and to the death; blunt duels are common
and follow rules.

Adult male orcs are gnarled, hairy, with **tusks that prevent
proper speech**. Slave orcs are smaller and meek. Female orcs are
human-sized, can pass for human women, have **golden or yellow
eyes** and **six to eight nipples** (the top pair largest). Anatomy
gives strongest offspring an advantage at birth in three-to-five
litters; weak ones are killed. Females never become slaves; they
are killed for severe transgression.

### Orc Language

Male orcs cannot pronounce labials because of their tusks.
**Never present yourself to orcs with a name they cannot
pronounce — it is an insult.** (To hear what they can say: hook
fingers in your lower lip and pull down.) Younger orcs could
speak human languages before their tusks come in but are beaten
for trying. Females do not have tusks and speak fluently. **Viraga**
females sometimes take names the males cannot pronounce to
emphasize their superiority.

Orcs have no written language. Some learned orcs use the human
alphabet phonetically. A few read elvish but rarely admit it.

### Orc Clans

Homeland is **Feulenmark** (the Clan Land), including **Arina
Forest**. Orcs roam everywhere.

#### The Urhur Clan

**Self-styled emperor Hroka the First and the Greatest** rules
from an abandoned elven cliff-fortress called the **Eye of the
Rose** (the Meromannian dwarves built the structure as a gift to
the elves; the elves abandoned it; the orcs claimed it after the
Blood Mist). The clan tries to civilize itself imperially —
adorns garishly, pretends interest in poetry, parodies
civilization. Hroka loves gold; doesn't know what to do with it.
Recently gave wife **Soria** the elven ruby **Iridne**. Court
includes humans and dwarves who play along; the trickster
**Hranga Gala** advises. The clan secretly negotiates with
Stonegarden Canides.

The name "Urhur" comes from "purple" (a word orcs cannot
pronounce). Symbol: purple hand. Face paint from **purple shells**
of the **Gargan Marshes**, traded from Saurians.

**Typical Urhur:**

- STR 4, AGI 3, WIT 3, EMP 2
- Skills: Might 2, Melee 2, Lore 1
- Gear: Scimitar, large shield, studded leather armor

#### The Roka Clan

Roka means "rock". Stoic, military, strict hierarchy. Originated
in armies that fought the Alderlanders. Hate magic and demons most
of all orcs. Symbol: monolith. Polished-stone jewelry. Mostly in
southern Arina Forest. Led by **General Archa**, the most respected
orc in the Forbidden Lands; old, scarred, worried about his sons.

**Typical Roka:**

- STR 5, AGI 2, WIT 2, EMP 2
- Skills: Might 2, Melee 2, Scouting 1, Survival 1
- Gear: Flail, studded leather armor

#### The Isir Clan

War-like, hates everyone, including most other orc clans. Roams
in large hunting groups through Arina Forest, Feulenmark, and
Heme Forest. **Isir warriors** undergo trials: catching live
vipers in the dark and eating them; plundering wasp nests and
eating larvae through the swarm. Survivors gain venom in their
saliva and resistance to poison. Some split their tongues
lengthwise.

Led by **Eldag the Ravager**, who dreams of taking Belderand
and is mapping its defenses.

**Typical Isir:**

- STR 4, AGI 3, WIT 3, EMP 2
- Skills: Might 2, Melee 3, Marksmanship 2, Survival 2
- Gear: Two-handed sword, studded leather armor, closed helmet
- Poison: hallucinogenic bite (Potency D3+2), Weapon Damage 1; +3 Endurance to resist poison

#### The Viraga

A coven-like group of female orcs from all clans. Barren females
seek them out. Function as the orcs' shadow government — a
unifying force across clan lines. Totem: **fox**; carry fox fur.
Wild forest rituals where living prey (often humans, dwarves, or
elves) is hunted, torn apart, and eaten after the Viraga ingest
holy plant extracts.

The organization is secret: most male orcs are unaware it exists.
**Empress Soria of the Urhur clan** leads the Viraga; Emperor
Hroka knows her only as his wife and court advisor, not as the
head of an inter-clan power structure. The Viraga's long-term
objective is to rally all orc clans under a single regime without
the males understanding that the females are orchestrating it.

Send especially human-passing females to infiltrate human
settlements as spies, kidnappers of useful craftsmen, and
collectors of knowledge. The **three Somaya sisters** in
**Grindbone** are Viraga agents disguised as human healer-witches;
they abduct craftsmen and learned individuals for the Urhur clan's
workshop-slavery program at the Eye of the Rose. Viraga with
human coloring are their most useful operatives. Pronounced victim
mentality; will die for the kin. Males call them "iraga" because
they cannot pronounce the V; the Viraga emphasize the V.

**Typical Viraga:**

- STR 3, AGI 4, WIT 4, EMP 3
- Skills: Melee 2, Stealth 2, Move 2, Marksmanship 2, Lore 2, Insight 3, Manipulation 2
- Talents: Path of the Killer 2
- Gear: Shortsword, dagger, four throwing knives, leather armor

#### Drifters

Banished or self-exiled orcs roaming in mixed bands. Tolerated by
clans (orcs hate oppression). Often plunder; sometimes hire on as
guards or soldiers. Many clan-members live as drifters for a
period and then return.

**Typical Drifter:**

- STR 4, AGI 3, WIT 2, EMP 2
- Skills: Might 1, Melee 2, Insight 2
- Gear: Scimitar, studded leather armor, studded leather cap

## Wolfkin

Mixed of human and wolf. Hate civilization; believe they have
returned to the original natural form humans abandoned. Suspected
by some to be **escaped experiments from Zygofer's older
laboratories** that survived in the Mist. Saying so makes them
furious.

Travelled the Blood Mist unharmed in the forests — they call it
**the breath of the Huntress**. Could not cross open plains under
the Mist. Result: many Wolfkin suffer **agoraphobia**. Unstable
appearance and temperament; range to madness. Wandering raging
ones are called **werewolves**.

Worship **Heme** as **the Huntress**. Ecstatic blood rituals tied
to seasons, breeding, hunting; drug themselves on narcotic
mushrooms; hunt and tear apart living prey including humans.
Surprising taste for **complex polyphonic music** — howl in
multiple harmonies over complicated drumming.

Despite shared interest in nature, elves and druids hate them as
half-demons. Aslene warriors who wear wolf pelts hate them too.
Outcast wolfkin work as trackers and mercenaries.

**Typical Wolfkin:**

- STR 4, AGI 4, WIT 2, EMP 2
- Skills: Might 1, Endurance 3, Melee 3, Scouting 3, Survival 2
- Talents: Path of the Forest 2
- Gear: Short spear, knife, leather armor

## Saurians

Live in **Gargan Marshes**, the **Blush** delta in the east, and
the **Elya River** between. Defend territory fiercely in Gargan;
less aggressive elsewhere. Dam waterways like beavers. Stay
underwater up to fifteen minutes. Train crocodiles as beasts of
burden; will eat them in famine.

Trade only at platform-stations along the southern Gargan rim:
they sell **fish, purple shells, frogs, bread root**; they buy
**knives, eel spears, spearheads**. Strict peace at trade
stations; everything alive in the marshes outside is prey. Not
interested in gold or silver.

Cannot chew. Tear food into pieces or let carcasses ferment to
falling-apart consistency, then rip pieces. Smell is bad and
breath worse. Build body fat for winter warmth; warm-blooded.
Worship some unknown creatures sighted from barges in the marsh.
Language: only vowels a, u, e, with eight g-like throat clicks
and six t-like smacking sounds. Long rambling harangues. Can be
learned to "make oneself understood, no more."

**Typical Saurian:**

- STR 4, AGI 3, WIT 2, EMP 2
- Skills: Endurance 1, Melee 2, Scouting 2, Survival 3
- Armor Rating 3 (scales)
- Gear: Trident

## Whiners

Small skittish humanoids hunted by orcs and humans for "sweet
meat" — their living flesh has healing and fattening properties.
Wounds covered with whiner flesh heal quickly. Orcs prize their
meat for health and potency. Gold inserted under their skin
**grows heavier in a day**.

Caught in traps and held in cramped cages to be "harvested"
slowly — painful and ultimately lethal. Whiners hate everyone
and attack travelers in the wild or in caves to torture or trade
back for captive brethren.

Live in **hollows** — sponge-like rock formations they make by
chewing stone and vomiting porous-rock saliva. Druids claim they
have ant-like colonies deep underground with an egg-laying queen.

**Typical Whiner:**

- STR 1, AGI 3, WIT 2, EMP 2
- Skills: Melee 2, Stealth 3, Move 3
- Gear: Short spear

## Halflings and Goblins

A single kin of two natures. Half of any litter is halfling, half
goblin; chance decides each. Born in **cradles** — hidden
birthing places on islands, in ruins, in glades, in ravines.
Males stand guard outside; mothers inside. Mothers keep children
of their own half-kin; goblin children go to a goblin mother,
halfling children to a halfling mother. Fathers are never told if
a newborn is theirs.

Origin myth: when the elves fell from the **Red Wanderer**, the
moon goddess **Eor** threw blue moonstones in jealousy. Dark
elflike beings grew from these — could not abide sunlight, claimed
the dark forests and mountains of eastern Ravenland. Tried to
trick the Raven god at the division and were split in punishment
into halflings and goblins; their moonstones were torn out and
taken to Aslene.

### Halflings

Small village farmers and gatherers. **Idyllic facade hiding shame
of the dark side of their kin.** Hidden burrows shelter old,
disfigured, and misfits. Drug use is rampant and well-hidden:
tobacco, alcohol, narcotic plants. Prefer to meet in taverns
rather than at home where flaws may be revealed.

Trade with all civilized kin but trust none. Some halflings,
restless, leave to become adventurers, thieves, assassins —
"born under the dark moon" or "when Eor slept," with implied
goblin parentage.

The **Penita** sect of moon-priests seeks the lost moonstones to
restore the original kin. The goblins oppose them.

**Typical Halfling:**

- STR 2, AGI 4, WIT 3, EMP 4
- Skills: Melee 1, Crafting 2, Stealth 2, Sleight of Hand 1, Insight 2, Manipulation 2
- Gear: Shortsword, sling, leather armor

### Goblins

Nomadic forest hunters and gatherers. Breed wolves to ride.
Cunning, adaptable, hired by orcs and humans as trackers,
couriers, hunters. Liberal view of ownership: take what they
need. Disliked by elves and druids for ignoring nature's rights;
view all intelligent creatures except halflings as prey. **Less
hypocritical than halflings**, willing to discuss the kinship,
sometimes protect halflings (the reverse rarely happens).

See in dark; sunlight hurts; prefer night and the moon (their
ally).

**Typical Goblin:**

- STR 2, AGI 4, WIT 4, EMP 2
- Skills: Melee 1, Move 1, Stealth 2, Marksmanship 1, Insight 2, Manipulation 2
- Gear: Shortsword or short spear, sling, D6 copper coins
- Nocturnal: see in dark, no penalty for darkness; sunlight uncomfortable

## Named Individuals: Raven's Purge Campaign

The following NPCs originate in the Raven's Purge source material
and are listed here for lore-level identification. Statblocks and
scenario mechanics are in `campaigns/ravens_purge.md`.

**Klotinda** — an elven woman who loved the first elf **Iridne**.
She helped Iridne re-assume flesh after Iridne's ruby was removed
by her father. When Iridne voluntarily became the Blood Star clasp
\u2014 an act Klotinda did not know was coming \u2014 Klotinda believed
the orcs had murdered Iridne and stolen her ruby. She has taken
Ent form and circles the **Eye of the Rose**, attacking any orc
she encounters. She carries perhaps ten humanoids in her branches
and functions as a significant combat force in any engagement
near that site. Her grief is the engine of a scenario: she is
not wrong that the ruby is in orc hands. She is wrong about
what it means.

**Merigall's offspring** \u2014 the demon Merigall has twelve children
scattered across all kin and regions of the Forbidden Lands.
All bear yellow eyes \u2014 the demon's tell. Each allows Merigall
to teleport to them instantly, without a roll and without
Willpower cost. If an offspring dies, that teleport point is
permanently lost. Merigall loves them without reservation and
is a mortal enemy to anyone who harms them. The children are
found among humans, dwarves, elves, half-elves, and orcs. Two
named examples: **Morelde** (blonde boy, ten years old) and
**Migalda** (dark-haired girl, twelve) living in Merigall's
pavilion at Mount Ora; **Hirena** (adult, serves as steward
in the fortress of Vond). Offspring used as bait can kindle
Merigall's hatred against a specific enemy.

**Bone Ferrets** \u2014 a named slaver company of roughly twenty
members headquartered in **Grindbone**. Led by **Misela Ferrumar**.
Operate as hunters, kidnappers, bodyguards, and assassins for
hire across the Forbidden Lands. Use wolfkin trackers from the
**Fangwoods** for prey that has fled into the forest. The Bone
Ferrets and the Grindbone Thieves' Guild operate jointly; the
settlement is effectively a two-faction criminal state.
