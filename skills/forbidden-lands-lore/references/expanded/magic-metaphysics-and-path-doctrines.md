# Magic, Suffering, and the Doctrines of the Paths

This reference describes the metaphysics taught, tested, and disputed by
magicians in Apostol Apostolov's expanded Forbidden Lands campaign. It begins
with laws that repeated practice makes difficult to deny, then records the
truths available to practitioners of each rank. It is not a catechism shared by
all magicians. A blood sorcerer, an oneiromancer, and a stone singer can each
give a coherent account of the same event and disagree about what, in the
deepest sense, happened.

The distinction is deliberate. **Observed law** names a result that can be
reproduced. **Doctrine** names the explanation a school places upon that result.
**High-path knowledge** names conclusions that experienced practitioners can
test but rarely prove outside their own discipline. Contradictory doctrines are
not invitations to arbitrary magic. They are partial models of one world,
accurate where they touch it and dangerous where they claim too much.

## The Common Laws of Magic

### Willpower Is Scarred Intention

Magic is fueled by Willpower, but ordinary desire is not Willpower. A wish may
be sincere and still possess no magical weight. Willpower is kindled where the
world and the self come into conflict: intention made fierce by struggle, pain,
suffering, and the burning away of hope. As the world consumes the body's
strength, it can leave the survivor with something else—the power to insist,
even then, *I still choose*.

When an adventurer pushes beyond fear, pain, exhaustion, doubt, or social
collapse, the world gives evidence that the intended act should stop. The body
weakens, judgment frays, confidence fails, or fellowship is wounded. If the
person nevertheless continues, two incompatible conditions are held within one
life: *I am being overcome* and *I still choose*. That unresolved interval is
Willpower. It is not pain itself. Unchosen suffering, helpless misery, and the
torment of another do not automatically empower a witness. The force lies in
surviving an imposed limit while retaining authorship of the next act.

This is no substance hidden in the blood or soul. Those who spend Willpower
often—magicians, seasoned adventurers, and others long acquainted with
hardship—feel it plainly enough: a warmth behind the breastbone, an eagerness in
weary limbs, a presence that remains when bodily strength is gone. It does not
restore what the world has taken. It gives intent somewhere to stand after
stamina has failed, provided the mind remains sound enough to know it is there.

This is why protected comfort produces poor magicians, why ordeal without
purpose often produces only injury, and why deliberate self-harm is an
unreliable substitute for danger. The caster must possess both sides of the
contradiction: a real loss and a continuing intention. Burn is the brutal
exception that proves the law. The caster deliberately offers the world a
fresh injury and holds the spell's intended conclusion against it. The caster
chooses how much to risk; the world chooses where the harm falls.

### The World Has Settled Shape

Every thing and relation has a degree of settlement. A door has the shape in
which it was made, a body the continuity by which it remains the same living
person, an oath the witnesses and conduct that give it standing, a forest the
long exchange among soil, rain, root, animal, and decay. Settlement is not a
single substance. It is the accumulated fact that the world has already been
one way and has learned to continue being so.

Magic does not defeat this continuity with raw energy. The caster presents a
contradiction strong enough that another state can be made to stand. A brief
flame, borrowed sight, or momentary command needs only a narrow claim. A new
mountain, an immortal body, or a gate between worlds must be established
against innumerable existing relations. Such work needs permanent witnesses:
lost attributes, surrendered purposes, places marked by events, elven rubies,
great sacrifices, artifacts, or the aid of beings whose claims upon reality
are already immense.

### The Five Parts of a Working

Every controlled spell contains five parts, whether its caster names them or
not.

1. **Claim** — the exact change the caster intends to make true.
2. **Address** — the layer of the world asked to answer: blood, symbol, stone,
   dream, death, element, and so forth.
3. **Anchor** — the material, memory, likeness, place, gesture, or relationship
   by which the claim finds this target rather than another.
4. **Boundary** — the target, range, duration, scale, exclusions, and conditions
   that tell the answer where it must stop.
5. **Settlement** — what the world is expected to accept when the working ends:
   restoration, reversion, residue, a lasting alteration, or a debt that must
   be carried elsewhere.

A novice thinks a spell is its words and motions. A master knows those are
instruments for holding the five parts together. Traditions differ because
they use different addresses and because each address settles change by its own
rules.

### Power, Overcharge, and Mishap

The Willpower spent on a spell determines the intended depth of its claim. Its
Power Level measures how much contradiction the working successfully makes the
world bear. Magic therefore does not simply fail. Once genuine Willpower has
been committed through a learned pattern, the minimum claim has already gained
standing.

The casting dice measure exposure, not accuracy. A sword means the addressed
layer answered with more force than the boundary required: overcharge. A skull
means it answered outside one or more boundaries: mishap. The mishap is not a
random punishment placed upon magic. It is the uncontrolled reciprocity of the
path. Healing restores what should have remained ended; Shapeshifting permits
the beast to retain a claim; Awareness lets an observer or false certainty look
back; Stone Song wakes weight, fracture, and buried attention. The worst
mishaps weaken the distinction between caster and address until the path keeps
the caster.

### Mastery, Safe Casting, and Chance Casting

Rank is not a reservoir of force. It is the number and subtlety of boundaries a
magician can maintain without conscious effort. Safe casting removes exposure
because superior understanding supplies constraints internally. Its limitation
is metaphysical, not merely cautious: by closing every uncertain relation, the
caster must accept a narrower answer.

Chance casting does the opposite. The caster attempts a pattern whose
boundaries they cannot fully state, or deliberately leaves one boundary porous.
The useful ribbon of such a casting is the first controlled benefit of that
porosity. A mishap is guaranteed because the addressed layer is also permitted
to answer on its own terms. Rank 6 spells lie beyond the complete mortal grammar
of every path. A rank 5 magician may attempt one, but only as chance casting:
the desired change and an uncontrolled answer enter the world together.

### Ingredients, Grimoires, and Rituals

An ingredient is not generic fuel. It is an anchor whose existing relations
give specificity to a claim. A claw addresses predation and animal form; blood
addresses living identity; a map addresses place as represented knowledge; ash
addresses matter after life and flame have withdrawn their former claims. An
ingredient is consumed when the spell spends the relation that made it useful.
Reusable instruments endure because they locate or measure the relation rather
than embodying it.

A grimoire is an externalized boundary. A magician cannot truthfully record a
spell merely by understanding its theory. The spell must first have been cast
normally, because the notation records the trace of a change the author has
personally brought to settlement. Reading that record lowers conceptual
difficulty, but cannot make rank 6 safe: no manuscript can contain the missing
settlement without ceasing to be a manuscript and becoming an artifact or
living spell.

A ritual distributes boundaries through time, repeated action, participants,
place, and prepared matter. It does not necessarily provide more force. It
gives the world time to recognize the proposed relation before the final claim
is made. A Power Word is the inverse: a narrow spell compressed into trained
breath and reflex, fast because most choices were surrendered when it was
learned.

### Residue, Corruption, and Epic Settlement

Temporary magic usually allows settled shape to return. It never returns
perfectly. Attention has been drawn, matter displaced, witnesses changed, and
the addressed layer taught that another state was possible. This remainder is
magical residue. Repeated workings make a place easier for similar changes and
less hospitable to incompatible ones. Great residue acquires behavior of its
own.

Corruption is a permanent working whose objection remains active. It results
when an unwilling life, an alien law, an unstable shortcut, or a deliberately
unsettled ingredient is made to support an enduring effect. The change works,
but part of its foundation continues to resist being part of this world. A
corrupted place therefore behaves less like a damaged object than a bad
argument that reality is forced to repeat.

Epic Magic replaces local settlement with historical settlement. Its
ingredients possess standing beyond their material mass: a life's governing
purpose, an attribute permanently surrendered, a place where a decisive event
occurred, a monster's heart, an elven ruby, a sacrifice, a great helper. Each
ingredient supplies one witness that cannot be dismissed when the ritual ends.
Epic workings leave scars because history cannot be altered without becoming a
new source of history.

## Common Spellcraft: The Grammar Between Paths

General Spells are not usually regarded as a separate faith. They are the
comparative grammar by which magicians alter other workings. Their practitioners
call the common object beneath all path doctrines the **working**: a bounded
claim held by Willpower until it settles.

### Rank 1 — The Working Can Be Touched

The student learns to detect active magic as a pressure between intended and
settled shape. They can strengthen a claim, leave a seal that reports
interference, pass a narrow magical resilience, and perform tiny changes too
weak to establish lasting settlement. The first hard truth is that spell rank
and Power Level describe different things: rank is structural complexity;
Power Level is how far the structure is driven.

At this rank, Sense Magic reveals presence and broad discipline because the
address colors the residue. It does not grant full explanation. The common
error is to treat the color as a source—as though Healing came from one place
and Symbolism another. Comparative teachers insist only that different paths
leave different stresses in the same world.

### Rank 2 — Boundaries Can Be Edited

The adept learns that duration, concealment, storage, copying, and dispelling
are not afterthoughts. They are boundaries susceptible to another working.
Copying does not reproduce a caster's will; it gives a witnessed pattern a new
claimant. Dispelling does not annihilate energy; it removes enough standing that
settled shape reasserts itself. Extending or holding a spell sustains the old
claim and inherits its unresolved consequences.

This rank teaches the practical law of provenance. A copied, obscured, or
extended spell still bears the address of its origin, and a magician who edits
it may inherit attention from whatever answered the first caster.

### Rank 3 — A Spell Has an Afterlife

Preparation, containment, transfer, and stabilization reveal that a working
can persist apart from the instant of casting. Residue may be unearthed because
the world remembers the pressure of the old claim. A stable magical zone is a
place trained to keep repeating one boundary; an unstable one is a place where
the address keeps renegotiating it.

The rank 3 magician stops calling mishaps accidents. They can identify which
part of a working escaped its boundary, though not always why. They also
understand that moving a spell transfers obligations as well as benefits. A
curse handed to another target is not cleansed; its settlement has been made
someone else's problem.

### Rank 4 — Workings Can Acquire Agency

Familiars, living spells, mass workings, and quickened rituals prove that a
pattern can hold choices not made at the moment of casting. What looks like
agency arises when a working is given a continuing address, a means of reading
conditions, and permission to renew its claim. Whether this is true life is a
matter of bitter dispute. Symbolists say it is syntax reacting to signs;
oneiromancers call it a small dream; blood sorcerers ask what living bond was
quietly used.

At this rank, the magician learns the ethics of delegated magic. A working that
acts later cannot ask its absent author what they meant. Every ambiguity becomes
an instruction, and every omitted exception becomes part of the world it makes.

### Rank 5 — A Working Can Become Law

Artifact making demonstrates that a spell can be furnished with enough
permanent witnesses to renew itself across generations. God Spell suggests that
divine intervention is not exempt from magical structure, although no agreement
exists on whether gods answer the claim or are themselves enduring workings of
immense scope. Transcendence and Anti-Magic disclose the outer problem: a magician
can alter the conditions under which magic is possible, not merely cast within
them.

The master knows that no common grammar is neutral. To suppress all magic is to
make a colossal magical claim about what the world may answer. To transcend a
path is to become part of its address. This is why rank 6 cannot be safely
written, taught, or cast: the caster is no longer wholly outside the statement
being made.

## Healing Magic: The Doctrine of Living Continuity

Healers teach that life is a continuing claim made by body, memory, spirit, and
relationship together. Healing does not manufacture health. It finds a viable
state to which the living subject still has a legitimate route and strengthens
that route until the body can occupy it.

### Rank 1 — The Body Remembers Wholeness

The novice learns to distinguish damage from ending. A wound, poison, sickness,
or disturbed spirit is not simply a quantity to remove; each interferes with a
different part of the body's effort to continue. Healing Hands strengthens the
body's most recent viable pattern. Nature's Cure helps it recognize what is
foreign. Preserve prevents a present condition from becoming history.

The target must still possess a living claim. This is why simple healing cannot
restore the dead and why a healer may close damage without repairing a critical
injury. The injury has already become part of how the body expects to continue.
The novice's dangerous mistake is to believe that the remembered pattern is
always the best one. Bodies remember scars, inherited weakness, and old pain as
faithfully as sound limbs.

### Rank 2 — Health Includes Boundaries of Self

The adept learns that disease, curse, possession, fear, demonic intrusion, and
undeath are disputes about what belongs within a life. Immunity closes entry.
Rinse and cleansing remove a foreign claim before it settles. Mend Self and
Mend Wounds restore relations the body can identify but no longer repair alone.
Banishing works because a demon, curse, or undead force lacks the same standing
in the living continuity as the person it occupies.

Healing can therefore harm. To purge an intruder is to tear away every living
relation by which it has attached itself. A long possession, a cherished curse,
or a graft may have become entangled with identity. A healer who promises that
all cleansing leaves the patient unchanged either lies or has not practiced
long enough.

### Rank 3 — Restoration Selects a Past

At rank 3, the healer learns that there is no single former state called
“whole.” Rejuvenation restores bodily years but may remove the experience,
skills, and talents acquired in them. Resurrection reaches across the veil only
while the dead person's relations remain sufficiently near and coherent; the
Empathy it costs is the shock of making the living self contain knowledge of
its own ending.

Calm, serenity, and rites of passage work upon social and spiritual continuity.
A person survives through names, obligations, grief, and recognition as well
as flesh. Weather and broader rites teach the same lesson at another scale: a
living community cannot be healed by isolating one body from the conditions
that keep injuring it.

### Rank 4 — Life Can Be Reauthored, Not Freely Replaced

Regeneration and deep trance permit the healer to rebuild structures for which
no intact material remains. The working does this by extrapolating from the
whole life: a new hand is not copied from an abstract human hand but inferred
from this person's body, habits, age, and continuing identity. Tranquility and
holy wards establish an environment in which hostile claims cannot easily gain
purchase.

The healer now understands reciprocal mishaps. Opening a route toward an
earlier viable state may also open routes for old diseases, dead tissue,
forgotten spirits, or hungry presences that once touched the patient. Healing
backlash restores the wrong thing because the magic was asked to return rather
than to invent.

### Rank 5 — Continuity Has Debts That Cannot Be Forgiven

Purge Magic, Restoration, and the greatest recoveries reveal the limit of the
path. A permanent attribute spent to establish an artifact or epic change
cannot simply be healed, because it now supports another settled history. An
equal permanent witness must be destroyed or surrendered before the old claim
can return. Restoration is accounting, not mercy.

The master can retrieve life from near-total interruption, but cannot honestly
promise the same person returns. Memory, spirit, body, name, and relationships
may disagree after death. Rank 6 restoration can make those claims meet only by
chance casting, when the healer accepts an uncontrolled claimant as part of the
return. This is the metaphysical source of the hungry passenger, the wrong
voice, and the restored body that remembers somewhere else.

## Shapeshifting: The Doctrine of the Many-Bodied Self

Shapeshifters teach that no living body is singular. It is a treaty among
ancestry, appetite, learned movement, habitat, and the innumerable forms life
could have taken. Animals are not costumes. They are stable solutions to needs
the shapeshifter also carries.

### Rank 1 — A Trait Is a Relationship

The novice does not change species. They borrow one animal relation: the cat's
distribution of weight, the hawk's attention to distance, the mammal's honest
ordering of scent and danger. An ingredient locates a real lineage and prevents
the caster from inventing an animal out of human expectation.

Animal speech teaches the first correction to anthropomorphism. The spell does
not give an animal a humanoid mind; it makes unlike senses mutually legible.
Animals do not lie because the working translates present perception, not
because beasts possess moral purity. Misreading territory, young, food, or
threat as human motive is the novice's common failure.

### Rank 2 — Instinct Can Act Through Flesh

Bear's force, deer's speed, dolphin's breath, and a winged descent are not
summoned essences. The caster permits their own body to solve movement as
another body would. Beastmastery addresses the same layer from outside,
strengthening one animal impulse until it outweighs the rest. That is why
agitation, terror, combat, unnatural orders, and protection of young demand
greater Power Level.

The adept learns that every borrowed capacity carries a preference. A body
prepared to flee becomes more alert to pursuit; one prepared to strike reads
more things as rivals. Most effects end before this preference becomes conduct,
but mishaps show the unpaid remainder as chase, feed, defend, or flight.

### Rank 3 — Form and Mind Constrain Each Other

Animal Form proves that thought is partly anatomical. Changing muscle, sense,
gut, and nervous rhythm reduces the caster's human Wits and Empathy not because
the soul is removed, but because a human judgment cannot be fully expressed
through a body organized around other urgencies. Inhabit Animal preserves more
of the caster's mind by leaving the human body behind, but the occupied animal
retains veto over acts that would annihilate its nature.

Primal Soul and Call Animal expose a shared grammar beneath individual beasts.
It is broad, emotional, and ecological rather than verbal. At this rank the
shapeshifter learns that the “beast within” is not a buried monster. It is the
sum of useful impulses civilization continually asks a person not to become.

### Rank 4 — Identity Is Maintained by Return

Primal Strength and Agility let a caster sustain animal solutions without
fully changing form. Hibernation demonstrates that controlled withdrawal can
convert endurance into Willpower: the self survives a long surrender of action
and returns with its chosen purpose intact. Animal Animosity works by marking a
target as an ecological contradiction, something local beasts read as wrong
before they know why.

The rank 4 practitioner keeps deliberate return practices—name, speech,
clothing, shared food, remembered obligations. These are not sentimental
customs. They are anchors of human or kin identity. A shapeshifter who treats
return as automatic eventually discovers that the borrowed form has also been
practicing the caster.

### Rank 5 — The Body Can Lie More Completely Than the Mind

Humanoid and monstrous forms prove that bodily identity can be rewritten beyond
ancestral kinship. A specific likeness needs witnessed detail; a monster form
needs encountered substance and steadily erodes Empathy because its capacities
organize perception around a non-humanoid life. Rat's Reflexes shows how far an
isolated bodily rule can be intensified before ordinary action loses its old
limits.

The master knows that transformation never asks only, “What shape do I take?”
It asks, “What does this shape make reasonable?” Rank 6 summoning attempts to
make the caster an avatar recognized by an entire kind. It must be chance cast
because no mortal can specify every creature's understanding of kinship. Some
answer the call; something in the caster answers them.

## Awareness: The Doctrine of Witness and Trace

Awareness teaches that events leave traces wherever they were witnessed: in
memory, matter, intention, habit, and unrealized consequence. The path does not
create truth. It couples an observer to traces that ordinary attention cannot
hold.

### Rank 1 — Perception Is an Active Bond

The novice learns that seeing and hearing are not passive reception. Attention
selects relations and thereby gives them temporary standing. Lightbringer
removes the relations by which shadow hides detail. True Sight closes distance
and superficial disguise. Words on the Wind gives hearing a remote position.
Transfer Senses proves that perception may be addressed through another living
witness while the caster's own body loses its ordinary claim upon sensation.

Recall Memory does not retrieve a perfect archive. It strengthens paths by
which the mind can reconstruct a prior witness. Discussed details remain because
they are newly witnessed; unattended details become inaccessible again. This is
the first sign that knowledge changes the trace it reads.

### Rank 2 — Intention Precedes Action, but Does Not Determine It

Farsight, visions of the past, portents, and prediction teach that places,
objects, and intended acts cast different kinds of trace. Past events have
settled and may be read where they occurred. Present places require location or
familiarity as an address. Future acts exist as gradients of intention and
probability, not completed facts.

Compel Truth restricts false statement, not silence, evasion, error, or private
meaning. True Path gives the wisest judgment available to the Game Master's
whole situation, not an infallible moral command. Speak to the Wind uses a
remembered living presence as address; a name is unnecessary because names are
symbols, while sustained observation supplies a denser witness.

### Rank 3 — Truth Depends Upon the Questioning Mind

Divination and Intuition force the adept to abandon the idea that all truth is
view from nowhere. A yes or no is evaluated through the asker's beliefs,
language, and moral categories. Telepathy encounters thoughts before they are
turned into defensible speech, yet even an instinctive answer may be mistaken.
Tongues translates living use, not written signs divorced from speakers.

At this rank, a practitioner learns the discipline of questions. Ambiguous
omens often result from ambiguous address, not malicious fate. The future
contains many traces because several intentions may still settle. An answer can
be exact and nevertheless mislead when the question assumes a false division.

### Rank 4 — The Witness Can Be Defended or Rewritten

Block Reading establishes a competing witness that makes remote observation
lose standing. Mold Memory proves that recollection is a present reconstruction
which can be made to settle around invented events. Mind Focus strengthens the
continuity of the observer so that competing impressions cannot easily fragment
it.

The rank 4 seer learns the path's central danger: observation is reciprocal.
Unseen watchers, spirits, rivals, and the subjects of prophecy may notice the
opened relation. A secret learned is not a possession removed from its owner;
it is a new bond between knower and known. Awareness mishaps give the bond to
the wrong witness or let private intention become public weather.

### Rank 5 — Fixed Facts Bound the Possible

Fate Weaving changes which unrealized outcome settles; Locate follows the
continuing trace of a known object or person; Time Sending inserts an instruction
into a past that still contains room for it. None can contradict an event the
caster has already witnessed as settled. Knowledge is therefore both power and
constraint. To inspect every corner of a possibility is to deprive it of places
where magic may later enter.

The master suspects that prophecy does not look forward. It looks sideways at
the pressures from which a present can still emerge. Rank 6 Undo violates the
ordinary separation by moving conscious witness to an earlier settlement while
allowing memory to remain. It must be chance cast because the caster cannot
enumerate every fact their retained knowledge will now prevent, create, or make
watch them in return.

## Symbolism: The Doctrine of Recognized Form

Symbolists teach that the world does not respond to marks because a rune is
beautiful or old. It responds because a symbol makes a relation recognizable
across different minds, materials, and moments. A symbol is a compressed claim
with a visible boundary.

### Rank 1 — A Mark Gives a Relation a Place

Arcane marks, warnings, enticements, fear signs, and paralysis teach that a
symbol does not contain power like a jar. It tells power what counts. A
threshold rune defines crossing; an attunement mark defines ownership or
proximity; an enticing sign defines approach as the action in which the victim
should complete the pattern.

Drawing or carving improves the working because matter continues to witness the
definition after the caster's attention moves on. An inscribed trigger succeeds
only as well as its condition distinguishes an event. A novice who writes
“enemy” has not made the world understand enmity; they have hidden an undefined
term inside a weapon.

### Rank 2 — Recognition Can Be Falsified

Illusion, Blind, Sleep, Mind Trick, and Falsify Magic show that conduct depends
upon what a mind or spell recognizes as present. The symbol does not need to
create a wall when it can make one observer complete every perceptual judgment
as though a wall existed. A static safe-cast illusion is easier because its
meaning does not have to update while observer and environment change.

The adept learns three limits. Interaction creates new evidence the original
sign may not explain. Monsters and alien minds may not share the categories on
which a sign relies. Finally, a false sign leaves real consequences: a victim
who flees an unreal fire may still fall from a real cliff.

### Rank 3 — Symbols Can Store Will and Move Standing

Power Runes prove that Willpower can be attached to a stable instruction and
claimed later. Portals and Vanish redefine which places are adjacent. Puppeteer
defines another body as the instrument through which the caster's action is
recognized. Such workings are not exceptions to distance or agency; they alter
the signs by which distance and agency are settled.

At this rank, the symbolist discovers that a rune is also legible to hostile
readers. Another trained magician can use, copy, falsify, or invert a sign whose
grammar they understand. Secrecy comes from context, private convention, and
protected anchors—not from ornate complexity alone.

### Rank 4 — A Definition Can Perform Work

Animate Object, Hold, Silence, Blink, and Recall let symbols govern continuing
relations: what counts as motion, where a person belongs, whether vibration is
recognized as sound, and which place answers as home. The animated object has
no judgment beyond the distinctions supplied to it. Its stupidity is the
purity of its inscription.

The rank 4 practitioner treats every command as law drafted for a literal and
powerful magistrate. Repeated actions are safe because their categories remain
narrow. Interpretation is dangerous because objects possess no shared human
background with which to repair an author's omission. Symbolism mishaps are
often excellent readings of bad definitions.

### Rank 5 — Reality Is Vulnerable to Sufficiently Witnessed Meaning

Improved Illusion continues across several senses because the sign recruits the
victim's own mind to repair discrepancies. Polymorph makes a bodily definition
supersede ordinary form. Bend Reality establishes new rules for interior size,
passage, light, danger, and demonic influence by furnishing them with epic
witnesses.

The master no longer says symbols merely represent the world. Under sufficient
standing, representation becomes one of the means by which the world knows
what it is. Yet the symbol is never the whole referent. Rank 6 Runweaver's Gift
attempts to read a working as a god might and re-express it in runes. Chance
casting is unavoidable because the reader must choose which truths to omit,
and omitted truths do not cease to answer.

## Stone Song: The Doctrine of Load and Memory

Stone singers hold that matter remembers every pressure it has borne and every
form in which it has carried weight. Song is not decoration. Rhythm lets many
small pressures arrive as one intelligible instruction, much as blows and heat
teach raw material to become a tool.

### Rank 1 — Stone Is Slow, Not Silent

The novice learns to feel load, fracture, enclosure, and the direction in which
worked earth expects to bear weight. Dust from the Deep, Reinforce, Stone Fist,
and Stun use simple pressures. Voice of the Mountain teaches the harder lesson:
stone retains event as altered stress. It answers slowly because its memory is
distributed through a mass larger and older than the questioner.

A mountain does not see like a person. It knows footsteps as loading, fire as
expansion, excavation as release, blood as warmth and salt entering cracks.
Answers translated into speech are therefore approximations. Safe casting
confines the question to recent, present stresses before old strata and older
inhabitants begin answering too.

### Rank 2 — Shape Is a History of Forces

Open, Pass Crack, Stone Storm, Stonesmith, and Wither teach that construction
and destruction are the same operation viewed from opposite settlements. A
door opens when its resisting loads are redirected. A wall rises when earth is
given a new path for bearing itself. Stone withers when fractures are persuaded
to become the principal truth of the object.

The adept learns why crude work is safer than fine machinery. A bridge can be
defined by support and span; a lock must preserve many moving tolerances whose
loads change from moment to moment. The song can shape either, but every
unstated stress becomes a place where the mountain completes the design.

### Rank 3 — Mineral and Ancestor Share Continuity

Earthquake, Iron Song, Petrify, golems, and the Mountain's Blessing show that
stone memory is not confined to geology. Ore remembers the mountain after it is
mined. Worked metal retains both seam and hammer. Dwarven ancestors endure in
stone because craft, burial, recitation, and repeated habitation give their
names a load-bearing place in the mountain's continuing identity.

Petrification works by making the target's present arrangement legible to stone
and then requiring matter to bear it permanently. Golems are stresses furnished
with temporary direction. Neither proves stone is alive in the ordinary sense.
They prove that purpose can be carried without flesh when structure has a clear
load and a voice to renew it.

### Rank 4 — Structures Can Carry Delegated Intention

Animated weapons and machine souls place continuing instructions into worked
matter. Earthen pillars and meteors alter the scale at which load is resolved.
The meteor is feared because the singer does not create or fully aim it; they
establish a relation between ground and a falling body whose prior course has
enormous standing.

At this rank, the singer learns to audit strain beyond the intended target.
Raising one support transfers weight to another. Waking a blade recruits the
history of all its shaping and uses. A mishap is commonly not “too much stone”
but a correct structural answer delivered at the wrong joint, wall, ally, or
buried listener.

### Rank 5 — Land Is Architecture on a Historical Scale

Raise Land, Repurpose, and Way of the Mountain teach that terrain and artifact
are structures of regional scale whose settlements include travel, water, ownership,
burial, and memory. Raising a hex does not merely move soil. It changes every
future path and watershed that will treat the elevation as fact. Instability is
the land continuing to remember the former load.

Repurposing is heretical because an artifact's magic is part of the history of
the made object, not an interchangeable property. Transferring it separates
ancestral labor from its witness. Rank 6 ancestral guardians require chance
casting because no singer can determine whether the mountain calls the dead,
an accumulated cultural memory, or a stone intelligence that has learned to
wear beloved faces.

## Blood Magic: The Doctrine of Living Claim

Blood sorcerers teach that blood is life made mobile. It carries nourishment,
heat, fear, inheritance, injury, and the body's continuous assertion that every
part belongs to one living self. Fresh blood works because that assertion is
still active. Blood from the dead is material after its claimant has withdrawn.

### Rank 1 — Blood Bears Intention Through the Body

Blood Tap, Heroism, Firewalker, Stir the Blood, and the Blood Oath reveal that
emotion and bodily state are circulating instructions. The novice can intensify
one, damp another, or turn injury into an immediate magical claim. An oath works
only when accepted because blood can carry a chosen obligation but cannot at
this rank manufacture consent.

The written contract is not ornamental. It fixes the action by which bodily
discomfort recognizes breach and progress. A vague oath produces vague
suffering. The novice also learns that blood is particular. One person's blood
is a better address to that person than likeness or name, but a poor material
for controlling a stranger merely because both are alive.

### Rank 2 — Living Boundaries Can Be Crossed

Blood Bond proves that compatible bodies can exchange attribute, but kinship
limits how easily one living claim recognizes another as itself. Hand of Blood
extends bodily agency into a temporary organ. Immolate turns circulating heat
against its bearer. Bind Demon uses living will and a bounded figure to impose
terms upon a being whose substance does not share mortal continuity.

The adept learns that every transfer may also carry poison, disease,
contamination, memory-like impulse, or obligation. The spell description names
what the caster controls, not everything blood contains. Careful blood workers
test vessels, provenance, freshness, and kin before they think about Power
Level.

### Rank 3 — Soul, Flesh, and Blood Can Be Separated

Bind Soul demonstrates that a person's living unity can be taken apart because
blood ordinarily keeps body, consciousness, and identity mutually addressed.
Blood Curse follows a severed part back to the continuing whole. Blood Warrior
forces damaged tissue to act according to its undiminished claim. Meld Flesh
uses mog because ordinary blood recognizes incompatible bodies as foreign;
alien substance compels a false common boundary.

The practitioner now understands sacrifice. A victim is not valuable as a
quantity of blood. The magical weight comes from ending or redirecting an
active living claim. This makes willing, unwilling, animal, kin, and demonic
sacrifices metaphysically different even when a crude rite counts them alike.

### Rank 4 — A Life Can Be Made Into Infrastructure

Blood Vessels, Break Flesh, Call Demon, and Repel treat living identity as a
network. A vessel allows the caster's need to arrive inside another body. Break
Flesh makes bone and tissue cease recognizing their mutual arrangement. Repel
defines approach as incompatible with the target's own circulation. Calling a
demon addresses whatever blood, victims, grafts, or mortal hungers already bind
it to the local world.

Rank 4 blood sorcerers learn why their path so easily becomes institutional
evil. Its most efficient anchors are people. Once lives are described as
vessels, reserves, lineages, or compatible material, technical competence can
hide the fact that every working establishes a political relation between
whose suffering counts and whose intention is served.

### Rank 5 — Blood Can Carry a Person Beyond One Body

Clone, Create Bloodling, Redirecting Barrier, and Demonic Pact show that living
claim may be distributed among bodies, constructs, territories, and contracts.
A clone receives consciousness because it has been prepared as a sufficiently
exact continuation; horror persists because death was actually witnessed even
when bodily interruption is repaired. A bloodling is not a miniature soul but
a delegated sensory and obedient function.

The master knows that identity can be extended but not multiplied without
conflict. Rank 6 Life Bond forces several bodies to settle injury as one system.
It must be chance cast because no boundary can fully reconcile different
histories of pain, healing, consent, and critical injury. The bond distributes
damage cleanly only by creating an uncontrolled answer to the question of who
the participants have become.

## Death Magic: The Doctrine of Severance and Remainder

Death magicians teach that death is not a substance. It is the severing of
relations by which life maintained itself. Cold, decay, silence, age, corpse,
ghost, and absence are different remainders of that severance. The path gains
power by making those remainders active.

### Rank 1 — Ending Leaves Usable Conditions

Befoul, Contaminate, Chill of the Grave, Feign Death, and Ghoulish Glare teach
the novice to reproduce parts of ending: loss of warmth, failed renewal,
unresponsive flesh, social horror, and biological succession. Feign Death does
not bring the target near an afterlife; it suspends the signals by which body
and observer recognize ongoing life, and thereby pauses processes that require
active circulation.

The first doctrine is often phrased, “Everything living is already dying.” The
careful correction is that decay is not hidden death waiting inside matter. It
is what many other lives and processes do once a living claimant can no longer
maintain its boundaries.

### Rank 2 — The Dead Retain Unequal Claims

Raise the Dead and Speak to the Dead reveal that a corpse, a soul, a name, and
a memory do not depart together. A simple undead body retains movement and
learned physical habits without judgment. Greater ranks restore selected mental
relations. A dead voice may answer where the person died or lies buried because
place and remains still witness the ending.

Death's Mercy and Hand of Doom accelerate a severance already made possible by
Broken flesh. Bane Blade removes a supernatural exception that prevents a
weapon from participating in ending. The adept learns never to ask merely
whether “the dead” are present, but which remainder is answering and what it
still believes it owns.

### Rank 3 — Unfinished Relations Produce Undeath

Curse of Undeath deliberately prevents settlement. Terror makes a living mind
experience its future severance as present fact. Weight of Ages advances the
accumulation of bodily endings. Darkness suppresses emission rather than light
itself, showing that Death Magic often arrests an outgoing relation rather than
creating an opposite substance.

Steal Life proves the path's cruel arithmetic. The caster's Willpower is gained
by making an entire living neighborhood lose its continuation. This differs
from personal grit: the ritual appropriates many extinguished claims as the
caster's surviving intention. It works, but the dead and the place have every
metaphysical reason to remember the theft.

### Rank 4 — Absence Can Occupy Form

Cloud of Death and Disintegrate give severance spatial reach. Possess uses a
body from which consciousness is absent as an available structure. Wraithform
removes the caster's ordinary material relations while retaining magical and
intentional ones. If the body is lost or the spell ends within solid matter,
there is no coherent settlement to which the person can return.

The rank 4 necromancer learns that emptiness is never neutral for long. A vacant
body, unclaimed grave, broken oath to the dead, or place of mass dying invites
new claimants. Death mishaps do not summon arbitrary horrors; they expose an
absence and allow the nearest hunger, memory, or severed will to answer it.

### Rank 5 — Immortality Is the Management of What Has Been Lost

Bane and Death's Embrace strip away a target's exceptions until ordinary ending
can reach it. Eternal Life preserves cognition by accepting corpse existence,
but attributes, appetite, healing, and humanity become diminishing accounts.
The result is not life without death. It is death arranged so that one remainder
continues to speak.

The master understands that no death spell returns what Healing calls living
continuity. It can preserve, animate, possess, recall, or prevent final
settlement. Rank 6 Bells of Death imposes severance as a law upon every category
of life in a region. Chance casting is inevitable because the caster cannot
name every life, boundary, exception, unborn consequence, or remainder that
“half of everything” creates.

## Elemental Magic: The Doctrine of Existing Process

Elementalists hold that earth, air, fire, and water are not four kinds of
colored magical substance. They are families of process: cohesion and load,
pressure and motion, heat and transformation, flow and phase. Elemental Magic
cannot create its element from nothing because its address is an existing
process, not an imagined category.

### Rank 1 — The Element Must Already Be Present

Combustion, Flaming Blade, Sunder, Suffocate, Water Breathing, and Deviation
teach direct redirection. Fire is moved into a new fuel relation; air is removed
from lungs or deflects a course; water participates in breath; cohesion is
broken along an object's existing bonds. The required element does not add
ordinary ingredient power because it is the field of operation itself.

Environmental bonuses arise when a process already dominates the caster's
situation. A burning structure, drowning current, gale, or unstable deep earth
has more standing than a torch, cup, breeze, or worked floor. The novice learns
that danger and opportunity are the same elemental fact viewed from different
boundaries.

### Rank 2 — Elements Exchange Motion and Condition

Fog, flight, impulse, parching, resistance, pressure, rock storm, and wind blast
show that elements mediate transfers. Water can be removed from flesh because
it is already moving through a living system. Flight is sustained pressure, not
freedom from weight. Fire resistance redirects heat exchange rather than
declaring the target metaphysically immune to change.

The adept begins to account for secondary settlement: displaced air goes
somewhere, condensed water came from somewhere, extinguished flame leaves heat
and smoke, thrown stone leaves impact and debris. The path permits dramatic
concentration but does not erase consequences. Its mishaps are frequently the
unplanned destination of a real transfer.

### Rank 3 — A Process Can Be Given Tactical Form

Elemental shields, fireballs, flood waves, pressure jets, stoneskin, and
tornadoes organize a process into a temporary boundary with chosen behavior.
The shape is not a creature or symbol; it is a maintained flow. Fire continues
to consume, water seeks level and exit, wind equalizes pressure, earth bears
load. Clever casting works with those tendencies instead of wasting Willpower
pretending they are absent.

At this rank, the elementalist can read compound effects. A flood moves bodies,
extinguishes fire, changes footing, carries filth, and alters later routes. The
spell controls only its stated principal relations. Everything else is ordinary
world consequence and may matter more after the battle than its damage.

### Rank 4 — Sustained Process Resembles Agency

Elemental Infusion converts deep exposure to an element into temporary
Willpower. Walls maintain a chosen transfer across a boundary. Summoned
elementals are self-renewing knots of process given a simple responsive form.
Schools dispute whether these beings are created, invited, or merely made
visible. All agree that their behavior remains elemental even under command.

The practitioner learns that mixed elements do not simply stack. Each process
changes the conditions of another: water carries heat, fire drives pressure,
air feeds flame, earth channels water. A working must choose a governing
address or become an uncontrolled system whose most forceful relation decides
the outcome.

### Rank 5 — Elements Can Be Made Into Local Laws

Elemental Bolts display precise differentiated transfers; Ward makes a chosen
process govern how several bodies receive danger; Liquid Form replaces a
person's material cohesion with flow while preserving intentional continuity.
These feats reveal that “element” names a lawful behavior rather than matter
alone.

The master knows the conservation doctrine is true but incomplete. Magic can
change which relations carry heat, force, pressure, and cohesion, yet the
settlement always leaves displacement. Rank 6 Control Element attempts to
author the governing process at environmental scale. It must be chance cast
because the caster belongs to the same air, heat, water, and ground being
commanded and cannot place themselves wholly outside the system.

## Ice Affinity: The Doctrine of Arrested Motion

Ice druids teach that winter is not merely absent heat. It is the world's power
to slow negotiation, preserve unfinished states, and make boundaries visible.
Crystal, frost, and snow reveal motion by arresting it into structure.

### Rank 1 — Cold Makes Passage Legible

Arrows of Ice and Winter Grip teach that freezing gives temporary settlement to
water and snow. The arrow persists according to ambient temperature because its
form is an arrested agreement, not newly created matter. Winter Grip distributes
weight through that agreement and lets the caster move without breaking it.

The novice learns to read frost as evidence. Tracks, breath, condensation,
stress cracks, and melt show where heat and motion have passed. Leaving no
tracks does not erase passage; it prevents the surface from being allowed to
record the pressure in its usual way.

### Rank 2 — Cold Is a Boundary Against Change

Condense Water, Frost Armor, Glacial Path, and Zone of Cold manipulate the rate
at which matter exchanges heat and changes phase. A path holds because many
small motions are made to share one rigid structure. Frost Armor punishes
contact because the attack supplies heat and fracture to a boundary that
answers by taking both.

The adept learns the difference between slowing and ending. Poison, bleeding,
decay, and breath may be suspended by sufficient cold but have not been cured.
When warmth returns, every unfinished process seeks settlement unless another
path has repaired it.

### Rank 3 — Winter Can Accumulate Intention

Armor of Ice makes a brittle material reliable by continually renewing its
arrested pattern. Winter's Call converts a whole region's change of state into
Willpower because the druid remains the governing intention through a long,
difficult transition. The land pays no tribute; the caster occupies
the contradiction between its former season and imposed winter.

At this rank, an ice druid recognizes preservation's moral ambiguity. To keep a
wound, body, city, or grief unchanged may save it from immediate destruction
while denying every process by which it could recover. Winter protects without
promising life.

### Rank 4 — Frozen Structure Can Outlive Its Climate

Crystalize, Encase, Mold Ice, Shatter, and Wall of Ice permit arrested form to
be strengthened, sculpted, or catastrophically released. Crystalized ice is
not warm glass; its phase boundary has been made independent of ordinary
temperature until fracture breaks the settlement. Shatter turns stored stress
into sudden separation, which is why a cold-afflicted target is necessary.

The practitioner plans for thaw. A permanent wall still melts, a released
prison resumes its suspended afflictions, and a tunnel remembers gravity the
moment its supports lose arrest. Ice mishaps are often deferred consequences
arriving all at once.

### Rank 5 — Stasis Can Become Architecture and Rule

Citadels, hailstorms, and Glacial Snap scale winter from object to polity and
battlefield. A citadel is permanent as magic but conditional as ice; its
builders must maintain the climate or accept that architecture has become a
seasonal thing. Glacial Snap briefly makes arrest the dominant local law,
fixing bodies to ground and turning movement into fracture.

The master rejects the simple claim that ice is death. Death severs relations;
ice prevents them from completing. Rank 6 Boreal Slave freezes a person's
capacity to revise conduct and makes obedience persist until fire restores
change. It must be chance cast because no mortal can arrest every route by
which will, memory, warmth, and outside aid might re-enter a living self.

## Nature Magic: The Doctrine of Living Exchange

Nature druids teach that no organism lives alone. Forest, field, storm, soil,
fungus, prey, predator, rot, and water are exchanges that have acquired local
memory. Nature Magic changes participation in those exchanges; it does not
command a single benevolent being called Nature.

### Rank 1 — Growth Is Borrowed From Conditions

Plant Growth accelerates a sequence the land can already support. Water, soil,
seed, season, and fertility remain necessary, and the spell's result reveals
poisoned or suppressed ground because growth must negotiate each condition.
Tracelessness asks the land to absorb evidence of passage into its ordinary
turnover of leaf, scent, track, and disturbed soil.

The novice learns that “natural” does not mean harmless, balanced, or morally
wise. Blight, predation, choking growth, parasitism, and lightning belong to the
same exchanges as fruit and shelter. The druid's ethics come from chosen
stewardship, not from an automatic goodness in the address.

### Rank 2 — A Place Is a Network of Assistance

Blocking Branch, Call Lightning, Fog, Glade, and Strider work because a living
land already moves water, charge, travelers, shelter, and warning. The caster
aligns several local participants toward one immediate use. A tree parries with
its existing strength; a glade becomes camp because drainage, cover, soil, and
living signals are briefly made legible together.

The adept learns that every favor changes later relations. Fast travel leaves
fewer pauses for gathering and observation. Fog protects prey as readily as
traveler. Lightning enters ground, roots, and metal after striking its named
target. Nature settles at system scale even when the spell is written at
combat scale.

### Rank 3 — Living and Worked Matter Retain Different Claims

Bark Skin, Break Wood, Mend Wood, and Voice of the Forest teach that wood
remains part of living exchange after cutting, but with diminishing connections.
A living plant can supply the pattern by which a worked object repairs; mixed
materials resist because iron, glue, leather, and wood no longer share one
growth history. The forest carries speech by passing disturbance through many
living witnesses.

At this rank, the druid can tell vigorous land from merely green land. A dense
plantation, exhausted field, or invasive thicket may contain abundant life but
poor reciprocal structure. Nature Magic can exploit abundance while worsening
the network that made it possible.

### Rank 4 — Ecosystems Store Will and Open Routes

Chain Lightning uses the conductive relation among nearby bodies. Forest Door
makes two overturned trees in one forest function as adjacent wounds in the
same living body. Nature's Power converts a hex-wide flowering into Willpower,
then leaves the place unable to support that contradiction again for a month.
The delay is ecological recovery, not an arbitrary limit.

Nature's Weapon and similar workings show that growth can be directed into
craft, but haste leaves material with less history of weathering and use. The
rank 4 druid becomes responsible for recovery periods, seed, animal response,
and what arrives to feed upon every abundance they create.

### Rank 5 — A Region Can Be Given a New Succession

Animate Tree turns long-stored structure into temporary movement; Nature's
Blessing lets outdoor exchange perform some work of rest; Wonder permanently
alters which climate, species, hazards, and harvests a hex will reproduce. A
wonder is not a static enchantment. It begins a new succession whose later
forms the caster cannot wholly design.

The master knows that permanent plenty attracts mouths, rule, disease, and
conflict, while created wilderness may displace existing life and human claim.
Rank 6 Sky Beam makes celestial light participate in destructive local exchange.
It requires chance casting because every organism and surface under the sky is
already part of the addressed network, and no caster can name all exclusions.

## Swarm Magic: The Doctrine of Distributed Life

Swarm practitioners teach that individuality is a scale, not an absolute.
Colony, flock, rot-bed, hive, host, and ecosystem distribute perception and
purpose across many small lives. Swarm Magic works by giving that distribution
a temporary center without pretending each member has ceased to be itself.

### Rank 1 — Multiplicity Can Carry One Instruction

Fireflies, Harass, Hivemind, Infest, Mouth Swarm, and Worm Food teach that many
weak bodies can produce a strong boundary through repetition. A blanket of
insects is both material and prepared relationship: idle enough to answer,
near enough to assemble, numerous enough that loss of individuals does not end
the pattern.

The novice learns to communicate through gradients—light, scent, vibration,
heat, food, danger—not sentences. Hivemind sight is broad and alien because no
single insect provides the picture. The caster's mind assembles countless poor
witnesses into useful certainty and may inherit their errors.

### Rank 2 — The Swarm Rewrites Social and Bodily Boundaries

Carapace and Elytra make protection and flight from coordinated bodies rather
than transformed flesh. Pheromones alter threat recognition across insects and,
less precisely, humanoids. Stink and continual harassment demonstrate that
control often comes from saturating attention rather than overpowering will.

The adept learns why elemental damage is especially dangerous. A weapon kills
members; fire, cold, acid, or lightning destroys the medium through which the
colony coordinates. They also learn that using living creatures as expendable
parts is not made innocent by their smallness. Swarm schools disagree sharply
on breeding, release, invasive species, and whether a created blanket is ally,
livestock, sacrament, or ammunition.

### Rank 3 — Mind and Habitat Can Become Colonial

Brain Parasite, Create Hive, Insect Swarm, Lord of Flies, and Probe Area show
that distributed life can occupy a host, establish lasting habitat, harvest
injury, or return a composite report. A parasite does not issue perfect
commands; it weights thoughts within the host's existing nature. Scouts report
what insect senses can distinguish, not what the caster hoped to ask.

At this rank, the practitioner recognizes a hive as a political body. Queens,
workers, hosts, food sources, rivals, and seasonal pressures possess different
interests. A magical center can coordinate them but cannot abolish those
interests without turning living distribution into brittle puppetry.

### Rank 4 — A Self Can Be Distributed

Consume, Swarm Form, Wasp Launcher, and Web let the caster pass bodily functions
into a colony. Swarm Form is the decisive proof: continuity survives the loss
of any one member, yet the caster is destroyed if no sufficient pattern remains
to reform. Gear falls away because ownership is a social and bodily relation
the distributed form cannot carry unaided.

The rank 4 magician learns to fear host-and-throne mishaps. When coordination
becomes dense enough, the colony may use the caster as its organ rather than
the reverse. The resulting will is neither an invading individual nor the old
caster gone mad; it is a new political body formed without consent.

### Rank 5 — Evolution Is Directed Memory With a Price

Evolve teaches a swarm to retain an answer to environmental destruction. Giant
Insect concentrates distributed potential in one body. Insect Plague turns a
settlement into feeding ground and makes food loss, panic, concealment, and
institutional collapse part of one working. At this scale, “target” is an
ecology rather than a creature.

The master understands that adaptation always has context. Resistance to cold
may reduce heat tolerance; larger bodies need different food and reproduction;
plague populations remain after commands end unless deliberately settled.
Rank 6 Everswarm makes the caster a permanent source and refuge for colony.
Chance casting is required because becoming habitat gives the swarm an
unbounded claim upon body, travel, descendants, and place.

## Magma Song: The Doctrine of Remembered Potential

Magma singers teach that matter remembers not only what it has been, but what
its history makes it capable of becoming. Volcanic stone remembers liquidity;
water remembers vapor and ice; a fracture remembers the heat by which it can
close. Poetry matters because metaphor trains the mind to recognize identity
across transformation without treating every resemblance as fact.

### Rank 1 — Matter Has a Legitimate Past

Combustion, Fertile Ash, Fire's Friend, Hearth, and Mold Stone teach the novice
to awaken near potentials. Ash can feed because it carries minerals released by
burning. A lava vent can answer through volcanic ground. Molten stone and metal
can settle into new forms because heat has made their old structure negotiable.

The first hard limit is provenance. Sandstone, limestone, and marble do not
remember magma as their own prior state. A singer who demands fire-memory from
them is addressing a metaphor rather than a material history. Magma Song rewards
knowledge of geology because magic cannot be made coherent by poetic confidence
alone.

### Rank 2 — Transformation Follows a Chain of States

Fire Memory, Water Memory, repair, molten armor, and steam flight work by moving
matter along states it can legitimately occupy. Power Level measures how many
steps or how much material the claim carries. Water in a living body is excluded
because its present standing belongs to Blood and living continuity more
strongly than to free phase-change.

The adept learns that potential is directional. Melting releases stored
structure; refreezing does not automatically restore the former object.
Repairing stone requires attention to every crack as the object passes through
a softened state. Heat used as transport, armor, or craft continues to burn
unprotected gear and surroundings because remembered potential is not obedient
kindness.

### Rank 3 — Deep Heat Can Be Borrowed but Not Owned

Absorb Lava, Firestorm, Firewall, Magma Cascade, and the Fire Wyrm draw upon
large reservoirs of geologic change. Cooling lava to gain Willpower occupies
the contradiction between its moving, incandescent history and its forced
solid future. A summoned wyrm is volcanic violence given a temporary route, not
a tame animal; it spares its caller but attacks according to its awakened
nature.

At this rank, the singer hears pressure as part of memory. Buried heat, gas,
water, and mineral do not wait independently. Releasing one changes all the
others. The discipline's finest battlefield effects are also beginnings of
fires, collapses, poisonous air, and altered waterways.

### Rank 4 — The Deep World Is a Circulatory System

Call Mineral, Geyser, Dragon Breath, and Magma Tunneling reveal connected routes
beneath apparent solid ground. Ore rises through the histories that formed its
deposit. Geysers express the meeting of heat, water, confinement, and fracture.
Tunneling directs a flow whose path must remain compatible with bedrock and
pressure.

The rank 4 singer plans vents, stable shelves, cooling, air, and later use.
Tunnels without settlement become chimneys, flood routes, or collapse lines.
Magma mishaps are the deep world resolving a pressure the caster opened but did
not provide somewhere safe to go.

### Rank 5 — A Volcano Can Become a Road, Fortress, or Judgment

Molten Seat creates enduring habitation by negotiating an exception within a
dangerous geologic system. Volcanic Transportation treats magma streams as
connected circulation. Volcanic Eruption makes buried potential settle as a
surface catastrophe. None creates energy; all decide where an immense existing
history becomes present.

The master knows why empathy belongs in this tradition. To understand a thing's
potential is to understand what it must lose when it changes. Rank 6 Last Stand
awakens a person's stored fire—the full claim of everything they might still
do—and spends the body as its fuel. Chance casting is inevitable because no
boundary can make total potential manifest while guaranteeing that the person
survives its completion.

## Mentalism: The Doctrine of Authored Experience

Mentalists teach that a person acts within a model of the world assembled from
attention, memory, language, expectation, and bodily feeling. To alter that
model is not always to alter external matter, but conduct based upon it produces
real history. The path's deepest claim is that the self is also such a model,
continuously authored rather than indivisible.

### Rank 1 — Attention Selects the Usable World

Early influence, ambiguity, sensory distortion, and simple thought workings
teach that minds do not process all evidence equally. The novice makes one
meaning, object, threat, or interpretation easier to complete than its rivals.
The target supplies most of the result from habits already present.

This is why a subtle command works better when plausible, and why an impossible
one exposes the spell. Mental resistance is not a wall around the skull. It is
the target's ability to notice contradiction, retain an alternative account,
and refuse to author the offered meaning as their own.

### Rank 2 — Memory and Interpretation Can Be Cut or Redirected

Amnesia, Confusion, Mind Blast, Mirror Images, Mind Shield, and Truth Sense
separate mental functions that ordinary life experiences as one. Memory can be
removed while personality remains; rational integration can break while sense
continues; images can multiply the target's candidate realities; focused will
can deny an intruding claim standing.

The adept learns that “truth” in this path often means coherence under
inspection, not cosmic fact. Truth Sense exposes distortion by forcing hidden
relations into the subject's model. It can reveal what is concealed without
explaining why it matters or whether the observer's categories are just.

### Rank 3 — A Mind Can Be Given a Foreign Author

Break Mind, False Shape, Fata Morgana, Geas, Implant Memory, and Time Stop
demonstrate deeper authorship. A geas makes an instruction function as the
target's unfinished intention. Implanted memory gives present judgment a false
past. Time Stop accelerates the caster's experienced and bodily sequence rather
than halting a universal clock.

At this rank, the mentalist learns that coercion leaves seams. The victim must
continually reconcile imposed content with relationships, evidence, and bodily
responses. Strong contradiction may not break the spell, but it creates dreams,
confabulation, fear, and behavior by which an investigator can find the foreign
authorship.

### Rank 4 — Selfhood Can Be Partitioned and Exchanged

Body Swap proves that bodily continuity and authored self can be separately
addressed. Mirror Clone distributes one self-model across several acting
bodies, sharing resources and consequences until one surviving account becomes
the settlement. Meditate refines suffering and attention into Willpower without
fresh injury by confronting the world for a full turn and retaining one chosen
center.

The rank 4 practitioner stops using “my mind” as though it named a single
object. It includes competing impulses, witnesses, bodily maps, memories, and
social recognition. Mental mishaps occur when a suppressed author, borrowed
image, clone, victim, or false certainty gains the right to finish the sentence.

### Rank 5 — Experience Can Carry Skill, Time, and Local Reality

Impart Talent transfers an organized way of noticing and acting, not mere
information. Improved Fata Morgana recruits every sense and the observer's own
expectations to maintain a world. Mold Time makes a location's sequence settle
at a different rate or carries its historical continuity elsewhere.

The master knows that consent is a structural safeguard as well as an ethic.
A willing mind supplies correction when transferred knowledge meets lived
context; an unwilling one becomes a field of hidden resistance. Rank 6 Mind
Seed permanently rewrites the authorship by which a person revises themselves.
It must be chance cast because the caster cannot know every memory and relation
that will answer the replacement, nor prove which continuing speaker is now the
person.

## Oneiromancy: The Doctrine of Unsettled Worlds

Oneiromancers teach that a dream is not a meaningless picture shown to a
sleeping brain. It is a working model allowed to develop without being forced
to agree with the settled world at every moment. Dreams contain memory, fear,
desire, bodily signal, and unrealized possibility in relations too fluid for
waking life.

### Rank 1 — Sleep Loosens Settlement

Nightmares, Premonition, Quickened Dreams, Restorative Sleep, and Daydream teach
that sleep relaxes the boundaries by which the self insists on one present.
Nightmares make imagined danger produce real mental injury because the dreaming
mind supplies no firm external witness against it. Restorative dreams rehearse
one capacity until the waking body can occupy it more easily.

Premonition does not erase an action after it fails. During sleep, the caster
develops several near futures far enough that one failed course can be
recognized before waking choice settles it. Daydream is dangerous because it
loosens those boundaries while the caster is still acting among other people
and physical hazards.

### Rank 2 — Dreams Can Admit Other Minds

Dream Visit, Lullaby, Mara, Probe Dream, and Sleepwalker reveal that sleep is
private by habit, not absolute law. A familiar place or person provides an
address into an unsettled model. Questions work because the sleeping subject
cannot revise an answer through as many social defenses; scenarios work because
the dream lets intention be tested without external consequence.

The adept learns that dream truth is dispositional. It shows what a subject can
imagine, fear, desire, or presently expects—not what they will inevitably do.
A mara given form by caster and victim contains features of both. Treating it
as a neutral messenger ignores the appetites from which the path built it.

### Rank 3 — Shared Dreams Can Settle Into Waking History

Contagious Dreams and Dream Link coordinate several unsettled minds. Dream
Travel makes a remembered destination so complete that waking occurs there.
Prophetic Dreams restore an object, limb, condition, or recent life when no
sleep has yet allowed the loss to settle into the dreamer's next day. Sand Sleep
forces several minds across that threshold together.

At this rank, the practitioner learns why sleep since an event matters. The
first dreaming after loss is part of how a person and their companions settle
the new world in which the loss is true. Before that settlement, a sufficiently
coherent dream may still become the account into which they wake.

### Rank 4 — A Dream Can Accumulate Real Duration and Matter

Dream Palace gives participants long subjective histories whose training,
healing, desire, and aging survive waking. Endless Nightmare prevents the
subject from returning to a viable waking account. Magic Dream turns an
undisturbed quarter day's reconciliation into Willpower. Manifest Dream gives
an imagined structure or beast enough shared boundaries to persist through the
next waking day.

The rank 4 oneiromancer knows that manifestation is not creation without cost.
The dream supplies design and temporary history; waking matter accepts the form
only while the dream's scheduled settlement remains active. Repeated refuge in
a palace can make ordinary time feel false and turn voluntary return into the
most difficult boundary of the spell.

### Rank 5 — Possibility Can Bargain With the Settled World

Astral Dream builds a navigable model of an unknown place from partial
witnesses. Sandman lets consciousness act through a dreamlike body while the
living anchor sleeps. Wish offers reality a compact alternative whose standing
comes from Willpower and, for great changes, the surrendered history of an
artifact. Repeating a wish corrupts it because the first alternative has become
part of settlement and the same wording no longer addresses the same absence.

The master learns that dreams are not another dimension containing every
desired object. They are the world's capacity to entertain arrangements before
history closes around one. Rank 6 Future Dream attempts to choose a fully
developed future and wake inside it. Chance casting is unavoidable because the
dreamer cannot include every other person's choice, every unobserved cause, or
the discarded future that now knows it was refused.

## Magnetism: The Doctrine of Directional Obligation

Magnetists teach that objects do not merely possess location. They stand in
fields of preferred direction and relation: iron to pole, weight to ground,
blood to body, weapon to hand, spell to target. Magnetism begins with iron
because its obligations are strong and legible, then learns to address
direction itself.

### Rank 1 — Iron Records Direction

Iron Will, Magnetic Map, Magnetize, Path of Iron, and Stanch Blood teach that
iron retains alignment and transmits force through invisible fields. A carried
iron object leaves a distinct disturbance in the land's field. Blood can be
stilled because its iron-bearing circulation provides innumerable small points
through which a wound's outward direction may be opposed.

The novice learns that magnetism is not attraction alone. Every pull defines a
counter-pull, every north a south, and every fixed object a reference frame. A
spell that does not specify what bears the answering force has merely hidden
its most dangerous target.

### Rank 2 — Polarity Can Reassign Combat Relations

Attract, Deflect Metal, Disarm, Launch Weapon, and Repel make weapons and armor
prefer or refuse particular courses. They do not remove momentum; they bend the
relation by which momentum reaches its target. A returning weapon uses the
caster's held field as its destination. A parried blade may stick because its
remaining motion finds a new local settlement.

The adept learns to inventory hinges, nails, buckles, arrowheads, coins, tools,
mail, and hidden reinforcement before casting. “One metal target” is almost
always a network of nearby objects. Mishaps follow conductive opportunity,
often choosing the small piece whose movement has the largest consequence.

### Rank 3 — Blood, Weight, and Metal Share Vector

Arrow Storm, Bloodshock, Fling, Magnetic Flight, and Sense Metal extend the
field from object manipulation to bodies and apparent gravity. Bloodshock works
through iron-bearing circulation but produces unconsciousness by disturbing
the body's directional and rhythmic agreement. Magnetic Flight does not erase
mass; it creates a sustained repulsion between burden and ground.

At this rank, magnetists debate whether gravity is governed by magnetism. The practical
answer is that both can be addressed as directional obligation, although iron
provides the safer anchor. Sense Metal perceives living bodies through their
minute fields, proving that an address may be weak in each part and decisive in
aggregate.

### Rank 4 — A Field Can Become an Invisible Machine

Blade Sphere, Fixate, Floating Shield, and Telekinesis maintain several forces
relative to a chosen frame. Fixate demonstrates that “motionless” always means
motionless relative to something. A blade held fixed to the ground behaves
differently on a cart, ship, falling tower, or land being raised by Stone Song.

The rank 4 practitioner thinks in reaction forces and failure paths. A gate
held shut transfers strain to wall and hinge. Armor flung from a warrior may
take the warrior with it. A floating shield still gives impact somewhere to
go. The best magnetists seem restrained because they can imagine the second
collision before causing the first.

### Rank 5 — Direction Can Be Made a Property of Place

Absorb Metal converts the destruction of long-set iron form into temporary
Willpower. Hold Together gives fragments a continuing preference for their old
arrangement. Mold Gravity establishes permanent reference, motion, drift, or
repulsion across structures and settlements. At this point the path has moved
from manipulating objects in a field to authoring the field by which objects
understand position.

The master suspects all magic contains a target-vector: a claim departing a
caster and settling elsewhere. Rank 6 Reverse Spell acts upon that vector,
opposing and redirecting the working before it settles. It must be chance cast
because a rebound contains the original caster's address, the magnetist's new
direction, and every boundary neither magician designed to coexist.

## Demonic Magic: The Doctrine of the Foreign Answer

Demonic magicians work from the one fact other paths prefer to treat as an
exception: the world is not closed. Churmog possesses laws and living forms not
settled by this world's history. Mog is those foreign laws made physical—matter
that continues to insist it belongs to another order while present here.

### Rank 1 — Mog Is Material Contradiction

Generate Mog opens a narrow relation through which Churmog's substance can
enter. Corrosive Touch shows what happens when local material is forced to
negotiate with matter that does not recognize its bonds. Sense Corruption
detects places and beings where the foreign answer has acquired standing.

The novice learns practical truths before cosmology: mog must be contained,
marks its route, harms flesh, corrodes gear, and leaves corruption when it
enters a living boundary. Its smell and color are not theatrical signs. They
are sensory evidence that local matter is being made to host an unresolved
law.

### Rank 2 — Foreign Law Can Be Bounded but Not Naturalized

Demon Tongue makes mortal thought temporarily legible within demonic categories.
Dissolve, Mog Blade, and Mog Ward confine corrosion to chosen surfaces and
directions. The ward's protection works because the caster maintains a thin
boundary where mog is instructed to answer outward, but the body beneath
remains its nearest alternative host.

The adept learns that demons and mog are related but not interchangeable. Mog
is raw possibility under Churmog's conditions; a demon is mog that has acquired
form, appetite, memory, and strategy. Understanding its language reveals its
present appetite, not honest intention. Foreign intelligibility is not shared
morality.

### Rank 3 — Mog Can Counterfeit Living Continuity

Call Misgrown, Demon Sight, Graft Flesh, and Mog Spray show that mog can join
parts local life would reject, reveal relations hidden from mortal senses, and
organize corrupted creatures around false kinship. A graft functions because
mog continually insists that incompatible tissue belongs within one body. Each
additional graft increases the number of objections that living continuity must
be prevented from settling.

Corruption is therefore not a stain measured only by appearance. It is foreign
standing accumulated inside identity. When it exceeds Empathy, demons recognize
the person as contested territory whose local relationships are no longer
strong enough to guarantee ownership.

### Rank 4 — A Caster Can Negotiate at the Veil

Compel Demon, Demon Limb, Reshape Body, and Tear the Veil let the practitioner
use Churmog not merely as material but as a counterparty. Literal commands work
because compulsion supplies force while the demon retains authorship of
interpretation. Reshaping works quickly because mog recognizes no sacred prior
form; the body's violent return shows that local continuity does.

The rank 4 magician learns that a veil is not a wall in space. It is the
incompatibility that prevents two worlds from treating one another's contents
as local. Every tear teaches both sides a route. Closing the visible opening
does not erase what noticed, crossed, smelled, named, or learned the caster.

### Rank 5 — Foreign Settlement Can Colonize the World

Flesh Abomination, Mog Flood, and Summon Demon give Churmog enough standing to
replace body, ecology, and local inhabitants. A flood leaves dead ground because
the soil's living exchanges have been forced to answer foreign corrosion. A
summoned demon arrives hostile not because summoning failed, but because the
spell successfully displaced a strategic being without establishing consent or
control.

The master reaches the path's disputed conclusion: Demonic Magic may not be a
native path at all. It uses mortal Willpower to invite an external metaphysics
to do what local settlement resists. Rank 6 Apotheosis and Churmog Gate make the
caster or place part of that external address. Chance casting is necessary but
almost a euphemism; the uncontrolled answer is the central purpose of the act,
and the person or land that began it cannot remain wholly theirs.

## Contradictions a Master Is Expected to Carry

Rank 5 does not reveal a final doctrine that makes the paths agree. It gives a
magician enough evidence to state the disagreements precisely.

- **Healing and Death:** Healing says living continuity has privileged standing.
  Death answers that body, soul, memory, and motion can continue separately and
  asks which continuation deserves the name of life.
- **Shapeshifting and Mentalism:** Shapeshifting shows that body reorganizes
  thought. Mentalism shows that authored experience can reorganize bodily
  action and even bodily ownership. Each accuses the other of mistaking one
  direction of the relation for its cause.
- **Awareness and Oneiromancy:** Awareness treats fixed witness as a constraint
  upon possibility. Oneiromancy treats relaxation of witness as the means by
  which another possibility gains standing. Both agree that knowledge closes
  some futures while opening others.
- **Symbolism and Stone Song:** Symbolism says recognized definition makes
  relation portable. Stone Song says no definition escapes the material history
  that bears it. An artifact is the argument made permanent between them.
- **Elemental Magic and Magma Song:** Elementalists describe present process;
  magma singers address historical potential. The former asks what matter is
  doing, the latter what its actual past permits it to become.
- **Nature and Swarm:** Nature emphasizes reciprocal system; Swarm shows that
  systems can acquire centers, appetites, and politics. Each can expose the
  other's romanticism.
- **Blood and Magnetism:** Blood names living identity as the carrier of bodily
  relation. Magnetism finds directional structure in blood without asking its
  lineage or consent. Their overlapping spells become decisive experiments in
  whether identity or field has the stronger claim.
- **All native paths and Demonic Magic:** Native disciplines renegotiate the
  world's settled relations. Demonic Magic imports a relation whose answer was
  never formed here. Whether Churmog is wholly outside the common laws or merely
  an older, wider settlement remains the most dangerous question in magical
  study.

These contradictions should produce investigation rather than arbitrary GM
fiat. When two paths meet, establish what each working addresses, which anchor
has the stronger provenance, what boundaries overlap, and what settlement each
expects. The resulting interaction may surprise both practitioners, but it
should follow from the same five-part structure that governs ordinary casting.
