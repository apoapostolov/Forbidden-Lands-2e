# Gods, Cults, and Religious Orders

This file is the canonical authority for divine names, faction
identity, religious practice, and the cosmological background.
Statblocks here are reference NPCs (typical members of the order),
not unique characters.

## The Protector God: Three Faces

A single Protector god is worshipped under three names by three
overlapping traditions. The names are not synonyms; they reflect
real theological splits that have produced wars.

### Wyrm — Congregation of the Serpent

The oldest tradition. Worships the Protector as the **serpent**
that the Raven held in its claws on the day of the Shift. Led by
**Psychopomps** (priests who guide souls), of whom **Vipera**
heads the order in Alderland and **Aspis** at **Farhaven**, where
the **Library of Farhaven** keeps records older than any other
human institution. The symbol is the **ouroboros** — the serpent
swallowing its own tail.

The Congregation is widespread but quiet. In most villages it
"keeps its head down so as not to challenge the newer faiths."

Named figure: **Psychopomp Vivírula** in Alderland; her nephew
**Dominik of House Viperion** is a sworn knight of the order.

### Raven — the Raven Church

Worships the Protector as the **raven** that led humans across
the ocean. The order is led by **Jamharda** and is known for its
**healers and shapeshifters**. Members specifically called the
**Raven Sisters** are the priesthood of the Ailander exodus —
they led the Ailanders out of Alderland 700 years ago and are
hated by Alderland orthodoxy as heretics and traitors.

**Typical Raven Sister:**

- STR 3, AGI 3, WIT 3, EMP 3
- Skills: Healing 3, Lore 2, Insight 3, Manipulation 2
- Talents: Path of Healing 2, Path of Shifting Shapes 1, or Path of Sight 1

#### Reapenters / Blackwings

A murderous Ailander schism. Led by **Badalar the Butcher** with
the elf **Father Geno** at his side. The cry is **"Nevermore!"**
The doctrine: the curse on the Forbidden Lands will only end when
the last human, dead or alive, has left it. So they kill any
human they meet who does not yet understand this.

**Typical Blackwing:**

- STR 3, AGI 4, WIT 2, EMP 2
- Skills: Stealth 3, Melee 2, Marksmanship 2, Survival 2
- Gear: Black-feathered cloak, knife, garrote, short bow

### Rust — the Rust Brothers

Worships the Protector as **Rust**, the iron god. The order claims
that **rusty iron** is uniquely effective against demons and that
the original divine substance was iron, not flesh. Led currently
by **Rust Lord Kartorda**. The high priests are **Misgrown** —
humans who have taken on demonic attributes through Zytera's
seduction. The military arm is the **Iron Guard** (knightly order).

The Rust Brothers run priories across the western and northern
Forbidden Lands. They tithe villages, purge restless dead, hunt
heresy, and are widely accused of agitating villagers to provoke
killings that produce ghouls — useful as soldiers and sleepless
guardians. They are said to know how to turn a ghoul into a
**Death Knight**.

**Typical Rust Brother:**

- STR 4, AGI 3, WIT 3, EMP 2
- Skills: Melee 3, Endurance 2, Manipulation 2, Lore 2
- Talents: Path of the Enemy 1
- Gear: Mace, rusty chainmail, iron Rust sigil

**Typical Iron Guard:**

- STR 4, AGI 3, WIT 3, EMP 3
- Skills: Melee 4, Move 2, Endurance 3, Animal Handling 2
- Talents: Path of the Enemy 2
- Gear: Two-handed sword, plate armor, closed helmet, Rust sigil shield

### Sisters of Heme

The female counterpart to the Rust Brothers. Heme is the goddess
of the hunt, of fertility, of the sacrifice. **Crude figurines**
of Heme appear in roadside shrines. The Wolfkin call her _the
Huntress_ and worship her in seasonal blood-rituals.

**Typical Sister of Heme:**

- STR 3, AGI 3, WIT 3, EMP 3
- Skills: Melee 2, Healing 2, Survival 2, Animal Handling 2, Manipulation 2
- Talents: Path of the Forest 1
- Gear: Sickle, leather armor, Heme figurine

The Rust Brothers and the Sisters of Heme together are the **Rust
& Heme Church**. The two are theologically married. Their union
produced (in myth) the daughter **Feroxa**, broken into the
hematite of the world; see `artifacts-and-named-relics.md`.

### Vampyr Heresy

The vampyrs (see `bestiary-creatures-and-monsters.md`) claim that
**they** keep the true rite of Rust and the Rust Brothers are the
corrupted ones — that Zytera is a false prophet who has sullied
the Protector's legacy with demonic influence. They worship Rust,
keep blood as the divine substance, and consider themselves
mankind's firstborn.

## The Older Gods

Worshipped across all three human peoples and most non-human kin.
The villages of the eastern Forbidden Lands keep them as primary
faith; the western villages keep them under the Rust Brothers'
shadow.

### Huge — Forge-God of the Dwarves

Created the dwarves to fill the void of space with matter. The
sun is **Huge's Hearth**; the stars are the **Outer Forges** he
has placed to entice the builders. The dwarves believe in
reincarnation as **tools of Huge** — when a dwarf dies in service,
Huge grants them a new body suited to a higher work. Dwarven oath:
**"Huge's whiskers!"**

The first traitor in dwarven myth is **Belial the Loose-Tongued**,
a mastersmith who carried the secret of steel to elves and humans
and was bound forever as the first **greater golem**, standing
guard outside Huge's divine forge. Greater golems are a dwarven
punishment-craft; the rune on the forehead means _traitor_ in old
dwarvish.

The **Iron Dragon** is the antithesis of Huge — the cosmic decay
that gnaws at the world the dwarves are building.

### Clay — the Sexless Creator

The creator deity. Made all life, was forced to root vegetation
in place after the Ents proved too powerful. Worshipped by the
**druids of the Elvenspring**, by the **Order of Maidens**, and
by the **Golden Bough**.

The **Shardmaiden** (see below) is Clay's prophet. Earth and clay
are sacred substances. **Maha symbols** — druidic pictograms on
clay tablets — are used in ritual and contemplation.

**Clay's Rosary** (see artifacts) controls weather. Its myth tells
of the maiden **Thousand-Heart**, the sea-behemoth **Gargal**, the
mischievous dolphin **Puld**, the trapped lover **Adembre** turned
to red shoreline algae, and the salt of the sea — Thousand-Heart's
weeping.

### Wail — Air Goddess

Goddess of wind. Her myth (see **Wail's Horn** in artifacts) tells
of how the oceans tried to keep her, how a toothed shark threw her
free, and how she made the shark larger and gave it the breath of
air — the first walrus.

### Flow — Water Goddess

Goddess of springs, rivers, and lakes. The **amphibians** are her
created guardians of pure water. Sacred sites at her springs are
called **Flow's Delight**; the rite at Springturn (overflowing the
village well) is named for her.

### Nightwalker — Oldest of the Gods

The moon is his eye. Smoke, including the smoke of cookfires and
hearths, is his network of spies. He slew the giants in their
sleep at the Raven's request. The **Dreamer in Falender** sought
his help and birthed the **wolfshadows**. **Giant spiders** are
said to be his first creation, his soldiers spinning his dreams.

The Nightwalker is feared and respected; his shrines are
half-ruined and worshipped quietly. The **Hourglass** artifact
came from a bat-faced messenger of his; see artifacts.

### Horn — Aslene Fire God

Volcano god. His physical manifestation is the volcano in Aslene.
**Horses** were given flaming manes by Horn at their creation.
The **dread raptor** is the eternal enemy of horses, also molded
by Horn, to keep cosmic balance — Aslene rider clans test their
young by sending them to a dread raptor's nest. The Quards are
flocking to **Zertorme** at **Amber's Peak**, hailed by their
prophets as Horn's chosen savior. **Erinya**, the great fire wyrm,
is Horn's servant and the source of the **Arrow of the Fire Wyrm**.

### Eor — Moon Goddess

Goddess of lies, deception, and hidden meanings. Threw blue
moonstones at the falling elves out of jealousy; from the
moonstones grew the kin that became **halflings and goblins**.
Eor averts her face every month in shame, which is why the moon
darkens.

The halflings worship her secretly and are ashamed; the goblins
worship her openly. The **Penita** sect of halflings seeks the
lost moonstones to restore the original kin.

### The Red Wanderer

A red star that wanders across the sky. The elves' rubies are
said to have fallen from it. **Redrunners** are named for it.
**Giant specters** may be the Red Wanderer's tormenting spirits
sent to keep humans in check.

## Other Powers

### The Shardmaiden

Clay's prophet, an elf, with **Morander** her companion. Resides
at **Maidenholm**. Founded the **Order of Maidens** — druids who
carry an emerald shard from the Shardmaiden fused into their
foreheads. The Maidens are the most aggressive druidic order;
they fight Zytera and the Rust Brothers as a primary calling.

**Typical Maiden Druid:**

- STR 2, AGI 3, WIT 4, EMP 3
- Skills: Melee 2, Marksmanship 2, Lore 2, Manipulation 2, Move 1
- Talents: Path of Shifting Shapes 2
- Gear: Short bow, dagger, leather armor, emerald shard in forehead

### The Golden Bough

The largest druidic order, led by elves of the **Stillmist**.
Their domain is **Dankwood** and **Vivend**. Most members are
**Elvenspring** half-elves. They protect the Stillelves who have
become Ents. They accept nature's cruelty as part of its course.

**Typical Golden Bough Druid:**

- STR 3, AGI 3, WIT 4, EMP 3
- Skills: Melee 2, Scouting 2, Lore 3, Marksmanship 2, Survival 3, Animal Handling 3
- Talents: Path of Healing 2
- Gear: Throwing spear, falchion, leather armor

### Order of the Silent

A necromantic assassin cult that creates **tupilaqs** — sewn-together
abominations bound to a single target. They take contracts from
those "who share their twisted world view."

### Stone Singers

Dwarven sorcerer-priests of the Belder clan who can shape stone
through rune-song. Many are on permanent pilgrimage, walking the
Forbidden Lands to find ripe slabs and sing formations from them
to extend the world according to Huge. Some follow trolls to
collect minerals.

The named female stone-singer **Porisia Silverthroat** currently
leads a crew extending the world-globe. Historical: **Aras
Carskenfoot**, **Dordela**, **Master Homb**, **Torwyld Gravel**.

### The Maha Language

The druidic pictographic script used on **clay tablets** for
ritual and contemplation. Sacred to Clay-worshippers across
Elvenspring, Maidens, and Golden Bough.

## How Religion Appears in Villages

The eastern villages keep crystal talismans from the Shardmaiden's
temple against evil. The western villages instead trust the Rust
Brothers and Zytera's authority. Some settlements are secretly
guarded by remaining Raven Sisters. Almost every village has a
humble central temple with someone ordained as priest who also
works another trade.

The villagers also venerate the older gods (Wyrm, Wail, Flow, Clay)
beneath whatever larger faith dominates locally. The Congregation
of the Serpent persists everywhere, quietly.

Treatment of the dead is itself religious practice: the burial
ground is walled off; restless dead (walkers) are spoken to with
music and patience; ghouls are killed; mirror-images are kept away
from walkers because the dead can become aggressive when shown
their reflection. **The Rust Brothers' practice of harvesting
restless dead for use as ghouls and Death Knights** is widely
disliked but rarely opposed openly.

## Faction Distribution Notes

When choosing which religious presence appears in a village,
consult both region and the village's age and history:

- **Western Forbidden Lands** (Alderstone, Vond, Misgrown lands):
  Rust Brothers and Iron Guard dominant. Heme worship strong in
  forested margins.
- **Eastern Forbidden Lands** (Stonegarden's reach, the coast,
  Margelda): Shardmaiden, Order of Maidens, Raven Sisters.
- **Northern villages** (orc lands' edges, Arina Forest borders):
  Raven Church and Congregation of the Serpent more visible;
  Cult of Horn for Quard families.
- **Anywhere there is a forge or mine:** Cult of Huge.
- **Anywhere there is a sacred spring:** Cult of Flow.
- **Roadside shrines:** most often Heme or Wyrm.
- **Mountain passes and high places:** Wail's prayer-flags from
  long-lost pilgrims.
