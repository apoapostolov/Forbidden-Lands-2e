# Forbidden Lands Lore Reference Index

Use this index to select the correct lore layer before loading individual
reference files. Do not treat every directory as the same kind of authority.

## Core Ravenlands References

The Markdown files directly in `references/` are the core setting library,
principally derived from the Gamemaster's Guide and approved manuscript lore.
They are the default sources for general Ravenlands questions:

- `setting.md` — world state, peoples, geography, currency, and magic context
- `tone.md` — genre, encounter authenticity, and prose register
- `history.md` — chronology, calendar, festivals, and historical figures
- `gods.md` — gods, churches, orders, heresies, and religious factions
- `kin.md` — kin, clans, cultures, customs, and named people
- `bestiary.md` — creature origins, ecology, folklore, and cultural roles
- `artifacts.md` — artifact legends, provenance, and public knowledge
- `places.md` — settlements, regions, geography, and adventure sites

## Custom Expanded Lore

`expanded/` contains Apostol Apostolov's generic, campaign-created expansions
to Forbidden Lands society, psychology, religion, culture, politics, economy,
regional life, magical metaphysics and institutions, warfare and aftermath,
and long-term relations with monsters. These references may combine published
canon, grounded inference, local belief, historical models, community theory,
and established personal campaign lore. They are not limited to a single
published campaign even where their inherited titles refer to the Ravenlands.
Start with `expanded/INDEX.md` for topic routing and claim levels. The
path-by-path magic doctrine and its companion social-magic reference are
non-spoiler expanded lore. The full-spoiler demonology and Merigall study also
lives in this layer; apply the spoiler gate in `../SKILL.md` before loading it.

Preserve this corpus at full depth and in its existing academic-for-AI voice.
Do not summarize, normalize, or silently replace personal lore with canon-only
material.

## Campaign References

`campaigns/` contains campaign-specific references and GM-facing secrets:

- `campaigns/ravens_purge.md` — Raven's Purge
- `campaigns/bloodmarch.md` — Legacy of Horn and Bloodmarch spoilers

Apply the spoiler gate in `../SKILL.md` before reading or revealing marked
campaign secrets.

## Expansion-Region References

`regions/` contains non-spoiler setting libraries for lands outside the core
Ravenlands:

- `regions/bloodmarch/` — Bloodmarch setting, kin, history, gods, creatures,
  artifacts, and places
- `regions/the-bitter-reach/` — Bitter Reach setting, kin, history, gods,
  creatures, artifacts, and places

Load a regional library only when the request concerns that region or needs an
explicit comparison across regions.
