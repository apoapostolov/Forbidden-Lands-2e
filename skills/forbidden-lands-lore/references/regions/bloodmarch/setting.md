# Bloodmarch — Setting

**Region:** Aslene (called "the Bloodmarch" by outsiders)
**Source:** Erik Granström, Free League Publishing
**Campaign:** Legacy of Horn

---

## World State

The Bloodmarch is Ravenland's neighbor to the west. Same
latitude, hotter and drier, farther from the ocean. The central
plains are black volcanic soil mixed with fertile ash from the
active volcano Horn at the country's heart. Surrounding mountain
ranges are high with few passes. Strange rock formations are
common, especially in the east. All mountain rivers converge
southward and vanish underground.

The country has been sealed for three hundred years following
Zygofer's Demon Flood in 875 AS. Shadowgate Pass reopened
recently during the events at Vond. The Bloodmarch is now
open to the outside world for the first time in three
centuries. What adventurers find is a war-scarred country
struggling with demon plague, clan politics, and scarce
resources — not the ruined wasteland rumor predicted.

**Entry points:**

- **Shadowgate Pass** — through the Thynde Range, the only
  confirmed land route from Ravenland. Just past the pass:
  the frontier settlement Taregyll.
- **Dwarven tunnels** — old passages from Järnhem northeast
  of the Bloodmarch, connected to Belderand and the Winding
  Ways of the Bitter Reach. The dwarves refuse to confirm
  or deny whether they remain functional.
- **Gander's Pass** (to Alderland, southeast) — collapsed
  during the Demon Flood. Currently blocked. Whether by
  earthquake or deliberate action is unknown.

## Terrain Types

### Standard Terrain

Forests, plains, hills, and high mountains in the Bloodmarch
use standard Forbidden Lands journey rules.

### Ashlands

Black deserts of volcanic ash. Fine gray-black powder as far
as sight allows in all directions.

- No animals to hunt.
- No foraging possible.
- All standing water is poison: Potency D6+3.
- Healing and attribute recovery while camped in Ashlands
  is not possible.
- Ash storms and sinkholes are constant hazards.
- Making camp here is miserable; most attempts fail.

*Mishap table:* Ashlands have their own D66 journey mishap
table (distinct from standard terrain).

### Firelands

Magma flows close to the surface. The ground bakes bare feet;
stone is hot enough in places to crack leather soles.

- No animals to hunt.
- Ground water is undrinkable: Potency D6+1.
- Geyser vents unpredictable; moving quickly through
  Firelands requires constant SURVIVAL attention.
- SURVIVAL roll required each Quarter Day or suffer 1 Strength
  damage from the burning ground.
- Sleeping in Firelands counts as bare ground in sweltering
  heat. SURVIVAL roll required to sleep at all. Characters
  wake THIRSTY regardless.

Firelands are sacred to priests of Horn. Several temples
stand on ground thick enough to build on.

*Mishap table:* Firelands have their own D66 journey mishap
table.

### Crimson Forest

Blood-red demon jungle. Dense, tangled, and malevolent. Hot
and faintly luminous. Flowers orient toward warm-blooded
creatures. Air carries spores — odorless, potentially
hallucinogenic or poisonous. Plants move with apparent will.
Predatory creatures here are neither entirely plant nor beast.

**Crimson Sickness:** Any warm-blooded creature that spends
time in a Crimson Forest risks contracting Crimson Sickness.
Progression has nine stages; each morning an infected creature
must roll ENDURANCE or drop to the next stage. Symptoms:

1. Bloodshot eyes
2. Blood vessels visible under skin on face and hands
3. Red pulsating vessels spreading across all limbs
4. One hand turns entirely red — it develops its own will
5. Red growths emerge from skin
6. Facial deformation; hands become talon-like
7. Large tentacles emerge from the upper back
8. Boils across the body; ooze seeps from skin
9. Death — the corpse dissolves and becomes Crimson Forest

Each stage also imposes Empathy penalties.

**Treatment:** Blue Tar (daily dose) halts progression but
does not cure it. To cure, Blue Tar must be heated on Have's
holy fire. Blue Tar heated on ordinary fire has no curative
effect.

**Red elves** are the only kin that live inside Crimson
Forests without risk of Crimson Sickness.

*Crimson Forests also have their own D66 journey mishap table.*

### Terrain Movement and Foraging Summary

| Terrain | Movement | Forage | Hunt |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active Volcano | Impassable (crater accessible) | − | − |

## Magic Traditions

New magic traditions of the Bloodmarch. Characters from
Ravenland or Alderland cannot begin with these traditions —
they must find teachers in the Bloodmarch.

### Magma Song

Practiced by Caprid priest-druids of Horn. A sub-tradition
of Stone Song — Caprid priests who know Stone Song can learn
Magma Song and keep both traditions at separate ranks. Other
kin who learn Magma Song typically do not know Stone Song.

**Philosophy:** Magma Song awakens the memory of matter rather
than adding energy from outside. Stone becomes lava by
remembering what it once was. Water boils by realizing its
potential. Understanding and empathy are central. Poetry
is valued. Magma Song does not work on limestone or sandstone —
these were never lava.

| Spell | Rank |
|---|---|
| Ignite | 1 |
| Fire's Friend | 1 |
| Mold Stone | 1 |
| Fire Memory | 2 |
| Water Memory | 2 |
| Steam Flight | 2 |
| Magma Cascade | 3 |
| Firewall | 3 |
| Summon Fire Wyrm | 3 |

**Ignite (1):** A non-living object within Near range
spontaneously ignites. Carried objects burn the bearer; worn
armor becomes an ongoing fire attack.

**Fire's Friend (1):** Caster (and one additional creature per
Power Level above 1) becomes immune to all fire damage — including
dragon fire and lava — for one turn per Power Level.

**Mold Stone (1):** Makes hot liquid stone or metal shape itself
and solidify in a desired rough form. Up to one cubic meter
per Power Level. Precise shapes require a CRAFTING roll.

**Fire Memory (2):** Instantly melts volcanic stone into flowing
lava. Up to one cubic meter per Power Level. Only affects stone
that was once magma — not marble or limestone.

**Water Memory (2):** Changes water one temperature state per
Power Level: ice → water → warm water → steam. Vaporized
water in closed containers causes explosive pressure. Frozen
water shatters locks and makes surfaces treacherous. Cannot
affect water inside living creatures. Bonus: caster can always
make any beverage the ideal temperature for free.

**Steam Flight (2):** Pressurized steam from a carried water
container lifts the caster — one meter vertical or one zone
horizontal per round until the water runs dry. Aimed at a
creature within Arm's Length, the steam deals Strength damage.

**Magma Cascade (3):** A chunk of volcanic rock explodes in
a cascade of burning magma. Everyone within Arm's Length of
the detonation suffers Power Level Strength damage. Can be
placed with a MARKSMANSHIP roll or set as a contact trap.

**Firewall (3):** A one-meter-thick wall of fire bursts from
volcanic rock. Length one meter per Power Level; width three
meters per Power Level. Crossing the wall inflicts Power Level
Strength damage.

**Summon Fire Wyrm (3):** Summons a fire wyrm from volcanic
ground nearby. Arrives within one turn and attacks everything
in sight except the caster. Size of the wyrm increases with
Power Level.

### Mentalism

Moon elf magic. Affects the perception, memory, and will of
intelligent creatures. The moon elves of the eastern continent
Anoma were masters; Bloodmarch moon elves have lost much of
that knowledge but remain excellent manipulators.

| Spell | Rank |
|---|---|
| Mental Strength | 1 |
| Wordplay | 1 |
| Traceless | 1 |
| Confusion | 2 |
| Amnesia | 2 |
| Truth Sense | 2 |
| False Shape | 3 |
| Fata Morgana | 3 |
| Geas | 3 |

**Mental Strength (1):** One allied creature per Power Level
becomes immune to Wits and Empathy damage and fear attacks
for one turn per Power Level.

**Wordplay (1):** Makes one person per Power Level reinterpret
what was said by exploiting a chosen word's ambiguity — effect
is permanent.

**Traceless (1):** Alters all visible traces of the caster and
one additional creature per Power Level (appearance, voice,
scent, body language) for one hour per Power Level. The caster
controls the alteration but not how others perceive the result.

**Confusion (2):** Reduces one target's Wits to zero for one
round per Power Level. Target is Broken without a critical
injury — incapable of rational thought. INSIGHT roll to resist.

**Amnesia (2):** Makes one person per Power Level forget the
previous turn. INSIGHT roll to resist. The person is not
otherwise changed but must reappraise everything around them.

**Truth Sense (2):** Caster detects lies, sees through
illusions, and understands hidden or distorted things.
Each Power Level reveals something significant about a
thing's true nature.

**False Shape (3):** Caster takes the appearance, voice, and
scent of another living creature between half and double their
size. Attributes and known languages are unaltered. INSIGHT
roll to see through; physical contact immediately breaks
the illusion.

**Fata Morgana (3):** Caster creates a controlled illusion
that looks and acts by the caster's will but is immaterial,
scentless, and traceless. Requires concentration to maintain.
INSIGHT roll to see through; contact breaks it immediately.

**Geas (3):** Forces a creature to perform one specified action.
Cannot compel suicide or actions leading directly to the
creature's death. INSIGHT roll to resist. Lasts until the
action is performed or one day per Power Level elapses.

### Oneiromancy

Dream magic. Taught by the Dreamstress at her tower in
Oxengelder. Usually performed while the caster is dreaming
or in trance; a few spells that affect others' sleep can be
cast while awake.

**Sleeping oneiromancer:** A caster who uses oneiromancy
during magical sleep wakes without spending the Willpower
Points the spells cost.

| Spell | Rank |
|---|---|
| Premonition | 1 |
| Nightmares | 1 |
| Restorative Sleep | 1 |
| Dream Visit | 2 |
| Mara | 2 |
| Lullaby | 2 |
| Dream Travel | 3 |
| Sand Sleep | 3 |
| Prophetic Dreams | 3 |

**Premonition (1):** Once per Power Level per day, the caster
can make any skill roll and decide after seeing the result
whether the action was actually taken. Cannot push premonitioned
rolls.

**Nightmares (1):** Injects nightmares into sleeping creatures
within Near range. Each suffers Wits damage equal to Power
Level. Zero Wits from this causes a critical fear injury.
Victims become SLEEPLESS the following day. No effect on
monsters.

**Restorative Sleep (1):** After restorative dreams, the
caster increases one attribute or skill by one for the day
per Power Level.

**Dream Visit (2):** While sleeping, the caster travels
invisibly to any previously visited location and observes
it. Additional Power Levels allow appearing to and
communicating with people there. Cannot act physically.

**Mara (2):** Summons a mara in dream form and sends it after
a target the caster can visualize. The mara attacks until
its task is done. Caster sees what the mara sees until waking.

**Lullaby (2):** A quiet lullaby puts humanoid creatures within
Short range into deep sleep unless they roll INSIGHT. Those
who plug their ears are immune. Sleeping creatures can be woken
normally.

**Dream Travel (3):** After a Quarter Day's sleep, caster
wakes at any previously visited location. Brings all carried
items and one additional sleeping person per Power Level
above 3.

**Sand Sleep (3):** Throw a handful of sand — all warm-blooded
living creatures of human size or smaller within Near range
fall unconscious. INSIGHT roll to resist. Only the caster
can wake them.

**Prophetic Dreams (3):** During sleep, caster conjures or
repairs a non-magical item of moderate value. Can also
recreate a lost body part, heal a condition, or resurrect
the recently dead — only if the caster has not slept since
the injury or death occurred.

### Magnetism

Iron-based magic developed within the Rust Church's warrior
cult, the Ironbrows. Not taught to outsiders under ordinary
circumstances.

| Spell | Rank |
|---|---|
| Path of Iron | 1 |
| Iron Will | 1 |
| Stanch Blood | 1 |
| Attract | 2 |
| Repel | 2 |
| Bloodshock | 3 |

**Path of Iron (1):** Track any person carrying iron items for
up to one day after they passed. Each iron item leaves a
distinct trace. Cannot track through water.

**Iron Will (1):** Manipulate metal-containing objects within
Near range — lock or unlock shackles and doors; make a sword
stick in its sheath; make a shield grip clench shut. Power
Level determines the size and weight of affected items.

**Stanch Blood (1):** Manipulates the iron in blood to stop
a fatal wound. Automatically saves the life of a person with
a fatal critical injury.

**Attract (2):** Magnetize an enemy in metal armor. Adds bonus
dice equal to Power Level to all metal weapon attacks against
that target. One round per Power Level.

**Repel (2):** Makes a target's metal armor repel metal weapons
including metal-tipped arrows. Negative modifier equal to Power
Level on all metal weapon attacks against the target.

**Bloodshock (3):** Manipulates an enemy's blood iron content
to render them immediately unconscious. Wits roll to resist.
Cannot be woken until the effect expires. Can affect additional
targets for every two Power Levels.

## Drugs and Potions

### Blue Tar

Produced by stripping bark from holy blue pines of Have and
tapping for sap. Boiled into thick bluish-black liquid.
Production requires a large stationary facility — cannot
be made in the field.

The sacred blue flame of Have burns at the temple of Ashenstead.
Any fire lit inside this temple turns blue and holy. Priests
of Have can transfer holy fire to a combustible material
but cannot multiply it — if five torches are lit from one
holy flame, only one remains blue.

**Against demons:** Blue Tar grants +1 damage against all
demons. Blue Tar combined with holy fire grants +2 total.

**Against Crimson Sickness:** Daily doses halt progression.
Tar heated on Have's holy fire cures it completely.

**Other uses:**

- Makes Horn's Astra glow briefly when smeared on them.
- Required to claim Astra ownership (see Blue Blood).
- Fuels the mechanical horse Witherbeam.
- Locally kills demonic vegetation and neutralizes
  spore tissue when poured on the ground.

### Blue Blood

Equal mixture of Blue Tar and the user's own blood. Used
only for ritual purposes — specifically to claim ownership
of Horn's Astra. See `references/campaigns/bloodmarch.md` for mechanics.

### Dense Water

Condensed water produced by Caprid dwarven druids. Looks
like ordinary water but lasts far longer — resource die
only lowers on a roll of 1. Said to wash away vermin, mange,
tattoos, scars, and disfigurements. Highly sought after in
Alderland.

Production requires a master druid, a live kidney, and
water run at specific astrological alignments. Not sold
outside the Bloodmarch except through specific merchants
(notably in Bann Guelder).

### Crimson Ooze

Viscous red fluid seeping through Crimson Forests. Produced
by slime snails and flows through Veinpearl root systems.
Crimson Forests spread along trails of Crimson Ooze.

Ingesting Crimson Ooze contracts Crimson Sickness immediately.
An elven ruby placed in Crimson Ooze and kept warm grows
into a red elf.

### Lycopodium Powder

A yellow powder made from the spores of a common plant.

**As a weapon against demons:** Blow the powder at a demon
within Arm's Length. MIGHT attack, base damage 1, cannot
be parried or dodged. On a hit, the demon ignites and burns
for 1 Strength damage per round until it succeeds at a
MOVE roll to extinguish itself.

**As a healing agent:** Burning Lycopodium Powder in a wound
caused by demonic plague grants a D12 artifact die for
HEALING. The burning is so painful the treated character
must roll ENDURANCE or pass out for a Quarter Day.

## Oneiromancy — Admission and Training

Oxengelder accepts students on criteria the school does not
publish. Applicants who fail are told only that they are
unsuitable. In practice the requirement is WITS 3 or
lower and EMPATHY 3 or higher.

Exception: any adventurer who has access to one of Horn's
five Astra is automatically accepted for study regardless
of their attributes. The Dreamstress is categorical about
this; Pirimena enforces it.

Training does not come with a payment structure. The
Dreamstress teaches freely. The social weight of the
school's loyalty expectations is the cost.

## Salterstay Rainbow — Mechanics

The Salterstay rainbow barrier is maintained by Vaerefor
van Reiben through a red elf host. It traps the ghost
warriors from the Drawn Lands inside a ring of color bands.
Each color band requires a specific counter to dissolve:

- **Turquoise band:** Dissolved by rubies cut fresh from
  a living red elf's chest, used as projectiles or applied.
  Also dissolved by salt-dipped arrows.
- **Violet band:** Dissolved by green jade.

Red elves cannot tolerate non-red colors. Any adventurer
wearing visibly non-red clothing who enters the rainbow
perimeter risks attracting red elf attention.

The entire rainbow (and Vaerefor's presence) can be
avoided: Vaerefor will spare the rainbow and withdraw
if offered the Goblet Staff of Have. The Goblet Staff
is Vaerefor's actual objective; the rainbow barrier is
leverage.

## Bann Guelder Gold — Economic Consequences

The burial mound at Bann Guelder contains a massive deposit
of pure gold. If it enters circulation in quantity, the
local economy collapses in stages:

1. Gold becomes nearly worthless in the immediate area
   as supply floods the market.
2. Merchants outside the area begin refusing gold from
   the region.
3. Saturation spreads outward over weeks or months as
   the gold is traded into neighboring territories.

This is not a campaign event that happens automatically
— it requires someone to actually move the gold. The
Sons of Nesos know this and it is part of why they have
not mined it themselves.
