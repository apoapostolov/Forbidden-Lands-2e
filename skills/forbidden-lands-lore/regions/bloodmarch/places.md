# Bloodmarch — Places

**Region:** Aslene (the Bloodmarch)
**Source:** Erik Granström, Free League Publishing
**Campaign:** Legacy of Horn

---

## Sub-Regions

### Ashenvale

Ashlands in the northeast. Named for the Ashes — the
Galdane sub-clans displaced by the Demon Flood who now
live as semi-nomadic ash-diggers and scavengers. Grim
territory. Travelers without guides are considered fools.

### The Black

Volcanic plain west of Horn. Black soil, black rock,
constant low-grade seismic activity. A number of Houn
farming communities are built here on soil so rich it
produces crops regardless of water. The ground occasionally
shifts. Everyone builds in ways that accommodate earthquakes.

### Vasnia

Vasnian heartland in the central-east. Older towns; more
permanent architecture. The Vasnians here regard themselves
as the original Aslene people and are not wrong. Relations
with the horse clans are complex and have been for centuries.

### Havenmark

Territory of Have worship. Ashenstead is here, on the
volcanic slope of Horn. Holy ground for both Horn and
Have pilgrims. Pyrolyte training facilities are in
Havenmark. The Warmistress resides here.

### Firestead

Underground Caprid dwarf city under the Firelands. Built
on basalt, heated from below, carved from volcanic rock.
Surface access is through reinforced shafts with heavy
gates. Visitors are processed at the gate and escorted.
The Caprids do not let outsiders wander freely.

### The Wailing

A narrow canyon complex in the east. The wind through
the canyon makes a constant sound. Wolfkin territories
predominantly — several permanent Umbra observation posts
along the canyon rim. The canyon has its own echo
properties that make it navigable by sound alone at night.

### Kreysel

Northern grassland between the Thynde Range and the
Galdane uplands. Mixed Quard and Galdane settlement.
Currently under increasing Rubor-Kolor pressure from
Galdane missionaries. Some Kreysel communities have
converted; others are resisting.

### Strilling

Western coast — Aslene borders a sea on the west.
Strilling is the coastal zone: fishing, salt production,
and trade. Horse clan kin here tend to be Houn-descended.
The Penite have a significant community in the Strilling
coastal towns.

### Druma

Dense woodland in the south-central area, distinct from
Crimson Forest. Standard deciduous woodland with hunting.
The Sisterhood of Viraga has its main complex in Druma —
the rites for orc women's coming-of-age hunts are conducted
here. Non-orcs are strongly encouraged not to be in
Druma during rite seasons.

### Varina

Former territory of the Galdane sub-clans that was hit
hardest by the Demon Flood. Three centuries later it is
partially recolonized, partially reclaimed by Crimson
Forest, partially still ruinous. The ruins are a draw
for scavengers, treasure hunters, and demons.

### Hadruma

Southern border territory near the ruins of Gander's Pass.
Quard territory predominantly. More mixed than any other
sub-region — Aslene orcs, Vasnians, Quards, and Penite
halflings all have communities here. The frontier feel
is real: without Gander's Pass as a trade route, Hadruma
became economically marginal and never fully recovered.

## Major Landmarks

### Horn (the Volcano)

Active volcano at the geographic center of the Bloodmarch.
Always smoking; occasionally erupting. The eruptions
follow a pattern the Caprid druids can predict with some
reliability, but "some reliability" means catastrophic
misjudgments have happened.

The volcanic slopes include Firelands at higher elevations
and black-soil plains at lower elevations. Ashenstead
temple is approximately one third of the way up from
the base.

The summit is not climbed. Horse clan tradition says the
summit is Horn himself and stepping on it is sacrilege.
Whether the Caprids share this view is unclear — they
simply don't mention the summit.

### Shadowgate Pass

The pass through the Thynde Range connecting the Bloodmarch
to Ravenland. Until recently blocked by demonic wards after
the events following the Demon Flood. Now open.

The pass is narrow, cold, and high. A journey through
takes two to three Quarter Days in good conditions. The
Thynde Range is not otherwise passable by conventional
means.

### Gander's Pass (Ruined)

Former pass through the southern mountains connecting
the Bloodmarch to Alderland. Collapsed during or shortly
after the Demon Flood. Whether the collapse was
earthquake or deliberate demolition is not confirmed
in any public record.

The rubble is a site of interest for engineers, scavengers,
and people who want to know why it really collapsed.

## Adventure Sites

### Taregyll

**Type:** Settlement — frontier town
**Location:** Fixed, just inside Shadowgate on the
Bloodmarch side
**Overview:** Taregyll sprang up when Shadowgate first
loosened. Population: rough and varied. No charter, no
formal government. Two executioners' guilds — the Ironclad
and Jagra's — are openly run by different people but
secretly controlled by the same man, Captain Brock, who
profits from keeping them competing.

Key locations: House of the Dead (Egressor Kurmena),
Fire Temple of Have (Ansika), Horn priest Strunde's lava
vent chapel, Father's Pocket (a banking and storage
facility run by Frisikos Pyrra), Madam Munna's Inn,
Meager Modler's Tavern, Fences' Guild (Antmar Stickyfingers),
the Coal Mill, Tar Distilleries, and the Silent Tower
(houses the demonic mass Gest, former First Keeper, now
sealed inside the tower).

The Stream of Galme runs through Taregyll: hot, sulfurous,
undrinkable. Sacred fire fish live in it and are protected
by local law. The stream cannot be waded safely.

### Ashenstead

**Type:** Temple complex and settlement
**Location:** Slopes of Horn in Havenmark
**Overview:** Joint Horn and Have temple. The central
structure predates the Demon Flood and was not destroyed.
The current complex has been expanded and repaired
multiple times.

The sacred blue flame of Have burns continuously in the
inner temple. Access to the inner temple requires at least
minor clerical status or the specific vouching of a
Warmother or Warfather. Pyrolytes have standing access.

**Notable feature:** Blue Tar heated on Have's holy fire
here cures Crimson Sickness. The flame is maintained
continuously by three unnamed vazuls under the supervision
of the high priests Liklaudos and Prunofilia. They do not
charge for its use against genuine Crimson Sickness; they
do charge for other uses.

### Salterstay

**Type:** Settler community
**Location:** Central Aslene, near Lake Suna and the Seindel stream
**Overview:** A community of about 50 adults who came from
Harga in Ravenland, probably following the initial reopening
of Shadowgate. Built on a hill above a salt mine cut into
the nearby mountains. The Seindel stream runs nearby into
Lake Suna, giving the community a fishing harbor as well.
Salt and fish are the main products.

Salterstay is currently in crisis. Red elves have occupied
the east bank of the Seindel and are holding a captured
rainbow. Vaerefor van Reiben is approaching with Kasabatoda
to paint the rainbow permanently red. The hedoness
Mortilla is present and has raised the old barrow dead
nearby for company. Drakewyrms circle the rainbow,
regarding it as a manifestation of Tincturus. The village
headman Turenfort cannot decide what to do about any of it.

### Oxengelder

**Type:** Fortified tower and settlement
**Location:** Eastern Bloodmarch, near Crimson Forest
**Overview:** A limestone formation shaped over decades
into a town. The central tower belongs to the Dreamstress,
an oneiromancer of significant power and uncertain
disposition. She is the primary (possibly only) teacher
of Oneiromancy in the Bloodmarch.

**The Dreamstress:** Does not advertise. Does not take
students on demand. She has tested candidates and
refused some who appeared qualified; she has accepted
some who appeared unqualified. Her criteria are not
public knowledge.

The town around the tower has developed to service both
pilgrims visiting the Dreamstress and travelers who
stop because the limestone architecture is defensible.

### Bann Guelder

**Type:** Trade town
**Location:** Central Bloodmarch, road junction
**Overview:** The best-supplied market in the Bloodmarch
for outsiders. Merchant **Kresos** runs the main trading
house. He specializes in Dense Water (the rarest product
of the Caprid dwarves) and acts as broker for other
exotic Bloodmarch goods.

Kresos does not discuss his suppliers. He will negotiate
on price but not on terms — he does not provide goods on
credit to unknown buyers.

**Products reliably available:**
- Dense Water (expensive; limited supply)
- Blue Tar (expensive outside Havenmark)
- Standard equipment and food
- Horses — ordinary ones, not Wingesteeds

### Watch of the Sisters

**Type:** Orc institution
**Location:** Druma
**Overview:** The main complex of the Sisterhood of Viraga.
At its center is the Metrochrone — a clock of unusual
design that the Sisterhood uses for timekeeping in rites.
The Metrochrone is Caprid-built. How the Sisterhood
acquired it involves a negotiation that neither party
publicly describes.

Non-orcs are not typically admitted to the Watch complex.
The Sisterhood will receive outsiders at the outer gate
for specific inquiries. Attempting to enter uninvited
is a fast way to become an ethics case.

### Tribolia of the Kogler

**Type:** Ruin
**Location:** Eastern Varina
**Overview:** The ruins of a Vasnian town that was fully
consumed by Crimson Forest during the Demon Flood. Aerial
observation (if you can manage it) shows the town's
original street plan under the forest canopy. Inside,
everything is red vegetation.

Treasure hunters have gone in. Some have come back.
What they find and what they bring out varies wildly.
The red elves in the forest tolerate scavengers at the
margins but not penetration to the interior.

### Agnostica

**Type:** Adventure site (location withheld)
**Overview:** A significant site in the Legacy of Horn
campaign. The general existence of a site called Agnostica
is known in Bloodmarch rumor. Its location, contents,
and significance are campaign spoilers.

See `campaigns/bloodmarch.md`.

## Geographic Features

### The Burn

A lava river flowing westward from the volcano Horn
until it crashes into Scorne Gorge. Forms the northern
border of Firestead. The Burn is impassable except at
the Grinagap bridge.

### Scorne Gorge

Wide gorge where the Burn lava river descends. The
collision of lava and the gorge creates intense heat
and sulfur fumes visible from a distance.

### Grinagap

The only natural bridge over the Burn. Held by dwarven
fighters during the worst Demon Flood attacks. It was
breach when the Tarkin clan sided with the demons,
enabling them to cross it. Now the bridge is held again
by the remaining dwarven clans.

### Helya

The only dwarven city in Firestead. Note: Firestead is
the region; Helya is the city within it. Mostly
inaccessible to outsiders. The Kogler's workshop is in
the lower levels of Helya.

### Mount Faltenvil

The specific volcanic mountain hosting Ashenstead temple.
Rises like a massive anvil; flat top with a horn pointing
east. Violent conflict is forbidden on the mountain and
in the village at its base. The mountain has three plateaus
(the Council Face, Horn Face, and Strike/Fire Face).
Climbing takes one or two Quarter Days. Not navigable
by horses — wingsteeds can make it. Called "Verga's Slope"
after a chieftain who had to crawl it as penance.

### Dunfalt Village

Settlement at the base of Mount Faltenvil. Monthly horse
market. Tent camps of Sabira and Quard riders around its
edges. Services include stables, veterinarians, blacksmiths,
and the Horn's Hammer inn.

### Guardian Temple

A field temple of Horn and Have built on a cliff
overlooking the central plains. A large stone horse statue
stands on the cliff top — this is Witherbeam, functional
but disguised. Inside: priest Valliman (Have), priestess
Insinera (Horn), a Motherpine (ancient blue pine
that exudes high-quality blue tar), a smithy, and a
simple marketplace. Young riders from multiple clans
come here for training and courtship.

### Lake Suna

Lake in the central Aslene interior near the Seindel
stream. Salterstay's fishing harbor is on its shore.

### Limestone Region

The lowlands below the eastern mountains near Oxengelder.
Horn's power does not extend to limestone terrain;
volcanic magic (Fire Memory, Mold Stone) does not work
there. Oxengelder is built on limestone — the Dreamstress
may have chosen this deliberately. The demon Krasylla,
if freed from the magma, also seeks limestone ground.

### Gåsbenen (Goose Bones)

Eight stone pillars that once flanked Gander's Pass,
each inhabited by one of the eight wisest Gander mage
guild members for their lifetimes. Several were destroyed
when the pass collapsed. Those that survive can still be
visited for counsel and prophecy; how much remains active
is unknown.

### Nuggler's Harrow

Dwarven mining districts in or near Vasnia. Contested
historically between Caprid dwarves and the Vasnians
who occupied the land above. The dwarves traded the
five Astra weapons to Sella specifically to obtain horse
clan military force to clear the Vasnians from Nuggler's
Harrow. The Book of Winds records this transaction.

### Reiben

The horse clan and Vasnian word for territories covered
by Crimson Forest — mainly the Havenmark borderlands
and surrounding demonic regions. Vaerefor van Reiben
takes his name from this term.
