# Bloodmarch — Artifacts

**Region:** Aslene (the Bloodmarch)
**Source:** Erik Granström, Free League Publishing
**Campaign:** Legacy of Horn

---

## Horn's Astra

Five sacred objects created at (or associated with) the
founding of the five horse clans. Together they are called
Horn's Astra — Astra is the Bloodmarch word for these
objects collectively.

Each Astra belongs to one of the five clans and is held by
that clan's leader or most trusted guardian. Each has been
with its clan since the beginning of recorded tradition;
in most cases the tradition extends back before any written
records.

When Blue Tar is smeared on an Astra, it glows briefly.
This is the public test of authenticity — it is how the
clans verify an Astra is genuine.

The true powers, provenance, and campaign significance of
the Astra are spoiler content. See `campaigns/bloodmarch.md`.

### Goblet Staff of Have

**Clan:** Quards
**Current Holder:** Jorgundos Ash, Quard chieftain
**Appearance:** A walking staff of dark wood, smoothed by
centuries of handling. The top is carved into a cup shape
that can hold liquid. The wood grain runs in an unusual
spiral pattern, as if it grew that way deliberately.
**Legend:** Said to have been given to the first Quard
chieftain by Have herself. The cup is described as never
going dry in stories, though in practice it is an ordinary
cup.

### Helm of Horn

**Clan:** Houns
**Current Holder:** Selligar Horne, Houn chieftain
**Appearance:** A full-face bronze helm with a moonstone
set into the center of the forehead. The moonstone is
approximately the size of a thumb. It glows faintly blue
in darkness. The helm shows no sign of age despite its
claimed antiquity.
**Legend:** Said to have been worn by Horn the god himself
before he became the volcano. The moonstone is why the
Houns describe themselves as "the people who see with
stone eyes."

### Sella's Dragonboot

**Clan:** Sabirians
**Current Holder:** Mommodar, Sabirian chieftain
**Appearance:** A single left boot. Armored, knee-high,
made from scaled hide of a creature no one has
satisfactorily identified. The scales are a dark bronze-
green. The boot fits any foot that wears it.
**Legend:** The right boot was lost. Most accounts say
it was destroyed in the Demon Flood. The Sabirians
do not publicly discuss why only one remains.

### Glasstooth

**Clan:** Caberians
**Current Holder:** Merdekai, Caberian chieftain
**Appearance:** A dagger blade of translucent crystal,
edge-sharp and harder than any known mineral. The hilt
is wrapped in aged leather. The crystal has no bubbles
or flaws — it appears grown rather than carved.
**Also called:** Serpent's Bane — a title that implies
specific power against serpentine creatures, though
the Caberians do not confirm or deny any specific
capability beyond "it cuts what needs cutting."

### Arrows of the Fire Wyrm

**Clan:** Galdanes
**Current Holder:** Contested within the Galdane clan
**Appearance:** Three arrows. The shafts are dark and
slightly warm. The arrowheads are a dull reddish metal —
not iron; something else. The fletching is fire wyrm
scale. Together the three arrows constitute a single
Astra. The set is never split.
**Legend:** Said to have been given by the fire wyrm
Erinya to the first Galdane who demonstrated sufficient
courage. The Galdanes alone can operate the arrows as
intended. (Whether this is literally true or simply
clan tradition is unverified.)

## The Stonechest of Horn

Not an Astra in the usual sense. A chest of stone that
appears to materialize when all five Astra are gathered
in one place. No clan possesses it — it arrives when
the condition is met. What it contains is the central
mystery of the Legacy of Horn campaign.

Public knowledge:
- The Stonechest is described in all five clan traditions
  as a promised object.
- Gathering the Astra is described as both a possibility
  and a warning.
- No living person in the Bloodmarch claims to have seen
  the Stonechest.

The true contents and what happens when it is opened are
campaign spoilers. See `campaigns/bloodmarch.md`.

## Witherbeam

A mechanical horse built by the Caprid dwarves. Considered
legendary by outsiders; the Caprids treat it as a known
construction, if an extraordinary one.

**Public knowledge:**
- Witherbeam is real.
- It runs on Blue Tar fuel.
- It is of Caprid manufacture — the craftsmanship is
  distinctive.
- It has been operational for longer than any living
  Caprid druid can verify from records. They believe it
  was built by masters who have since died.
- It moves faster than any living horse.
- It does not eat, tire, or get sick.
- It cannot be permanently destroyed — damage repairs
  over time through an internal mechanism involving heat.

The extent of Witherbeam's capabilities beyond these
publicly known facts is spoiler content. See
`campaigns/bloodmarch.md`.
