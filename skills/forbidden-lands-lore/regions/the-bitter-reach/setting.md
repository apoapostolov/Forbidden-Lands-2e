# Bitter Reach — Setting

**Region:** The Bitter Reach
**Source:** Magnus Seter, Free League Publishing
**Campaign:** Wake of the Winter King

---

## World State

The Bitter Reach is a lawless ice realm north of Ravenland.
The rule of the strong trumps all. No king, no law, no
standing army — only the harsh climate, buried elven
ruins, and the greed of treasure hunters who rarely
return.

The land was buried in eternal ice by Blaudewedd's curse
three thousand years ago and has never fully thawed.
The Morma Glacier recently began to melt — a consequence
of the Seal of Stars being broken. Cracks are opening in
the ice cap for the first time in millennia.

There is no Blood Mist here. The climate is what kept
the region isolated and unexplored, not demonic sealing.

## Geography

**Entry by sea:** Ships sail from northern Alderland or
from Far Vivend and Margelda in Ravenland, navigating
storms using rare magical weatherstones. A handful of
captains know the route. The king of Alderland watches
the departing ships with suspicion.

**Entry by land:** Two routes exist but neither is easy.
The Stillmist passes through elven-controlled dreamways —
non-elves cannot use it without an elven guide. The
Winding Ways are dwarven subterranean passages beneath
the Southern Range — only the oldest clan records know
which tunnels lead north.

The coast is black volcanic sand. Ships must navigate
around icebergs to reach landable beaches. From the
coast, land rises westward across tundra, glacier, and
eventually the impassable White Peaks.

## Regions

### The Silver Coast

Easternmost strip. Populated by settlers, fishermen,
and treasure hunters. Fishing villages harvest cod.
Hunters take seals, Nanuiks, and Pikebeasts from the
ocean. Named with false optimism by the first settlers.
Black volcanic beaches. Northfall is the largest
settlement here.

### Fallowmoor

West of the coast. Flat icy tundra, rough winds, thin
snowpack. Petrified tree groves. Older ruins already
picked over by early treasure hunters. A small goblin
clan lives here, feuding with wolfkin over hunting
grounds.

### The Glacial Sea

Northern border. Endless frozen ocean. Fishermen work
the open water. Hunters follow ice floes. Legends speak
of icebergs large enough to carry ancient elven ruins.

### The Morma Glacier

Enormous ice cap west of Fallowmoor. Deep crevices
crisscross it. Hot springs dot the surface from volcanic
activity mixed with ancient magic. The glacier has
started to melt — crevices opened in recent years lead
down to the kingdom beneath the ice. The rivers Ised
and Keld flow from within it eastward to the coast.

### The Barren Hills

Low mountain range piercing the glacier, west of
Fallowmoor. Ancient ruins, most not yet explored.
The three orc clans guard these mountains from all
intruders. The grip of the ice hardens here. Life
still clings to existence despite the never-ending
winter.

### Icemoor

Beyond the Barren Hills. Ice cap continues for a frozen
plateau, hundreds of fathoms thick. Few adventurers
who have come this far west ever returned alive.

### The White Peaks

Immense peaks where the wind howls and snow falls
eternally. No living being has managed to climb them.
The largest strongholds of the lost elven civilization
were built here. No one knows what lies beyond.

### Ised and Keld

Two rivers flowing from the Barren Hills down through
Fallowmoor to the coast. They run partially under the
glacier. Ancient artifacts occasionally wash down to
the coast. Adventure hunters pan the cold streams for
treasure. The rivers can be followed upstream under
the ice to find undiscovered ruins.

### The Southern Range

Impassable mountain range forming the border with
Ravenland. Peaks reach through the clouds. Only two
known passages: the elven Stillmist and the dwarven
Winding Ways.

## Climate and Cold

The cold is a constant, lethal threat — not a background
condition. There is no warmth that can be assumed.
Settlements are small and cramped. Everyone is in some
way dependent on someone else to survive.

**Adequate protection when hiking, foraging, hunting,
fishing, making camp, or exploring:** warm clothes
and shoes are sufficient.

**Adequate protection when resting or sleeping:**
requires a fire or other heat source in addition.

**Grease:** smearing with blubber-based grease gives
+2 to Endurance rolls against cold. One dose lasts a
day. The stench gives −2 to Manipulation rolls against
newcomers. Crafting one dose requires one unit of Blubber
or Tallow, a knife, fire, and a cauldron. Supply: Common.
Cost: 1 silver.

## Weather

Each morning roll D6 three times: once each for wind,
snowfall, and cold. Combine results to set conditions.
Weather can shift mid-session from a mishap or at
the GM's discretion.

**Wind:** 1–3 Light Breeze (no effect); 4–5 Strong Wind
(+1 to cold roll, −1 to Make Camp); 6 Storm (+2 to cold
roll, −2 to Make Camp, all PCs must succeed at Endurance
or cannot Hike for a Quarter Day).

**Snowfall:** 1–3 None; 4–5 Light Flurry (−1 to Lead
the Way); 6 Heavy Snowfall (−2 to Lead the Way, all
PCs must succeed at Endurance or cannot Hike, −2 if
there is also a Storm).

**Cold:** 1–3 Cold (Endurance roll each Quarter Day
without adequate protection); 4–5 Biting (roll every
Quarter Hour); 6+ To the Bone (even protected PCs roll
every Quarter Day, unprotected every Quarter Hour at −2).

## Searching for Fuel

Firewood is scarce. Primary fuels are peat (coastal) and
dried animal droppings (reindeer, waddlerbirds). A PC can
spend a Quarter Day Searching for Fuel using the same rules
as Foraging. A successful roll yields units of Fuel equal
to successes. Each unit increases the Torches Resource Die
one step.

## Beneath the Ice

Under Ice Cap hexes lies a complex tunnel system from the
winter elf kingdom. It is always dark below. No weather
affects the traveler — wind and snowfall are absent and
cold is always a steady 1–3. Passage types range from
narrow holes and crevices to vast Elven Ruin chambers
that function as full adventure sites.

Exploration takes a Quarter Day and requires a Lead the
Way roll. Success allows rolling on the Treasures Beneath
the Ice table. Failure causes a mishap from the Beneath
the Ice table.

## Magic Traditions

### Elemental Magic

Created by the winter elves of Rodenvale. Outlawed by
Blaudewedd and rare today, though some sorcerers outside
the Bitter Reach are rumored to know it. Manipulates the
four basic elements: earth, wind, water, and fire.

Elemental Magic requires an ingredient to cast — the
relevant element must be physically present. The ingredient
does not grant the normal ingredient bonus; it is a
hard requirement. Spells range from Combustion and Sunder
(rank 1) to Flood Wave and Summon Elemental (rank 3–4).

Key spells: Sunder (splits any non-magical object),
Combustion (ignites non-living objects), Suffocate (pulls
air from lungs), Rock Storm, Water Breathing, Flight,
Heat of the Moment, Parch, Tornado, Fireball, Flood Wave,
Stoneskin, Summon Elemental (Fire, Water, Wind, or Earth).

The orcs of the Bitter Reach are the most skilled
practitioners, particularly the Frostwind Clan — some
are even rumored to know Elemental Magic, a closely
guarded secret even among the orcs.

### Ice Affinity

The druid discipline of the Bitter Reach. Druids here
often devote themselves to it, drawing magical energy
from the frozen land itself. Ice Affinity uses a piece
of ice or a fistful of snow as ingredient — easily found
in the Bitter Reach but hard to come by in warmer lands.
Unlike Elemental Magic, the ingredient is not required;
it only enhances.

Spells range from Cold Snap and Frost Walker (rank 1) to
Blizzard, Icicle Spray, and Icy Breath (rank 3).

Key spells: Heart of Ice (turns victim's heart to ice,
damages Wits), Cold Snap (zone-wide freezing), Snow Cave
(creates magical shelter), Frost Walker (immunity to cold
effects), Sword of Ice, Shield of Ice, Blizzard, Icicle
Spray, Icy Breath (freezes a victim in place).

## Champion Profession

The Champion is a new profession available in the Bitter
Reach (and in Ravenland). Champions have sworn allegiance
to a higher power — a god, demon, person, or place.
They draw power from their oaths and dedication.

Key Attribute: Strength. Skills: Melee, Might, Move,
Endurance, Survival.

Three profession talents: Path of Protection (tackle
opponents, intercept attacks), Path of Fate (foresee
enemy moves, avoid damage through fate), Path of the
Holy Vow (artifact dice from acts of devotion).

Champions are recognized in the Bitter Reach as respected
warriors who have bound themselves to a cause.

## Regional Talents

Three new general talents reflect life in the Bitter Reach:

**Mountaineer** — enhanced climbing and ability to assist
others on difficult terrain.

**Warm-blooded** — rolls half as often against Cold.
At rank 3, can keep a companion warm through physical
contact.

**Harpooner** — attack bonus with harpoon and throwing
spear. At rank 2, hits cause bleeding. At rank 3, adds
D8 Artifact Die to ranged harpoon attacks.
