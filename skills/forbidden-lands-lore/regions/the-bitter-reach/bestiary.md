## Dire Wolf

Twice the size of a normal wolf. White fur, thick and
warm, valuable as a pelt — but only the reckless hunt
one. Dire Wolves hunt in large packs, relying on both
fury and cunning. Reindeer are the preferred prey.
Large packs have taken down Nanuiks and groups of
treasure hunters.

Dire Wolves avoid larger settlements when prey is
available, but hunger will push them toward a village.
Only orcs and wolfkin have successfully tamed them,
using them as settlement guards.

## Frost Dragon

When Ferenblaud ruled Rodenvale, he tamed the Frost
Dragon Scrana — said to be the sister of Scarne, the
Dragon of Ravenland. Scrana became his mount and her
offspring served in the winter elf armies. When the
curse fell, the surviving Frost Dragons retreated into
deep mountain caves to nurse their wounds and brood
over forgotten treasure hoards, dreaming of Rodenvale
rising again.

Frost Dragons are more cunning than their southern
cousins — only the strongest survived the civil war.
Their powers are bound to cold and ice rather than
fire. They harbor a deep hatred of summer elves and
the humans who now intrude in their land. They are
neutral toward orcs, who were servants of the winter
elves.

Scrana still lives. She is imprisoned in the Palace
of the Winter King with Ferenblaud. Should he awaken
fully, she rides with him.

**Special:** Cold Aura — all opponents within arm's
length must make an Endurance roll each round or
become Cold. Double damage from fire (Cold Heart).
Weak Spot revealed by a Scouting roll.

## Ice Giant

Created by Blaudewedd from one thousand statues of
snow, ice, and earth — each given the spark of life
from the elements themselves. Their sworn task was
to guard the five Seals of the curse. After three
millennia, only a few dozen remain.

Each Ice Giant is unique in appearance but shares a
roughly humanoid shape, more than three times human
height and twice as wide. Most have stone teeth and
red glowing eyes. They wear armor forged from ice
at the moment of their creation and carry great clubs.

Ice Giants are taciturn beings given to long
philosophical contemplation. They possess excellent
sight and spend much of their time secretly observing
the Bitter Reach. All are skilled sculptors — they
document what they see in ice sculptures, left on
the Morma Glacier and mountaintops across the region.

**Legend fragment:** *In the mountains live the giants,
shepherds who herd flocks of wind and snow. They watch
over the White Peaks and herd the clouds that gather
around the mountaintops, so that they don't spill
their rage on settled lands. But it happens that a
blizzard escapes the grasp of the giants, for they
are often busy bickering among themselves.*

King Bele, the eldest and largest, meditates on
whether the ancient oath still holds meaning. His
advisors Bor and Rym counsel him on the newcomers.
The demon Wurda visits the palace to sow discord
among his court.

## Nanuik

Enormous polar bears. Name derives from the elven
word for "raging beast." Bred by the winter elves
as war machines capable of carrying riders or fighting
independently. When the curse toppled Ferenblaud's
kingdom, the Nanuiks went feral.

Nanuik fur is the finest status symbol in the Bitter
Reach. It is also one of the most dangerous trophies
to acquire. The Nanuik builds to furious rage before
attacking — it doesn't rush in cold-blooded and
calculating. By the time it moves, it has committed
to total destruction.

**Legend fragment:** *The Nanuik has inhabited the
Bitter Reach for thousands of years. It is the most
noble of all creatures that wander the cold wastes
and truly the king of the north.*

## Pikebeast

The most dangerous creature of the glacial sea.
Roughly the size of a fishing boat. At the front
of its armored skull sits one or two great horns
used in combat with others of its kind.

The Pikebeast hunts by smell. It is known for
ramming boats from the side to sink them, then
feeding on whoever goes into the water. Legends
say it is yet another monstrosity loosened from
Rodenvale when Ferenblaud's kingdom fell.

**Resources:** A killed Pikebeast yields D6 BLUBBER,
2D6 MEAT, 2D6 PELTS. Extracting resources takes a
Quarter Day. BLUBBER can substitute for TALLOW.

## Stone Guardians

Statues given life through winter elf magic.
Soulless constructs programmed to follow standing
orders. Used as guards in locations where living
beings could not survive or be trusted. Heavily
armored by their stone bodies. Still animate in
ruins; triggered by intruders crossing into their
defined territory.

## Stone Ravens

Created by dark elf magic: shards of meteorite
stone — star metal — infused with animated life.
Assigned to guard temples and burial sites.

At rest they appear as piles of black rubble.
When intruders enter their territory, the shards
spring into the air and form attacking ravens with
sharp beaks and wings. The magic that animated
them is still potent enough to sustain them in
ruins abandoned for thousands of years.

**Special:** Flock rule — a Stone Raven flock counts
as a single monster. A single attack can never deal
more than one point of damage to the flock.

## Wendigo

Born from a murderer who dies in the Bitter Reach
filled with hatred and greed. The rage of the
sleeping winter elves — their fury at the curse
that shackled them — animates the dead body and
twists it into something that should not walk.

The worse the murderer's crimes in life, the greater
the risk of transformation. Not all murderers who die
here become wendigos — only those whose hearts were
rotten enough.

Physical form: roughly humanoid, arms twice the normal
length, claw hands. Skin gray, dry, stretched tight
across bones. Sunken yellow eyes. Raw, bloody lips.
A crown of razor-sharp reindeer antlers. Always
surrounded by a cold wind and the stench of an open
grave — victims often smell it before they see it.

The wendigo wants to devour and consume everything
alive. It is relentless once it has a victim in sight,
but loses interest quickly if the prey escapes beyond
immediate reach.
