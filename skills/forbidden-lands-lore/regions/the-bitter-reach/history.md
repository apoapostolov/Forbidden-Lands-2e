# Bitter Reach — History

**Region:** The Bitter Reach
**Source:** Magnus Seter, Free League Publishing
**Campaign:** Wake of the Winter King

---

## The Kingdom of Rodenvale

Three millennia ago the elves arrived in this world in a rain
of shooting stars — children of the Red Wanderer, sent to
lead all life to a higher purpose. They emerged from fire
and smoke and subdued the dwarves, goblins, trolls, giants,
and other kin already living in these northern lands. There
they founded a vast kingdom called Rodenvale, home of the
elves north of what would later become Ravenland.

Rodenvale endured for thousands of years. The Winter King
Ferenblaud ruled over all he laid eyes on, enslaving all
kin the elves encountered as they expanded. The principal
slaves were orcs — believed to have been created as a slave
race by Ferenblaud's own sorcery. The orcs served both as
manual labor and as shock troops in elven armies.

Ferenblaud was foremost among elemental sorcerers. He
drove his scholars to study the moon, the sun, and the
stars, and to seek contact with beings from other
dimensions. Their experiments yielded immense knowledge
but regularly ended in disaster as entities from other
dimensions broke into the world, causing great devastation.

Something that answered their call from the stars is said to
have buried itself beneath Rodenvale, where its energies
poisoned the soil and clouded Ferenblaud's mind until his
own kin turned against him.

## The Civil War and the Curse

A divide grew slowly between those elves who viewed
dominion over all other kin as their natural right, and
those who saw only ruin in that ambition. The divide
widened until civil war split Rodenvale.

Ferenblaud branded his opponents traitors and had them
hunted and chained. Against him stood Blaudewedd of the
First — wisest of the elves, mightiest of all druids. Her
sisters and brothers fell in scores.

The battles were many and grim. In the end Blaudewedd
cast a powerful curse that buried the realm in eternal ice
and froze Ferenblaud to his throne. Terrible storms swept
across the land. Icy shackles fettered him and his subjects
where they stood. All they could do was curse Blaudewedd's
name before they fell into magical torpor.

Those hundreds who survived were trapped by the ice and
fell into deep slumber, kept alive by the magical energies,
dreaming horrible nightmares that would never end.
Ferenblaud's followers became known as the winter elves.

Gazing out over all the suffering the war had wrought,
Blaudewedd was filled with regret and shame. She wandered
south to Ravenland and swore never again to let the elves
rule over the world. She created the Stillmist as the
final haven of her kin — dominance and arrogance
replaced by serenity and observation.

## The Five Seals

Before leaving, Blaudewedd fortified her icy spell with
five magical Seals. Each Seal was imbued with the power
of a natural element and placed in a different location
across the realm. Protecting each Seal was a Guardian —
dragons and other monsters that once served Ferenblaud,
now under Blaudewedd's control.

The five Seals and their Guardians:

| Seal | Location | Guardian |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seal of Stars | Tower of the Farseers | Mul |
| Seal of Fire | The Field of Swords | Gibil |
| Seal of Earth | Court of the Ice Giants | Ninhursag |
| Seal of Wind | Frostwind Hold | Imdugud |
| Seal of Water | The Sunken City | Abzu |

In the centuries that followed, Ferenblaud's kingdom
became known as the Bitter Reach. Most inhabitants
succumbed to the terrible new conditions. Some kin and
creatures adapted and survived. In legend, Ferenblaud
became the Winter King.

## The Ice Giants

Blaudewedd shaped one thousand statues of ice and stone
and infused them with the life force of the elements. These
giant beings swore to guard the fettered winter elves. Over
three millennia their numbers dwindled to a few dozen. Now
they guard their palace in deep contemplation, questioning
whether their ancient oath still binds them and what
purpose remains in their existence.

## Arrival of New Kin

Dwarves came to the Bitter Reach as prisoners of war
during Ferenblaud's reign. Some — the Forgethralls —
were enslaved as weaponsmiths in the elven forges and
survived the curse deep beneath the ice.

Orcs survived the curse in the Barren Hills. When they
emerged they swore never again to serve another kin and
have guarded those mountains from intruders since.

Wolfkin crossed from Ravenland centuries later seeking new
hunting grounds. Humans arrived only a few hundred years
ago, and only in significant numbers within the last few
decades — lured by rumors of buried elven treasures.

## The Breaking of the First Seal

The Seal of Stars has recently been broken by adventurers
deceived by the demon Wurda. The breaking triggered a
rain of shooting stars over the ocean and caused the
Morma Glacier to begin melting. This is the opening event
of the Wake of the Winter King campaign.

The breaking has awakened isolated winter elves from
their magical slumber. Some lose their minds and wander
the wastes as vengeful spirits. Those of sterner stuff
seek the Palace of the Winter King.

## Key Dates

- **3000+ years ago:** Ferenblaud founds Rodenvale
- **~3000 years ago:** Civil war. Blaudewedd's curse falls. Kingdom buried in ice.
- **~3000 years ago:** Blaudewedd creates the Ice Giants, places the Five Seals, departs south.
- **Centuries ago:** Wolfkin cross from Ravenland to the Bitter Reach.
- **Few centuries ago:** First humans arrive on the Silver Coast.
- **Recent decades:** Treasure hunters flood in from Alderland and Ravenland.
- **Campaign start:** Seal of Stars broken. Glacier melts. Campaign begins.
