# Bitter Reach — Artifacts

**Region:** The Bitter Reach
**Source:** Magnus Seter, Free League Publishing
**Campaign:** Wake of the Winter King

---

## Overview

Artifacts are the primary prize drawing treasure hunters
to the Bitter Reach. They fall into two types:
non-magical artifacts and named magical artifacts.
Adventuring in the Bitter Reach is largely structured
around finding these items.

Named magical artifacts have playing card versions
in the Maps & Stickers Pack.

## Non-magical Artifacts

### Weapons

Forged from star metal — metal taken from meteorites
fallen from the sky. Heavier than ordinary weapons
but give +1 damage. A light weapon weighs as much
as a normal weapon; a normal weapon weighs as much
as a heavy one. Found as shortswords and daggers most
often.

### Armor

Made from star metal and woven spider silk from cave
spiders beneath the ice cap. Weigh half as much as
ordinary armor. Otherwise no special properties.

### Jewelry

Silver or star metal, set with diamonds, sapphires,
and white opals. Designs inspired by the night sky
and ancient legends. Rings and bracelets are most
common. Necklaces, earrings, and headpieces are rarer.
Value: D6 gold.

### Statuettes

Silver and marble figures depicting heroic scenes from
winter elf legends — warriors, strange trees and
flowers, hideous monsters. Highly sought by Alderlander
collectors. Value: 5D6 silver.

### Coins

Ancient silver coins from the Bitter Reach can be
used as regular currency. One elven coin equals one
common silver coin. Collectors may pay up to five
times more for rare specimens. Roll D6: 1–5, unremarkable;
6, rare coin worth substantial more to collectors.

### Sigil

Clay tablets or stone-carved seals bearing a weak
magical aura from the last civil war battles. Used
as protective wards against evil spirits both in the
Bitter Reach and as decorative art in Alderland.
Worth up to 3D6 silver to the right buyer.

## Named Magical Artifacts

### Assare's Harpoon

A wolfkin woman, Assare, was the greatest hunter to
have sailed the northern ocean. Her harpoon was carved
from a single piece of bone from an unknown monster
she slew as a young adventurer — the shaft etched
with scenes from her life. She vanished one night in
a fierce storm while hunting the sea serpent Logrim.
The harpoon got stuck in Logrim's armored shell and
is lodged there still.

**Properties:** Grip 1H. Gear Bonus +2. Damage 2.
Range Long. Artifact Die D8. Light, Pointed, Harpoon.
If the harpoon deals damage it lodges in the prey.
The thrower can attempt to reel in a lodged prey with
an opposed Might roll. The prey is pulled to arm's
length if the thrower wins. Each round the harpoon
remains lodged, the prey takes one point of damage
that cannot be Dodged, Parried, or absorbed by armor.

**Drawback:** At sunrise each day spent on the ocean,
roll D6 to see if sea monsters are drawn by the
harpoon's supernatural call: 1–2 nothing, 3 Pikebeast,
4 Giant Squid, 5 Sea Serpent, 6 Logrim (Strength 26).

### Bjarke's Warming Flagon

A small clay jug, barely bigger than two closed fists,
made three hundred years ago by the dwarf Bjarke —
brother of Ramman (see Ramman's Thundering Drum).
Bjarke wandered the Bitter Reach hunting undead who
could not find rest. He eventually fell to a Death
Knight and now wanders as a spirit himself. His body
lies in a ruin at the feet of the Death Knight who
slew him. The flagon is there with him.

Bjarke's spirit can be encountered and will lead PCs
to the ruin. He asks only that someone bury him and
swear to continue his mission. He can find peace only
if that oath is kept.

**Properties:** When shaken under a starlit night
sky — or when stars shine on the snow above — the
flagon glows from within and provides warmth equivalent
to a small campfire for a Quarter Day. This works
indoors if stars shine on the snow outside.

### Bur's Boots

Bur was the foremost scout among the Ice Giants,
capable of reaching terrain that no other creature
could cross. His enchanted boots let him stride across
sinksnow, leap bottomless crevices, and dance on the
surface of the ocean. One day he disappeared without
a trace. The boots were lost with him.

The boots adjust size to whoever wears them. They
are knee-high, thick-soled, fur-lined, and treated
with whale oil. They count as clothing and do not
encumber the wearer. A side effect: the wearer becomes
an exceptional dancer.

**Properties:** +2 Gear Bonus and D8 Artifact Die to
Lead the Way rolls. +2 Gear Bonus and D8 Artifact Die
to Endurance rolls against cold. Wearer can spend 1 WP
to walk on water for a Quarter Hour per WP spent.

### Lerge's Hammer

A Redrunner, Lerge, was one of Blaudewedd's most
skilled infiltrators. No wall or mountain could stop
her. She was eventually betrayed by the fallen
Redrunner Iarann and killed by his cursed arrow.
The silver hammer remains where she fell — possibly
lodged in an ice wall above a crevice, or in an
outlook tower of the winter elves.

The hammer is unassuming: a small two-headed silver
hammer with one flat face and one pointed face. When
it strikes, the blows land softly and quietly, as
if falling on padded leather.

**Properties:** Functions as a warhammer with D8
Artifact Die. Also gives +2 Gear Bonus and D8 Artifact
Die to Move rolls when climbing.

### Wings of Marudak

Marudak was Ferenblaud's most feared servant — he
soared ghost-like over enslaved lands and executed
those who displeased the king. He was eventually
killed when he flew too close to the nest of a monster
that took him for prey. Many breathed easier when
the news arrived. The Wings remained in that nest.

A pair of gloves sewn from black leather cut from
a Wyvern's belly, stitched with silver thread in
patterns of Gryphons and Dragons. The nest may now
belong to a Wyvern, Gryphon, or Frost Dragon.

**Properties:** +2 Gear Bonus and D10 Artifact Die
to Move rolls when jumping. Wearer can spend 1 WP
to glide for a Quarter Hour per WP spent.

**Drawback:** When gliding, the wearer risks being
consumed by euphoria. If they want to land before
the Quarter Hour expires, they must succeed at a
Wits roll. Failure means they cannot land that round.
If the effect expires before landing, the wearer
falls from a height of 2D6 meters.

### Namtarel's Black Arrow

Namtarel was Ferenblaud's executioner. His black
arrow killed Cûnbel, one of the greatest of
Blaudewedd's Redrunners, during the final battle.
Consumed by grief, Blaudewedd struck Namtarel down —
but too late. That was the moment she decided the
war had to end, whatever the cost.

The arrow is long. The shaft is made from an unknown
black material. The tip is star metal, always coated
with a black oily film of poison. The fletching came
from a black Gryphon Namtarel hunted and killed himself.
Believed to rest in Cûnbel's barrow — likely near
Namtarel's own resting place at Bleakness Keep.

**Properties:** If the shooter hates their target
with fervent passion, gain D12 Artifact Die to the
attack. Damage also poisons the target (Potency 9,
lethal). Spend 1 WP to have the arrow return after
firing.

**Drawback:** Poisoned meat from the arrow is not
suitable food — but could be used as Potency 6
poison against an enemy.

### Ramman's Thundering Drum

The dwarven druid Ramman — brother of Bjarke —
spent his life searching for the songs of wind and
winter, wandering deep caves and tall mountains with
drum and flute. Monsters that threatened him were
answered with thunder. His drum is now in a
settlement somewhere in the Bitter Reach, sitting
under dust with a peddler who has no idea what
it is.

A shallow drum of Frost Dragon skin stretched over
a frame of whale bones. Clouds and thunderbolts are
painted on the leather. Hold your ear to it while
thrumming with a finger and you hear the distant
rumble of a storm.

**Properties:** Triggers an Earthquake spell effect
at Power Level 3. Can be used once per Quarter Day.
Spend WP and roll for mishap as normal.

**Restriction:** Usable outdoors only, and only when
the weather is cloudy or stormy.

### Ferenblaud's Star

Ferenblaud wore this silver amulet to spy on his
enemies from across his domain. Blaudewedd tore it
from his neck in the final battle before he fled
to his palace. She threw it to the ground in contempt
and left it there. It has lain beneath the snow
since — until now. It may have surfaced in a crack
in the ice or washed down a river to the coast.

A silver star set with a clear ice crystal. Hangs
from a thin iron chain. Look into the crystal and
see visions of distant lands in ages past.

**Properties:** Functions as the Farsight spell.
Spend WP and roll for mishap as normal.

**Drawback:** Ferenblaud can see and hear the wearer
if they stand under clear skies. If the wearer looks
into the star, they briefly meet the gaze of the
Winter King. When the artifact's power is used,
Ferenblaud sees what the wearer sees.

### Tarik's Mysterious Cap

Tarik was a bard and agitator in Ferenblaud's kingdom.
Popular among the common kin, untouchable despite the
king's hatred — until Tarik secretly led a movement
to unseat Ferenblaud and Marudak was dispatched to
kill him. Tarik escaped. His followers fell into the
curse's slumber along with everyone else. Fleeing orcs
stole the cap in the chaos. It may now be found in
an orc village.

A garish cap made of red and blue fabric with silver
leaves and bells. Large earflaps keep the wearer warm
and block sudden sounds.

**Properties:** Spend 1 WP to activate — then roll D6
to determine the effect: 1 Serenity (PL1), 2 Animal
Speech (PL1), 3 Intuition (PL1), 4 Telepathy (PL1),
5 True Sight (PL1), 6 Invisibility for a Turn. Roll
for mishap as normal.

**Drawback:** When the effect ends, roll D6: 1–4 nothing,
5 the wearer is affected by Paralyze at PL2,
6 Paralyze at PL3.
