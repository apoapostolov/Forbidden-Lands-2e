## Quality Standards

### Description expansion (all entries)

- 3–5 paragraphs, 150–350 words total
- Order: physical appearance → habitat/ecology → hunting behavior → setting
  integration → melancholy register
- Physical before atmospheric. Small-scale before epic.
- No "they are not X, they are Y" split-negation lines
- No "it is the [abstract] of [abstract]" opener constructions
- No em-dash overuse (max 1–2 per description)
- No rule-of-three padding
- No standalone gravitas sentences
- No named emotional states in vignettes
- Voice: harsh, practical, oral-culture patterned

### Random encounters (02A creatures + humanoids)

- 2 per entry, written from scratch (these entries have none currently)
- Each encounter has:
  - An italicized physical-scene epigraph — shows a moment, not a summary
  - Moral dilemma prose — no clean kill option
  - Terrain type tags line
- Avoid: "monster is hungry" as the only hook, obvious kill-and-move-on
  structures, adventure hooks that are just "the monster did a thing"
- Model quality: match the 02B encounter standard (Gallows Hill, Reed Bride,
  Oracle Well, etc.)

### What NOT to touch

- RESOURCES blocks — do not modify
- Lore Roll tables — do not modify
- Monster Attacks tables — do not modify
- Statblock bullets — do not modify
- "What they carry is what is left when they fall." lines (humanoids) — keep
- Existing encounters in 02B monsters — keep (they are already strong)
