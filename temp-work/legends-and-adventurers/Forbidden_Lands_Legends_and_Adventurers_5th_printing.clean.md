## Legends & Adventurers

### Life Generator

This section lets you flesh out your player characters and give them unique backstories with a few dice rolls.

### Reference Guide for Rolling Up an Adventurer

1. Roll or choose on the table for your kin (page 3). Write down the kin talent for your kin.

2. Roll or choose on the table for your home region (page 4).

3. Roll once on the table for your childhood for your kin (pages 5–14). Write down your attributes based on the roll. Write down your skills based on the roll.

4. Roll or choose on the table for profession (page 15).

5. Roll or choose on the table for profession talent (page 15).

6. Roll once on the table for formative events for your profession (page 16–23). Write down skills you acquire based on the roll. Write down the talent you acquire based on the roll. Write down any gear you acquire based on the roll.

7. You are now Young. You can choose whether you want to start the game Young (go to step 10), or if you want to roll once more for Formative events and  become Adult (repeat step 6, then go to step 8). For Elves, progressing to step 8 is mandatory.

8. You are now Adult. Lower an attribute of your choosing one point. You can choose whether you want to start the game as an Adult (go to step 10) or if you want to roll once more for Formative events and become Old (repeat step 6, then go to step 9). Elves must go directly to step 10.

9. You are now Old. Lower an attribute of your choosing by one point. No attribute can go to zero. You must now start the game, by going to step 10.

10. You may redistribute one attribute point of your choice, up to a maximum of 6 in the key attribute of your kin or profession.

11. Roll once on the table How Did You Meet? on pages 24–26 to determine how you met the other adventurers in the group. Joined together, these events will form a  narrative of how your group was formed. One player doesn’t roll.

12. (Optional) Roll a D3 for Pride, Dark Secret, and relationships with other adventurers. Use the examples in the profession descriptions and count from the top.

13. Start the game with silver and Resource Dice according to your profession description.

With the help of the following tables you can easily and quickly roll up an adventurer, complete with childhood and formative events. This alternate method of creating your adventurer is completely optional, and something that you as a group should decide amongst yourselves if you are going to use or not.

If you do, these rules fully replace the normal character generation rules, except when indicated otherwise below.

| 21–22 | **All Night Bender.** You do not remember much of the evening, only that there was a proper celebration. You have been friends since. |
| 23–24 | **Searched for an Artifact.** You searched long and hard for the treasure, traveled across the land, and braved dangers. But just when you were about to get your hands on the artifact, it turned out that some other adventurers had the same plan. | 25–26 | **Saved by Adventurers.** You were in trouble. Maybe you had been captured by the Iron Guard, maybe you had been caught by slave traders. But fate had other plans, and you were dramatically saved by some other adventurers at the last moment. |

| 41–42 | **Fought a Bloody Battle.** You fought in a battle where many lives were lost. It was a difficult fight.
