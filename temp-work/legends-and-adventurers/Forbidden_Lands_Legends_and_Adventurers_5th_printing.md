## REFERENCE GUIDE FOR ROLLING UP AN ADVENTURER

1. Roll or choose on the table for your kin (page 3). Write down the kin talent (page 33 in the _Player’s Handbook_ ) for your kin.

2. Roll or choose on the table for your home region (page 4).

3. Roll once on the table for your childhood for your kin (pages 5–14). Write down your attributes based on the roll. Write down your skills based on the roll.

4. Roll or choose on the table for profession (page 15).

5. Roll or choose on the table for profession talent (page 15).

6. Roll once on the table for formative events for your profession (page 16–23). Write down skills you acquire based on the roll. Write down the talent you acquire based on the roll. Write down any gear you acquire based on the roll.

7. You are now Young. You can choose whether you want to start the game Young (go to step 10), or if you want to roll once more for Formative events and  become Adult (repeat step 6, then go to step 8). For Elves, progressing to step 8 is mandatory.

8. You are now Adult. Lower an attribute of your choosing one point. You can choose whether you want to start the game as an Adult (go to step 10) or if you want to roll once more for Formative events and become Old (repeat step 6, then go to step 9). Elves must go directly to step 10.

9. You are now Old. Lower an attribute of your choosing by one point. No attribute can go to zero. You must now start the game, by going to step 10.

10. You may redistribute one attribute point of your choice, up to a maximum of - 6 in the key attribute of your kin or profession (see the _Player’s Handbook_ ).

11. Roll once on the table How Did You Meet? on pages 24–26 to determine how you met the other adventurers in the group. Joined together, these events will form a  narrative of how your group was formed. One player doesn’t roll.

12. (Optional) Roll a D3 for Pride, Dark Secret, and relationships with other adventurers. Use the examples in the descriptions of professions in Chapter 2 in the _Player’s Handbook_ and count from the top.

13. Start the game with silver and Resource Dice according to your profession description in Chapter 2 of the _Player’s Handbook_ .
