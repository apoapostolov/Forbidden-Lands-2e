## Introduction

> *"Magic is the golden thread that weaves all things together and conducts all the energy that life needs to exist," explained Master Elrinor. "Nothing exists without magic and magic does not exist without matter. They are complementary, since reality is composed in the same way by the sum of its parts."*

Welcome to **Spells & Sorcerers**, a new supplement from Xiphos Games Studio for **Forbidden Lands**. In this book you will find new rules and a large number of spells to vary the way you and your players use magic in your adventures.

Now demologists will be able to experiment on the flesh and souls of mortals, as we know is frequently done in Vond. Necromancers will be able to raise their hordes or create powerful Death Knights. Zertorme will be able to learn Fire magic as a separate discipline from the other elements.

We decided to make this book because there were certain issues associated with sorcery in the game that did not seem clear enough to us despite its simplicity. They also did not fully convince us as an adaptation of arcane abilities even in the context of the Forbidden Lands system.

In conclusion, there is much more to choose from and explore in the development of a sorcerer, as well as a greater integration of the rules of magic with the background of what we know of the Forbidden Lands.

Additionally, we offer a significant expansion of the official magical disciplines. We have practically redesigned the magical disciplines from scratch. We think it is better to have a single source of spells than to jump from book to book, and the new rules we are proposing make it worthwhile to revise what came before.

Magic now has more ways of manifesting itself and is more consistent with the published material, offering more options to the characters and reflecting what we know is possible in the Forbidden Lands.

## New Rules

> *When Iolvor drew the last line of the symbol in the air and the fallen branch flew rapidly in his direction, as if shot from a bow, he could not contain his smile. It was his first victory, small, but a victory nonetheless. Although it seemed impossible, reality responded to the powers of the symbols hidden in nature.*

This chapter presents and develops all the new rules we want to introduce to magic. The basis remains the same as in the Player's Handbook, but there are substantial changes that need to be explained. If a rule is not specified, it means that it is not changed from its original version.

We reflect this in two sections related to the disciplines of sorcery: **rarity** and **secretism**. Both are broad classifications that serve as a minimum guide when narratively managing the appearance of a sorcerer who knows the discipline, as well as how difficult it is to find a teacher.

## The Magic

**Rarity:** Represents the frequency, or how easy it is, for the people of the Forbidden Lands to see a person use a spell of the discipline, taking into account that magic itself is not common to witness. A discipline that does not alarm most people when used is called **known**, because most people know of its existence and assume it is possible that they will come across a sorcerer who uses it. In the **strange** category are the disciplines that are very striking for ordinary people because of how infrequent they are in their lives, but for adventurers who travel the world and face all kinds of dangers, they are not surprising. The **disturbing** category is for disciplines that would alarm ordinary people and surprise even experienced adventurers. Finally, there is the category of **prohibited**, disciplines that are simply not visible to ordinary people or whose effects might not even be understood as magic.

**Secretism:** This is the difficulty of finding a teacher willing to teach the magic talent that gives access to the discipline, or alternatively, the magnitude of what a teacher would ask in exchange to teach those secrets to another sorcerer. In the **initiation** category are disciplines whose teachers teach their secrets to initiates they adopt for various reasons: helping people or nature, finding enlightenment, or simply spreading knowledge. The future apprentice must demonstrate that they are worthy of that knowledge, but for this a good talk may be enough. Within this category are almost all druidic disciplines and very few sorcerous ones. The **demonstration** category includes masters who require a test to accept an apprentice. The **journey** disciplines involve the aspirant agreeing to travel part of the Forbidden Lands and complete a whole series of adventures to find the master or satisfy their request before they are willing to teach.

## Learning Magic Talents

Achieving the first level in a magic talent means understanding the fundamentals of a discipline of sorcery, learning to manipulate the threads of reality in a certain way that corresponds to and is unique to that branch of arcane knowledge. This is something very complex that should imply a personal search on the part of the sorcerer and therefore has narrative roots.

Because of this concept, it should not be possible to learn level 1 of a magical talent without a teacher. The XP cost is the normal one for any talent, but there is no option to pay triple to compensate for the lack of a master.

Depending on the discipline, the search for the master will be more or less difficult, as will the care that initiated magicians take in keeping their secrets.

## Improve Magic Talents

Once a character has learned level 1 of a magic talent, they no longer need a teacher to instruct them at higher levels. They already understand the basis of the discipline that allows them to shape the world in that particular way. What remains is to increase their connection with that type of magic, a process that can be very personal.

The XP cost is normal for improving talents, but without a master it costs triple the points. This master must be at least 1 level above the character in the discipline in question.

## Learn Spells

Understanding the fundamentals of a magical discipline is only the first step toward mastering it. Then each concrete manifestation of its power, the spells, requires deep study to be understood, memorized, and then manifested in reality. When a sorcerer gains a new level of a magical discipline, they gain a free spell of that level, most likely the one they were narratively practicing while trying to increase their talent. The player can choose a spell of the corresponding level and add it to their list.

Beyond those free spells, the sorcerer can learn a new spell if they are able to read or hear its characteristics somewhere or if they are explained by a teacher, although they can also work it out on their own, which takes a week per level. Then they must pass an Intellect check, one attempt per quarter day. Once this is done, add the spell to the list after paying XP equal to its level. If they do not have a master, triple the cost of the spell.

## Sacrifice

To gain Willpower Points with which to cast their spells, the character must have pushed rolls and taken points of damage to their attributes. This implies that magic is an exchange of forces and energy, where the sorcerer gives part of themself so that their powers manifest.

With this concept in mind, and so that the game does not force sorcerers to wait for an opportunity to push rolls and suffer damage before being able to cast, we introduce the **sacrifice** rule.

When you want to cast a spell, you can voluntarily inflict damage on yourself to gain Willpower Points. Narratively, you are performing an exchange with your own body or mental state.

The damage can be distributed among several attributes, but with this method you can obtain up to a maximum of WP equal to your level in the discipline of the spell you are going to cast.

Sacrifices only apply to spells you are going to cast, or rituals you are going to initiate. You cannot use these WP for other purposes, nor can you save them to spend later.

## Ingredients as Consumables

Spell ingredients are a consumable, like torches. Individual units are not counted, but are listed as a die.

When dealing with rare ingredients, it will be necessary to roll the resource die more than once. In these cases, to consider that you have enough of the ingredient, you must have at least a D6 at the end of all rolls except the last one.

You can purchase a level of this die for 3 silver from an apothecary.

## Initial Spells

Because our rules of magic require the learning of each spell individually, the logical question arises as to how many spells the character begins his adventures with when he is created.

Each level of a magical talent allows the character to choose two spells from the discipline to have learned. You can substitute any of them for general spells.

## Grimoires

Grimoires are scrolls or books of magic, both very rare objects. Grimoires no longer serve to cast spells with a lower level than usual.

We must make a distinction between scrolls, grimoires that contain a single spell, and books that have the arcane theory of a discipline.

The **scrolls** allow you to cast a spell that the wizard has not yet learned, but since he does not fully understand it, reading and interpreting it takes him as much time as the casting time of the spell it contains: a quick action for a power word, a slow action for a normal spell, or a quarter of a day if it is a ritual.

## Risk Conjuration

The only way to cast spells of a higher level than your magic talent is through a grimoire.

Grimoires can contain spells of any level, making them available for you to experiment with powerful magic.

However, for every level the written spell is higher than your level in the talent, you must suffer an automatic magic mishap, since you do not understand the forces you are manipulating.

The scrolls serve as teachers to learn the spell.

The **magic books** fulfill the role of teachers in their discipline, and are as extremely rare as they are useful. Depending on the level it covers is the scope of the teacher they can have. The higher the level, the obviously greater its rarity and possibilities.

The books usually have several spells explained, which is the same as having several scrolls since the same rules apply.

Nothing prevents a character from writing a magic book with scrolls included. Each spell takes a quarter of a day and each level of discipline takes as many quarters of a day as its level.

## Official Characters

Because this book alters the basic rules of magic, it stands to reason that the character sheets in all official books must be altered to make sense.

Doubles the level of the characters' magical disciplines. Regarding the spells they have learned, simply allow them to cast the ones you consider appropriate for each moment.

## Magic Talents

What allows characters to have access to magic disciplines are the talents oriented to them. Each level of talent allows you to use up to that level of discipline.

We've changed the level cap for talents, which is actually already covered in the core rules. Don't restrict yourself to three levels as there is now a higher power cap for all disciplines.

On the other hand, now not only druids and sorcerers can access talents that allow them to use magic. There are exceptions in which other professions, but especially certain races, can also learn disciplines that are strongly associated with them. For this reason, the following table specifies which types of characters are capable of learning each talent.

Whoever has the weapon will be able to invoke the power to cause, unexpectedly for the enemy, a powerful magical wound. As the group prepares for combat, the sorceress lends her dagger to Pelek, who will be able to take advantage of the right moment._

ossessing knowledge about magic and the ability to manipulate it allows those people with this power Pthe possibility of manifesting certain magical effects that are typical of the management of the energy of the world and are not directly linked to any discipline.

The general spells, then, are common to all known disciplines, they are effects that any magic user can access if they have a sufficient level in a magical talent, whatever it may be.

When a character gains a level in any discipline, they can choose to learn one of these spells for free instead of one specific to the discipline. Likewise, learning general spells costs XP like all other spells.

## Magic Strike

- Rank: 1
- Range: Near
- Duration: Immediate
- Ingredient: Pebble (1 roll)

You can cast pure magical energy after concentrating it in your hands, altering the threads of reality in the direction of an enemy, who receives a powerful blow. The victim suffers 1 point of Strength damage and a critical injury from the bludgeoning table. The power level gained is doubled and added to the D66 result.

## Perceive Magic

- Rank: 1
- Range: Short
- Duration: Immediate
- Ingredient: Glass object (1 roll)

After learning this spell, you can automatically sense when someone is using magic or if an object is charged with magic, as long as it is within a **short** distance.

Casting this spell allows you to know exactly the effects of the magic you perceive, as long as you are able to understand it. This implies that the Power Level of this spell must be equal to or greater than the level of the magic you are sensing.

If you do not achieve a sufficient Power Level, you know the level of the magic but are not able to understand it, so its effects and discipline remain behind a veil of mystery.

## Magic Protection
- Rank: 1
- Range: Arm's Length
- Duration: Quarter Day
- Ingredient: Piece of wood (1 roll)

You protect a place or a living being with the objects they carries. The approximate total size will not be larger than a person. The Power Level grants dice protection to the place or living being.

Each time the protected is the target of a spell, roll the protection dice. Each success subtracts 1 from the spell's Power Level.

## Identify Object
- Rank: 2
- Range: Arm's Length
- Duration: Immediate
- Ingredient: Glass object (2 rolls)

By interpreting the threads of reality that weave an object, you can gain knowledge of all its characteristics. Not only if it has spells or curses, but also its age, materials and origin. All available information about the object will be revealed to you.

The Power Level allows you to overcome any effect that is hiding the information you seek. It may also be the case that there is a consciousness within the object that opposes the spell (such as an elven ruby  or a possessed weapon). In these cases, consciousness must overcome the Power Level with a Wits roll.

## Imbue Magic
- Rank: 2
- Range: Arm's Length
- Duration: One minute per Power Level
- Ingredient: Piece of clear fabric (1 roll)

By manipulating the threads of the world and the flow of energy in general, you can cause an object to retain magical power for a certain amount of time. This allows, for example, a weapon to deal magic damage, but does not offer any additional specific benefits. The duration of the effect is one minute per Power Level.

## Nullify Spell
- Rank: 2
- Range: Short
- Duration: Immediate
- Ingredient: Iron ore (1 roll)

By focusing near a place, object, or person that has an active spell, you can nullify the power of the magic that is concentrated there.

This spell always disperses the desired magic as long as the Power Level is equal to or greater than the level of the spell you want to nullify, regardless of its Power Level.

## Conceal Spell
- Rank: 2, POWER WORD
- Range: Personal
- Duration: Ilimitada
- Ingredient: Piece of dark fabric (1 roll)

When you want to cast a spell that is difficult to perceive, the method at your disposal is Conceal Spell. To do this you must spend at least one Willpower Point on Conceal Spell in the normal way of casting.

Additionally, you roll dice to determine the Power Level of this spell. Any method of detecting or understanding the characteristics of the hidden spell must equal or exceed the Power Level you have achieved in Conceal Spell.

## Charge Object

- Rank: 3
- Range: Arm's Length
- Duration: One day
- Ingredient: Amethyst (1 roll)

You focus a large amount of magical energy into an object so that it becomes overloaded. The first living being to touch the object releases that entire charge at once, receiving as many points of damage to Strength as the Power Level of this spell in addition to flying 1D6 meters backwards due to the force unleashed. The victim can reduce the damage taken by 1 for every success rolled on an **endurance** check.

## Contain Spell
- Rank: 3
- Range: Personal
- Duration: Quarter Day per Power Level
- Ingredient: Aquamarine (2 rolls)

You can contain the power of a spell within an object to release it whenever you see fit. Each Power Level obtained is a quarter day that the spell remains contained in the item. Releasing a contained spell costs one fast action.

The original spell is cast at the same time as this one, but you must spend Willpower Points for each one as normal, and both can cause magic mishaps.

When the spell is released, it does so with the Power Level obtained in the original roll made at the time of containing it. Any character with the item can invoke it.

## Weaken Spell

- Rank: 3, POWER WORD
- Range: Short
- Duration: Immediate
- Ingredient: Iron ore (1 roll)

If a spell is cast in your presence, you can attempt to negate it at that moment with your own magic. Weaken Spell is reactive and breaks the initiative order.

The Power Level of the opponent's spell is reduced by a number of points equal to the Power Level you reach with Weaken Spell. If the result is zero or less, the effect is nullified. You can only use this spell on a newly cast spell; if you don't react in time, you won't be able to undo the magic later.

## Locate Magic
- Rank: 4
- Range: Distant
- Duration: Immediate
- Ingredient: Pointed object (1 roll)

By concentrating on feeling and recognizing the echoes of magic, you can follow with your mind the ripples that a spell leaves in reality.

Within a kilometer radius, you can know where magical effects are active, where spells are currently being cast, or where a ritual is being prepared. The number of spells you can locate is equal to the Power Level, prioritizing those that are closest.

## Transfer Energy
- Rank: 4
- Range: Near
- Duration: Immediate
- Ingredient: Bloody fabric (1 roll)

You know the way to mobilize the willpower of living beings through the magical threads that unite all things.

You can transfer Willpower Points from your pool to any other character, equal to the Power Level of this spell. Obviously, those invested in this spell do not count towards transferable points. It is also possible for another character to transfer their points to you voluntarily, respecting that maximum amount.

Likewise, you can steal Willpower Points from another character up to a maximum equal to Power Level -1. In this case, the victim will roll with a number of dice equal to their WP pool. Subtract 1 from the Transfer Energy Power Level equal to the successes rolled.

## Enchant Item

- Rank: 5, RITUAL
- Range: Arm's Length
- Duration: Permanent
- Ingredient: Quartz (2 rolls)

Through this ritual you can imbue an object with a spell, making it magical and giving it the ability to manifest the spell even if it is not wielded by a magic user.

You must cast the spell you wish to bind as normal. Rituals cannot be enchanted into objects. When you perform this ritual, both spells can cause magical mishaps, but casting the item spell is completely safe.

The Power Level of Enchant Item determines the number of dice that must be rolled each time the spell is cast within the item. Therefore, when summoned, it can at most have a Power Level equal to what you got by enchanting it.

Each success obtained when invoking the item's power translates into 1 Power Level. However, each 1 weakens the item's magic by one die for future casts, as if it were equipment that wears out.

You decide the method by which the spell is cast: by saying certain words, shaking the object, holding it in a certain way, opening it, or any other option you imagine.

To learn these details about an enchanted object without resorting to trial and error, it would be necessary to use the Identify Object.

You can use this same spell to recharge an enchanted item that is not fully charged. To do this you must do a ritual (quarter day) and spend 1 WP, but you must not make rolls nor run the risk of magic mishaps.

## Multiply Spell
- Rank: 5
- Range: Personal
- Duration: One round
- Ingredient: Amethyst (2 rolls)

You supercharge your ability to cast your magic, causing your next spell to be cast an additional time for each Power Level of Multiply Spell. The Power Level of the spell is the same in all its copies, but the target may be different.

You must cast one spell on your next turn at most or you will lose your accumulated magic power. Falling Broken or receiving any critical injury causes the accumulated power of Multiply Spell to disappear, even if you are healed immediately.

So much effort generates inevitable consequences on your body. The number of copies of the spell you cast (including the original) causes you a magic mishap.

## Magic Nova

- Rank: 6
- Range: Short
- Duration: Immediate
- Ingredient: Sapphire (3 rolls)

You overload the threads of reality around you, unleashing an uncontrolled magical explosion. This causes an impact on the soul of all living beings in the area of effect, but especially those with a connection to magic.

All characters in the spell's range take Strength and Wits damage equal to the Power Level, although they can reduce it by 1 for each success on an **endurance** check. Additionally, they receive a critical injury on the blunt force table: the Power Level gained is tripled and added to the D66 result.

You cannot differentiate between friends and enemies when using Magic Nova effects.

## Anti-Magic Zone

- Rank: 6, RITUAL
- Range: Long
- Duration: One day
- Ingredient: Aquamarine (3 rolls)

Through this ritual, you can reduce the flow of magical energies over a wide area, making it more difficult to cast spells and make them effective.

For the next day, all spells cast in the area reduce their Power Level by an amount equal to the Power Level of the ritual, regardless of who is casting it, even your own spells will be affected there.

## Healing

> _Stran lay on the bed of the humble inn, coughing uncontrollably. For four days, cold sweat had run through his entire body while his life was slowly fading away; he was no longer able to get up without falling after a few steps. His companions waited next to him, anxious, fearing the worst. It was at dusk that Ramzo arrived, he had been studying a ritual to cure his friend._

Mastering the secrets of life and applying that knowledge to assist in the healing of living beings is the ideal of any user of the Healing discipline.

There are many druids who focus their lives on becoming experts in this type of magic and thus fulfill their purpose according to the values transmitted from master to disciple for generations.

This discipline allows not only to prolong life, but also to make it fuller both in body and mind, as well as on a spiritual level, reaching transcendence in communion with nature at its highest levels. In that sense, there are no natural limits that cannot be overcome by the power of magic.

## Relieve Pain
- Rank: 1
- Range: Arm's Length
- Duration: Immediate
- Ingredient: Healing ointment (1 roll)

By placing your hands on a person, you can relieve the pain they are suffering, whether physical or mental. If the spell's Power Level equals or exceeds the amount of damage in the attribute you wish to relieve, the victim regains 1 point. You cannot use Relieve Pain on yourself.

## Relieve Condition
- Rank: 1
- Range: Arm's Length
- Duration: Quarter Day per Power Level.
- Ingredient: Incense (1 roll)

By placing your hands on another person's shoulders, you can completely relieve the conditions they are suffering or are going to suffer. The conditions are still there, but the character does not suffer damage or debuff from them for the duration of the spell. You cannot use Relieve Status on yourself.

The Power Level is tripled and must be equal to or greater than the Virulence of the disease to make it disappear.

## Cure Poison

- Rank: 1
- Range: Arm's Length
- Duration: Immediate
- Ingredient: Healing herbs (1 roll)

You stop the effect of any poison that is affecting a person, including yourself. The substance disintegrates and stops inflicting damage on its victim. The Power Level is tripled and must be equal to or greater than the Potency of the poison to neutralize it.

## Raise Corpse
- Rank: 2, RITUAL
- Range: Short
- Duration: Quarter Day
- Ingredient: Brain mass (2 rolls)

Through this spell you manage to achieve the main objective of Necromancy: reanimate the dead. To do this you must have access to a corpse and prepare a ritual. After all the evocation of mystical energies, the dead rises again in its new form. At power level 1 you can animate the corpse as a Restless Dead, at level 2 the corpse leaves behind all its flesh to rise as a Skeleton, and at level 3 you can reanimate the corpse as a powerful Ghoul. You can reduce the Power Level by 1 to extend the duration of the spell by an extra quarter of a day. You can see the points of these undead in the Gamemaster's Guide.

## Inhabit Corpse
- Rank: 2
- Range: Short
- Duration: One turn per Power Level
- Ingredient: Brain mass (1 roll)

You can transfer your consciousness to that of a corpse, with which you are able to speak, move, and even cast spells. In other words, the corpse becomes your body, while your true body remains inert in the place where you were.

Inhabit Corpse allows you to move the body, but it is very weak and clumsy, considering its Strength and Agility as if they had a single point. You can also inhabit even a small part of a corpse, such as a hand or head, with the limitations that this entails.

## Talk to Spirits
- Rank: 2

- Range: Short
- Duration: One minute per Power Level
- Ingredient: Piece of old fabric (1 roll)

## Enter the Twilight
- Rank: 4, RITUAL

- Range: Personal
- Duration: One minute per Power Level
- Ingredient: Old fabric (2 rolls)

You can leave your body behind for a few minutes, causing your soul to enter the Twilight, the world beyond the physical plane in which the dead dwell. There your vision is supernatural, there is no light or darkness, and the spirits wander restlessly.

In the Twilight there is no space as in the physical plane, and you can transport yourself wherever you want with your mere will in moments (to a place that you know or can visualize). However, you are unable to perceive the physical world as anything other than confusing shadows and distant echoes. Trying to communicate with the living is just as difficult and enigmatic. Still, you can see their souls directly as if through a dark mirror.

The actions of the living cast a shadow in the Twilight: the more death or emotional impact they have caused, the greater the distortion in the place, which can become very confusing and changing. Many creatures have left their spectral impression there or have not been able to achieve peace even if they cannot manifest themselves on the physical plane, especially those who have suffered a violent or unjust death, or have unfinished business. You are able to speak with these spirits telepathically as if it were natural.

Within the Twilight you only exist with your Wits and Empathy points. If you are Broken, your soul is lost and takes 1D6 hours to rebuild (heal 1 point), enough time for another being to possess your empty body.

You can cast Necromancy spells during your journey through the Twilight, those that do not affect a physical body.

Once the duration of this spell has expired, your soul is sucked into your body at dizzying speed, unless it has been occupied by another entity.

## Demonic Magic

> _The prisoner, chained and sobbing, felt her tears turn into black flames, announcing the end of the ritual. The sorcerer had finished working on his work, sculpting with mog the once scattered bones, now united to the girl's back, causing a pair of demonic wings to emerge that stood with gloomy majesty._

he origins of this discipline in Ravenland are completely hidden by the mists of time and Tmystery. It is very unlikely that humans developed it, much less dwarves and elves, so the only credible candidates are the demons of Churmog themselves, the other world that lies behind the veil, influencing mortals with their insidious whispers, awaiting the moment for a total invasion.

And they did it. Their greatest success was tempting Zygofer to experiment with all the means at his disposal in order to expand this discipline in a world that was not ready. Already mad with power, after having corrupted the body and mind of countless people and finding himself cornered in a losing war, he did what so many demons had been patiently waiting for: he opened a portal for them so that they could pass through the veil, like hungry animals after the smell of fresh blood.

Demonic magic is ultimately a connection with something unnatural, foreign to this world. It is becoming increasingly corrupt by pursuing an end that only the sorcerer, in the best of cases, can glimpse from a distance. Perhaps there is someone in the Forbidden Lands who uses this discipline for good, but if so, he is surely paying too high a price.

## Generate Mog

- Rank: 1
- Range: Personal
- Duration: Immediate
- Ingredient: Moss (1 roll)

You manage to open a tiny crack towards Churmog so that the lilac green substance known as mog flows from the palm of your hands. One unit of mog per Power Level emerges, as a weak stream that falls to the ground.

Mog is a liquid that is as dangerous as it is mysterious. It has many uses, although all perverse, and is under constant study by the few who aspire to decipher all its secrets.

Mog is corrosive to skin and flesh, causing 1 point of damage to Strength for each turn in contact with it unless a Hard (-2) **endurance** check is passed. This damage corresponds to the exposure of a single part of the body to the substance, each part can suffer 1 point of damage and requires the roll.

Mog can also join biological parts if used properly, creating new nerve connections and blood channels that function to supplant limbs, add new ones, or even fuse living beings. However, these procedures are difficult to understand and carry out, and are at the discretion of the Gamemaster.

Normally, you will not be able to use the mog as an attack, unless you position your hands in such a way that the stream comes out in front and can touch an enemy within **arm's length** (it will not come out with enough force to reach a greater distance). In such cases, each unit thrown can drench a part of the victim's body to force the aforementioned **endurance** check.

The mog is an essential requirement for some spells in this discipline that cannot be cast unless you have the units indicated in their description. It is most advisable to carry mog in a barrel.

E RARITY: Strange E SECRETISM: Demonstration INGREDIENTS: Spells in this discipline are not cast with ingredients, so their Power Level cannot be increased by this means.

DEEP SLEEP: The ingredients are replaced by deep sleep. A character can only reach this level of rest if they **sleep** while **camping** or if they are calm in a comfortable bed in a room, whether in a house, inn, or fortress. Deep sleep adds 1 to the spell's Power Level.

RANGE: In the case of this discipline, you will notice that the spells usually have **distant** and **unlimited** range. Since the sorcerer is asleep when casting the spell, **distant** refers to a distance relative to the place where they are, like an entire town around them, while **unlimited** knows no distance limits. In both cases, the oneiromancer must have clear in their mind the name or face of the character they want to affect.

PREPARATION: The great limitation of this discipline, although also its meaning of existence, is that its spells must be cast while the sorcerer is asleep. To achieve this, the oneiromancer must be clear about what spells they will cast during their dreams and on which characters. This is a very difficult skill that the sorcerer trains during their period of learning the discipline. The oneiromancer must choose which spells they will cast, toward which characters, and how many Willpower Points they will spend on each, all before falling asleep. Willpower Points are not spent until the spell is finally cast.

SACRIFICE: The sorcerer can sacrifice their attribute points to gain Willpower Points and use them in their spells of this discipline while they sleep. However, they will not recover those points during that rest. They can choose to use the sacrifice rule just before casting the spell, they do not have to do it before going to sleep.

INTERRUPTION: The spells of this discipline can take effect while the sorcerer is asleep. This does not mean that the character must sleep an entire quarter of a day for the spell to work; it is enough for them to be asleep within the period of time indicated in the duration. If the character cannot finish their **sleep** activity during a trip, for example, they may end up **sleepy** but it does not mean that the spell has not worked normally; are independent facts.

To function, it always requires one or more pieces of iron that the sorcerer can have in their hands as a catalyst for power, which is why they usually wear metal gauntlets as well as heavy weapons and armor. This does not mean that the pieces they carry have to be the target of the spells, but rather that they are required components to cast them.

Although the Ironbrows are a very small and exclusive part of the Rust Brothers who are mainly present in Alderland, throughout History a few sorcerers have been sent to carry out missions in other territories, leaving a mark with their very useful manipulations of magic, which are seen as proof of the presence of the god Rust in the world and the power he can grant to his greatest followers.

RELIGIOUS FERVOR: The belief that Magnetism is a manifestation of Rust's power can cause magic to be enhanced through appropriate religious rituals. It is common for sorcerers to pray to their god before having to face a situation where they need their spells. Their magic will be boosted by 1 level for one turn (15 minutes) if the sorcerer spends one minute of fervent, uninterrupted prayer. If they do it for an entire turn (15 minutes) in front of a statue of their god, this buff will last for a quarter day. If they are not in a quiet place, they must pass an **insight** check to maintain the necessary concentration, but the Gamemaster will impose a penalty between -1 and -3 depending on the noise or stress situation in which they find theirself.

Many Ferrale have been initiated into Magnetism or have surrounded themselves with these sorcerers as a way to exert their dominion more effectively. It is believed that Teramalda herself was an expert in this discipline, she had ironbrows in her ranks, or that Rust himself granted her his highest gifts to survive and become the living armor that roams the Forbidden Lands.

Over the centuries some Ironbrow have had the permission or the audacity to teach their secrets to a select few who have kept the discipline alive in lands beyond Alderland and who today are seen as true champions of the faith, as admired by the Order as they are feared by their enemies when they see firsthand what they are capable of achieving.

## Iron in Objects

The magic of this spell allows you to magnetize an object containing iron, causing it to adhere to metal surfaces. Among the many narrative possibilities, with some creativity you can make very useful artifacts like a compass.

The magic of this discipline is mainly based on manipulating objects that contain iron, but they do not have to be completely iron nor be pure iron.

Whenever you read about an iron object, the rules also include iron parts, and even iron alloys like steel.

Likewise, if "metal" is mentioned, only those metals that contain iron are included.

- Duration: Quarter Day per Power Level You can magically charge one of your iron objects to point the direction of a character carrying iron objects, like a compass. The trail does not last more than a full day and is cut when crossing water.

## Reparation

- Rank: 1
- Range: Arm's Length
- Duration: One turn per Power Level

The object will move according to the movement you have achieved with the Power Level as if it were a character in a running action. If you don't even reach level 1, it means you can't move the object.

By manipulating the iron of an object that has been broken, you can put its pieces back together and make them stay together for a limited time. You restore as many points as the Power Level: in the case of a weapon its Gear Bonus, and in the case of an armor its Armor Rating. When the effect comes to an end, the pieces separate again. ## Iron Fingers
- Rank: 2
- Range: Near
- Duration: One minute per Power Level Through this spell you gain precise control over iron objects that allows you to manipulate them from a distance as if you had your hand on them. This magic has a variety of applications that are very difficult to determine, but primarily it gives you a bonus to your **sleight of hand** rolls equal to the Power Level to apply to small objects made of iron, such as chains, locks, and shackles, without the need for keys or lockpicks. Another possibility is to stop a weapon in place, preventing the character from being able to draw or brandish it freely; In cases like these, opposing your control requires a **might** roll with a penalty equal to the Power Level (which does not count as an action).

## Attract Object
- Rank: 2

- Range: Short
- Duration: Immediate

This spell allows you to attract an object containing iron toward your own object, as if you had a powerful magnet. Power Level determines movement speed, but decreases by 1 for each weight class: **light** -1, **normal** -2, and **heavy** -3.

If another character has the object, they can oppose the power of the spell by making a **might** roll to reduce the Power Level for each success and prevent you from attracting it, this includes weapons they are holding (otherwise they are disarmed).

If you do this with armor or other clothing containing iron, you draw the character towards you but the Power Level is reduced by the weight of their body. An average would be -3, but the Gamemaster must determine it in each case.

## Ferrous Shot
- Rank: 2
- Range: Short
- Duration: Immediate

You turn an iron object into a lethal projectile that you throw from your hands at the speed of an arrow. You gain a bonus to **marksmanship** equal to the Power Level. The object becomes a throwing weapon with Gear Bonus +0 and damage 1; The Gamemaster must determine the type of damage.

The objects that are already weapons lose their features since they are not being used as they were designed to be effective. For example, a sword affected by this spell simply does not have the same potential nor will it cause the same damage as if swung normally, it is just being thrown at high speed. Even an arrow does not have the same direction and penetration capacity as if shot from a bow.

## Ceremonial Armor
- Rank: 6, RITUAL
- Range: Arm's Length
- Duration: Quarter Day per Power Level You charge an armor with magnetic magic, which becomes increasingly resistant as you complete the ritual. When it is finished, its different pieces are united by an unbeatable force and any small damage they may suffer is instantly repaired by the magic that molds the iron to its original and perfect shape.

As it is more resistant and its parts are perfectly joined, its Armor Rating is doubled to neutralize any attack, no matter how precise it may be. Results of 1 do not degrade it as it is invulnerable. Only magic capable of shaping metal could harm it.

The magic of this ritual can only be used on plate armor or similar, those that can cover the entire body. The best case scenario would be an armor forged specifically for this ritual.

## Teramalda

This book assumes that Ferrale Teramalda was wearing plate armor with Ceremonial Armor magic when she was captured by the dwarves. In her agony, either because she was a great sorceress of Magnetism, because she obtained help, or because she was truly blessed by her god, the spells Ferrous Strength and Immortal Iron were cast on her, which have remained permanently active after her death.

The character who is pierced by iron can never again be a mortal. It would be expected that it is a volunteer, since no one in his right mind would make a being immortal against their will and then have them as an enemy.
